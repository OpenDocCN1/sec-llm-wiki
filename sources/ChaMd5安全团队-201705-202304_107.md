![](../Images/40893858e9ce0a07d023e76d5bddae21.png)查看源码发现会像 changeapi.php 里面提交数据

![](../Images/5f0b8c6ac09c488349c9e413a75f9a59.png)

![](../Images/267b509eb600755575da93bf45c1f5bb.png)

![](../Images/938e8f3f4fd67eac188b116b3337db78.png)
