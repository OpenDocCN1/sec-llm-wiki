>       http://seclists.org/nmap-dev/2016/q1/273 \[Daniel Miller\]
>     
> *   Integrated all of your IPv4 OS fingerprint submissions from October to  
>       January (536 of them). Added 104 fingerprints, bringing the new total to  5089. Additions include Linux 4.2, more Windows 10, IBM i 7, and more.  Highlights: http://seclists.org/nmap-dev/2016/q1/270 \[Daniel Miller\]

Highlights:  http://seclists.org/nmap-dev/2016/q1/271 \[Daniel Miller\]
