## 1.免杀过程

安装nim先安装mingw

https://nim-lang-cn.org/install\_windows.html

![](../Images/b269513eed4275c47d3da9b35a93f75c.png)

![](../Images/ab6f63d9ff71a9eb423c006780df3a8c.png)

配置环境变量：

![](../Images/e295889d3359a9ce107b5b2c2b54eb33.png)

在nim文件夹下.\\finish.exe根据提示完成安装

![](../Images/d8f46c176b0fd27f9caa6894a962332a.png)

踩坑，切记安装nim的路径不能用中文路径。不然后续会出错

![](../Images/6c6b583c5c294fab909b7bcdadcc8c3b.png)

https://github.com/khchen/winim安装winim（中文路径下的nim会报错显示找不到bin文件）

再去下载nimshellcode的加载器

https://github.com/aeverj/NimShellCodeLoader

![](../Images/5e20d8fdf71d0bf5d8e6f7ca1a6a89b5.png)

编译这两个文件，然后点击就行了

![](../Images/6de2c9357579f074de2ab4b980d50043.png)

![](../Images/96806b8f5c65bd59ccdcc65eb4699e40.png)

![](../Images/6a9569d6c6293adbc4330d33692daf52.png)

shellcode path直接拖文件进去，不是输入路径

生成的exe文件

![](../Images/4a179d8d1ff7324826812ddd235829e1.png)

测试免杀效果

![](../Images/c97e6f7a937a024a407d377fc8fc7e9c.png)

动态查杀

![](../Images/6648a8ad3c2ae5e6f93121a33b27e468.png)

完了完了，不过一直不操作的话也是能上线

![](../Images/5711cfda36ca635c70706a91ed3dd39f.png)
