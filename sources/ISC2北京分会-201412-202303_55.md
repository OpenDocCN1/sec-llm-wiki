# 【活动通知】(ISC)²北京分会第12期沙龙：「移动和可穿戴设备的安全及管理」

时间：2016年10月11日（周二）晚上18:30-20:30

  

地点：北京饭店莱佛士（会议室待通知）

  

演讲者：Alexander Trewby（谷歌）

  

演讲者介绍：Alexander Trewby works for Google and is responsible for Enterprise Wearables. Before Google, he co-founded Divide, a mobile securiy startup which was acquired by Google in May, 2014. Prior to that, Alexander spent 10 years working for Morgan Stanley in New York where he was Vice President of Mobile. Alexander holds a B.S. in Economics from City University and currently lives with his son in London.

  

演讲议题：Mobility and Wearable Device Security and Management

  

议题介绍：

It will cover the following areas:

*   Enterprise mobility in the West
    
*   Management technology
    
*   Device management
    
*   Application management
    
*   Identity and access management
    
*   Content management
    
*   Containment
    
*   Wearable devices and the future of enterprise mobile management
