本期(ISC)2北京分会请来Jim Molini分享他与(ISC)2的经历。Jim于1993年获得CISSP证书，是CSSLP第一人和该证书的主要架构师，他对(ISC)2的多个认证都有贡献，在(ISC)2美洲顾问委员会任职8年。Jim目前是微软云安全部门的高级经理。


在本次沙龙活动，Jim将花1小时介绍CISSP知识域在2015年新的变化、信息安全趋势对亚洲安全从业人员的影响、CISSP考试的学习策略，和提升个人专业技能的经验技巧。第2小时是互动环节，Jim将回答大家关于信息安全、云安全的开放性提问。


Jim Molini平时在美国西雅图，这次在北京的交流机会很难得，欢迎报名！


议题介绍：


The CISSP Certification Program: One Perspective


Later this year, the new CISSP exam will be available in Chinese. Jim Molini was one of the first people to receive the CISSP credential in 1993. He has helped (ISC)2 develop several of the credentials offered to security professionals. In this presentation, he will talk about the new CISSP certification exam and changes to the domains of knowledge. He will also describe changes in the field of information security and how those changes affect security professionals in Asia. His presentation will include strategies for studying to take the CISSP exam and tips on developing your professional expertise. After the presentation he will answer your questions about information and cloud security.


The presentation will be given in English.


嘉宾简介：


Jim is a Senior Program Manager at Microsoft, on the Operations Team for cloud identity services in the Identity and Security Services Division.


He has more than 20 years experience in the field of Information Security, including extensive experience in system and software security, intrusion detection, and risk management. He has worked in government and the private sector, including time as CEO of Hyperon, Inc., VP of Data Security at First USA Bank, and Computer Security Coordinator for NASA?ˉs Space Shuttle Flight Software development team.


Jim has been a CISSP for more than 20 years and sat on the America?ˉs Advisory Board at (ISC)2 for 8 years He was also a primary architect of the CSSLP certification. He has written and spoken internationally on the topic of Information Security since 1989. He won the (ISC)2 President?ˉs Award in 2001 and 2010.

关于(ISC)2北京分会：


(ISC)2是推出信息安全领域金牌认证CISSP的美国非盈利教育组织，在中国的(ISC)2北京分会成立于2014年11月1日，专注信息安全，旨在建立北京及周边地区的一个安全技术交流的网络，促进分会会员之间的信息分享、经验交流、技术讨论和个人职业发展。
