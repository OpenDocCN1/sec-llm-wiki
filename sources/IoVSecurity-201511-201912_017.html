演讲题目："电动汽车、自动驾驶汽车以及未来移动出行的网络安全注意事项。''

本演讲将调查和分析汽车行业中将影响电动汽车，自动驾驶和未来出行市场的最新网络安全趋势。我们将研究车辆内部网络安全功能的采用情况，与车辆结构和安全运营中心相关的因素。我们还将研究WP.29以及ISO / SAE 21434引入的驱动因素以及型号批准的影响。


演讲嘉宾：Paul Sanderson，网络安全高级技术专家，SBD

个人介绍：Paul专门研究网络安全中的威胁评估和漏洞分析（TARA），并领导SBD的网络安全团队。他领导并参与了发起Jaguar Land Rover网络安全计划的团队，并曾在国防行业工作，在复杂软件和系统开发的整个生命周期中拥有22年的知识和经验。他对安全开发生命周期，在成熟度模型中建立安全性，安全模式和汽车网络安全感兴趣。他是特许IT专业人员，并且是英国计算机学会的会员，并且经常在有关汽车网络安全性的行业会议上发表演讲。


Presentation Topics: “Cybersecurity considerations for EV, Autonomous and the future of Mobility.”

This presentation will investigate and analyse the latest cybersecurity trends seen in the automotive industry which will affect the EV, autonomous and future mobility market.  We will investigate the adoption of cybersecurity features within the vehicles, the factors to consider with respect vehicle architecture and security operations centre.  We will investigate what the drivers are and the effects of up and coming type approvals within WP.29 and the introduction of ISO/SAE 21434.


Speaker: Paul Sanderson, Cyber Security Senior Technical Specialist, SBD

Bio: Paul specialises in Threat Assessment and Vulnerability Analysis, (TARA) within Cybersecurity and leads SBDs Cybersecurity Team. He led and was part of the team that initiated Jaguar Land Rover’s Cybersecurity program and has previously worked in the defence industry with 22 years knowledge and experience across the full lifecycle of complex software and systems development. He is interested in the Security Development Lifecycle, Building Security in Maturity Model, Security Patterns and Automotive Cyber Security He is a chartered IT professional and member of the British Computer Society and often presents at industry conferences speaking on Automotive Cybersecurity
