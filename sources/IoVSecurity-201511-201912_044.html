演讲题目：“当车联网信息安全遇到安全认证“

随着汽车越来越高的智能化以及互联网化，车联网的信息安全也飞速发展，而潜在危险系数也随之增高，车联网的信息安全成为汽车安全的「新战场」。

本演讲从车联网面临的信息安全威胁出发，引出国内外主流信息安全认证机制，并示例说明车联网产品信息安全认证的流程、必要性。


演讲嘉宾：张海春，开源网安车联网安全实验室高级研究员

简介：多年安全行业从业经验，熟悉主流安全信息技术、风险评估模型，擅长安全评估、信息安全测试。个人研究方向为集成电路设计，嵌入式系统安全、汽车信息安全、网络安全。曽参与多个研究项目：某研究所舰船电力监测系统调试项目、FPGA老化仿真研究项目、某研究所PUF项目、某公司汽车风险评估等。




Presentation Topics: " When IOV Security Meets Security Certification "

With the increasing intelligence and Internet of automobiles, the information security of the Internet of Vehicles is also developing rapidly, and the potential risk factor is also increasing. The information security of the Internet of Vehicles has become a "new battlefield" for vehicle safety. This speech starts from the information security threats faced by the Internet of Vehicles, and leads to the mainstream information security authentication mechanism at home and abroad, and illustrates the process and necessity of the information security certification of the Internet of Vehicles products.


Speaker: Haichun Zhang, Senior researcher,  Vehicle Security Laboratory, Seczone Group

Bio: Mr. Zhang has many years of experience in the security industry, familiar with mainstream security information technology, risk assessment model, good at security assessment, information security testing. Personal research direction is integrated circuit design, embedded system security, automotive information security, and network security. 曽 Participated in a number of research projects: a ship's ship power monitoring system commissioning project, FPGA aging simulation research project, a research institute PUF project, a company's automotive risk assessment.
