**演讲题目**：“ 国内外汽车信息安全趋势分析及测试技术研究 “

• 国内外汽车信息安全现状分析；

• 国内外汽车信息安全趋势分析

• 以及国外汽车信息安全测试技术研究。

  

**演讲嘉宾**：侯昕田，信息安全测试研究部项目经理，检测认证事业部软件测评中心，中国汽车技术研究中心有限公司

**个人介绍**：侯昕田，女，山西人，毕业于湖南大学，现任中国汽车技术研究中心有限公司检测认证事业部软件测评中心信息安全测试研究部项目经理。参与多项汽车信息安全标准编写，有丰富的汽车信息安全项目经验，熟知信息安全测评技术。

**Presentation Topics**: " Research on Automotive Cybersecurity Trend and Testing Technology in the Domestics and Overseas"

• The current situation analysis of automobile information security at home and abroad;

•  Analysis of domestic and foreign automobile information security trends; 

•  and Foreign automobile information security test technology research.

  

**Speaker:** Xintian Hou, Department of Security Testing and Research  PM, Detection and Certification Division ·Software Evaluation Center, China Automotive Technology and Research Center Co.,Ltd.

**Bio:** Ms. Hou xintian, born in Shanxi Province, graduated from hunan university, is currently the project manager of  security testing and research department of Software Evaluation Center, China Automotive Technology and Research Center Co.,Ltd. ，participated in making of automotive cyber-security standards，with rich experience in automotive security projects, familiar with automotive security assessment technology.
