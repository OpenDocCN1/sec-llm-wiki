Future Value Chain Integration of Cybersecurity in Car


The future involves OEMs patenting cybersecurity technologies or acquire specialist cybersecurity companies to stay ahead of the competition.



OEMs will be pushed towards Cybersecurity Solutions


Cybersecurity is a cost constraint to OEMs as it cannot be offered as a feature to the end consumer. OEMs today have to bare the cost of securing their cars.


OEM Activities


Luxury brands like BMW, Audi and Mercedes Benz are working with specialist companies to improve in vehicle security and Volvo, GM, and Ford are currently focusing on securing connectivity and infotainment systems.


How is Aviation Industry Framing it’s Cybersecurity Solutions


The aviation industry has complex design and architecture, the NIST, FIPS, ISO, ISACA COBIT, RTCA, EUROCAE, CIPAC, CSIRT, CERT, CERIAS and CAST are working together to standardize the system


Secure Technology Layers that Address Safety

Engineers in the automotive industry are incorporating security solutions from design and production stages. This will take a considerable amount of time as there are no standardizations yet.
