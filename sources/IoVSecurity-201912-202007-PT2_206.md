**演讲题目**：“ 探讨特定于V2V通信的网络安全挑战和对策；权衡DSRC，C-ITS和C-V2X部署的优势和挑战“  

  

**演讲嘉宾**：田野，网络安全研究员/项目经理，安全技术部，中国移动通信集团公司研究院  

**个人介绍**：田野博士是中国移动研究院安全技术部网络安全研究员和项目经理。他从事C-V2X安全和蜂窝网络安全研究多年，目前主要致力于垂直行业的4G / 5G网络服务，C-V2X安全解决方案研究，C-V2X安全技术标准化和行业应用推广。他毕业于西安电子科技大学，并于2009年获得通信和信息系统博士学位。在为中国移动服务之前，他是中国电信技术研究院大唐移动的网络系统研究和标准工程师。

* * *

  

  
**Presentation Topics:** "Cybersecurity challenges specific to V2V communications; Weighing the benefits and challenges of DSRC, C-ITS and C-V2X“

  

**Speaker:** Ye Tian, Network security researcher and Project manager, Department of Security Technology, Research Institute of China Mobile Communications Corporation

**Bio**: Dr. Tian Ye is network security researcher and project manager in department of security technology, research institute of China Mobile. He is engaged in C-V2X security and cellular network security research for many years, currently mainly focus on 4G/5G network service for vertical industries, C-V2X security solution research, C-V2X security technology standardization and industry application promotion. 

He graduated from Xidian University and obtained PhD in communication and information system in 2009. Prior to serving China Mobile, he was a network system research and standard engineer at Datang Mobile, China Academy of Telecommunications Technology.
