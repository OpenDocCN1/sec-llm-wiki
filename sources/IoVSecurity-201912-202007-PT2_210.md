**演讲题目**：“面向智能网联汽车大数据安全可信共享的云链融合模型与方法“

  

**演讲嘉宾**：黄永峰，清华大学电子系和清华信息科学技术国家实验室教授，博士，博导，副所长

**个人介绍**：黄永峰，清华大学电子系和清华信息科学技术国家实验室教授，博士，博导，副所长，闽江学者讲座教授，首届全国十佳网络安全优秀教师。主要从事网络及网络安全技术的研究和教学。先后主持了国家自然基金委重点、面上基金，科技部重点研发专项、973、863和支撑计划等项目30多项。现为IEEE Senior Member、中国电子学会信息隐藏与多媒体安全专家委员会委员和云计算专家委员会委员、中国计算机学会互联网专家委员会委员、中国兵器学会信息安全与对抗专家委员会委员等。担任了多家国际著名期刊编委和重要国际会议Co-chairs等。已在IEEE Transaction IFS和中国科学等国内外著名期刊和AAAI和ACL等重要国际会议发表论文100多篇，其中SCI收录40余篇；出版专著4部、译著2部，教材2部。申请和授权发明专利10余项。获省部级科技成果奖一等奖1项、二等奖3项等主编的《计算机网络教程》获中国电子教育学会优秀教材一等奖；多次获清华大学教学成果奖、先进工作者和优秀共产党员等荣誉。

**Presentation Topic:** "Cloud chain fusion model and method for intelligent connected vehicle big data security and trusted sharing"

  

**Speaker: **Yongfeng Huang, Professor, Ph.D., Ph.D Supervisor, Deputy Director, Minjiang Scholar Chair Professor, Department of Electronics and Tsinghua National Laboratory for Information Science and Technology, Tsinghua University,

**Bio:** Yongfeng Huang, Professor, Ph.D., Ph.D Supervisor, Deputy Director, Minjiang Scholar Chair Professor, Department of Electronics and Tsinghua National Laboratory for Information Science and Technology, Tsinghua University, the first National Top Ten Excellent Teacher of Network Security. Mainly engaged in research and teaching of network and network security technology. He has chaired more than 30 projects including the National Natural Science Foundation of China's key and general funds, the Ministry of Science and Technology's key R & D projects, 973, 863, and support plans. He is currently a IEEE Senior Member, a member of the Information Hiding and Multimedia Security Expert Committee of the Chinese Institute of Electronics and a member of the Cloud Computing Expert Committee, a member of the Internet Expert Committee of the Chinese Computer Society, and a member of the Information Security and Confrontation Expert Committee of the Chinese Ordnance Society. He has served as a member of several internationally renowned journal editors and important international conferences such as Co-chairs. He has published more than 100 papers in IEEE Transaction IFS, Chinese science and other well-known domestic and foreign journals, and important international conferences such as AAAI and ACL. Among them, more than 40 have been included in SCI. He has published 4 monographs, 2 translations, and 2 textbooks. Applied and authorized more than 10 invention patents. The "Computer Network Tutorial" edited by the provincial and ministerial level scientific and technological achievements awards 1 first prize, 2 second prizes and 3 prizes won the first prize of outstanding teaching materials of the Chinese Institute of Electronics Education; many times won the Tsinghua University teaching achievement award, advanced workers and Outstanding Communist Party members and other honors.
