**演讲题目**：“车联网安全未来在电动汽车，自动驾驶，移动出行等领域的考量”

本演讲将调查和分析汽车行业中最新的网络安全趋势，这些趋势将影响电动汽车，自动驾驶和未来的出行市场。我们将研究车联网安全功能的采用情况，与车辆结构和安全运营中心相关的考虑因素。我们还将介绍WP.29和ISO / SAE 21434。

  

**演讲嘉宾：**周罡松，车联网安全专家，SBD Automotive

**个人介绍**：周先生毕业于英国伯明翰大学网络安全专业（GCHQ认证），在网络安全领域尤其是车联网安全领域有着多年的经验。他主要参与了日本，欧洲，北美和中国的OEM与供应商的车联网安全项目包含：威胁建模，渗透测试，风险评估，安全系统设计审查等。他还为两个由英国政府资助的创新项目提供顾问服务。现在他被派遣回中国主要负责SBD汽车的中国车联网安全相关项目。

  

* * *

  

**Presentation Topic: ** “Cybersecurity considerations for EV, Autonomous and the futureof Mobility.”

This presentation will investigate and analyse the latestcybersecurity trends seen in the automotive industry which will affect the EV,autonomous and future mobility market.  We will investigate the adoptionof cybersecurity features within the vehicles, the factors to consider withrespect vehicle architecture and security operations centre.  We willinvestigate what the drivers are and the effects of up and coming typeapprovals within WP.29 and the introduction of ISO/SAE 21434.

  

**Speaker: **Gangsong Zhou, Cyber Security Specialist, Secure Car Department, SBD Automotive 

  

**Bio:** Mr. Zhou has many years of work experience in automotive cybersecurity area and holds a MSc in Cyber Security degree which also certificated by GCHQ in accredited from University of Birmingham. He has token part into many automotive cybersecurity projects including thread modelling, penetration testing, risk assessment, security system designing and review etc for the OEMs and suppliers from Europe, Japan, NA, and China. He is also providing consultancy to two novel UK Government funded Innovate UK research programs. Now he relocates into China and mainly responsible for Chinese cyber security related projects in SBD Automotive.
