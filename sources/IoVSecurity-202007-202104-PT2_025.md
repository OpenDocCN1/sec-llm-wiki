演讲题目

  

**" 基于V2X安全芯片的5G车联网安全****"**

  

  

演讲者嘉宾

  

**王雪聪博士，北京华弘&华大智宝技术中心负责人、教授级高工、北京市科委专家。**

  

个人简介：王雪聪博士，北京华弘&华大智宝技术中心负责人、教授级高工、北京市科委专家。曾获得国家科学技术进步二等奖；获得多项核心技术发明专利；在国家级期刊发表论文十余篇；曾参与科技部、北京市科委多项重大专项评审，参与多项行业标准规范制定；承担工信部、电信运营商及行业认证机构多个重点项目建设，在物联网、无线通信、信息安全等领域有着多年的重大项目建设和创新产品的经验。

  

  

  
![](../Images/7ba06d8b4ca954808676fc8ce22f30da.png)

  

Topic

  

  

**"5g Internet of vehicles security based on v2x security chip ****"**

  

Speaker

  

**Dr. Xuecong **Wang **, Director of Beijing Huahong & Huada Zhibao technology center, professor level senior engineer and expert of Beijing Science and Technology Commission.**

  

  

Bio: Dr. Wang xuecong, director of Beijing Huahong & Huada Zhibao technology center, professor level senior engineer, expert of Beijing Science and Technology Commission. He has won the second prize of national science and technology progress award, won a number of core technology invention patents, published more than 10 papers in national journals, participated in a number of major special reviews of the Ministry of science and technology and Beijing Municipal Science and Technology Commission, and participated in the formulation of a number of industry standards and specifications. He has undertaken the construction of many key projects of the Ministry of industry and information technology, telecommunications operators and industry certification agencies, and has carried out the construction of Internet of things, wireless communication and information security We have many years of experience in major project construction and product innovation in the field of all-round development.

Personal profile: Chief Engineer of Automotive Information Security, Beijing Automotive Research Institute. 20 years of experience in the communications and security industry, formerly working for Gemalto, a leading global digital security company
