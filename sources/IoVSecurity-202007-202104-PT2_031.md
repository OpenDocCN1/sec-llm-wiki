演讲题目

  

**" ****智能网联汽车企业信息安全技术框架与最佳实践 ****"**

  

随着汽车越来越“电动化、智能化、共享化、网联化”，车企面临的网络安全风险也越来越多元化。国内造车势力要引领时代的变革，实现汽车行业的弯道超车，必然会在第一梯队迎接接踵而来的网络安全威胁与挑战。安全建设架构先行，优秀的计划和设计组织的、概念的、逻辑的、物理的组件的规程和相关过程，使这些组件能够以一致的方式进行交互，并与智能网络出行生态的业务需求相适应，从而使已存在或者即将面临的安全风险得到良好的管控。本次演讲主要阐述广汽蔚来在新时代汽车企业信息安全技术框架方面的探索和相关安全技术措施的实践分享。

议题亮点：智能网联汽车企业安全建设三个方向、NIST CSF和SABSA框架方法论分析、自动化安全工具链条规划与建设。

  

  

  

演讲者嘉宾

  

**郑霄，****信息安全负责人，****广汽蔚来新能源汽车科技有限公司**

广汽蔚来新能源汽车科技有限公司信息安全负责人，曾任中国网安三零卫士信息安全专家。安全牛CISO/CSO评审专家，IACIS(International Association of Computer Investigative Specialists)成员，BSC2020北京网络安全大会演讲嘉宾。拥有CISP、CISSP、Security+、TOEIC、GCFA等专业资质证书。专注于企业安全架构设计、智能网联安全建设、DevSecOps等领域，具有多年攻防经验，多次组织参与广东省市和全国的攻防演练。

Topic

  

  

**"Information Security Technology Framework and Best Practices of Intelligent Connected Vehicle Enterprise ****"**

  

As cars become more "electric, intelligent, shared, and networked," the network security risks faced by car companies are becoming more and more diversified. If domestic car-making forces want to lead the change of the times and realize the overtaking in the corners of the automotive industry, they will inevitably meet the coming cyber security threats and challenges in the first echelon. Safety construction architecture first, excellent planning and design of organizational, conceptual, logical, and physical component procedures and related processes, so that these components can interact in a consistent manner and adapt to the business needs of the intelligent network travel ecosystem , So that the existing or upcoming security risks are well managed. This speech mainly elaborated on the exploration of GAC NIO in the information security technology framework of automobile enterprises in the new era and the practical sharing of relevant security technology measures.

  

Topic highlights: Three directions for the safety construction of intelligent connected vehicle enterprises, analysis of the methodology of the NIST CSF and SABSA framework, and the planning and construction of the automated safety tool chain.

  

Speaker

  

**Edgesky Zheng,  Head of Information Security, GAC NIO New Energy Automobile **  

  

Bio: Edgesky is currently the Head of Information Security of GAC NIO New Energy Automobile Technology Co., Ltd., He was the former information security expert of China Electronic Technology Information Security 30Wish company.  CISO/CSO review expert of aqniu.com, member of IACIS (International Association of Computer Investigative Specialists), guest speaker at BSC2020 Beijing Cyber Security Conference. Edgesky have professional qualification certificates such as CISP, CISSP, Security+, TOEIC, GCFA, etc. Focusing on enterprise security architecture design, intelligent network security construction, DevSecOps and other fields, he has many years of offensive and defensive experience, and has organized and participated in offensive and defensive exercises in Guangdong Province, cities, and the whole country.
