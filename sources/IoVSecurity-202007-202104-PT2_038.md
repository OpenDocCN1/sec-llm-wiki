演讲题目

  

**" ****国内外汽车安全标准概况；智能汽车数据共享与安全；国汽的融合安全标准方案****"**

  

  

演讲者嘉宾

  

**廖继伟**

**智能网联汽车创新技术运营专家，高级总监**

**国汽（北京）智能网联汽车研究院有限公司**

  

  

个人介绍：25年汽车行业背景，10余年大型外企中高层管理经验，10年精益生产管理与项目管理，运营与供应链管理（制造业环境）经验，2年国家级新型研发机构运营经验；5年海外留学与工作背景。历任工程师，高级工程师，供应商质量经理，运营经理，总经理助理，运营总监，副总经理，供应链总监，供应商发展总监，高级精益培训师，工信部智能网联汽车技术与运营专家等职。接受过博世精益生产体系和丰田生产体系全面系统培训。

领导并参与博世工厂的重大供应链精益项目，核心供应商工业4.0项目，精通博世精益生产体系的实际运用，熟悉现代制造业的流程及运营管理；参与领导国家智能网联汽车创新中心筹建与运营管理，熟悉智能网联汽车前瞻共性交叉技术的现状和发展趋势。

  

供应链管理专家，精益生产与工业4.0资深专家，生产运营管理专家，智能网联汽车创新技术专家。国家智能网联汽车创新中心创始团队主要成员，参与制定国家智能网联汽车国家战略与技术路线，负责国汽智联对外合作、信息安全平台项目与云控平台项目。

Topic

  

** "Overview of **domestic** and overseas automobile security standards ; data sharing and **security** of smart cars; The integrated **security** standard scheme ****of China Intelligent and Connected Vehicles (Beijing) Research Institute Co.,Ltd"  **

  

  

Speaker

  

**Jiwei Liao, Intelligent and Connected Vehicle Innovation Technology Operation Expert, Senior Director, China Intelligent and Connected Vehicles (Beijing) Research Institute Co.,Ltd**

  

  

**Bio:**  25 years of automotive industry background, more than 10 years of middle and senior management experience in large foreign companies, 10 years of lean production management and project management, operation and supply chain management (manufacturing environment) experience, 2 years of national-level new research and development organization operation experience; 5 years of overseas study With working background. Previously held positions as engineer, senior engineer, supplier quality manager, operation manager, assistant general manager, director of operation, deputy general manager, director of supply chain, director of supplier development, senior lean trainer, intelligent networked automobile technology and operation expert of the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, etc. . Received comprehensive system training on Bosch Lean Production System and Toyota Production System.

  

Leading and participating in major supply chain lean projects of Bosch factory, core supplier industry 4.0 projects, proficient in the practical application of Bosch lean production system, familiar with modern manufacturing process and operation management; participated in leading the preparation and operation of the National Intelligent Networked Automobile Innovation Center Management, familiar with the current status and development trend of the forward-looking common cross technology of intelligent networked vehicles

  

Supply chain management expert, lean production and industry 4.0 senior expert, production operation management expert, intelligent networked automobile innovation technology expert. The main member of the founding team of the National Intelligent Connected Vehicle Innovation Center, participated in the formulation of the national strategy and technical route of the National Intelligent Connected Vehicle, and was responsible for the external cooperation, information security platform project and cloud control platform project of the National Automobile Intelligent Connected Vehicle.
