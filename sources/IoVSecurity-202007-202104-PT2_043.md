演讲题目

  

**" ****智能网联汽车道路测试示范区信息安全建设思考****"**

1、智能网联汽车道路测试安全保障需求

2、现有的法律法规与标准体系

3、智能交通系统网络和数据安全体系建议

4、推进建议

  

演讲者嘉宾

  

**王艳艳**

**上海智能网联汽车技术中心有限公司研究员**

  

  

个人介绍：王艳艳，CISP，现任上海智能网联汽车技术中心有限公司研究员，2016年以来一直从事智能网联汽车信息安全方向的研究，拥有丰富的智能网联汽车道路测试示范区的建设经验，参与了智能网联汽车多项政策法规和标准规范的制定。

  

  
![](../Images/7ba06d8b4ca954808676fc8ce22f30da.png)

  

Topic

  

  

**"Thinking about the Information Security Construction of the Road Test Demonstration Zone for Intelligent Connected Vehicles"**

  

1\. Security requirements for road testing of intelligent connected vehicles

2\. Existing laws, regulations and standard system

3\. Recommendations for intelligent transportation system network and data security system

4\. Advancing suggestions

  

Speaker

  

**Wang Yanyan**

**Researcher, Shanghai Intelligent Connected Automotive Technology Center Co., Ltd.**

  

  

Bio: Wang Yanyan, CISP, currently a researcher at Shanghai Intelligent Connected Vehicle Technology Center Co., Ltd., has been engaged in research on the information security of intelligent connected vehicles since 2016, has rich experience in the construction of intelligent connected vehicle road test demonstration areas, and participated in the formulation of a number of policies, regulations and standards for the intelligent connected vehicle .
