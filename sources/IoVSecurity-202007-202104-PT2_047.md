演讲题目

  

**" 互联车辆安全– GAS和第三方应用程序 "**

  

本演讲将介绍为什么联网汽车的安全性很重要和联网汽车的发展发展演进。您还将了解到汽车中新兴的安全性问题、采用Google汽车服务和第三方应用程序；Android漏洞需要频繁修补，这超越了OEM今天所能解决的能力。您还将听到有关数据隐私问题的信息，包括汽车行业的GDPR和Google Play保护数据收集可能不是透明的。

  

  

演讲者嘉宾

  

**Dragos Dabija, 安全经理, 埃森哲**

  

**个人介绍：**Dragos是负责IoT / OT / Connected Vehicle安全实践的安全经理，在IoT安全，汽车安全，Android安全，网络安全，嵌入式安全，PKI安全，云安全，应用程序安全等领域拥有8年以上的经验。 他拥有计算机工程学士学位和信息安全硕士学位。 他获得了CISSP，CISM，CSSLP，CIPM，CIPP / E的有效认证，并且正在进行汽车安全解决方案的专利申请。

  

  

  
![](../Images/7ba06d8b4ca954808676fc8ce22f30da.png)

  

Topic

  

** "****Connected Vehicles Security – GAS & 3rd Party Apps**** " **

  

This presentation will introduce why connected vehicle seuruity and evolutions of the connected vehicle. You will also learn the emerging security topics in automotive, and adoption of google automotive services& 3rd party apps; Android vulnerabilitites require frequent patching-more than OEM can handle today; you will also hear about data privacy issues including GDPR in automotive and Google play protect data collection may not be transparent.

  

  

  

Speaker

  

**Dragos Dabija, Security Manager, Accenture**

  
**Bio:**  Dragos is a security manager leading the Romanian IoT/OT/Connected Vehicle security practice, with more than 8 year experience in areas like : IoT Security,  Automotive Security, Android Security, Network Security, Embedded Security, PKI Security, Cloud Security, Application Security. 

 

He holds a bachelor degree in Computer Engineering and a Master Degree in Information Security. He has active certifications from CISSP, CISM, CSSLP, CIPM, CIPP/E and an ongoing patent application on an automotive security solution.
