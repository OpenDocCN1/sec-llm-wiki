演讲题目

  

**" TISAX---汽车行业供应链信息安全评估 "**

  

TISAX已成为德国汽车行业通用的信息安全能力要求，从OEM到传统零部件供应商，再到自动驾驶、智慧出行等更广泛的合作伙伴，整个产业链均遵循同样的信息安全标准（VDA-ISA），通过第三方审计机构出具信息安全审计报告，获得相应范围和级别的TISAX Label，既保护了OEM和供应商各自的关键信息资产和核心知识产权，同时也助于企业建立可持续改进的网络和信息安全治理能力。目前，国内已有近百家企业通过了严格的TISAX评估，这将有助于整个行业实现从ISMS到CSMS、从企业管理到产品/服务生命周期的全面安全可控。

  

  

演讲者嘉宾

  

**邬敏华，毕马威中国 管理咨询 副总监**

  

个人介绍：目前担任毕马威企业咨询（中国）有限公司网络安全服务的副总监，在信息安全领域有超过15年的工作经验。长期参与国家和行业网络和信息安全政策、标准制定，对ISO27001、ISO21434等信息安全标准，国内的网络安全法和等级保护都有深刻的理解和丰富的咨询成果。自2017年TISAX成为行业强制审计要求后，一直负责毕马威在国内的TISAX审计业务，已帮助超过50家国内外汽车相关企业成功通过TISAX评估。

Topic

  

** "TISAX---Information security assessment of automotive supply chain" **

  

TISAX has become the general information security capability requirement of German automotive industry. From OEM to traditional parts suppliers and more extensive partners such as automatic driving and intelligence mobility, the whole industry chain follows the same information security standard (VDA-ISA), and obtains the corresponding TISAX Label through the third-party audit report, which not only protects the key information assets and core IPs of OEMs and suppliers, but also helps enterprises establish continuous cyber governance capabilities. Nearly 100 enterprises have passed this strict assessment in China, which will help the whole industry to realize the comprehensive cyber security governance, from ISMS to CSMS, from enterprise management to the life cycle of its product & service.

  

  

Speaker

  

**Frank Wu, Associate Director of KPMG China**

  

Bio: As the Associate Director in Cyber services of KPMG China, Frank has more than 15 years' experience in cyber security. Participated in national and industrial information security policies and standards for a long time, he has a profound understanding and rich consulting achievements in ISO27001, ISO21434 and other cyber standards, and China cyber security law, MLPS as well. Since TISAX became an industry mandatory audit requirement in 2017, Frank has been responsible for KPMG's TISAX audit business in China and has helped 50+ enterprises successfully passed TISAX assessment.
