演讲题目

  

**“自动驾驶数据安全及评测体系研究”**

  

1、自动驾驶数据安全风险分析；

2、自动驾驶数据分级及安全防护架构；

3、信息安全评测体系研究。

  

  

演讲者嘉宾

  

**陈幼雷，首席安全架构师，智能汽车解决方案BU政策与标准专利部，华为技术有限公司**

  

**个人介绍**：陈幼雷，现任华为技术有限公司智能汽车解决方案BU政策与标准专利部首席安全架构师。原诺基亚西门子高级研究员，长期从事网络安全工作，在可信计算、云计算及大数据安全有丰富的经验，参与多项汽车信息安全标准编写。

  

  
![](../Images/7ba06d8b4ca954808676fc8ce22f30da.png)

  

Topic

  

** "Research on Data Security of Autonomous Driving and Evaluation Framework" **

  

1\. Risk analysis of data security in automatic driving;

2\. Data classification of automatic driving and security architecture

3\. Research on cybersecurity evaluation framework.

  

  

Speaker

  

**Youlei Chen, Chief Security Architect in the Policy and Standard Patent Department of Intelligent Automotive Solutions BU of Huawei Technologies co., LTD. **

Bio: Youlei Chen is currently the chief security architect in the policy and standard patent department of intelligent automotive solutions BU of huawei technologies co., LTD. He is a former senior researcher of nokia Siemens. He has been engaged in network security work for a long time, participated in making of automotive cyber-security standards. He has rich experience in trusted computing, cloud computing and big data security.
