演讲题目

  

**“汽车网络安全的未来国际法规“**

  

本演讲将向您阐述对机动车辆及其挂车造成网络攻击的风险；全球汽车网络安全标准法规的现状；联合国制定关于汽车网络安全的国际法规；与主管部门和技术服务部门一起测试国际法；在不同国家实施联合国法规情况介绍；以及未来展望

  

演讲者嘉宾

**Dr. Kai Frederik ZASTROW, 国际汽车制造商组织(OICA) 技术委员会主席; PSA集团法规认证标准专家**

  

Kai Frederik ZASTROW博士目前担任PSA集团法规认证标准专家，OICA技术委员会主席。Dr. Zastrow 在研究了电气和电子工程之后，他于1995年开始在柏林的自动运输系统领域工作。2001年，他加入了巴黎PSA集团。自2005年以来，他一直在车辆监管和认证领域工作。2007年，他成为联合国欧洲经济委员会联合国欧洲经济委员会GRRF刹车和行走机构专家组（主动安全）内部国际汽车制造商组织（OICA）的发言人。2016年，Dr. Zastrow 成为Groupe PSA的全球23位专家之一。Dr. Zastrow 是联合国网络安全和软件更新工作组的行业发言人，自2017年以来担任OICA技术委员会主席。

  

  
![](../Images/7ba06d8b4ca954808676fc8ce22f30da.png)

  

Topic

  

** "The Future International Regulation on Automotive Cybersecurity"**

This presentation will introduce the Risks of cyberattacks to motor vehicles and their trailers;  Current situation of worldwide standards and regulations on automotive cybersecurity; Development by the United Nations of an international regulation on automotive cybersecurity; Test phase of the draft international regulation with authorities and technical services; Implementation of the UN Regulation in the different countries and the Outlook

  

Speaker

  

**Dr. Kai Frederik ZASTROW, Chairman of OICA Technical Committee; Master Expert Regulation Certification Standards, Groupe PSA**

  

Bio: Dr. Kai Frederik ZASTROW is currently the Master Expert Regulation Certification Standards of Groupe PSA, and the Chairman of OICA Technical Committee. After study of electrical and electronic engineering, he started working in the field of automated transport systems in Berlin in 1995. In 2001, he joined Groupe PSA in Paris. Since 2005, he has been working in the field of vehicle regulation and certification. In 2007, Dr. Zastrow became the spokesman of the International Organisation of Vehicle Manufacturers (OICA) inside the GRRF Expert Group for Brakes and Running Gear (Active Safety) of UNECE United Nations Economic Commission for Europe. In 2016, he became one of the worldwide 23 Master Experts of Groupe PSA. Dr. Zastrow is the industry spokesman in the United Nations Task Force on Cybersecurity and Software updates and since 2017 the Chairman of the OICA Technical Committee.
