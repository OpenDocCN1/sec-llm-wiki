演讲题目

  

**“ 面向智能网联汽车大数据安全可信共享的云链融合模型与方法“**

  

演讲嘉宾

  

黄永峰，博士，教授，博士生导师，首届全国网络安全优秀教师，清华大学信息认知与智能系统研究所副所长。IEEE Senior Member；中国信息隐藏与多媒体安全专业委员会委员等。多年来一直从事互联网及其信息安全理论与技术的研究和教学工作。先后主持了国家重点自然基金、科技部973、863、重点研发专项等科研项目40余项。担任了多家国际著名期刊的编委，以及重要国际会议程序委员或主席；已在SCIENCE、IEEE Tran. ACM Tran.、中国科学等国内外著名期刊和AAAI、ACL等重要国际会议发表学术论文300多篇（其中SCI收录100余篇）；出版专著6部、译著2部，教材2部。申请发明专利20余项（已授权11项，技术转让2项）。获得省部级科技成果奖一等奖2项，二等奖3项；优秀教材一等奖1项、清华大学教学成果奖2项。

  

  
![](../Images/7ba06d8b4ca954808676fc8ce22f30da.png) Topic

  

“Cloud chain fusion model and method for intelligent connected vehicle big data security and trusted sharing”

 

  

**Speaker**

  

Huang Yongfeng, Ph.D., professor, doctoral tutor, the first outstanding national network security teacher, deputy director of the Institute of Information Cognition and Intelligent Systems. Senior member of IEEE; member of China Information Hiding and Multimedia Security Professional Committee, etc. Has been engaged in the research and teaching of the Internet and its information security theory and technology. He has presided over more than 40 scientific research projects such as the National Key Natural Fund, the Ministry of Science and Technology 973, 863, and key R&D projects. , As well as important international conference program committee or chairman; already in SCIENCE, IEEE Tran. ACM Tran. , China Science and other well-known domestic and foreign journals and AAAI, ACL and other important international conferences published more than 300 academic papers (including more than 100 SCI); published 6 monographs, 2 translations, 2 textbooks. Won the provincial and ministerial level scientific and technological achievement awards 2 first prizes, 3 second prizes; 1 excellent teaching materials first prize, 2 teaching achievement awards of Tsinghua University; more than 20 patents (11 authorized, 2 technology transfer) . .
