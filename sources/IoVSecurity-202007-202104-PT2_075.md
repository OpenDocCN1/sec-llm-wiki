演讲题目

  

**“ ‘5G+车联网‘ 安全：需求与挑战“**

  

**梗概**  

  

——立足新形势，对5G和车联网安全的认识

——立足问题导向和应用导向，看“5G+车联网”安全保障思路

——提高“5G+工业互联网”应用水平与安全保障能力的思考与建议

  

**演讲嘉宾**

  

安晖，博士，正高级工程师，中国电子信息产业发展研究院副总工程师，民盟中央科技委员会副主任。专门从事电子信息产业发展与政策规划等方面研究工作，对电子信息、云计算、大数据、人工智能、数字经济等有深入研究。作为主要成员参与国家多部规划、政策的起草。作为课题负责人完成工业和信息化部、国家发展改革委、商务部等多个部委及多个地方政府委托的课题研究工作。出版专著《数字经济：新时代  再起航》、译著《云经济学》等。

  

  
![](../Images/7ba06d8b4ca954808676fc8ce22f30da.png) Topic

  

“Security of 5G + Internet of Vehicles : Requirements and Challenges”

 

**Abstract**

——Based on the new situation, understanding of the safety of 5G and Internet of Vehicles

——Based on the problem-oriented and application-oriented, look at the "5G + Internet of Vehicles" security assurance ideas  

——Thinking and suggestions on improving the application level and security assurance capabilities of "5G+Industrial Internet"  

  

  

**Speaker**

An Hui, PhD, senior engineer, deputy chief engineer of China Electronics Information Industry Development Research Institute, deputy director of the Central Science and Technology Committee of China Democratic League. Specializes in research work in the development of electronic information industry and policy planning, and has in-depth research on electronic information, cloud computing, big data, artificial intelligence, and digital economy. Participate in the drafting of many national plans and policies as a major member. As the subject person in charge, he completed the subject research commissioned by multiple ministries and commissions such as the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, the National Development and Reform Commission, the Ministry of Commerce, and many local governments. Published the monograph "Digital Economy: A New Era Restarting", the translation of "Cloud Economics", etc.
