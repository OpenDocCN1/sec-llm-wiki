演讲题目


“车辆内部网络的网络安全问题与解决方案“


梗概

随着汽车智能化的快速发展，不同等级、不同场景的自动驾驶技术将逐渐得到实际应用，逐渐对车辆内部网络的通信安全提出了需求。本演讲从威胁分析与风险评估、最佳实践、标准、解决方案等角度，覆盖CAN/CANFD、以太网等总线，就车辆内部网络的网络安全防护体系进行了探索。



演讲者嘉宾

李允，博士，为辰信安/电子科技大学


个人简介

个人介绍：李允博士长期从事嵌入式系统和网络安全方面的研究与应用工作。先后开展了航空电子、飞行控制、舰船电子、智能手机、汽车电子等领域嵌入式软件平台的研究与产品开发工作，主持了汽车电子基础软件方面的国家"核高基"重大专项，成功实现智能汽车网络安全方面的产品研制与应用工作。



 



Topic


“Cybersecurity Issues and Solutions for In-vehicle Networks”

 

Abstract

With the rapid development of automotive intelligence, autonomous driving technologies will gradually be put into practical use, and requirements for the security of the in-vehicle network are gradually raised. This presentation covers CAN / CANFD, Ethernet and other buses from the perspectives of threat analysis and risk assessment, best practices, standards, and solutions, which explores the network security protection system of the in-vehicle network.


Speaker

Yun Li, Ph.D, Vecentek Co., Ltd./University of Electronics Science & Technology of China


Bio

Dr. Li has undertaken embedded software platform related projects funded by the national 863 plans, NSFC and some other major special projects, covering application domains such as automotive electronics, avionics, flight control, naval ship electronics and smartphones, etc. Meanwhile, the industrializations of these platforms are also implemented in the corresponding domains.
