第五届 ACSS 2020  (Automotive Cybersecurity Security Summit) 中国汽车网络信息安全峰会将于2020年11月2日至4日盛大开幕。本届峰会以“合法合规建设有效智能网联汽车安全保障体系“为主题，线上线下同步展开。


ACSS2020 | 议程 Agenda




演讲题目：“自动驾驶数据安全及评测体系研究”

1、自动驾驶数据安全风险分析；

2、自动驾驶数据分级及安全防护架构；

3、信息安全评测体系研究。


演讲嘉宾：陈幼雷，首席安全架构师，智能汽车解决方案BU政策与标准专利部，华为技术有限公司

个人介绍：陈幼雷，现任华为技术有限公司智能汽车解决方案BU政策与标准专利部首席安全架构师。原诺基亚西门子高级研究员，长期从事网络安全工作，在可信计算、云计算及大数据安全有丰富的经验，参与多项汽车信息安全标准编写。


Presentation Topic: "Research on Data Security of Autonomous Driving and Evaluation Framework "

1. Risk analysis of data security in automatic driving;

2. Data classification of automatic driving and security architecture

3. Research on cybersecurity evaluation framework.


Speaker: Youlei Chen, Chief Security Architect in the Policy and Standard Patent Department of Intelligent Automotive Solutions BU of Huawei Technologies co., LTD. 

Bio: Youlei Chen is currently the chief security architect in the policy and standard patent department of intelligent automotive solutions BU of huawei technologies co., LTD. He is a former senior researcher of nokia Siemens. He has been engaged in network security work for a long time, participated in making of automotive cyber-security standards. He has rich experience in trusted computing, cloud computing and big data security.


 




演讲题目：“自动驾驶中的功能安全性和信息安全性”

无人驾驶对汽车安全框架的设计提出了严格的要求，既要求功能安全又需要信息安全。借此机会，泰雷兹（Thales）将分享他们在航空自动驾驶领域的经验，以促进自动驾驶增长的繁荣未来。


演讲嘉宾：卜涛，汽车移动服务，泰雷兹 


Presentation Topic:" Function Security and Information Security in Autonomous Driving "

Autonomous driving raises stringent requirements on design of car security framework, both functional security as well as information security. Takes this opportunity, Thales would like to share their experience in aviation autonomous driving field to facilitate prosperous future in growth of autonomous driving at ACSS2020.


Speaker: TAO Haukka, Mobility Service, Thales

 





演讲题目：“车联网安全未来在电动汽车，自动驾驶，移动出行等领域的考量”

本演讲将调查和分析汽车行业中最新的网络安全趋势，这些趋势将影响电动汽车，自动驾驶和未来的出行市场。我们将研究车联网安全功能的采用情况，与车辆结构和安全运营中心相关的考虑因素。我们还将介绍WP.29和ISO / SAE 21434。


演讲嘉宾：周罡松，车联网安全专家，SBD Automotive

个人介绍：周先生毕业于英国伯明翰大学网络安全专业（GCHQ认证），在网络安全领域尤其是车联网安全领域有着多年的经验。他主要参与了日本，欧洲，北美和中国的OEM与供应商的车联网安全项目包含：威胁建模，渗透测试，风险评估，安全系统设计审查等。他还为两个由英国政府资助的创新项目提供顾问服务。现在他被派遣回中国主要负责SBD汽车的中国车联网安全相关项目。



Presentation Topic:  “Cybersecurity considerations for EV, Autonomous and the futureof Mobility.”

This presentation will investigate and analyse the latestcybersecurity trends seen in the automotive industry which will affect the EV,autonomous and future mobility market.  We will investigate the adoptionof cybersecurity features within the vehicles, the factors to consider withrespect vehicle architecture and security operations centre.  We willinvestigate what the drivers are and the effects of up and coming typeapprovals within WP.29 and the introduction of ISO/SAE 21434.


Speaker: Gangsong Zhou, Cyber Security Specialist, Secure Car Department, SBD Automotive 


Bio: Mr. Zhou has many years of work experience in automotive cybersecurity area and holds a MSc in Cyber Security degree which also certificated by GCHQ in accredited from University of Birmingham. He has token part into many automotive cybersecurity projects including thread modelling, penetration testing, risk assessment, security system designing and review etc for the OEMs and suppliers from Europe, Japan, NA, and China. He is also providing consultancy to two novel UK Government funded Innovate UK research programs. Now he relocates into China and mainly responsible for Chinese cyber security related projects in SBD Automotive.


 






议题："车企WP29网络安全合规如何应对"


• UNECE GRVA WP29网络安全合规背景介绍

  组织架构、合规背景、适用车型范围、取缔国国家应对态度等

• WP29和ISO21434关联关系说明

  WP29合规要求框架—WP29和ISO21434对应关系

• 车企的WP29合规现状差距以及整改路线

  安全治理层面现状差距— 安全业务执行层面现状差距— WP29车企全方位整改路线

• 德勤WP29项目经验以及服务能力介绍

  德勤WP29项目经验介绍—德勤WP29项目实施核心方法论介绍


TOPIC:  " Automotive OEMs - How to Deal with WP29 Cyber Security Compliance"


• UNECE GRVA WP29 Cyber Security Compliance Background Introduction

Organizational structure, compliance background, scope of applicable vehicle models, response attitudes of the banned country, etc.


• Description of the relationship between WP29 and ISO21434

WP29 compliance requirement framework—correspondence between WP29 and ISO21434


• Car companies’ WP29 compliance status differences and rectification routes

The main target difference of safety governance—The main difference of safety business execution target—The WP29 car company's comprehensive rectification route


• Introduction of Deloitte WP29 project experience and service capabilities

Introduction to Deloitte WP29 Project Experience—Introduction to the core methodology of Deloitte WP29 project implementation


演讲嘉宾：张震，合伙人，风险咨询/ 国际注册内部审计师 CIA / 国际信息系统审计师CISA

个人介绍：张震先生目前是德勤风险咨询部门合伙人，他在网络安全、企业新业务新技术应用流程风险控制、企业自动化风险管理规划与咨询实施是专家。他对企业的内部财务，新业务，新技术规划，使用及其风险控制方面有深入理解，他在应用系统包括传统ERP、商业智能等产品实施咨询等方面也拥有经验，同时帮助企业新技术或系统满足中国本地法规要求。网络安全领域，张先生在企业网络安全战略和规划，中国网络安全法合规，个人信息保护，GDPR合规，数据安全治理与咨询方面有丰富经验。张先生还是德勤应用系统安全与控制服务负责人，包括应用系统安全控制，流程风险信息化规划，GRC产品实施，参与众多企业应用系统实施过程协助、实施选型，监理和上线后检查工作。张先生服务的客户主要是汽车行业，服务国内领先大型车企有丰富经验，对汽车行业新业务新技术，例如智能网联汽车，工控安全方面也有深入研究。除此以外，他在其他制造业，消费品行业也有不少经验。他的主要客户有：上海汽车、上汽通用、华晨宝马、一汽大众、FCA亚太、奇瑞汽车、吉利汽车、保时捷汽车、延锋饰件、延锋江森、青岛海尔、广州地铁等。


Speaker: Boris Zhang, Engagement Partner, CIA、CISA. Deloitte

Bio: With more than 15 years of work experience in Deloitte, Boris Zhang is familiar with information security, internal audit and risk & control services. He is an experienced expert on data security, personal information protection, application security and control, automated GRC system consulting and implementation. He is the champion to lead national China Cyber Security Law service in Deloitte, works as speaker in external seminars, writes articles and blogger about insight and interpretation of Cyber Security Law and leads interaction matters with China authorities. 

He has intensive experience and deep understanding on local regulations, and assists clients in complying with their local business and system to meet regulatory requirement including Cyber security law, CSOX and PRC accounting localization requirement etc. He also has extensive industry experiences covering automotive, retail, transportation and logistics etc. In Cyber Security Law service, he has been engaged in several compliance readiness engagements of China Cyber Security Law for large companies, help client build the information security management framework and assess the gaps to comply with Cyber Security Law and propose the follow-up improvement activities, remediation plan and the priorities.
