This year marks the 10th anniversary of KCon and the first year of revival after the COVID-19 pandemic.


What does ten years mean?


“All begins with one and ends up with ten.”

“People used to keep records by tying knots dot and carry one.”

“Ten means full and all. “Horizontal” means east and west, and “vertical” means north and south. If the two strokes are crossed, it means full and all.”


“Ten” is a number representing the extreme; In the meanwhile, it means a full ending, renascence and the beginning of a period. At the 10th anniversary of KCon, we aim to go far beyond ten.



“Ten” is consisted of “horizontal” and “vertical”, it means everything.


Political strategists sow discord, driving all vassal states to fight against each other. ——Huai Nan Zi · Lanmingxun


When Seven Powers in the Warring State Period coexisted, the powerful and weak countries resorted to the diplomatic strategy of "alliances vertically and horizontally" to achieve the purpose of annexation and defense.


Offensive and defensive games used to be unpredictable, making it necessary to assess the situation. Only by accomplishing a task with exquisite techniques can make it possible to make differences.


The essence of alliances vertically and horizontally lies in balance. Only by the probe into others’ mindset and understanding the truth can we predict with miraculous accuracy and forestall the enemy.


The 10th KCon Hacker Conference, KCon 2021, is themed with alliances vertically and horizontally and renascence.

Founded by Knownsec, KCon Hacker Conference is an interesting hacker conference seeking practical real stuffs; Besides, it is an exchange platform for frontier cyber security attack and defense techniques, which is the youngest, the most dynamic and the most influential in international cyber security sector.


KCon Hacker Conference has always followed the aim of “gathering the wits of global hackers”. We hope that you can show yourselves as an eye-catcher at KCon 2021, if you are obsessed in the research of hacker technologies and crazy about technological exchanges and sharing.

II. Duration of Topics Solicitation


From now until July 2021.

July 2021.
