通过PowerShell命令查找进程加载了DLL：


ps | ? { $_.Modules.ModuleName -contains 'amsi.dll' }


通过WMI命令识别带有已加载 DLL 的进程：


gcim CIM_DataFile -Filter 'Drive = "C:" AND Path = "\\Windows\\System32\\" AND FileName = "amsi" AND Extension = "dll"' | gcai -Association CIM_ProcessExecutable


通过wmic.exe 获取具有特定DLL的进程列表


wmic PATH CIM_DATAFILE WHERE "Drive='C:' AND Path=' \\ Windows \\ System32 \\ ' AND FileName='amsi' AND Extension='dll'" ASSOC:list /ASSOCCLASS:CIM_ProcessExecutable
