### Responsibility

*   Monitor systems and products on a daily basis to ensure systems are performing as expected
    
*   Maintain important data center environmental systems, disaster recovery, monitoring, and alerting systems
    
*   Document internal processes and procedures related to systems administration
    
*   Adhere to established service level agreements in consultation with end users to establish expectations and time frames for problem resolutions
    
*   Participate as a member of an on-call rotation to support multiple systems during off-hours
    
*   Work evenings and weekends as needed
    

### Required skills

*   5+ years of related experience
    
*   Solid Linux administration skills with Red Hat Enterprise Linux (RHEL), CentOS, and Ubuntu
    
*   Ability to effectively communicate with a global team
    
*   Experience with automated configuration management workflows and technologies like Puppet, Chef, or CFEngine
    
*   Experience with Red Hat-based Linux distributions
    
*   Experience with monitoring tools like Nagios and Zabbix
    
*   Experience with virtualization technologies, especially Red Hat Enterprise Virtualization (RHEV) and KVM
    
*   Linux certifications are an advantage
    
*   Excellent interpersonal skills, especially with listening and questioning
    
*   Application and process support experience in a 24x7 environment
    
*   Ability to work flexible hours
