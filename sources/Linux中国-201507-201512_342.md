# 庆祝 Linux 24 岁生日！

今天是 Linux 的 24 岁生日。

在 1991 年 4月，芬兰的赫尔辛基大学年仅 21 岁的学生 Linus Torvalds 开始做一个他自己的操作系统。在 24 年前的今天，即 1991年8月25日，他在 usenet 的 comp.os.minix 新闻组中发布了 Linux 的第一个公告，宣布了 Linux 的诞生。以下是当年 Torvalds 写的邮件：

```
1.  `Path: gmdzi!unido!fauern!ira.uka.de!sol.ctr.columbia.edu!zaphod.mps.ohio-state.edu!wupost!uunet!mcsun!news.funet.fi!hydra!klaava!torvalds`
    
2.  `From: torv...@klaava.Helsinki.FI (Linus Benedict Torvalds)`
    
3.  `Newsgroups: comp.os.minix`
    
4.  `Subject: What would you like to see most in minix?`
    
5.  `Summary: small poll for my new operating system`
    
6.  `Keywords: 386, preferences`
    
7.  `Message-ID: <1991Aug25.205708.9541@klaava.Helsinki.FI>`
    
8.  `Date: 25 Aug 91 20:57:08 GMT`
    
9.  `Organization: University of Helsinki`
    
10.  `Lines: 20`
     
11.  
     
12.  
     
13.  `Hello everybody out there using minix -`
     
14.  
     
15.  `I'm doing a (free) operating system (just a hobby, won't be big and`
     
16.  `professional like gnu) for 386(486) AT clones.  This has been brewing`
     
17.  `since april, and is starting to get ready.  I'd like any feedback on`
     
18.  `things people like/dislike in minix, as my OS resembles it somewhat`
     
19.  `(same physical layout of the file-system (due to practical reasons)`
     
20.  `among other things). `
     
21.  
     
22.  `I've currently ported bash(1.08) and gcc(1.40), and things seem to work. `
     
23.  `This implies that I'll get something practical within a few months, and`
     
24.  `I'd like to know what features most people would want.  Any suggestions`
     
25.  `are welcome, but I won't promise I'll implement them :-)`
     
26.  
     
27.  `		Linus (torv...@kruuna.helsinki.fi)`
     
28.  
     
29.  `PS.  Yes - it's free of any minix code, and it has a multi-threaded fs. `
     
30.  `It is NOT protable (uses 386 task switching etc), and it probably never`
     
31.  `will support anything other than AT-harddisks, as that's all I have :-(. `
     
```

在该年的九月， Linux Kernel 0.01 发布到了芬兰大学和研究网（FUNET） 上的一个 FTP 服务器（ftp.funet.fi）。当时仅有 10239 行代码。到了十月，又发布了 0.02。
