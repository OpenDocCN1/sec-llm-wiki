## 失败的第三方提权

![](../Images/7c37c8d51e76b3ab29208e6c3bc9cc6f.png)

![](../Images/8290c679e1084997fb9b7dc4c1fbc240.png)

![](../Images/935050fc569a9f80d03770f343dd903e.png)
