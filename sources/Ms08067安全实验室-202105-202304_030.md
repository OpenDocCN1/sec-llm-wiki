### 第五关

![](../Images/3c30e660598f0bfb90198fd790c09b4e.png)

输入测试字符<>"JavAScriPT'/:on;，on被替换成o\_n

  
![](../Images/163c181c1894a790b047def723880cf6.png)

输入<script>alert(123)</script>，<script被替换为<s\_cript

  
![](../Images/1840c33e76d806732a664a8222d645b7.png)
