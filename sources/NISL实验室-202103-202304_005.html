Agenda



1.【论文分享】FIRMWIRE: Transparent Dynamic Analysis for Cellular Baseband Firmware

  • Presenter: 孙文厚

  • Conference: NDSS'22

  • Authors: Grant Hernandez, Marius Muench, Dominik Maier, Alyssa Milburn, Shinjo Park, Tobias Scharnowski, Tyler Tucker,  Patrick Traynor, Kevin R. B. Butler

  • Abstract: This paper proposes the first full-system emulation platform for baseband processor firmwares, allowing for scalable baseband security testing and bugs discovery.

  • Link to paper: https://www.ndss-symposium.org/wp-content/uploads/2022-136-paper.pdf


2.【论文分享】GET/out: Automated Discovery of Application-Layer Censorship Evasion Strategies

  • Presenter: 王一航

  • Conference: USENIX Security'22

  • Authors: Michael Harrity, Kevin Bock, Frederick Sell, and Dave Levin

  • Abstract: This paper presents a general solution for discovering new censorship evasion techniques at the application layer.

  • Link to paper: https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity22/presentation/harrity
