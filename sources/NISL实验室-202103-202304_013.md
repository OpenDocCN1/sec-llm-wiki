**Agenda**

  

  

1.**【论文分享****】**Phishing URL Detection: A Network-based Approach Robust to Evasion

*   **Presenter: **李岩
    
*   **Conference:** CCS'23
    
*   **Authors: **Taeri Kim, Noseong Park, Jiwon Hong, Sang-Wook Kim
    
*   **Abstract:**
    
     This paper proposes a new graph-based phishing URL detection algorithm, which achieves the state-of-the-art phishing URL detection effect and is robust to the evasion strategies that attackers may adopt.
    
*   **Link to paper: **https://arxiv.org/pdf/2209.01454.pdf
    

  

2.**【论文分享】**DARWIN- Survival of the Fittest Fuzzing Mutators

*   **Presenter: **徐尚志
    
*   **Conference: **NDSS'23
    
*   **Authors:** 
    
    Patrick Jauernig
    
    , Domagoj Jakobovic
    
    , Stjepan Picek, Emmanuel Stapf, Ahmad-Reza Sadeghi
    
      
    
*   **Abstract:**
    
     This paper presented a novel mutation scheduler, which uses an Evolution Strategy to systematically optimize and adapt the probability distribution of the mutation operators during fuzzing. 
    
*   **Link to paper: **https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.11783.pdf
