**Agenda**

  

  

1.**【论文分享****】**Beyond The Gates: An Empirical Analysis of HTTP-Managed Password Stealers and Operators

*   **Presenter: **张镇睿
    
*   **Conference:** USENIX Security'23
    
*   **Authors: **
    
    Athanasios Avgetidis, Omar Alrawi, Kevin Valakuzhy, and Charles Lever, Georgia Institute of Technology; Paul Burbage, MalBeacon; Angelos D. Keromytis, Fabian Monrose, and Manos Antonakakis, Georgia Institute of Technology
    
*   **Abstract:**
    
     Password Stealers (Stealers) are commodity malware that specialize in credential theft. This work presents a large-scale longitudinal study of Stealers and their operators.
    
*   **Link to paper: **
    

https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity23/presentation/avgetidis  

2.**【论文分享】On the Evolution of (Hateful) Memes by Means of Multimodal Contrastive Learning (本篇不进行线上分享）**

*   **Presenter: **朱文宇
    
*   **Conference: **IEEE S&P'23
    
*   **Authors:** Yiting Qu, Xinlei He, Shannon Pierson, Michael Backes, Yang Zhang, Savvas Zannettou
    
*   **Abstract:**
    
     This paper leverages multimodal contrastive learning models, in particular OpenAI's CLIP, to identify targets of hateful content and systematically investigate the evolution of hateful memes.
    
*   **Link to paper: **https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.06573
