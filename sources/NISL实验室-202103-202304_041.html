Agenda



1.【论文分享】ROV-MI: Large-Scale, Accurate and Efficient Measurement of ROV Deployment

  • Presenter: 陈熠豪

  • Conference: NDSS 2022

  • Authors:

    Wenqi Chen, Zhiliang Wang, Dongqi Han, Chenxin Duan, Xia Yin, Jiahai Yang, Xingang Shi

  • Abstract:

    This paper proposes an ROV deployment inference framework, ROV-MI, by utilizing RPKI-invalid prefixes in the wild and Bayesian inference techniques.

  • Link to paper:

    https://www.ndss-symposium.org/wp-content/uploads/2022-214-paper.pdf

2.【论文分享】FlakeRepro: Automated and Efficient Reproduction of Concurrency-Related Flaky Tests

  • Presenter: 路翕丞

  • Conference: ESEC/FSE 2022

  • Authors:

    Tanakorn Leesatapornwongsa, Xiang Ren, Suman Nath

  • Abstract:

    This paper proposes FlakeRepro that helps developers reproduce a failed execution of a flaky test caused by concurrency.

  • Link to paper:

    • https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/3540250.3558956
