**Agenda**

  

  

1.**【论文分享****】**Ghost Calls from Operational 4G Call Systems: IMS Vulnerability, Call DoS Attack, and Countermeasure

*   **Presenter: **杨雅儒
    
*   **Conference: **MobiCom 2020
    
*   **Authors:**
    
    Yu-Han Lu, Chi-Yu Li, Yao-Yu Li, Sandy Hsin-Yu Hsiao, Tian Xie, Guan-Hua Tu, Wei-Xun Chen
    
*   **Abstract:**
    
    This paper identifies three vulnerabilities from VoLTE/VoWi-Fi, and designes an attack framework which could perform DoS attack stealthily.
    
*   **Link to paper:**
    

    https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3372224.3380885

2.**【论文分享】**SmartDagger: A Bytecode-Based Static Analysis Approach for Detecting Cross-Contract Vulnerability

*   **Presenter: **陈龙飞
    
*   **Conference: **ISSTA 2022
    
*   **Authors:**
    
    Zeqin Liao, Zibin Zheng, Xiao Chen, Yuhong Nan
    
*   **Abstract:**
    
    This paper proposes a new framework for detecting cross-contract vulnerability through static analysis at the bytecode level.
    
*   **Link to paper:**
    

https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/3533767.3534222
