Agenda



1.【论文分享】Gossamer: Securely Measuring Password-based Logins

  • Presenter: 薛镇青

  • Conference: USENIX Security 2022

  • Authors:

    Marina Sanusi Bohuk, Mazharul Islam,Suleman Ahmad, Michael Swift, Thomas Ristenpart,Rahul Chatterjee

  • Abstract:

    This paper designs and implements the gossamer system, and open source it to help researchers solve the problem of difficult data collection in password related research due to the high sensitivity of user password data.

  • Link to paper:

    https://www.usenix.org/system/files/sec22-sanusi-bohuk.pdf


2.【论文分享】Fuzzle: Making a Puzzle for Fuzzers

  • Presenter: 王嘉炜

  • Conference: ASE 2022

  • Authors:

    Haeun Lee, Soomin Kim, Sang Kil Cha

  • Abstract:

    This paper addressed the bug synthesis problem by casting it as a maze generation problem since the method of directly injecting bugs into existing programs may contain unexpected bugs.

  • Link to paper:

    https://softsec.kaist.ac.kr/~sangkilc/papers/lee-ase22.pdf
