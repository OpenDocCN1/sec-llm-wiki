**Agenda**

  

  

1.**【论文分享】**Automating Cookie Consent and GDPR Violation Detection

*   **Presenter: **郎峙煜
    
*   **Conference: **USENIX Security 2022
    
*   **Authors:**
    
    Dino Bollinger, Karel Kubicek, Carlos Cotrini, and David Basin  
    
*   **Abstract:**
    
    This paper analyses potential GDPR violations in cookie banners in almost 30k websites and develops a browser extension to give users the power to protect their privacy.
    
*   **Link to paper:**
    
    https://www.usenix.org/system/files/sec22-bollinger.pdf
    

2.**【论文分享】**An In-depth Analysis of Duplicated Linux Kernel Bug Reports

*   **Presenter: **马哲宇
    
*   **Conference: **NDSS 2022
    
*   **Authors:**
    
    Dongliang Mu, Yuhang Wu, Zhenpeng Lin, Yueqi Chen, Chensheng Yu, Xinyu Xing, and Gang Wang
    
*   **Abstract:**
    
    This paper discusses the causes, prevalence, and possible solutions to the problem of duplicate crash reports in kernel fuzzing.
    
*   **Link to paper:**
    
    https://gangw.cs.illinois.edu/ndss22-linux.pdf
