**Agenda**

  

  

1.**【论文分享】**Weak Links in Authentication Chains: A Large-scale Analysis of Email Sender Spoofing Attacks

*   **Presenter: **仓永康弘
    
*   **Conference: **USENIX Security 2021
    
*   **Authors:**
    
    Kaiwen Shen, Chuhan Wang, Minglei Guo, Xiaofeng Zheng, Chaoyi Lu, Baojun Liu, Yuxuan Zhao, Shuang Hao, Haixin Duan, Qinfeng Pan, and Min Yang
    
*   **Abstract:**
    
    This paper analyzes email attacks that can bypass SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and user interface protections, conducts large-scale experiments on 30 popular email services and 23 email clients, and proposes key mitigations to defend against new attacks.
    
*   **Link to paper:**
    
    https://www.usenix.org/system/files/sec21-shen-kaiwen.pdf
    

2.**【论文分享】**𝜇AFL: Non-intrusive Feedback-driven Fuzzing for Microcontroller Firmware

*   **Presenter: **孙文厚
    
*   **Conference: **ICSE 2022
    
*   **Authors:**
    
    Wenqiang Li, Jiameng Shi, Fengjun Li, Jingqiang Lin, Wei Wang, and Le Guan
    
*   **Abstract:**
    
    This paper extends AFL to embedded devices by native trace collectors on real devices to acquire coverage knowledge.
    
*   **Link to paper:**
    
    https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3510003.3510208
