**Agenda**

  

  

1.**【论文分享】**An Extensive Study of Residential Proxies in China

*   **Presenter: **张镇睿
    
*   **Conference: **CCS 2022
    
*   **Authors:**
    
    Mingshuo Yang, Yunnan Yu, Xianghang Mi, Shujun Tang, Shanqing Guo, Yilin Li, Xiaofeng Zheng, and Haixin Duan
    
*   **Abstract:**
    
    This paper carried out the first in-depth characterization of residential proxies (RESIPs) in China and uncovered a set of interesting findings as well as several security implications.
    
*   **Link to paper:**
    
    https://arxiv.org/pdf/2209.06056.pdf
    

2.**【论文分享】**MobFuzz: Adaptive Multi-objective Optimization in Gray-box Fuzzing

*   **Presenter: **高泽豫
    
*   **Conference: **NDSS 2022
    
*   **Authors:**
    
    Gen Zhang, Pengfei Wang, Tai Yue, Xiangdong Kong, Shan Huang, Xu Zhou, and Kai Lu
    
*   **Abstract:**
    
    This paper proposes a gray-box fuzzer for multi-objective optimization (MOO) to select the objective combination and adaptively allocate energy to the seeds.
    
*   **Link to paper:**
    
    https://www.ndss-symposium.org/ndss-paper/auto-draft-199/
