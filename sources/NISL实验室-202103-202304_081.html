Agenda



1.【论文分享】TsuNAME: Exploiting Misconfiguration and Vulnerability to DDoS DNS

  • Presenter: 许威

  • Conference: IMC 2021

  • Authors:

    Giovane C. M. Moura, Sebastian Castro, John Heidemann, and Wes Hardaker

  • Abstract:

    This paper identifies TsuNAME, a vulnerability where some recursive resolvers can greatly amplify queries, potentially resulting in a denial-of-service to DNS services. TsuNAME is caused by cyclical dependencies in DNS records.

  • Link to paper:

    https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3487552.3487824

2.【论文分享】Semantic Image Fuzzing of AI Perception Systems

  • Presenter: 张书樵

  • Conference: ICSE 2022

  • Authors:

    Trey Woodlief, Sebastian Elbaum, and Kevin Sullivan

  • Abstract:

    This paper introduced a novel approach that automatically generates test-cases with sensor reading and ground-truth interpretation pairs for AS perception systems.

  • Link to paper:

    https://conf.researchr.org/details/icse-2022/icse-2022-papers/172/Semantic-Image-Fuzzing-of-AI-Perception-Systems
