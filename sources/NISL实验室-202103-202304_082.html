Agenda



1.【论文分享】Many Roads Lead To Rome: How Packet Headers Influence DNS Censorship Measurement

  • Presenter: 李想

  • Conference: USENIX Security 2022

  • Authors:

    Abhishek Bhaskar and Paul Pearce

  • Abstract:

    This paper explores the role packet headers (e.g., source IP address and source port) have on DNS censorship.

  • Link to paper:

    https://www.usenix.org/system/files/sec22-bhaskar.pdf

  • Conference: ISSTA 2022

  • Authors:

    Chenyang Lyu, Hong Liang, Shouling Ji, Xuhong Zhang, Binbin Zhao, Meng Han, Yun Li, Zhe Wang, and Wenhai Wang

  • Abstract:
