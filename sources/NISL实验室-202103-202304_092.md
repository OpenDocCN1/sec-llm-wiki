**1.【专题分****享】Membership Inference Attacks in Machine Learning**  

  
**Presenter**:  Yue Qin（Ph.D. candidate, Indiana University Bloomington）**Abstract: **Recent studies have shown that ML models are vulnerable to membership inference attacks (MIAs), which aim to infer whether a data record was used to train a target model or not. MIAs on ML models can directly lead to a privacy breach that leaks sensitive information of the training data. This talk introduces membership inference attacks under the white-box, black-box, and label-only scenario.**Papers:**

a). Membership inference attacks against machine learning models (S&P'17)

b). Systematic Evaluation of Privacy Risks of Machine Learning Models (Usenix'21)

c). Label-Only Membership Inference Attacks (PLMR'21)
