**2.【论文分享】****Automatic Firmware Emulation through Invalidity-guided Knowledge Inference** 

  
**Presenter**: 孙文厚（清华大学计算机系硕士生）**Conference**: Usenix Security 2021**Authors**: Wei Zhou, Le Guan, Peng Liu, Yuqing Zhang**Abstract: **

本文提出的μEmu方法可以自动化模拟固件，为每次外设寄存器的访问推断响应规则，以弥补仿真环境中外设的缺失。

This work proposes μEmy to emulate firmware with unknown peripherals automatically by inferring rules for each peripheral register access.

**Link to paper: **https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity21/presentation/zhou
