招聘博士与博士后, 欢迎各位老师同学帮忙转发:

Positions

10+ Ph.D. (Scholarship), Postdoc Positions on Trustworthy AI for Cyber Security.

Research Projects

We have several exciting research projects on AI Security, Robustness and Explaianability. Candidates with expertise from the following aspects will be given in priority.

  1. Malware Detection

  2. Intrusion/Anomaly Detection

  3. Crypto Attribution

  4. Insider Threat Attribution

  5. Security & Confidentiality

  6. Robustness

  7. Explaianability

  8. AI Testbed Platform & Interfaces

The tentative candidate would work at Nanyang Technological University, National University of Singapore, and Singapore Management University, which is well equipped with powerful servers, hardware/software infrastructure, etc.


The position involves conducting basic research, developing tools, working as part of a large research team, traveling, giving presentations, with an exciting chance to collaborate with industrial partners and local agencies. The working language is English.

Requirements

Apart from the specific requirement to the topic, general candidate requirements are:

  • Strong motivation for a successful personal career.

  • A strong background in one or multiple related areas of AI, and Cybersecurity.

  • Strong background in operation system, especially cloud infrastructures.

  • Strong programming and communication skills.


The salary is from 100k to 150k SGD (after tax and 1 USD = 1.35 SGD), and much higher salary could be discussed based on the candidates' expertise.
