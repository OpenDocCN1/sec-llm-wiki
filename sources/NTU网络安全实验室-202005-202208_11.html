招聘博士与博士后, 欢迎各位老师同学帮忙转发:

Positions

10+ Ph.D. (Scholarship), Postdoc Positions on Testing, Analysis, Verification, and general-purpose quality assurance for Machine Learning System, Autonomous Vehicles, Cyber-physical System, AIoT, and AI Chips.


CSL of Nanyang Technological University Singapore

PANGU Intelligent Software Center of Kyushu University Japan

Research Projects

We have several exciting research projects on:

  1. Quality, Safety and Security Assurance for Machine Learning and AI System Engineering

  2. Modeling, Testing, Analysis, and Verification 

  3. Testing and Analysis of Autonomous Vehicles (Real car with license available, ready to run immediately)

  4. Testing and Analysis of Computer Vision, Speech Recognition, and Natural Language Processing Software System.

  5. Testing and Analysis of Reinforcement Learning and its application in Industrial Video Games

  6. Explainable AI (XAI) and its application in human brains, medical science, and material engineering

  7. AIoT and AI chip and embedded system security


The tentative candidate would work at either the Cyber-Security Laboratory at Nanyang Technological University or Pangu Lab Kyushu University, Japan, which is well equipped with powerful 40+ GPU servers, drones, autonomous cars.


The position involves conducting basic research, developing tools, working as part of a large research team, traveling, giving presentations, with an exciting chance with industrial collaborations top top-tier Software systems and AI players. The working language is English.

Requirements

Apart from the specific requirement to the topic, general candidate requirements are:

  • Strong motivation for a successful personal career.

  • A strong background in one or multiple related areas of SE, PL, AI, Security, Control, Robotics, CV, Speech, NLP, BCI, Bioinformatics.

  • Strong background in math and logic.

  • Strong programming and communication skills


The term is currently one to three year+ starting from January 2021 or Later.


The salary is 4k to 8k SGD per month, and much higher could be discussed based on the candidates' expertise.
