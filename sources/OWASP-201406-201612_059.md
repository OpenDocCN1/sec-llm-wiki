2\. OWASP Snakes & Ladders Project V1.10 发布（蛇梯棋项目）

中文版V1.02 ： 

[http://www.owasp.org.cn/owasp-project/snakes ](http://www.owasp.org.cn/owasp-project/snakes)

  

The OWASP Snakes & Ladders Project has released v1.10EN of "OWASP Snakes and Ladders - Web Applications". Snakes & Ladders is a simple educational board game for all sizes of people, promoting awareness of application security controls and risks, and in particular knowledge of other OWASP documents and tools.

  

This release updates the virtuous behaviours (ladders) to the secure coding practices defined in the 2016 version of the OWASP Top Ten Proactive Controls. The print-ready PDF is free to download and use, and can be obtained from:

[https://www.owasp.org/index.php/File:OWASP-SnakesAndLadders-WebApplications-EN.pdf](https://www.owasp.org/index.php/File:OWASP-SnakesAndLadders-WebApplications-EN.pdf)
