OWASP APPSEC EU FORECAST

欧洲峰会预报


活动预报：

 OWASP AppSec EU 2022 欧洲峰会将于2022年6月6日至10日线上举行。其中，6月9日至10日为会议时间，6月6日至8日提供为期1天、2天和3天的教育培训课程。

KEYNOTE SPEAKERS




Helpful hackers

We aim to make the digital world safer by reporting vulnerabilities we find in digital systems to the people who can fix them. We have a global reach, but do it Dutch style: open, honest, collaborative, and for free.


Astrid Oosenbrug

Mede-oprichter & Voorzitter Dutch Institute for Vulnerability Disclosure


June 10，9:00 AM - 10:00 AM


Insider threats and new economy industries

What are intentional insider threats, and why are the new economy industries so vulnerable to them? Are they just bad apples, or is something more complex going on? How can developers, business owners, and investors defend against these threats whilst maintaining an open, friendly, and collaborative corporate environment?


Lisa Forte

Partner， Red Goat Cyber Security LLP


June 9，9:00 AM - 10:00 AM



15 years of OWASP top 10 - has the cloud made a difference?

In 2006 AWS was born, and in 2007 Insecure Cryptographic Storage was first presented at OWASP Top 10. That vulnerability later changed into the sensitive data exposure, which transitioned into cryptographic failures. That’s just one example of the changes cloud infrastructure brought to application security. In this talk, I will go over more changes and revolutions the public cloud brought to us, and show you that “web application security” and “infrastructure security” are closer than you think.


Shira Shamban

Co-Founder and CEO Solvo


June 9, 4:00 PM - 5:00 PM



Cloud attack surface management



Alex Shulman-Peleg

Managing Director of Cloud Cybersecurity，Ernst & Young


June 10, 4:00 PM - 5:00 PM
