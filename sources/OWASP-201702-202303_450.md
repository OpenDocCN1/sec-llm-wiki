OWASP would like to welcome our new incubator project, OWASP Threat Dragon, to the fold. Threat Dragon is an online threat modeling application which includes system diagramming and a rule engine to auto-generate threats/mitigations. The tool focuses on UX design, a powerful rule engine and integration with other development lifecycle tools.
