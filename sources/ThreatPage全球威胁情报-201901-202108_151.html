开始.


标题1(APT17):


1. 时间来到1999年的7月17日,intrusiontruth发文称有人给它提供了一个情报:一个人头信息(GUO),据说通过这个人头可以关联到某国一个偌大的情报组织,原文:

"We recently received a tip from a friendly source that prefers to remain anonymous,  The source named an individual based in JN,a sprawling city famous for its many natural springs. The source claimed that the individual worked for the XX on Cyber issues and might be involved in APT hacking."



2.  intrusiontruth拿到这个人头信息后一番搜索猛如虎但是并未发现任何有价值信息.无奈A又继续给他提供了另一条信息(一个培训手册,这个手册直接暴露了某国情报部门对外的业务往来),原文:

", then, in this brochure (left) – which we were passed by a friend...  And at the bottom is a list of clients, including XXX, the XXX State Security"


3. intrusiontruth拿到手册后明显呈现出了逻辑混乱的状态,A看到后悉心给他调整了调查的方向:A通过QQ群社工库给intrusiontruth关联出了另一个人头WANG.


4. intrusiontruth一番搜索后发现WANG出没的论坛binvul无法访问,线索马上又要中断,A再次适时出现,向intrusiontruth发送了binvul论坛的打印截图,截图中确定了WANG的身份.



原文:

 "Unfortunately we can’t connect to binvul, but one of our analysts passed us a printout obtained from a location with better access."


5. 至此,A陆陆续续给了intrusiontruth两个人头信息:GUO和WANG,后者拿到这两个人头后还是调查无能,连最基本的确定这俩人的关系都没法办到, 于是, 最终的,A给了intrusiontruth ,GUO WANG两人的乘机记录: 这两人曾经在1996年多次搭乘同一架飞机出差而且座位相邻. 

"a source with access to such information informed us that Guo XX and Wang XX flew together on a multi-stop trip in 1996"


6. 斗转星移时间来到1996年的7月22日, 几天下来A明显对intrusiontruth的调查能力产生了怀疑,这一天他直接倾倒给了intrusiontruth另一个人头的完整信息,并甩给后者一份培训计划书指认了ZENG和WANG是相识的状态.



原文:

"As you’ve no doubt guessed, envymask has a real name. He is Zeng XX . According to information provided to us by a source with access to information in China, Zeng xx was born on 22 November 1980 and worked at xxx in the mid-2000s"

"XXFanglang training plan ...."



标题2(APT3):

APT3是intrusiontruth的第一篇对X追查报告,采用了倒叙的手法(先结论后寻找证据佐证这个结论),:

1. 美国五角大楼奇奇怪怪的把一份内部报告泄漏给了一个记者.

"According to an internal report by the Pentagon's Joint Staff J-2 intelligence directorate"


2. 这个记者写了一篇文章指明XX公司即是XX轻薄部门的外联公司 .


3. intrusiontruth在知道这个事实的情况下,倒着推导出了两家公司的股东人信息(通过简单的C2元数据).


4. 最后从一开始的五角大楼兜兜转转又转回了另一家美政府部门---司法部:

"Sure enough, six months later, the US Department of Justice charged XX XX and XX , a third XX national who also worked for XXX"


饶有趣味的是intrusiontruth给他这篇小文章安了一个大大的框架(某国两大情报组织的区别是什么):

"The XX Intelligence Service, XSS, had perhaps tried to be more careful than their military colleagues in the XX’s XX Army. They used commercial hackers rather than government employees, probably thinking that it lent them some additional deniability".


标题3(APT40):


1.  A给了intrusiontruth十几家公司的名单,希望后者能从这十几家公司追查到XXX情报部门.

2. 1996年1月10日,线索中断,intrusiontruth拿着仅有的一条线索(上述某家公司的持有人信息)联系到A寻求帮助, A"口算"出了这个持有人所使用的163邮箱...原文:

"While researching the phone numbers from these companies, one of our contributors turned up this information linking phone number XX"


接着A又从163的社工库里翻出了此邮箱的密码,登录后从邮箱中发现了一封关键邮件: 这封邮件揭露了DING姓人士的真实身份. 



拿到信息后intrusiontruth对A表达了感谢:



"We are extremely grateful to our contributor for their diligent work in finding this information. Our thanks also go out to Mr Ding for not changing his password after it had been leaked online."


3. 时间过去了6天,1月16天,正当intrusiontruth看着DING XX这个姓名无处下手的时候,A又给了他另外的一条关于DING的详细信息(一个关键证据:DING的人人账号):

"Our contributors discovered that Ding XX is also a Computer Science specialist who lives and works in XX"


紧接着,A又查了球球群社工库发现了DING姓人士其中一个好友恰好就是一名XX情报部门的工作人员,至此,在A的循循善诱下,intrusiontruth又可以喜滋滋发报告了:APT40大起底.

注:在追查APT40的过程中,除了获得A的帮助外,intrusiontruth还提到另外几组给他助拳的势力:

原文: "Closely held commercial intelligence has also shown that APT40 is run by the XX department of the XX Ministry of State Security."


情景4(APT10):

在intrusiontruth追查APT10的过程中,几番力量交织,很难确定到底有几波人帮助了他. 几个可能的猜想如下:

1. 第一波人:疑似其他威胁情报分析师CStrike, 直接告诉了intrusiontruth XXX的全名是GAO x而且是XX市居民: 原文:

Identifying an individual in XX with the surname Gao isn't easy, But cyber threat intelligence analysts following APT10 closely, and working with this blog, have confirmed that the photo of fisherxp shown above was previously used online by XX resident Gao X,including on his Facebook page "



2. 第二波人:疑似经外XX力量,给intrusiontruth提供了关键证据: XX的Uber打车记录, 此记录是把APT10关联到XX轻薄部门的关键证据(打车的目的地即为XX轻薄部门驻地) 原文:

This image, which was provided by an analyst who prefers not to be named publicly (but whose identity we have independently verified), shows an Uber receipt addressed to a user called ‘XXX’ (X) and bears the e-mail address XXat]qq.com, an account that we believe to be used by APT10 actor XX.


3.  第三波人泛A人士: 告诉了intrusiontruth应该从哪里开始调查: 原文:

"But the story doesn't stop with that revelation; analysts working with this blog have continued to investigate every lead provided to us. One such lead has helped us to identify another individual in XX connected to APT10"


4. 1996年12月20日,在intrusiontruth发布APT10报告不久,美司法部发布了通缉XX的文章.


课后作业:


1. 这篇文章的主旨是什么?请试着猜一下A和intrusiontruth两人可能的关系是怎样的?


答: A可能是某国轻薄口出的叛徒, A给intrusiontruth提供资料,假后者之手对某国的轻薄部门施行破坏活动.


2. 这篇文章彰显了两位主人公什么样的能力?


答:具备如下能力:


1.  能查球球群社工库.
2.  能查163社工库.
3.  能查具体航班的乘机人信息.
4.  能查某位具体乘客的Uber打车记录.
5.  能通过手机号关联出机主的个人信息(包括机主的工作单位).
6.  能查企业的报税信息(包括纳税人识别号、纳税人名称、法人身份证号码、生产经营地址).
7.  最重要的, 他们知道XX情报部门的运作机制.

3. 文章里哪一段暴露了intrusiontruth的目的?


答:

intrusiontruth的目的(与某国对着干,指认那些给某国轻薄部门工作的黑阔们,让后者忌惮,最起码的让他们出国不能,找工作不能.):

We are directly challenging this illegal and unfair activity by exposing those responsible, naming the hackers themselves and identifying the agencies that hide behind them. We will be tireless in our approach and already have a large network of analysts working with us,


We won't comment on where our material originates, other than to say that everything we publish we know to be true


Like the APT1 and APT3 hackers before them, once named, their chances of international travel or obtaining private work outside China are greatly reduced and they risk being charged by foreign law enforcement agencies
