\----  网易云热评

一、创建监听器

1、点击 Cobalt Strike -> Listeners->Add，其中内置了八个Payload，

wndows/beacon\_dns/reverse\_dns\_txt

windows/beacon\_http/reverse\_http

windows/beacon\_https/reverse\_https

windows/beacon\_bind\_pipe

windows/beacon\_tcp

windows/beacon\_extc2

windows/foreign/reverse\_http

windows/foreign/reverse\_https

  

2、windows/beacon为内置监听器，包括dns、http、https、smb、tcp、extc2六种方式的监听器；windows/foreign为外部监听器。

![](../Images/26c858228771bd2a83851bdbd7d2557e.png)

![](../Images/2c988fc7f38ea61d261f22d12dcce2c3.png)

  

二、生成木马

1、点击Attacks->Packages->Windows Executable，点击Generate生成，选择生成的路径及文件名保存即可。

![](../Images/d916b5afbc65ee592e06812849a3df9f.png)

![](../Images/ef76bf61f5d6b348cdd43e2124933d8f.png)

  

三、运行木马

将生成的exe文件上传到目标主机运行，自动在客户端上线
