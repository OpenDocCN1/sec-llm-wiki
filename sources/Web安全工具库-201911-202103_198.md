二、arp协议探测

1、选择模块：use auxiliary/scanner/discovery/arp\_sweep

![](../Images/ad406aa3ec99605da5041fbe98464a8a.png)

2、设置参数：show options

![](../Images/df06166ff1d5fb22fbabc2d57d2bf027.png)

3、设置扫描网段：set rhosts 192.168.1.0/24

4、设置扫描线程：set threads 30

![](../Images/7997a4c938f884b8e0db05d585dbf3cc.png)

5、运行：run

![](../Images/027e2bfd7151e188fe741d2460dcb78e.png)
