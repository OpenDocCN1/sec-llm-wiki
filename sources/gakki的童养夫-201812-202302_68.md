```

3.  `cve_2019_0708_bluekeep.rb -> /opt/metasploit-framework/embedded/framework/modules/auxiliary/scanner/rdp/cve_2019_0708_bluekeep.rb`
    
4.  `cve_2019_0708_bluekeep_rce.rb -> /opt/metasploit-framework/embedded/framework/modules/exploits/windows/rdp/cve_2019_0708_bluekeep_rce.rb`
```
