# 【美国资料】意外情况下的中情局（CIA）局长接替顺序

2019年6月14日，白宫发布了总统备忘录，提供了“中央情报局内部的继承令”。继任顺序决定了在中央情报局局长（现为吉娜·哈斯佩尔）去世，辞职或其他无法履行职责的情况下，将由谁（12人的名单）履行中央情报局局长的职能和职责。

**Memorandum on Providing an Order ofSuccession Within the Central Intelligence Agency**

**NATIONAL SECURITY &DEFENSE**

 

 Issued on: June 14, 2019

MEMORANDUM FOR THE DIRECTOR OF THECENTRAL INTELLIGENCE AGENCY

SUBJECT:      Providingan Order of Succession Within the  
Central Intelligence Agency

By the authority vested in me asPresident by the Constitution and the laws of the United States of America,including the Federal Vacancies Reform Act of 1998, as amended, 5 U.S.C.3345 *et seq*. (the “Act”), it is hereby ordered that:

Section 1. Order of Succession.  Subject to the provisions of section 2of this memorandum and to the limitations set forth in the Act, the followingofficers of the Central Intelligence Agency (CIA), in the order listed, shallact as and perform the functions and duties of the Director of the CentralIntelligence Agency (D/CIA), during any period in which the D/CIA has died,resigned, or otherwise becomes unable to perform the functions and duties ofthe office of D/CIA:

(a)  Deputy Director, CIA;

(b)  Chief Operating Officer,CIA;

(c)  Deputy Director of CIAfor Operations;

(d)  Deputy Director of CIAfor Analysis;

(e)  Deputy Director of CIAfor Science and Technology;

(f)  Deputy Director of CIAfor Digital Innovation;

(g)  Deputy Director of CIAfor Support;

(h)  General Counsel, CIA;

(i)  Deputy Chief OperatingOfficer, CIA;

(j)  Senior CIA Representativefor the United Kingdom;

(k)  Senior CIA Representativefor the East Coast; and

(l)  Senior CIA Representativefor the West Coast.

Sec. 2. Exceptions.  (a)  No individual who is serving in anoffice listed in section 1 of this memorandum in an acting capacity, by virtueof so serving, shall act as D/CIA pursuant to this memorandum.

(b)  No individual listed insection 1 of this memorandum shall act as D/CIA unless that individual isotherwise eligible to so serve under the Act.

(c)  Notwithstanding theprovisions of this memorandum, the President retains discretion, to the extentpermitted by law, to depart from this memorandum in designating an actingD/CIA.

Sec. 3. Revocation.  The Presidential Memorandum of September 16,2016 (Providing an Order of Succession Within the Central Intelligence Agency),is hereby revoked.

Sec. 4. General Provisions.  This memorandum is not intended to, anddoes not, create any right or benefit, substantive or procedural, enforceableat law or in equity by any party against the United States, its departments,agencies, or entities, its officers, employees, or agents, or any other person.

DONALDJ. TRUMP

关于在中央情报局内部提供继任命令的备忘录

国家安全与国防

发布于:2019年6月14日

中央情报局局长的备忘录

主题:提供继承权的顺序

中央情报局

  

以美利坚合众国宪法和法律赋予我的总统权力，包括经修正的《1998年联邦职位空缺改革法案》，《美利坚合众国法典》第5章3345节等。(“法令”)，现命令:

第一节 继承的顺序

受本谅解备忘录的第2部分的规定和限制的规定,在任何时期的CIA局长去世,辞职,或者无法履行职责，下列顺序列出的中央情报局(CIA)人员,应当作为中央情报局局长履行职责:

(a)中央情报局副局长;

(b)中央情报局首席业务官;

(c)中央情报局行动副主任;

(d)中央情报局分析副主任;

(e)中央情报局科学和技术副主任;

(f)中央情报局数字创新副主任;

(g)中央情报局支持副主任;

(h)中央情报局总法律顾问;

(i)中央情报局副首席业务干事;

(j)中央情报局英国高级代表;

(k)中央情报局东海岸高级代表;

(l)中央情报局西海岸高级代表。

第二节 例外

(a)任何以行动身份在本备忘录第1条所列办事处任职的人士，均不得因该等行为而成为本备忘录所指的D/CIA。

(b)本备忘录第1条中所列的个人不得担任局长，除非该个人另有资格根据本法担任局长。

(c)尽管本备忘录有规定，在法律允许的范围内，总统保留在指定代理局长时脱离本备忘录的自由裁量权。

第三节 撤销 

2016年9月16日的总统备忘录(提供中央情报局内部的继任命令)特此撤销。

第四节 一般规定

本备忘录不打算也不打算为任何一方创设任何可在法律上或衡平法上执行的、实质性的或程序性的权利或利益，针对美国、其部门、机构或实体、其官员、雇员或代理人或任何其他人。

  

唐纳德·j·特朗普
