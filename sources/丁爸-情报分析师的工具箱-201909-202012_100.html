Strategic & Competitive Intelligence Professionals (SCIP) 

2020 SCIP/CICI 竞争情报 (中国) 网络视频研讨会

时间：北京时间 9 月 19 日周六 上午 7：30-10：00



2020SCIP CICI竞争情报（中国）网络视频研讨会定于9月19日上午7:30-9:30在北京举行，仅限CICI SCIP会员参加，请联络研究院培训部010-88507861 确认参会资格。



议程安排： 

演讲议题一：

September 18, 7:30pm -8:00pm（美国时间）

Mr Cam Mackey 

Executive Director of SCIP 

SCIP CEO， Sir Cam Mackey to introduce benefits that our SCIP members can enjoy, including development perspective for SCIP, potential cooperation with Chinese competitive intelligence community and some new services that SCIP can provide to Chinese members.

SCIP执行董事

演讲议题二：

September 18, 8:00pm -8:40pm（美国时间）

Mr Paul Santilli 

HPE Competitive Intelligence Leader Chairman of SCIP’s Board of Directors

保罗·桑蒂利先生
HPE竞争情报负责人SCIP董事会主席

September 18, 8:40pm -9:30pm（美国时间）

Mrs Tracy Berry 

Senior Competitive Strategy Manager at ServiceMax 

Mrs Tracy Berry to discuss how can provide competitive intelligence consulting services to those Chinese companies that listed in the US or have close business relationship with the US from the perspective of a senior consulting advisor.


特雷西·贝瑞女士
ServiceMax高级竞争战略经理
特雷西·贝瑞女士从高级咨询顾问的角度，讨论如何为那些在美国上市或与美国有密切商业关系的中国公司提供竞争情报咨询服务。
