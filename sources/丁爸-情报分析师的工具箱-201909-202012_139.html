【情报实战】丁爸邀请你来挖掘黎智英的美国帮凶马克·西蒙（Mark Simon）看他会躲在哪里？

香港警务处国家安全处10日采取行动，拘捕乱港分子、“壹传媒”创办人黎智英、及其两儿子和多名壹传媒高层等人，身处海外的黎智英美籍助手马克•西蒙（Mark Simon）则被警方通缉。

马克·西蒙（Mark Simon）曾是“壹传媒”广告总监，其经历“十分神秘”，外界一度怀疑他是美国中央情报局（CIA）人员。6年前马克·西蒙称自己的父亲任职中情局35年，还说自己于1986年至90年代初，于美国海军情报局任潜艇分析员，此后没有在美国政府任何部门任职。有报道称，马克·西蒙亦曾是美国共和党的香港支部主席，与美国驻华大使有紧密联系。他与黎智英关系密切，多次陪伴黎智英到美国，平日也负责处理黎智英的捐款。他于今年6月接受媒体访问时透露，自己现时身处台湾，在台湾及美国都有业务，每年平均只会花5个月留港。


网报马克·西蒙资料

年龄：56

家庭：已婚，育有两个孩子

学历：东卡罗来纳大学；国防情报学院；乔治敦大学

职业：从1987年5月到1990年7月，在马里兰州舒特兰（Sutland, Md）的海军作战情报司令部海军情报特别项目部做威胁分析工作；1991-1993年，海陆服务局经理；1994-1995年，K-Line Shipping，全球客户经理；Trans-Pacific Lines，首席运营官；2000年至今，Next Media，高级主管

父亲：35年CIA雇员

政治：共和党。2002-2005年，香港共和党人副主席兼后来的主席

爱好：红酒，古巴雪茄


网报马克·西蒙2004年写给美国副总统办公室的信件：

29 March 29, 2004

Mr. Kevin Kellems, Office of the Vice-President

Public Affairs Office

202-456-2973


Dear Mr. Kellems


Gary Schmitt of the Project for the New American Century passed me your contact details and I think he spoke with you about our desire to get someone to interview the Vice-President while he is on his way to China.   


Next Media is the largest non-government publishing company in Taiwan and Hong Kong.   Apple Daily, our flagship newspaper, is the largest audited newspaper in Hong Kong and number two in Taiwan.   In the recent Taiwan election we were the number one newspaper in terms of advertising spend by the political parties according to AC Nielsen.  


Next Media has one more virtue, and I think many in DC from CATO to PNAC will attest too this.  We are a champion of free markets and free peoples.  And we have the only pro-American editorial page in all our Hong Kong.   


I doubt that you’ll get good words from the US Consulate on us, as we are not happy at all with their position on Hong Kong, and they know it.  I think they also consider us a bit too mass for their taste.


The market choice I think works for us in terms of reach.  As for the moral case, it wouldn’t hurt to have the US Vice-President choose the only non-tycoon controlled newspaper in Hong Kong to give an interview.   It would be noted by most, especially international media.


I can be reached at 852-9886-6662, I will be in DC on April 2nd on business and at your ease could come by for a quick word on the 2nd or the 3rd, as I know you work Saturdays around there.


I do thank you for taking the time


Mark Simon, General Manager

Apple Daily
