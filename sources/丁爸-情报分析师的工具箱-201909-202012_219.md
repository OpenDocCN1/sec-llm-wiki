**高级法庭科学线上培训课程——李昌钰法庭科学研究院**

**Advanced Forensic Science training on line by the Henry C. Lee Institute of Forensic Science.**

  

![](../Images/b83718b866df31fe510b2eff29caebda.png)

  

**线上授课时间**

  

Advanced Forensic Science Training, June 22 - 26, 2020, 2:00 pm - 4:00 pm EDT

高级法庭科学培训课程，6月22日至26日，下午两点至四点

  

![](../Images/17fa5e2683317ff909164ec8c06671d7.png)

**五天课程表**  

This is an on line training course sponsored by the Henry C. Lee Institute of Forensic Science at the University of New Haven. The course will be held live on Zoom and will be conducted by Dr. Henry C. Lee and Institute instructors from 2:00 pm til 4:00 pm EDT each day. The course topics will include New concepts in crime scene investigation and reconstruction by Dr. Lee, Bloodstain pattern examination and analysis, Trace evidence - Advanced instrumentation and microscopy, DNA Evidence and controversial topics in DNA analysis, and crime scene documentation. The final day, June 26th, will include a panel presentation by instructors.

本次线上培训课程由纽海文大学李昌钰法庭科学研究院支持。课程使用在线Zoom软件，并由李昌钰博士和学院其他讲师联合主持。培训课程的主题包括：犯罪现场勘查与重建的新概念（李昌钰博士主讲）、血迹形态检验与分析、微量物证——高级仪器与显微分析技术，DNA物证以及DNA分析存在的争议问题、犯罪现场记录。最后一天，6月26日，所有讲师将一同出场与大家共同讨论。

  

![](../Images/75c40b35d02261d779ad87095032002e.png)

  

This on line course is designed for law enforcement, attorneys, forensic science, and teachers. High school teachers will benefit from the information which can inform new curriculums in forensic science.

本次线上培训课程为下列人员设计：执法人员、律师、法庭科学家和教师。

  

![](../Images/e6d326e018bbf90974fd55a746b7f231.png)

**讲师阵容**

A certificate will be issued for completion of the course.

完成培训的学员将获得培训证书。

Options may include attending 1, 2, 3, 4, or the entire 5 days. The 5 day course registration fee is $100.00. Individual day attendance is $25.00 per day.

大家可以自行选择参加单独一天或者全部5天的课程。5整天的课程注册费为100美元，参加每一天的费用是25美元。
