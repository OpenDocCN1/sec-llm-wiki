一、ATF快速介绍

  

In 1972, when ATF was established as its own agency, it had 3,829 employees, including 1,622 special agents and 826 industry operations investigators.  The agency’s budget was less than $74 million.1972年，ATF（美国烟酒、枪支、爆炸物管理局）建立，组建初期3829名工作人员，当时的预算为七千四百万美元。In fiscal year 2018, ATF had 5,101 employees, including 2,630 special agents and 842 industry operations investigators.  The agency’s 2018 enacted budget was $1.274 billion.2018年ATF已有5101名工作人员，其中2630名专门工作人员，842名调查员。2018年预算约为13亿美元。ATF was established as a separate component within the Department of Justice pursuant to Title XI of the Homeland Security Act of 2002, Public Law 107-296, on January 17, 2003.2003年归属美国司法部

![](../Images/ffe2cba32058cb8a0ebd63deddf26d38.png)

ATF is a law enforcement organization committed to safeguarding lives. ATF’s mission is to protect the public from crimes involving firearms, explosives, arson and the diversion of tobacco products; to regulate lawful commerce in firearms and explosives, and to provide support to law enforcement, public safety and industry partners.  
ATF作为执法部门负责保护公民的生命。ATF使命，保护公众远离犯罪，包括：枪支、爆炸、放火和毒品犯罪等；为其他执法部门、公共安全部门和行业合作机构提供支持。  
![](../Images/e78ed0e5db093f4b9e57e487b0d3cd3d.png)**ATF组织结构**  
  

二、ATF放火案件调查

  

![](../Images/ae1bb8c72b8705215c67eeccb4afb8d6.png)

The Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives (ATF) is the Federal agency primarily responsible for administering and enforcing the criminal and regulatory provisions of the Federal laws pertaining to destructive devices (bombs), explosives, and arson. Over nearly 40 years, ATF has developed scientifically proven investigative capabilities, expertise, and resources that have positioned ATF as the Nation’s primary source for explosives and fire investigative knowledge and assistance.

经过40年的发展，ATF已经成为全美国权威的火灾与爆炸调查机构，为放火和爆炸案件提供科学调查方法、专业知识和资源。

Crimes involving arson and explosives are the most devastating crimes confronting our society. They destroy property, disrupt human lives, and place an ever increasing economic burden upon our citizens. The increasing number of violent acts involving arson and explosives has established a greater need for state and local investigators with a high level of expertise.

放火与爆炸案件是威胁社会最严重的暴力犯罪形式。这类案件毁坏财物、扰乱社会秩序、破坏经济稳定形式。放火与爆炸案件的调查需要执法部门具备更高级别的专业知识与素质。

四、ATF火灾物证实验室 

Fire Research Laboratory

  

The ATF Fire Research Laboratory (FRL) is the first in the U.S. to provide a variety of services including both on-scene support and remote technical assistance in the fire investigation community.

ATF火灾物证实验室是全美第一个为火灾现场提供支持，也为远程机构提供技术服务的专业实验室。

FRL is an innovative partnership among law enforcement, fire services, public safety agencies, academia and the private sector using advanced scientific, technical, educational and training methods to make ATF and its partners’ leaders in fire investigation science to serve and protect the public.

该实验室具有革新性的合作模式，与执法机构、火灾机构、公共安全机构、学术机构和私人组织保持合作，使用高级科学技术、教育和培训方法，使自身与合作机构逐步成为火灾科学调查领域的领导者，旨在服务和保护公众。

FRL’s facilities, equipment and staff work on important fire investigation issues such as: fire scene reconstruction; flashover studies; validation of fire pattern analysis indicators; impact of accelerants on fire growth and spread ignition studies and electrical fire cause analysis. All analyses are conducted in support of ATF CFI fire origin and cause determinations.

实验室的设施、设备和工作人员在火灾调查领域开展以下专业研究：火灾现场重建、火燃烧研究、火灾形态分析与有效性验证；助燃剂对火势的影响；分散起火点研究；电器起火研究等。所有研究旨在支持ATF对起火原因和起火点的判断与决策。

六、爆炸火灾追踪系统

What is BATS?

  

  

![](../Images/2fc155b4f57b7df25738b455921165c7.png)

The Bomb Arson Tracking System (BATS) is a web-based case management system which provides you, State and local arson & explosives investigators, access to up-to-date arson and explosives investigative data from across the nation at no charge. Unlike anything you may currently use, it's not just limited to your local jurisdiction or state. BATS provides you access to national arson and explosives incident information.爆炸火灾追踪系统是一个网络版本的案件管理系统，该系统免费提供全美最新的火灾与爆炸调查数据、案件信息和相关信息。**爆炸火灾追踪系统的运行 ****How Does BATS Work?**  
BATS is a secure, user friendly, web-based computer application. All you need is a browser and an Internet connection to use it. BATS is available to you around the clock. You can share your arson and explosives incidents with other BATS users across the country, and you can search and analyze national data to discover trends, patterns, and leads. You can also use BATS to create a variety of reports customized to your needs, and track and report incidents to justify budget requests. BATS is agency-administered and reports are printed with your agency’s letterhead. BATS is also part of the national curriculum at the Hazardous Devices School (bomb technicians) at Redstone Arsenal in Huntsville, Alabama and the National Fire Academy’s Fire/Arson Origin-and-Cause Investigations course in Emmitsburg, MD. In addition, BATS is part of the FBI’s “Model for Bomb Squad Standard Operating Procedures” (SOPs).该系统是一个案件、便捷、基于网络的计算机应用程序。工作人员只需联网就可以登录使用。所有信息24小时更新。人员可以在该系统内相互分享信息，查询和分析数据来发现趋势、走向和线索等信息。还可以使用该系统创建适合自身需求的报告、追踪和了解相关案件的信息并根据这些信息制定预算。该系统与诸多系统联合互通。

**系统特征**

**Features & Benefits**

  

BATS features state-of-the-art case management technology. Users are able to capture details of explosives and fire cases, including improvised explosive device (IED) components, incendiary devices, origin-and-cause or device placement, suspect information, casualties, dollar losses, fire descriptors, collateral crimes, witness interviews, juvenile firesetters, and descriptions of how a device was delivered. BATS is separate from the NFIRS database. NFIRS reports the fire department suppression efforts; BATS documents the follow-up investigation. BATS allows users to search for case similarities nationwide to discover whether the same individual, IED, or arson is related to an investigation in another jurisdiction.使用者可以获得爆炸和火灾案件的详细信息，包括：临时组装爆炸物成分、引爆装置、嫌疑人信息、案件伤亡情况、财产损失、火灾情形、犯罪情形、证人证言、消防员扑救情况和爆炸装置运输情况等案件相关信息；该系统的文档与调查紧密相关；使用者可以在系统中查询全国范围内相似的案件，可找寻是否具有相同的嫌疑人或者同类放火案件调查的相关信息。![](../Images/25db67690bd5e3a80bb039bbd23e6eb5.png)**系统界面**![](../Images/7d2d23c247836fa26a3c53331324da4b.png)**系统界面**
