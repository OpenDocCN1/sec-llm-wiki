# 支付宝“劫持”红包简单分析

> 原文：[http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?\_\_biz=MzA4NjQxMDcxNA==&mid=2709352203&idx=1&sn=98418bba719ef0dcac94b459b0059caa&chksm=bb40dbbe8c3752a875b4cb84d3d5fd8e32adeec38fcc61a0b754ad47f848b8a483a8cc11255f&scene=126&sessionid=1681402797#rd](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzA4NjQxMDcxNA==&mid=2709352203&idx=1&sn=98418bba719ef0dcac94b459b0059caa&chksm=bb40dbbe8c3752a875b4cb84d3d5fd8e32adeec38fcc61a0b754ad47f848b8a483a8cc11255f&scene=126&sessionid=1681402797#rd)

为什么一点开链接就能打开支付宝？我猜是伪协议，一看果然是。

  

首先这是领赏金的二维码：

![](../Images/37e4579659947244534e8121679a221f.png)

  

扫描以后会得到如下url

  

https://qr.alipay.com/c1x08470phqb9pe05xafq0b

  

浏览器访问主要是经历这几跳：

  

![](../Images/869aa9a39a17bf8dd5f51b2410348bbe.png)

  

最主要的是这段url

  

https://ds.alipay.com/?from=mobilecodec&scheme=alipays%3A%2F%2Fplatformapi%2Fstartapp%3FsaId%3D10000007%26clientVersion%3D3.7.0.0718%26qrcode%3Dhttps%253A%252F%252Fqr.alipay.com%252Fc1x08470phqb9pe05xafq0b%253F\_s%253Dweb-other

  

解码看下

  

https://ds.alipay.com/?from=mobilecodec&scheme=alipays://platformapi/startapp?saId=10000007&clientVersion=3.7.0.0718&qrcode=https://qr.alipay.com/c1x08470phqb9pe05xafq0b?\_s=web-other

  

仔细看scheme=alipays这句，实际上就是调用了支付宝的伪协议，就好比一些网站上，提示你点击就能打开某个app一样的道理。

  

我们只要构造如下html，调用支付宝的伪协议就能在浏览器中打开支付宝

  

window.location.href='alipays://platformapi/startapp?saId=10000007&clientVersion=3.7.0.0718&qrcode=https://qr.alipay.com/c1x08470phqb9pe05xafq0b?\_s=web-other'

  

如果把https://qr.alipay.com/c1x08470phqb9pe05xafq0b替换成任意网址呢
