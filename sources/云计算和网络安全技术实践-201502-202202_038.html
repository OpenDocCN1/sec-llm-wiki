最近有需要又开始研究OSM，最新版本是9.0了，安装就不说了，跟以前一样，界面漂亮了不说，后台已经进化到基于纯kubernetes的了，

值得关注的是，VNFD和NSD的模板，都已经进化到SOL006的标准了，

还是拿我司VNF来做实验，本期先搞个单机，并指定接口地址，

NSD模板如下，

  nsd:
  - description: Generated by OSM package generator
    designer: OSM
    df:
    - id: default-df
      vnf-profile:
      - id: '1'
        virtual-link-connectivity:
        - constituent-cpd-id:
          - constituent-base-element-id: '1'
            constituent-cpd-id: hillstone-vnf-eth0
          virtual-link-profile-id: hillstone-mgt
        - constituent-cpd-id:
          - constituent-base-element-id: '1'
            constituent-cpd-id: hillstone-vnf-eth1
          virtual-link-profile-id: hillstone-west
        - constituent-cpd-id:
          - constituent-base-element-id: '1'

启动NS实例的时候传参如下，

{vld: [ {name: hillstone-mgt, vnfd-connection-point-ref: [ {member-vnf-index-ref: "1", vnfd-connection-point-ref: hillstone-vnf-eth0, ip-address: "10.0.0.11"}, {member-vnf-index-ref: "1", vnfd-connection-point-ref: hillstone-vnf-eth1, ip-address: "10.0.2.11"}, {member-vnf-index-ref: "1", vnfd-connection-point-ref: hillstone-vnf-eth2, ip-address: "10.0.3.11"}]}]}

这就完成了一个多接口VNF实例的创建，网络是OpenStack那边提前准备好的，也可以不提前准备，靠传参新建。
