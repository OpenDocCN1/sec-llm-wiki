# Tacker VNF Scaling的实践

> 原文：[http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?\_\_biz=MzA3MjM5MDc2Nw==&mid=2650744362&idx=1&sn=c9883e69e84e066fe4d77aa8db9fb5ea&chksm=8714872ab0630e3c9083e6e115914c0663ef31be0355b9d7686dfe69c248d9fd66b7ed2829ae&scene=27#wechat\_redirect](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzA3MjM5MDc2Nw==&mid=2650744362&idx=1&sn=c9883e69e84e066fe4d77aa8db9fb5ea&chksm=8714872ab0630e3c9083e6e115914c0663ef31be0355b9d7686dfe69c248d9fd66b7ed2829ae&scene=27#wechat_redirect)

继Tacker NS的实践之后，本周又完成了Tacker VNF Scaling的实践，

首先给VNFD里添加Scaling策略，

policies:  
    - SP1:  
        type: tosca.policies.tacker.Scaling  
        properties:  
          increment: 1  
          cooldown: 120  
          min\_instances: 1  
          max\_instances: 5  
          default\_instances: 2  
          targets: \[VDU1\]

创建VNFD，

![](../Images/9c524d28e4596011e070ce75e536b520.png)

创建VNF，

![](../Images/ac287855fd7e4df3f3e8a96b40ec60e2.png)

查看VNF虚机实例，可以看到按照策略默认起了两个，

![](../Images/99c469366ae7280e7b7fea51ab20ed45.png)

通过命令触发Scaling，

tacker vnf-scale --vnf-name tosca-vnf-scale --scaling-policy-name SP1 --scaling-type out，

开始Scaling，

![](../Images/2700f8b6768f99d3408cef1ac97855cd.png)

Scaling成功，

![](../Images/b88e3c58fb6313ff5f015c91904df059.png)

![](../Images/c620c7070421b40c8aa1fdfe7595ca5c.png)
