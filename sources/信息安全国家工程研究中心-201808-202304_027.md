2月24日，**“2023年中国网络和数据安全产业高峰论坛--商用密码创新应用分论坛”**在成都成功举办。本次论坛以**“推动密码应用创新，夯实网络安全基座”**为主题，围绕基础通信、车联网、工业互联网和关键信息基础设施等重要行业和领域，对商用密码产业创新发展的新思路、新模式、新方法进行了深入探讨。中国工程院院士沈昌祥围绕“密码智能化跨越发展夯实网络强国、数字中国安全基石”作主旨演讲。他指出，以密码为基因的主动免疫可信计算使密码工作跨入了智能化新时代。面对复杂多变的网络环境，我们应当基于密码构建网络空间安全防御体系，坚持自主创新。**以下是分享PPT：**![](../Images/000057d83f233716d83657b614f101fc.png)![](../Images/7b4bcb5777c2fe5b47d1bb71dcaa5773.png)![](../Images/ce5b4d9fb6d19cc7f4791e98e41dd6a5.png)![](../Images/a4aeac25f9f33b74090431e013c579a5.png)![](../Images/d47052c9acbcc1d2201fd2ecb84609ec.png)![](../Images/d4c69c0eb7e448f37f518ad0cdf59d20.png)![](../Images/46aa763995dd8d9a39e65700a0c828ea.png)![](../Images/03232482d6b89ac82c2f22df65ea5c61.png)![](../Images/a2722bf4301573b26141be2cbb0ffba4.png)![](../Images/c299eedad6ae1b7dad97618a679b1c86.png)![](../Images/2d9889f201e23e2753bf488c0577e4a2.png)![](../Images/1b9c92f884ffcfce2e6705288a9ab613.png)![](../Images/3801207684f2e669a3b63d9f8b70d812.png)![](../Images/6f92a4a6c8a4b843517bc6f0b0b4d107.png)![](../Images/39e0c6330413ab26bbb9ddbde836042b.png)![](../Images/dbb4c36e4dbe779c588846f12c10858d.png)![](../Images/06a4e6da2bf797ce419724850d2dfc4b.png)![](../Images/67d40376b92e278fe0a1bd6831b2049a.png)![](../Images/c2bcdb9841b46cade8473d9ee70f28c1.png)![](../Images/58016f653f207aaa9eca6bb68773fcda.png)![](../Images/1a0502f327fbcc75ae38cc17290348c4.png)![](../Images/2396b3216c08dda07c661ecfa2e1e324.png)![](../Images/8e7d98ee975ad67d90b0b8586081d986.png)![](../Images/85e99e3c6aa57ea856fa51f9f30529d8.png)![](../Images/089d2bc437258910da3d031d3e32392e.png)![](../Images/2817df808f40431b13148464406505f8.png)![](../Images/795125864e7fe3bc85024dd0bb603a91.png)![](../Images/4ed6136b588211601ab4508d5365d71c.png)![](../Images/eb7ece41d4348d95c918722618197053.png)
