主题：数论与密码

        主持人：吕昌副研究员

题 目：

An Extension of Euler's Factoring Fermat Number F_{5}


报告人：

   Huilin Zhu (Xiamen University)


  报告摘要：

In 1732, Euler found Fermat Number F_{5} is a composite number and has a small divisor 641. In this talk we give an extension of the structure of factoring F_{5} and get some results and have some questions. This is a joint work with Bennett and He.
