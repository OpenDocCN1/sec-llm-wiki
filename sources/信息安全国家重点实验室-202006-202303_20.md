**主题：数论与密码**

  

  

**        时    间：**2020年10月22日 10:00-11:00

**       地    点****：**益园C2-301会议室

**        主持人：**吕昌副研究员

**题 目：**

An Extension of Euler's Factoring Fermat Number F\_{5}

  

**报告人：**

   Huilin Zhu (Xiamen University)

  

**  报告摘要：**

In 1732, Euler found Fermat Number F\_{5} is a composite number and has a small divisor 641. In this talk we give an extension of the structure of factoring F\_{5} and get some results and have some questions. This is a joint work with Bennett and He.
