# 全知科技CEO 方兴 谈中国网络安全发展趋势——人民网专访

      近日，全知科技CEO方总接受**人民网英文专栏专题采访**，就本次创客汇赛后感受谈谈未来中国网络安全发展趋势。专题报道也同步发表在**Facebook和Twitter**上。

  

您这次全程参与了创客汇比赛，您对于BCS创客会的有什么样的评价呢？

创业公司/项目比赛是一个很好的方式，让创业公司对外宣传，让行业了解产业创新的方向。奇安信作为安全行业内的领军企业推动创业比赛是非常有意义的。

  

  

您对自己参赛的表现满意嘛？

还不错。安全其实是非常专业的领域，想在3分钟把自己的项目说清楚很难，因此在表达时需要选择最重要最容易被理解的点去演绎，这一点很重要，很关键。我是国内网络安全最早一批的研究员，无论从技术研究方向上看还是从前两次创业上看，都是走在国内甚至世界的最前沿，而对应未来的发展又验证了正确性和准确性。因此强调这种历史的前瞻性给大家带来的体感比讲清楚自己现在的技术方向更容易让人接受和信服。

  

  

您觉得其他选手的参赛表现怎么样？您会怎么评价其他选手的公司定位？

通过三分钟演讲+5分钟问答来区分公司好坏方向正确与否是不太可能的。比赛更多是对CEO准备演讲内容能力的考验，演讲如何抓住眼球（投资人评委），你的项目是否已经有了足够的用户场景（甲方评委），你的方向创新度和趋势度（专家评委）。其他项目很多创业者也是我朋友，他们项目也很棒。赢得冠军只是我比他们更懂如何在这个场合演讲。

  

  

全知科技是您第三次创业了，这次创业会给您带来什么新的感受吗？

前两次创业，安全行业非常小众，非常艰难。但自从总书记419讲话以来，网络安全产业获得了空前关注，整个产业的发展与变革非常迅猛，这是本次创业与前两次创业感受最大的区别。我相信在这种形势下，安全产业会迎来一个高速成长期，许多技术创新的安全企业会涌现并快速成长成为行业领军企业，共筑中国的网络安全长城。

**”****Chinese cybersecurity startups ready to fledge and fly**

  

For Fang Xing, it was not an easy to start a business in the “niche” industry of cybersecurity. Yet the man has incubated three startup projects and won another prize with his latest.

  

Fang and his Quanzhi Tech team brought home the champion title at the startup competition during the 2019 Beijing Cyber Security Conference in August.“I’m a cybersecurity engineer myself, and from what I’ve done with my last two entrepreneurial projects, my experience and projects have both proved my judgement correct and far-sighted. Within three minutes \[during the presentation\], it is critical to show you are forward-looking in strategy and technology,” Fang said.Fang’s company aims to protect data security during data flow, while his peer competitors have set eyes on various other fields, from IoT and data privacy to supercomputing and even cybersecurity insurance.“Different from my previous experience in starting a business in cybersecurity, this time I can feel the whole industry under spotlight and each day something new comes up in the industry. The development and reform is tremendous,” Fang told People’s Daily.Like Innovation Sandbox at the RSA Conference in the US, startup competitions began to catch public attention in China since 2016. Over the years, a number of Chinese startups have taken off from such competitions.“It’s delightful to see how each startup today has its own focus, and that they are increasingly focused on everyday services and data applications, which goes along with innovative security models,” said Wu Yunkun, president of Qi An Xin Group, who hosted the competition.China’s cybersecurity market spending reached $7.35 billion in 2019 and experts estimate that the annual growth rate will be 25.1%, making it a leader in the global market. By 2023, the market volume will reach $17.9 billion, according to the latest IDC report.The booming cybersecurity market is also consistent with the overall high-tech industry in the past years. With more enterprises aiming high for technology that is more cutting-edge and economic progress, the continuous investment in R&D and innovation also in turn expand the horizon of cybersecurity players, said Cai Jingjing, founder of Integrity Tech, a Beijing-based cybersecurity company.There is also a rising demand for better cybersecurity products and services, as Chinese enterprises become more proactive in cyber defense, Cai told People’s Daily.China has tightened its grip on national cybersecurity over the past years, especially after it announced the nation’s first Cyber Security Law in November 2016, which took effect one year later. The nation has hence picked up pace in cyber defense, repeating its pledge to become a major cyber security power.“The cybersecurity industry is bound to take off and more startups will rise and gradually lead in the industry to safeguard the nation’s cybersecurity,” Fang said.
