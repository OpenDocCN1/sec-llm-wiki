**关键词：时间敏感网络；网络设备；设备测试**

**网址：**

[https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/JAJUJwLfQAViYwC3mZz5KA](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzI0MjE4NDUwNA==&mid=2652110643&idx=1&sn=b1e3b39f729330e9fd08c8e2f2bdb5c6&scene=21#wechat_redirect)

**网址：**

[https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/Ku\_ViZOysog4pB3Plqq8ZQ](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzAwMDEwNTE0MA==&mid=2651120300&idx=2&sn=7b302395abc6b2fb15c54f2c5f9d478d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)
