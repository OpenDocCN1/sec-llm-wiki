there are two flags in the form of xnuca{…}. Please submit the concatenation of the two string between the brackets (brackets not included). E.g. if the first flag is xnuca{here\_is\_flag1}, the second flag is xnuca{here\_is\_flag2}, please submit: here\_is\_flag1here\_is\_flag2.

#### 题目描述

#### 题目解答

利用ioport write将opaque->memorymode设为1，就可以通过设置`opaque->req.offset`，在`vexx_cmb_write`以及`vexx_cmb_read`函数中对`opaque->req.req_buf`进行越界读写，由于该buf后存在一个timer结构体，可以利用越界读获取堆地址以及程序基址。然后利用越界写将timer结构体中的`cb`覆盖为`system`地址，`opaque`覆盖为参数地址，最终触发timer，拿到flag：`flag{SOEASY_Escape_qemu_from_timer}`
