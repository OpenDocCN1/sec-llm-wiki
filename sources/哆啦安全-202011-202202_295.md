# App绕过iOS手机的越狱检测

> 原文：[http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?\_\_biz=Mzg2NzUzNzk1Mw==&mid=2247487843&idx=1&sn=ba2328578b35a48e46a2b7d686ec75fe&chksm=cebb542df9ccdd3b002b1d3f86fde4a9c5f1174dbeabb737985d07c4c87e0b0cf48731522b11&scene=126&sessionid=1681666867#rd](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=Mzg2NzUzNzk1Mw==&mid=2247487843&idx=1&sn=ba2328578b35a48e46a2b7d686ec75fe&chksm=cebb542df9ccdd3b002b1d3f86fde4a9c5f1174dbeabb737985d07c4c87e0b0cf48731522b11&scene=126&sessionid=1681666867#rd)

iOS越狱之后，某些App会检测到iOS设备已越狱后，一些功能就会被禁用，导致无法正常使用App里面的所有功能

  

**1.iOS绕过越狱检测的所需环境**

Cydia 、Liberty

  

**2.添加Cydia源**

**(1).在手机桌面上找到Cydia的图标，点击进入**

![](../Images/a0f97fbaceae216f1a094f68a4b44fc6.png)

  

**(2).先点击下方软件源，然后再右上角编辑**

![](../Images/d627259b46aa4801b02b5feb8c7c6c03.png)

  

![](../Images/643a3643098ed959d42ba9e01f9de103.png)

  

**(3).点击添加**  

![](../Images/58522c70fa32cf0d6cc39e0c3a3ab5ea.png)

  

**(4).在弹框中输入要添加的源**

*   
*   
*   
*   
*   
*   
*   

```
雷锋源http://apt.abcydia.com
或
威锋源http://apt.feng.com
```

  

![](../Images/65f9292b4998df922a02c12a4a170c83.png)

  

![](../Images/6200aba97a360667a3af63203a302369.png)

  

**(5).点击添加源后，会弹出软件源警告，点击仍然添加**

![](../Images/d627259b46aa4801b02b5feb8c7c6c03.png)

  

**(6).等源加载完毕后，点回到Cydia**

![](../Images/fe145c06de653ffa11078ab99cd94265.png)

  

**(7).查看到添加的雷锋源或威锋源被添加到软件源列表中**

![](../Images/1347c12c303bf11637439de0de9152fb.png)

  

![](../Images/9d5d66d457b87d45dca676229cf39871.png)

  

**3.Cydia中搜索Liberty下载并安装Liberty**

**(1).安装Liberty**

![](../Images/376153a2e2dc295a08fa31d42c9a042f.png)

  

****(2).**点击安装后会重启手机，然后打开Cydia，点击继续队列**

![](../Images/56d70dd6b8fb05bdfe482cd2095369cd.png)

  

**4.设置Liberty**

**(1).打开设置，找到Liberty**

![](../Images/472ac48cdb6eb99cbae0b321526ae2c3.png)

  

**(2).点击启用，打开屏蔽越狱检测，就可以绕过iOS App的越狱检测**

![](../Images/13d4ff24bb320789f052333511c1761d.png)
