# 思科前员工恶意删除 456 个虚拟机，造成直接损失 1652 万人民币

> 原文：[http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?\_\_biz=MzI5NTM4OTQ5Mg==&mid=2247502445&idx=4&sn=42bbd40d2f663030322d3145d1cba739&chksm=ec56da36db215320a6101c10351c564080fa73da2d584e21b454f31f6c6c5d11a71f542da729&scene=126&sessionid=1681313888#rd](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzI5NTM4OTQ5Mg==&mid=2247502445&idx=4&sn=42bbd40d2f663030322d3145d1cba739&chksm=ec56da36db215320a6101c10351c564080fa73da2d584e21b454f31f6c6c5d11a71f542da729&scene=126&sessionid=1681313888#rd)
