# 70款未完成整改APP被广东省通信管理局点名

> 原文：[http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?\_\_biz=MzI5NTM4OTQ5Mg==&mid=2247575224&idx=4&sn=5060139050bc9cd9aa1518f5388e30df&chksm=ec57b6e3db203ff5f64264674b9f4739f40a3c7c7c44becfa883379be5837fc999df824b4938&scene=126&sessionid=1681313036#rd](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzI5NTM4OTQ5Mg==&mid=2247575224&idx=4&sn=5060139050bc9cd9aa1518f5388e30df&chksm=ec57b6e3db203ff5f64264674b9f4739f40a3c7c7c44becfa883379be5837fc999df824b4938&scene=126&sessionid=1681313036#rd)
