# 网络安全等级测评与检测评估机构“航天开元科技”存在问题，被要求整改

> 原文：[http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?\_\_biz=MzI5NTM4OTQ5Mg==&mid=2247585154&idx=3&sn=19929cffc152654f0c98c8e5644aef1e&chksm=ec579dd9db2014cf0642a97e6c08f07dbdde36a0e75b9f04ff126551f0d733d89fac4befbed0&scene=126&sessionid=1681312945#rd](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzI5NTM4OTQ5Mg==&mid=2247585154&idx=3&sn=19929cffc152654f0c98c8e5644aef1e&chksm=ec579dd9db2014cf0642a97e6c08f07dbdde36a0e75b9f04ff126551f0d733d89fac4befbed0&scene=126&sessionid=1681312945#rd)
