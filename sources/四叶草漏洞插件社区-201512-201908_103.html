seclover.php文件代码，主要是对关键词的过滤，根据关键词的长度进行循环替换。那么可以把 select 中间加一个 select 就可以了。比如 selselectect 进过一次替换后，还是select，除了符号其他关键字都可以用这种方法替换。

<?phpfunction seclover($content){$keyword=array("select","union","and","from"," ","'",";",'"',"char","or","count","master","name","pass","admin","+","-","order","=");$info=strtolower($content);for($i=0;$i<=count($keyword);$i++){$info=str_replace($keyword[$i], '',$info);
}return $info;
}?>
