附演讲稿：  

大家好，我叫谷XX，今天，我想给大家讲一讲我和国宝的故事。

在我小时候，读过一本历史书，上面讲述了一个在我们河南出土的 “镇国之宝”——后母戊鼎。书上说，它是商朝的一个王为了纪念他的母亲而铸造的。我很想亲眼看看这个寄托着对母亲的爱的国宝。

 在我五岁那年，妈妈带我来到首都北京，在国家博物馆里，我终于亲眼看到了它。

经过了上千年，它依然十分引人注目，厚重的身体，精致典雅，气势恢宏。讲解员告诉我：这么庞大的青铜器，这么高超的工艺，它是已经发现的最大的青铜器。

 我骄傲，它来自我的家乡。我自豪，它代表了华夏文明的历史源远流长。我们的祖先用智慧创造了历史。我们新一代少年儿童，会做好国宝守护人，传承历史，开启文明新天地。

 

Hello, my name is Gu XX. 

Today, I want to tell you a story about myself and the national treasure. 

When I was a little girl, I read a history book about Houmuwuding, the "National treasure" which was excavated in Henan Province. 

  

The book said, one of the king in Shang Dynasty made it in commemoration for his mum. I would like to see this National treasure full of Mum’s love with my own eyes. 

  

At the age of five, my mother took me to Beijing, and I saw it with my own eyes at National Museum.  

  

After thousands of years, it is still very elegant and magnificent. The guide told me that so far it is the largest bronzes, and it is so huge, so high level skill.  

  

I am proud of it, it was excavated in my hometown. I am proud of it, which represents a long history of China. Our ancestors created history with wisdom. We will be the guardian of the national treasure, and inherit the history and creat the future.

  

注：apple是我的女儿，7岁半，小学二年级。
