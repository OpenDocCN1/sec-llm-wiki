**寻宝**

![](../Images/3d5599b0862c70f3bb636028f0640e9d.png)

  

  

   原附件每个字节的高位和低位互换得到压缩包文件

   f = open("寻宝", 'rb')

  hex\_list = ("{:02X}".format(int(c)) for c in f.read())

   buflist = list(hex\_list)

   for i in buflist:

   i = i\[::-1\]

   print(i+' ')

![](../Images/678fed249ef53169f9ef687778d7ad59.png)
