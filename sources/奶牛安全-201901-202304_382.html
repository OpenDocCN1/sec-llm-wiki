Controls are put into place to reduce the risk an organization faces, and they come in three main flavors: administrative, technical, and physical.
控制措施是为了降低组织面临的风险，这些控制措施主要有三种：管理控制、技术控制和物理控制。
Administrative controls are commonly referred to as “soft controls” because they are more management oriented.
管理控制通常被称为“软控制”，因为它们更多地是以管理为导向的。
Technical controls (also called logical controls) are software or hardware components, as firewalls, IDS, encryption, and identification and authentication mechanisms.
技术控制(也称为逻辑控制)是软件或硬件组件，如防火墙、入侵检测系统、加密以及识别和验证机制。
physical controls are items put into place to protect facility, personnel, and resources.
物理控制是为保护设施、人员和资源而设置的条目。
These control types need to be put into place to provide defense-in-depth, which is the coordinated use of multiple security controls in a layered approach.
这些控制类型需要到位以提供深度防御，即在分层方法中多个安全控制的协调使用。

The rule of thumb is the more sensitive the asset, the more layers of protection that must be put into place.
首要法则是，资产越敏感，必须实施的保护层数就越多。
The different functionalities of security controls are preventive, detective, corrective, deterrent, recovery, and compensating.
安全控制的不同功能包括预防、检测、纠正、威慑、恢复和补偿。

        Preventive Intended to avoid an incident from occurring
        预防   意在避免事故发生

     Detective Helps identify an incident’s activities and potentially an intruder
        检测   帮助识别事件的活动和潜在的入侵者。
       Corrective Fixes components or systems after an incident has occurred
        纠正   在事故发生后修复组件或系统。
        Deterrent Intended to discourage a potential attacker
        威慑   旨在阻止潜在袭击者
         Recovery Intended to bring the environment back to regular operations
        恢复   旨在使环境恢复正常运行。
        Compensating Controls that provide an alternative measure of control
        补偿   提供另一种控制措施的控制


Preventive: Administrative
预防性：行政管理。
        •Policies and procedures
        ·政策和程序。
        •Effective hiring practices
        ·有效的招聘做法。
        •Pre-employment background checks
        ·职前背景调查。
        •Controlled termination processes
        ·受控终止流程。
        •Data classification and labeling
        ·数据分类和标注。
        •Security awareness
        ·安全意识。
Preventive: Physical
预防性：物理。
        •Badges, swipe cards
        ·徽章、刷卡。
        •Guards, dogs
        ·警卫，警犬。
        •Fences, locks, mantraps
        ·栅栏、锁、陷阱。
Preventive: Technical
预防性：技术。
        •Passwords, biometrics, smart cards
        ·密码、生物识别、智能卡。
       •Encryption, secure protocols, call-back systems, database views,         constrained user interfaces
        ·加密、安全协议、回拨系统、数据库视图、受限用户界面。
    •Antimalware software, access control lists, firewalls, intrusion prevention system
        ·反恶意软件、访问控制列表、防火墙、入侵防御系统

a compensating control is just an alternative control that provides similar protection as the original control, but has to be used because it is more affordable or allows specifically required business functionality.
补偿措施只是一种替代措施，它提供与原始控件类似的措施，但必须使用，因为它更实惠，或者允许特定需要的业务功能。
