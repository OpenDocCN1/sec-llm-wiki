职位

Global Senior Infrastructure Project Manager

（职级P8-P11）

岗位职责

1.Act as domain expert in both Infra and business, Infra project manager who focusing on client relationship management, is responsible for building and establishing positive relationship between Tencent overseas Infra and its internal/external business clients.

2.In-depth understanding of Tencent internal/external business clients, continuous communication of clients’s infra needs, able to collect & identify business clients’ pain points, problems and potential demands, then transform them to infra-language requirements, ensure the orderly management of Infra requirements.

3.Infra Solution adoption: promote existing infra solutions to business based on need/value. Represents service feedback from the business to infra.

4.As a member of Tencent oversea Infra team, organize business demands review and infra requirement review meeting, write high quality business requirements review reports and other infra requirements documents.

5.According to the company's business development, manage and optimize infra projects, pay attention to delivery efficiency and operating costs, and be responsible for business satisfaction and delivery quality.

6.Work with project sponsors to identify project scope, define success criteria, build and manage project budget, and outline resource requirements.

7.Break down plans into digestible parts and lead team throughout the lifecycle of the demand including go-live support.

8.Effectively communicate with relevant, x-functional stakeholders (business and infra in both HQ and Overseas) regularly to manage expectations and to provide product and project updates on scope, budget, velocity and key performance metrics.

9.Engage project steering committee for guidance and key decisions.

10.Provide simple, to the point, quality stat using consistently to different audience including executives

11.Identify and manage risks, issues, action items and decisions.

12.Foster relationships and collaborate with cross functional teams and different organisation levels including but not limited to business stakeholders, design teams & engineering teams 

13.Continuously improve infra product and management processes, enhance Speed to Market, such as but not limited to, process optimization, automation, self-service and supportability. 

14.Define and deliver quantitative and qualitative data driven analytics/metrics to drive and measure product capabilities adoption, customer satisfaction and continuous improvements.



职位要求

1.Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science. MS/MBA preferred.PMP certificated perferred.

2.5+ years of leading and delivering infra project delivery.

3.Strong understanding of infra architecture like public cloud(AWS/GCP/Azure), public planning process and infra project management.

4.Candidate can operate across business and infra team, confident enough to ask questions of both infra and business experts.

5.Outstanding communication and presentation skills, both written and verbal, to all levels of an organization

6.Good logical thinking ability, strong data analysis ability, strong insight and judgment on business infra needs.

7.Familiar cloud native PaaS & SaaS implementation is preferred.

8.Experienced in famous global game company and familiar with game operation and demand

9.Fluent in Chinese and English is preferred in order to communicate with various stakeholders in headquarters



Base：深圳
