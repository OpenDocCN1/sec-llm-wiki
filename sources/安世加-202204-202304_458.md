**01 **

  

**Senior Analyst, Risk Management**  

Swire Coca-Cola is looking for a Senior Analyst under the Information Security and Risk Management team in the Digital Departmen.

  

The job holder will perform the security assessment to ensure all the projects and

initiatives follow the security controls and compliance requirements. He / She provides

the risk assessment report to identify all the risk with corresponding remediations.

  

**Job Description**

*   Leads the security assessment throughout the Design Authority process by collaborating with cross functional teams, business units and 3rd party sevice providers
    
*   Identifies the possible security risks, prioritizes the impact & likelihood, and
    
    determines the best security measure and control
    
*   Coordinates with project manager to confirm the risk type, likelihood, and mitigation plan with project owner. Provide recommendation and/or conduct escalation on unresolved risk items
    
*   Works closely with Data Privacy and Legal to identify the risk on data privacy from different jurisdictions’ law and regulatory requirement especially the CSL,DSL, PIPL in China
    
*   Develops the information Security policies ensuring the compliance to the local law & regulation, Swire and The Coca-Cola Company security requirement
    
*   Identifies non-compliance and areas of potential improvement proactively via reviewing the security process, control and requirement with different functional heads
    
*   Ensures the compliance, execution of controls, annual audit on ISMS and related certifications such as ISO 27001, ISO 27701, MLPS
    
*   Identifies the security risk severity level and confirms the mitigation option for management approval
    
      
    

**The Successful Applicant**

*   At least 3 years of experience
    
*   Bachelor degree holder in Information Security or related qualification
    
*   Possess the following qualifications: CISA, CISM, CRISC,
    
*   Familiar with China laws and regulations such as CSL, DSL, PIPL
    
*   Good understand on security framework such as NIST, CIS, ISO
    
*   Broad knowledge on security technologies in different aspects such as
    
    Authorization, Authentication,Encryption, Detection & Response, Data Protection, Cloud Security, etc
    
*   Solid experience on identifying business risks and provide recommendation on
    
    mitigation option
    
*   Excellent interpersonal skills to interact with management, stakeholders at
    
    multiple levels and facilitate team’s interactions
    
*   Good analytical and writing skill to provide a thoughtful assessment report to indicate all the security and business risks with recommendations.
    
*   Excellent written and verbal communication in both Mandarin and English
    
*   Critical thinker with strong problem-solving skills
    
*   Self-initiated, a good team player team player with strong communication and
    
    influencing skills
    
*   Business travel required
