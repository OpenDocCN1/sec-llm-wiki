Sensor Networks Call for Papers: Special Collection on End-to-End IoT Security and Privacy

> 期刊名称：International Journal of Distributed Sensor Networks
> 
> 全文截止：2018年4月15日
> 
> 影响因子：1.239
> 
> 期刊网址：http://journals.sagepub.com/page/dsn/collections/special-issues/internet-of-things-security
> 
>   
> 
> Due to the wide proliferation of sensors, cloud and mobile computing techniques and smart devices, internet of things (IoT) has gained tremendous momentum in recent years with a variety of applications. IoT provides the capabilities of connecting intelligent sensors/actuators, objects, and smart gadgets anywhere and anytime to the Internet. 
> 
>   
> 
> Although recent years have seen major and remarkable developments in the field of Internet of things, end-to-end IoT security and privacy issues are not comprehensively covered. The end-to-end IoT ecosystems involve various components, including sensors, appliances, cloud servers, mobile devices, etc. As a result, the security and privacy issues in any of these components can pose a severe threat to the entire IoT ecosystem. For example, compromised cameras and other IoT devices formed a large botnet and a huge DDoS was deployed against Dyn DNS servers in October 2016. Vulnerable cloud servers may leak the credentials of IoT users.
> 
>   
> 
> To ensure end-to-end security and privacy guaranteed service delivery in the IoT ecosystem, there is a critical need for research into new designs and implementations for making IoT more reliable and secure from the perspective of both users and vendors.
> 
>   
> 
> This Special Collection seeks original articles with novel perspectives and solutions to address the end-to-end security and privacy issues in all fields of IoT. The solicited areas are broad. We expect this Special Collection to lay a foundation on current and future IoT research and seek papers covering security and privacy issues related to the IoT device end, the cloud and the control app end (which is often on a smart device).
> 
>   
> 
> Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:
> 
> *   Cryptography in IoT
>     
> *   Case studies for end-to-end security and privacy
>     
> *   Security vulnerability and risk assessment in IoT
>     
> *   Theoretical foundation and models for IoT under attack
>     
> *   Resiliency of IoT under attack
>     
> *   Threat monitoring and detection in IoT
>     
> *   Attack attribution in IoT
>     
> *   Integrated and test bed validation for IoT
>     
> *   Cloud computing/big data analytics for IoT security
>     
> *   Security and performance tradeoffs in IoT design
>     
> *   Privacy issues in IoT
>     
> *   Security, privacy and trust for IoT
>     
> *   Secure IoT architectures
>     
> *   Security in machine-to-machine (M2M) for IoT
>     
> *   Joint security and privacy aware protocol design in IoT
>     
> *   Secure network control for IoT
>     
> 
> Please submit your manuscript according to the following timetable for the special collection:
> 
> Paper Submission Deadline： **April 15, 2018**
