Detection of Intrusions and Malware & Vulnerability Assessment(DIMVA)是 **CCF C **类会议，一直以来会议侧重研究入侵检测、恶意分析、漏洞评估等方面的专题会议，其论文资料和研究点往往具有实际应用价值。2020年DIMVA接收了13篇论文(2019年:23, 2018年:18篇)，录用率26%(2019年:26.74%, 2018年:32.14%)，录用论文标题如下：

### Vulnerability discovery and analysis

•Automated CPE labeling of CVE summaries with Machine Learning

> Emil Wåreus and Martin Hell

•ackstabber's Knife Collection: A Review of Open Source Software Supply Chain Attacks

> Marc Ohm, Henrik Plate, Arnold Sykosch, and Michael Meier

•Putting Attacks in Context: A Building Automation Testbed for Impact Assessment from the Victim’s Perspective

> Herson Esquivel-Vargas, Marco Caselli, Geert Jan Laanstra, and Andreas Peter

### Attacks

•Fast and Furious: Outrunning Windows Kernel Notification Routines from User-Mode

> Pierre Ciholas, Jose Miguel Such, Angelos K. Marnerides, Benjamin Green, Jiajie Zhang, and Utz Roedig

•HAEPG: An Automatic Multi-hop Exploitation Generation Framework

> Zixuan Zhao, Yan Wang, and Xiaorui Gong

•Understanding Android VoIP Security: A System-level Vulnerability Assessment

> En He, Daoyuan Wu, and Robert H. Deng

### Web security

•Web Runner 2049: Evaluating Third-Party Anti-bot Services

> Babak Amin Azad, Oleksii Starov, Pierre Laperdrix, and Nick Nikiforakis

•Short Paper - Taming The Shape Shifter: Detecting Anti-fingerprinting Browsers

> Babak Amin Azad, Oleksii Starov, Pierre Laperdrix, and Nick Nikiforakis

•It Never Rains but It Pours: Analyzing and Detecting Fake Removal Information Advertisement Sites

> Takashi Koide, Daiki Chiba, Mitsuaki Akiyama, Katsunari Yoshioka, and Tsutomu Matsumoto

•On the Security of Application Installers & Online Software Repositories

> Marcus Botacin, Giovanni Bertão, Paulo de Geus, André Grégio, Christopher Kruegel, and Giovanni Vigna

### Detection and containment

•Distributed Heterogeneous N-Variant Execution

> Alexios Voulimeneas, Dokyung Song, Fabian Parzefall, Yeoul Na, Per Larsen, Michael Franz, and Stijn Volckaer

•Sec2graph: Network Attack Detection based on Novelty Detection on Graph Structured Data

> Laetitia Leichtnam, Eric Totel, Nicolas Prigent, and Ludovic Mé
