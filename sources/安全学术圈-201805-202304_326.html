Dr. Zhang is currently an assistant professor in the School of Computer Science and Engineering at Nanyang Technological University. His field of research lies at the intersection of cyber security, computer systems, and artificial intelligence. He is interested in building high-performance, trustworthy, reliable and intelligent computer systems.

Dr. Tianwei Zhang is looking for several PhD students, post-docs and research assistants for the following projects (Security, AI, Distributed Systems, and Autonomous Systems). Candidates with strong background and enthusiasm in cyber security, deep learning, autonomous systems and distributed systems are welcome to apply. If interested, please email Dr. Zhang with your CV to discuss potential opportunities (tianwei.zhang@ntu.edu.sg) .
