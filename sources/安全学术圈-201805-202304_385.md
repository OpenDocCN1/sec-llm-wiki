Applied Networking Research Workshop(ANRW)关注应用网络研究的新成果以及IETF标准和协议，由ACM，IRTF和ISOC共同赞助。2019年共录用13篇，分为5个方向，其中包含DNS安全，隐私保护，IPV6协议安全等。小编将一些跟自己相关的论文标记出来供大家查阅，所有录用论文标题如下：

**SESSION: QUIC**

*   A performance perspective on web optimized protocol stacks: TCP + TLS + HTTP/2 vs. QUIC
    
    *Konrad Wolsing, Jan Rüth, Klaus Wehrle, Oliver Hohlfeld*
    
*   Performance measurements of QUIC communications
    
    *Fabio Bulgarella, Mauro Cociglio, Giuseppe Fioccola, Guido Marchetto, Riccardo Sisto*
    

**SESSION: DNS and security**

*   **Who is answering my queries: understanding and characterizing interception of the DNS resolution path**
    
    *Baojun Liu, Chaoyi Lu, Haixin Duan, Ying Liu, Zhou Li, Shuang Hao, Min   Yang*
    
*   **Oblivious DNS: practical privacy for DNS queries: published in PoPETS 2019**
    
    *Paul Schmitt, Anne Edmundson, Allison Mankin, Nick Feamster*
    
*   Analyzing the costs (and benefits) of DNS, DoT, and DoH for the modern web
    
    *Austin Hounsel, Kevin Borgolte, Paul Schmitt, Jordan Holland, Nick Feamster*
    

**SESSION: Time, fairness, and neighbors**

*   **Securing IPv6 neighbor discovery and SLAAC in access networks through SDN**
    
    *Daniel Nelle, Thomas Scheffler*
    
*   Towards core-stateless fairness on multiple timescales
    
    *Szilveszter Nádas, Gergő Gombos, Ferenc Fejes, Sándor Laki*
    
*   **What time is it: managing time in the internet**
    
    *Sathiya Kumaran Mani, Paul Barford, Ramakrishnan Durairajan, Joel Sommers*
    

**SESSION: Measurement and optimization**

*   **What can you learn from an IP?**
    
    *Simran Patil, Nikita Borisov*
    
*   Can we containerize internet measurements?
    
    *Chris Misa, Sudarsun Kannan, Ramakrishnan Durairajan*
    
*   **Supporting multi-domain Use cases with ALTO**
    
    *Danny Lachos, Christian Rothenberg, Qiao Xiang, Y. Richard Yang, Börje Ohlman, Sabine Randriamasy, Farni Boten, Luis M. Contreras*
    

**SESSION: Network functions and middleboxes**

*   mmb: flexible high-speed userspace middleboxes
    
    *Korian Edeline, Justin Iurman, Cyril Soldani, Benoit Donnet*
    
*   Checking-in on network functions
    
    *Zeeshan Lakhani, Heather Miller*
