1. Redis未授权访问

1.1 查看Redis监听端口为0.0.0.0

1.2 关闭redis服务

ps -ef | grep redis-server | awk '{print $2}' | xargs kill -9

1.3 修改监听端口为127.0.0.1

将#bind 127.0.0.1修改为bind 127.0.0.1

vim /etc/redis/redis.conf

1.4 启动Redis服务

redis-server /etc/redis/redis.conf

或者将/etc/redis/redis.conf中的 # requirepass foobared 改为 requirepass foobared
