【关于乐队】


玫瑰木子弹乐队于2014年由前JACKY DANNY乐队主唱吉他手李博于北京组建，继承了传统硬摇滚的审美核心，同时加入了更多风格元素的可能性。

 

玫瑰木是一种名贵的乐器常用木材，出于保护目前已被全球禁运。玫瑰木材质的子弹，尖锐而不冰冷，穿透但不致命，华丽也会粉身碎骨。我们崇尚善屈伸、知进退，不与现实硬碰的理想主义，拒绝将脑袋埋进乌托邦里，不靠抱怨维持精神温饱，用智慧与坚韧接受来自现实地狱的挑战。

 


Rosewood Bullet is a rock band started atthe beginning of 2014, by Li Bo, songwriter, former lead vocal & guitaristof hard rock band Jacky Danny.


Rosewood is common but precious materialfor instruments. A bullet made of rosewood is something sharp but not cold-blooded,penetrative but non-lethal，resplendent yet willing tosacrifice.


Early works of the band still have heavyinfluence of old school hard rock, which is the musical foundation of the band,but it was just a starting point for a new exploration without any style limit.
