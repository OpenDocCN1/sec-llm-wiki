岗位概述


Orange Cyberdefense China is looking for a CyberSOC Manager in Shanghai.


The role of the CyberSOC Manager is responsible for management of CyberSOC Security Analysts within the CyberSOC. Reporting to the Country Manager and working closely with the majority of GSD (Global Service Delivery) roles to ensure the delivery of CyberSOC Services.




岗位职责


Key Responsibilities - Strategic


· Implementation of the CyberSOC Operations overall strategies

· Improving quality 

· Participates in the development of CyberSOC Services

· Point of contact for the SDM’s regarding customers in the respective area

· Working with the Coordinator for Technical Delivery Managers in new customer implementations

· Working in the CyberSOC management team and develop current and new strategies together with other managers, Director of CyberSOC and Service Owners

· Analysing and providing monthly KPI reports based on the cadence and  instruction 


Key Responsibilities - Tactical 

· Monitor local service activation projects

· Responsible for customer critical incidents such as issues with the service delivery towards a specific customer you are responsible for

· Being involved in the customer deliveries related to the team

· Making sure that monthly reports are created, and quality reviewed

· Making sure that service contracts are updated with accurate data


Key Responsibilities - People Management


· Responsible for the performance and well-being of the team


Supervisory requirements of the role


· People management of direct reports in the local CyberSOC team

· Following up yearly goals and targets for individual team members

· Work planning including working schedules and vacation

· Team leaders working with distributed teams must align together before agreeing on vacation and absence

· Educational planning for the team


Key Responsibilities – Customer Facing


· CyberSOC manager will occasionally meet customer to help clarify the service portfolio


任职要求：


· University degree in Computer Science with focus on Security or Networking Security

· Proven success within IT Security Industry with at least 5 years of experience

· People oriented and having people management experience over 2 years

· Knowledge including Information Security, Threat Hunting, Incident Analysis and Response, Malware analysis, End Point Protection, Application Security, Vulnerability Management and Penetration Testing

· Proven experience with Splunk or other SIEM solution tool

· Linux/Unix, Mac OS X and Windows

· ISO 27001, China Cyber Security Law and MLPS 2.0

· One or more of the following certifications is a plus:

· CompTIA Security + or CEH

· CISSP, OSCP

· Power Point Skill

· Fluent in Chinese and English
