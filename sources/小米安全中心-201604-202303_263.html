在2019到来之际，小米安全中心代表小米数亿用户，衷心感谢大家帮助小米产品更安全，向以下132位安全专家/团队致敬！

Letter of Thanks to Worldwide White-hats  in 2018

 

Congratulations to the following experts who have been qualified for Xiaomi Security Award. On behalf of our hundreds of millions of users, we would like to express sincere gratitude to you all for helping make Xiaomi products safer.

 

 

Over the past year,  those security vulnerabilities reported by you are of significant help and importance for Xiaomi Security. 

 

Thus, we would like to acknowledge all of you, security researchers and experts for reporting issues, and we are looking forward to involving more White-hat individuals and teams in the future.
