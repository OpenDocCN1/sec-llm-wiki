**战队介绍**

  

![](../Images/1200dc7fcdc2df02e19c54b7c034ee78.png)

  

r3kapig is a united CTF Team mostly emerges from Eur3kA and FlappyPig since 2018. And just so you know, r3kapig is a delicious dish that can be grilled and fried, and the mission of the team is to provide the most delicious food for the host. 

  

r3kapig战队部分战绩总结：

  

r3kapig战队由FlappyPig战队和Eur3kA战队联合组成，斩获各类CTF赛事奖项数十项，包括TCTF冠军、XCTF冠军、BCTF冠军、WCTF季军等各项冠亚季军十余项，连续四年闯入Defcon总决赛，深耕漏洞挖掘实战、学术论文发表等领域。
