**岗位职责**

**1.Act as a Cybersecurity point of contact for the site(s)**

· Apply Cybersecurity requirements following Regional Cybersecurity Officer’s request

**2.Control the application of the Valeo ISSP (Information Systems Security Policy) and the specific Cybersecurity rules/exemptions of the site(s)**

· Realize the Site Information Compliance Assessment and update it when requested and/or following a major change in the site(s). Alert Regional Cybersecurity Officer in case of major deviation.

· Control that the Group Cybersecurity standards, rules and best practices are respected

· Act as internal Cybersecurity risk auditor for the other sites of the ‘Region’ (upon request of the Regional Cybersecurity Officer)

**3.Manage locally the Cybersecurity events and incidents**

· Suggest capitalization in perimeter following Cybersecurity events and incidents

\- Provide the reporting of the site(s) to the Regional Cybersecurity Officer

· Participate to the Regional Cybersecurity Officer’s Cybersecurity meetings

· Propose improvements of Group standards to Regional Cybersecurity Officer

**4.Other**

· Upon request, act as Regional Cybersecurity Officer delegate to perform some specific missions

  
**任职要求**

**1.Required: **Bachelor’s degree or Master’s degree in Computer Science and/or Cybersecurity or related Majors

**2.Optional: **Certification(s) in some Cybersecurity standards/technical domains

· >3 years of relevant experience in Cybersecurity

· CIA/CISA/CISSP certifications, preferred

· Knowledge and experience linked to Cybersecurity standards (ISO 2700x, NIST, NIS…)

· Knowledge and experience in technical topics such as malware, patch management, firewalling,…

· Other infrastructure / network / system / database / application experience

· Deep understanding of current IT technologies (IOT, Cloud, etc.)

· Know SoC, SIEM and Splunk Platform, Preferred

· Experience in the management of data privacy within a global company is preferred
