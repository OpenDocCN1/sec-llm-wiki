Practical Radio-Frequency Sensing in the Age of Artificial Intelligence

Abstract

Promoted by the increasing compactness of Radio Frequency (RF) sensors (i.e., radars), contact-free RF sensing has gained increasing popularity due to its low-cost, non-intrusiveness, and privacy preservation. However, realizing the promises of RF sensing can be highly non-trivial, given typical challenges such as multipath and interference.  Although leveraging the latest deep learning techniques to build direct mappings from RF domain to target domains sounds promising, earlier solutions exploiting only simple feature extraction and classification modules have largely ignored the distinctive nature of RF sensing. In this talk, we first explain the basic idea of RF-based contact-free sensing, then we present a unified framework to prepare RF sensing data for deep analytics. Building upon this framework, we further explain the peculiarity of RF sensing data (against, e.g., conventional image data) and present our approaches in designing deep interpreted RF sensing, using some of our latest developments and products as examples.

  

BIO

    Dr. LUO Jun is a tenured professor in NTU. He has been working on wireless sensing, deep learning, and computing system integration for more than two decades and has established a solid foundation on all these topics. Building upon this foundation, his research team has made contributions on mobile/ pervasive computing and smart sensing technologies, by leading several national research projects and corporate labs (including MoE Tier2, BMW, SAP, and CSIJRI, with more than S$10 million funding), aiming to transfer the research outcomes to practical applications. 

    In the meantime, Dr. Luo and his team have kept publishing at relevant venues such as IEEE and ACM top conferences and journals, including MobiCom, CVPR, SenSys, INFOCOM, UbiComp, ToN, and TMC. With only 130+ publications, he has earned over 8000 Google Scholar citations, with the top-cited paper bearing more than 1,200 of them. In the past few years, Dr. Luo has put most his efforts on contact-free sensing (and the corresponding data analytics as well) that leverages wireless media such as visible light, acoustic waves, and radio frequency signals, in order to solve practical problems in our daily life and work. More detailed information can be found at: https://personal.ntu.edu.sg/junluo/.
