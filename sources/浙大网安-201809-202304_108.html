2021年4月，ACM SIGSAC 将进行新一届委员会选举，请支持王晓峰教授竞选主席（Chair），姚丹凤教授竞选副主席（Vice-chair）！投票时间2021年4月，具体的选举流程和日程安排参见ACM网站 https://www.acm.org/elections/sigs/pol_proc（点击文章最下方的阅读原文，可跳转到本链接）。


ACM（Association for Computing Machinery）是世界上最大的兼备教育和科研的计算机学会，它为促进计算机科学和专业的发展做出了巨大的贡献。ACM 学会中设立了许多特别兴趣组——Special Interest Group（SIG），每个领域都有其专属的SIG。其中和计算机安全相关性最大的要数“ACM Special Interest Group on Security, Audit and Control (SIGSAC)”，它的使命是“通过举办高质量的研究会议和研讨会来发展信息安全行业”。

竞选宣言

It is an honor and pleasure to run for SIGSAC Chair. Inthe past 3 years, I have the honor to serve as SIGSAC Vice Chair, CCS PC Co-Chair and AsiaCCS SC member, overseeing the transitions of SIGSAC conferences to amulticycle model and their move to the online eventsduring the pandemic, and also supporting diversity inthe community.

If elected, I will work closely with the executivecommittee to ensure the continued success of SIGSAC during the pandemic and post-pandemic hard times. Particularly, I will help SIGSAC conferences and TOPS editorial board to continuously adjust their reviewmodels and organization, to improve publication quality while maintaining their trends of growth under theimpacts of the pandemic. I will continue to push foropen-access and make the community more inclusive, through supporting women-in-security and other outreaches to underrepresented groups, free or low-cost student membership and SIGSAC chaptersaround the world. I will strengthen the connections withthe industry, and foster new sub-communities. Also in consulting with the community, I will initiate newservices (e.g., job postings) and mechanisms tocelebrate excellence (e.g., impact paper award like thatprovided by SIGSOFT).


很荣幸能够参加 SIGSAC 主席的竞选。在过去的三年中，我有幸担任 SIGSAC 副主席，CCS PC 联合主席和 AsiaCCS SC 成员，期间参与监督 SIGSAC 会议向多周期模式的过渡、并在疫情期间转移到线上活动的过程，我还为社区的多样性提供了帮助。

如果我当选，我将与执行委员会紧密合作，确保 SIGSAC 的工作在疫情期间和疫情结束后顺利进行。我将帮助 SIGSAC 会议和 TOPS 编辑委员会不断调整其审核模式和组织架构，使得出版刊物在疫情的影响下仍旧能够保持质量和数量增长趋势。我将继续通过支持 women-in-security 和其他非代表性的组织、提供免费或低成本的学生会员资格、建立 SIGSAC 全球分会，从而推动开放访问并使社区更具包容性。我将加强与工业界的联系，并建立新的子社区。此外，我也将开放新的服务（例如职位发布）和机制来激励优秀的研究成果（例如 SIGSOFT 提供的影响力论文奖）。

竞选宣言

I have a deep passion for serving our international SIGSAC community. I hope to have the distinct honor of contributing to this outstanding group.

Inclusive excellence – encouraging, engaging, andshowcasing researchers that are underrepresented interms of gender, race, age, experience, geographic location, as well as research interests – has been my focus in the past four years of serving as the SIGSAC secretary/treasurer. Besides founding multiple diversity workshop initiatives (iMentor and CyberW) and requiringCode of Conduct in SIGSAC sponsored conferences, I also helped recognize work that addresses practical cybersecurity, privacy, and cryptography challenges(e.g., ACM DTRAP special issues for research withartifacts). Embracing a broader definition of novelty would enable SIGSAC to effectively contribute to society.

If elected, I also plan to establish a mechanism for SIGSAC publications to appear in the Research Highlights of Communications of the ACM. It will propel SIGSAC members to be visible in the broader ACM computing community -- and more importantly -- to widely disseminate our expertise and advance this field.

Fostering supportive and caring community atmospheres will continue to be my goal. To that end, I also plan to organize experience-sharing seminars for tech professionals on research journeys and mental health.


我有极大的热情为国际SIGSAC社区工作。我希望能够为这个杰出的团队献出自己的一份力。

在担任SIGSAC秘书和财务主任的过去四年中，我致力于实现“包容的卓越（inclusive excellence）”，即鼓励、吸引那些即使在性别、种族、年龄、经验、地理位置或研究领域等方面代表性不足，但具备卓越成果的研究人员。除了提出多个多样性研讨会计划（包括 iMentor 和 CyberW）外，我还帮助表彰了一些解决现实中网络安全、隐私和密码学挑战的工作（如 ACM DTRAP 研究工件的特殊问题）。我认为采用更广泛、新颖的定义将使 SIGSAC 能够有效地为社会做出贡献。

如果成功当选，我将设法让 SIGSAC 的出版物出现在ACM通讯这一杂志的研究亮点专栏中。这会帮助 SIGSAC 成员在 ACM 计算社区这一更为广阔的平台上崭露头角，更为重要的是，广泛传播我们的专业知识并推动领域的发展。

我的目标仍然会是营造充满关怀的社区氛围。为此，我还计划为技术专业人员组织有关科研工作和心理健康的经验分享研讨会。
