Security, Privacy, and Transparency Issues with Online Advertising

Abstract

Online advertising serves as a significant source of revenue for web-based businesses and is crucial to a thriving Internet ecosystem. In the complex online advertising ecosystem, ad networks (e.g., Google and Baidu) serve like a “Big Brother” to all other parties, including advertisers, publishers, and users. Ad networks learn the demand of advertisers, and determine what publishers (i.e., websites or mobile apps) to display the advertisers’ ads and what ads shown to a specific user based on their powerful user behavior tracking techniques. In this talk, I will present several of my works studying on the security, privacy, and transparency issues with the online advertising ecosystem. 

  

BIO

Dr. Haitao Xu is a tenure-track Assistant Professor at Arizona State University (ASU). His research interests include Web Security, Network Security, Cybercrime Investigation, and User Privacy. Prior to joining ASU in July 2018, Dr. Xu was a Research Assistant Professor at Northwestern University. He obtained his Ph.D. degree in Computer Science from the College of William and Mary in December 2015.
