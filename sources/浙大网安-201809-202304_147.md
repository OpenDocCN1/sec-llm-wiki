Attacker Identification on In-Vehicle Networks

****Abstract****

Autonomous vehicles are expected to significantly enhance the safety and comfort of driving. However, attacks on vehicles have been discovered and have demonstrated serious security threats to automatic driving. As the foundation of precise defence against these security threats, attacker identification has been proposed. In this presentation, I will introduce the background of the attacker identification research issue, and its latest achievements as well as the problems of existing. Moreover, I will share our experimental platform built from the real vehicle driving data.

**BIO**

Yuanfang Chen received her Ph.D. and M.S. degrees from Dalian University of Technology, China, and a second Ph.D. degree from the University Pierre and Marie CURIE, France. She currently works at Hangzhou Dianzi University as a Professor. She was an assistant researcher at the Illinois Institute of Technology, USA with Prof. Xiangyang Li. She has been invited as the Session Chair of several conferences, an associate editor of Industrial Networks and Intelligent Systems, and the guest editor of MONET.
