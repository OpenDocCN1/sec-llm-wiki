Secure Skyline Queries

Abstract

In this talk, I will present a fully secure skyline protocol on encrypted data using two non-colluding cloud servers under the semi-honest model. It ensures semantic security in which the cloud servers know nothing about the data, including indirect data patterns, query, as well as the query result. Besides, the client and data owner do not need to participate in the computation. I also present a secure dominance protocol that can be used by skyline queries as well as other queries.

In addition, I will briefly present the first end-to-end private data marketplace with model-based pricing (Dealer) towards answering the questions: How can the broker fairly assign value to data owners based on their contribution to the models to incentivize more data contribution, and determine prices for a series of models for various model buyers to maximize the revenue with the arbitrage-free guarantee.

  

BIO

Jinfei Liu is a joint postdoctoral research fellow at Georgia Institute of Technology and Emory University. His research interests include data queries, data privacy and security, and private data market. He has published over 20 papers in premier journals and conferences, including VLDB, TKDE, ICDE, CIKM, and IPL.
