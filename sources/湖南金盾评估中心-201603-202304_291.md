就按文章中的步骤来开启Stack Pointer，Options----General----勾选Stack Pointer

![](../Images/0070c8efe82784526b484c28113703c2.png)

可以看到报错的4012AC的stack pointer值为0x070

![](../Images/6020fd751ef0343963720a125a971b7a.png)

![](../Images/be31adabf70e2341c52ab945b96b454f.png)

![](../Images/aefc8aa37fcd6bd8263af823bf7ff196.png)

402138的值即从v3开始的42位，恰好是v3+v4+v5+v6+v7，为
