第一步

/src/AppBundle/Controller/Admin/AppPackageUpdateController.php

跟进downloadPackageForUpdate

/src/Biz/CloudPlatform/Service/Impl/AppServiceImpl.php

先用getCenterPackageInfo获取升级包的信息，再用downloadPackage去下载升级包

跟进createAppClient

此时app_api_url被取出了，新建了一个EduSohoAppClient对象并调用其的getPackage方法，跟进。

/src/Biz/CloudPlatform/Client/EduSohoAppClient.php

跟进callRemoteApi

/src/Biz/CloudPlatform/Client/EduSohoAppClient.php

跟进sendRequest
