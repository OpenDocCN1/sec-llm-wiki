**标题：****FormBook Malware Targets US Defense Contractors, Aerospace and Manufacturing Sectors**

时间：2017-10-09

摘要：Attackers spreading new malware called FormBook are singling out aerospace firms, defense contractors and some manufacturing organizations in the United States and South Korea.

  

**标题：Equifax Hackers Stole Info on 693,665 UK Residents**

时间：2017-10-10

摘要：Equifax Inc. said today an investigation into information stolen in the epic data breach the company disclosed on Sept. 7 revealed that intruders took a file containing 15.2 million UK records. The company says it is now working to inform 693,665 U.K. consumers whose data was stolen in the attack.

  

**标题：Israel hackers caught Russian cyber spies abusing Kaspersky AV to steal NSA secrets**

时间：2017-10-11

摘要：Israeli hackers compromised the Kaspersky infrastructure and caught Russian spies using AV tool to harvest NSA exploits. Kaspersky was not aware of the hack.

  

**标题：iOS Flaw Makes Apple ID Passwords Prone to Phishing Attacks**

时间：2017-10-11

摘要：Apple’s policy to repeatedly ask users for their iTunes password needlessly exposes iOS device owners to possible phishing attacks, according a mobile app developer Felix Krause.

**焦点漏洞**

**◆焦点漏洞**

Microsoft Office 内存破坏漏洞

**◆NSFOCUS ID**  

37787

**◆CVE ID **

CVE-2017-11826

**◆受影响版本**

Microsoft Word 2016

Microsoft Word 2013

Microsoft Word 2010

Microsoft Word 2007

**◆漏洞点评**  

Microsoft Office是微软公司开发的一套基于Windows操作系统的办公软件套装。Microsoft Office未正确处理内存对象，在实现上存在远程代码执行漏洞，可使攻击者在当前用户上下文中运行任意代码。该漏洞几乎影响微软目前所支持的所有office版本，现已发现有黑客利用此漏洞进行攻击。请用户及时升级系统补丁，谨慎打开来路不明的可疑office文档，同时开启系统安全软件进行防护。
