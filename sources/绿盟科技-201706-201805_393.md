**威胁信息回顾**

**标题：**Supervisord远程命令执行漏洞分析（CVE-2017-11610）  

**时间：**2017-08-10  

**摘要：**近期，Supervisord曝出了一个需认证的远程命令执行漏洞（CVE-2017-11610），通过POST请求Supervisord管理界面恶意数据，可以获取服务器操作权限，存在严重的安全风险  

  

**标题：**SENRIO公布gSOAP整型溢出漏洞  

**时间：**2017-08-08  

**摘要：**2017.7.18日，SENRIO在官方博客公布了gSOAP整型溢出漏洞(CVE-2017-9765)细节，并将该漏洞命名为Devil’s Ivy。根据genivia官方说明，当向服务器发送超过2Gb的XML数据时，将导致整型下溢，最终导致缓冲区溢出，可执行任意代码。该漏洞最初在Axis M3004摄像头中发现，但其影响范围远不止Axis摄像头，任何使用了gSOAP的应用均受影响。  

  

**标题：**Beware of Security by Press Release  

**时间：**2017-08-10  

**摘要：**On Wednesday, the security industry once again witnessed an all-too-familiar cycle: I call it “security by press release.” It goes a bit like this: A security firm releases a report claiming to have unearthed a major flaw in a competitor’s product; members of the trade press uncritically republish the claims without adding much clarity or waiting for responses from the affected vendor; blindsided vendor responds in a blog post showing how the issue is considerably less dire than originally claimed.  

  

**标题：**How Top Companies Accidentally Leaking Terabytes of Sensitive Data Online  

**时间：**2017-08-08  

**摘要：**An anti-malware detection service provider and premium security firm has been accused of leaking terabytes of confidential data from several Fortune 1000 companies, including customer credentials, financial records, network intelligence and other sensitive data.

**◆漏洞点评**  

Windows Search服务可为文件、电子邮件和其他内容提供内容索引、属性缓存和搜索结果。在Windows某些版本中Search服务存在安全漏洞，若未正确处理内存对象，可导致远程代码执行。攻击者会向 Windows Search 服务发送特殊设计的消息，有权访问目标计算机的攻击者可以利用此漏洞提升特权并控制目标计算机。目前，微软已经发布安全补丁，请用户及时升级系统，更新安全补丁。
