# 【博士招生】德国CISPA信息安全中心诚招多个博士和博士后，3月11日访问清华大学期间可面谈

CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security (https://cispa.saarland/) is looking for multiple fully-funded Ph.D. students and postdocs working on cutting-edge topics on information security and privacy. CISPA is the newest member of the Helmholtz Association - the largest scientific organization in Germany fully committed to scientific excellence. CISPA as Helmholtz's first investment in computer science is a world-leading research center for information security. For example, during the past 3 years, CISPA researchers have published 45 papers in the leading security conferences IEEE S&P, ACM CCS, Usenix Security, and NDSS. CISPA is constantly ranked top-3 in the field worldwide, see, e.g., CSrankings.org: (http://csrankings.org/#/index?sec&world).

 

Prof. Dr. Michael Backes, director of CISPA, will lead the CISPA delegation that consists of Dr. Mario Fritz, Dr. Sven Bugiel, and Dr. Yang Zhang to visit **Tsinghua University **on **March 11th, 2019**. An onsite interview with potential Ph.D. students and postdocs will be arranged in the afternoon.

 

Successful applicants can work on any topics of information security and privacy, in particular:

● Machine Learning Security and Privacy

● Biomedical Privacy

● Computer Vision and Deep Learning

● Android Security

● Autonomous Security

 

Requirements for Ph.D. students:

● A bachelor/master degree in Computer Science, Information Security, Mathematics with excellent grades

● Excellent English

● Excellent programming skills

Requirements for postdocs: Excellent track record in the field of information security and privacy What we offer:

● Excellent research environment and world-class collaboration opportunity

● Strong individual supervision

● Full-time working contract (12 months a year)

 

# **To join the interview, please send your CV to Dr. Yang Zhang (yang.zhang@cispa.saarland). ****Closing Date for Application: March 6th, 2019**

**We also have open positions for interns, if you are interested, please also send your CV to Dr. Yang Zhang (yang.zhang@cispa.saarland).**
