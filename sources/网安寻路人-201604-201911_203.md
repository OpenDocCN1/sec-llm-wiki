作为Twitter的重度用户，近日Twitter为GDPR合规做出的隐私政策更改吸引了我的兴趣。

![](../Images/d21d90b88269c69f9db934c7b75fc12b.png)

  

在其新版隐私政策中，对定向推送内容和广告做出了重大变更。

 

*   We give you control through your settings to limit the data we collect from you and how we use it, and to control things like account security, **marketing preferences**, apps that canaccess your account, and address book contacts you’ve uploaded to Twitter. You can also always download the information you have shared on Twitter.**【针对广告推送，可以变更】**
    
      
    
*   In addition toinformation you share with us, we use your Tweets, content you’ve read, Liked,or Retweeted, and other information to determine what topics you’re interested in, your age, the languages you speak, and other signals to show you more relevant content.** We give you transparency into that information, and you can modify orcorrect it at any time.****【针对个性化内容推送，提供透明度，也可以变更】**
    

  

以我自己的账户为例，很有意思额一个新特性是，Twitter展示出其推测出的关于我的兴趣。也就是以下74项。  

![](../Images/cc6511254884b5259c441fb1b57c5191.png)

  

我把这74项兴趣，截图下来给大家看，然后Twitter还允许我自主修改这74项特性。

  

![](../Images/45093741c26a2aea75c9306b1d7ac49d.png)

虽然还是没有告诉大家定向推送算法的基本内容，但是把算法形成的画像标签告诉了用户，并支持用户自己修改。是不是也不错呢？
