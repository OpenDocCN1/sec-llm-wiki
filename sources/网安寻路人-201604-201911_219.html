这又是一篇读书笔记。本文标题A Grand Bargain to Make Tech Companies Trustworthy，于2016年10月3日发表于《大西洋月刊》网站上。作者是Balkin教授（耶鲁大学法学院教授）和Zittrain教授（哈佛大学法学院教授）。对这两位教授，研读网络法的同学想必如雷贯耳。


  • Jack M. Balkin is the Knight Professor of Constitutional Law and the First Amendment at Yale Law School, and the founder and director of Yale's Information Society Project. Balkin教授是耶鲁大学法学院教授。

  • Jonathan Zittrain is a professor at Harvard Law School and the Kennedy School of Government. He is also a professor of computer science at the Harvard School of Engineering and Applied Sciences, and the co-founder of the Berkman Klein Center for Internet & Society.Zittrain是哈佛大学法学院教授。


本文的核心观点：医生和律师禁止将客户的信息用于为自己谋利，为什么谷歌和Facebook不应该也是这样？（Doctors and lawyers are prohibited from using clients’ information for their own interests, so why aren’t Google and Facebook?）
