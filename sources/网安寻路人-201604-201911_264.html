凭什么数据就该自由流动？

无意之间看到这篇文章，觉得很有意思，贴出来和大家分享。文章的核心思想就如题目所言，数据自由流动能带来什么好处？确切地说，在提倡、坚持“数据自由流动”前，更应该问一个问题——数据自由流动，能为谁带来什么样的好处？


文章主要的观点如下：


The principle that data should be borderless and that it should flow freely is a policy decision that has profound effects.数据应该自由流动，不是先验的真理，而是一个政策选择。这个政策选择对现实有着巨大深远的影响。


Some base that principle on the idea that data is a commodity that should be freely traded. But the idea that data should flow freely does not actually flow logically from the idea that data is a commodity: commodities are taxed and the producers of raw material are compensated for providing that material to the industries that transform it and add value to it. 很多坚持数据应该自由流动的人认为，数据是商品，因此应该自由流动。但是现实中，商品也非天然地自由流动。


Further, the idea that data is a commodity to be freely traded contradicts fundamental human rights. 数据是自由流动的商品，这个观点和基本人权相违背。


And the benefits of free flow of data have been overstated: indeed free flow of data likely increases income inequality. 数据自由流动能够带来的好处被过分夸大了：实际上从全球来看，数据的自由流动增加的收入的不平等程度。


There is no obvious justification for policies favouring the free flow of data other than to allow OTTs to continue to accumulate huge profits (often monopoly profits) by extracting and refining data, without paying taxes and without compensating the users who produce the data in the first place. 数据自由流动带来的好处主要集中于大企业手中。


As a consequence, there should be a moratorium on negotiations regarding the free flow of data. 因此，是否应该让数据自由流动，需要大讨论。
