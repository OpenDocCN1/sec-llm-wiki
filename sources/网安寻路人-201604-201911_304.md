# 欧盟委员会E-PRIVACY Regulation草案简述（一）

在2017年1月，欧盟委员会提出了Proposal for a REGULATION OF THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND OF THE COUNCIL concerning the respect for private life and the protection of personal data in electronic communications and repealing Directive 2002/58/EC (Regulation on Privacy and Electronic Communications)。通过此项立法，欧盟意在对2002年生效的“E-Privacy Directive”的改革和升级为“E-Privacy Regulation”，使其符合欧盟《通用数据保护条例》（GDPR）的要求，并于2018年与GDPR同期生效。

迄今为止，大多数人对“E-Privacy Directive”认识，主要是其对cookies的规范上，因此“E-Privacy Directive”往往被当成cookies law。【但实际上，“E-Privacy Directive”的内容绝不仅仅是cookies而已。】  

  

2002年生效的“E-Privacy Directive”只要求网站告知用户，并提供opt-out的机会，就可以在用户的终端设备上存储cookies: “Member States shall ensure that the use of electronic communications networks to store information or to gain access to information stored in the terminal equipment of a subscriber or user **is only allowed on condition that the subscriber or user concerned is provided with clear and comprehensive information in accordance with Directive 95/46/EC, inter alia about the purposes of the processing, and is offered the right to refuse such processing by the data controller.”**

  

但在2009年，欧盟对“E-Privacy Directive”进行了修订，在用户的终端设备上存储cookies必须从opt-out改为opt-in：“Member States shall ensure that the storing of information, or the gaining of access to information already stored, in the terminal equipment of a subscriber or user **is only allowed on condition that the subscriber or user concerned has given his or her consent, having been provided with clear and comprehensive information, in accordance with Directive 95/46/EC, inter alia, about the purposes of the processing.**  ”   
  

此次，欧盟委员会新提出的E-Privacy Regulation的文本，主要有哪些特点呢？本文尝试概述如下：

**1\. 急剧扩张的“电子通信”的定义**

  

欧盟委员会2016年10月提出的，DIRECTIVE OF THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND OF THE COUNCIL establishing the European Electronic Communications Code 。在这个指令中，‘electronic communications service’ 被定义为 a service normally provided for remuneration via electronic communications networks, which consists encompasses 'internet access service' as defined in Article 2(2) of Regulation (EU) 2015/2120; and/or 'interpersonal communications service'; and/or services consisting wholly or mainly in the conveyance of signals on electronic communications networks, including telecommunications services and such as transmission services in networks used for the provision of machine-tomachine services and for broadcasting, but exclude excludes services providing, or exercising editorial control over, content transmitted using electronic communications networks and services。

  

在这个定义中，“电子通信服务”包含“互联网访问服务”、人际通信服务，以及通过电子通信网络传输信号的服务。**总的来说，这个定义的覆盖面非常广，而这个定义也将成为欧盟在电子通信领域立法的基本出发点，也是E-Privacy Regulation中关于电子通信的定义。**

首先，REGULATION (EU) 2015/2120 OF THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND OF THE COUNCIL中，“互联网访问服务” ‘internet access service’ 是 a publicly available electronic communications service that provides access to the internet, and thereby connectivity to virtually all end points of the internet, irrespective of the network technology and terminal equipment used.   

其次，人际通信服务，还包括the definition of ‘interpersonal communications service’ shall include services which enable interpersonal and interactive communication merely as a minor ancillary feature that is intrinsically linked to another service。这就包括了IP通话、短信服务及基于网络的电子邮件服务等等所谓的OTT服务。而且根据这个定义，如果网络游戏只要提供了玩家沟通服务，也算是提供了人际通信服务。

再次，“通过电子通信网络传输信号的服务”，主要是指传统意义上的电信服务、**机器间的信号传输服务（机器与机器间的通信）**、广播服务。但是“电子通信服务”不包含内容制作、编辑等活动。此次欧盟委员会专门针对IoT时代，提出要提供full protection of the rights to privacy and confidentiality of machine-to-machine communications, and to promote a trusted and secure Internet of Things in the digital single market。   

  

另外值得注意的是，按照上述定义，浏览互联网，访问具体的网站或网页，这样的行为在欧盟的规则中被认为是一种electronic communication，即个人与网站、网页之间存在一种信号传递，因此是一种电子通信。

  

如此宽广的定义，按照欧盟委员会的说法：important technological and economic developments took place in the market since the last revision of the ePrivacy Directive in 2009. Consumers and businesses increasingly rely on new internet-based services enabling inter-personal communications such as Voice over IP, instant messaging and web-based e-mail services, instead of traditional communications services. These Over-the-Top communications services ("OTTs") are in general not subject to the current Union electronic communications framework, including the ePrivacy Directive.   

  

换句话说，此次欧盟委员会要做到**全覆盖**。当然，如果电子通信网络并不面向公众，例如closed groups of end-users such as corporate networks, access to which is limited to members of the corporation. 在这种情况下，E-privacy Regulation并不适用。  

  

**2\. E-privacy Regulation的管辖范围以及适用对象**

  

从欧盟委员会提出的现有文本来看，E-privacy Regulation管辖范围非常广，甚至要大于GDPR的范围。看看Article 3:

  

Article 3 Territorial scope and representative  
1\. This Regulation applies to:  
(a) the provision of electronic communications services to end-users in the Union, irrespective of whether a payment of the end-user is required;**【注：在限制适用范围方面，GDPR还有语言和支付货币等门槛，E-privacy Regulation索性就没有限制。】**  
(b) the use of such services;  
(c) the protection of information related to the terminal equipment of end-users located in the Union.  
2\. Where the provider of an electronic communications service is not established in the Union it shall designate in writing a representative in the Union.**【注：electronic communications service的提供者必须在欧盟境内指定一个代表】**

3\. The representative shall be established in one of the Member States where the endusers of such electronic communications services are located.   

  

再来看看适用对象：  

  

This Regulation should apply to providers of electronic communications services, to providers of publicly available directories, and to software providers permitting electronic communications, including the retrieval and presentation of information on the internet.   

  

This Regulation should also apply to natural and legal persons who use electronic communications services to send direct marketing commercial communications or collect information related to or stored in end-users’ terminal equipment.

  

对如此广的管辖和适用，欧盟委员会是这么说的：This Regulation should apply to electronic communications data processed in connection with the provision and use of electronic communications services in the Union, regardless of whether or not the processing takes place in the Union. Moreover, in order not to deprive end-users in the Union of effective protection, this Regulation should also apply to electronic communications data processed in connection with the provision of electronic communications services from outside the Union to end-users in the Union。  

  

按照现在这个规定，中国本土的即时通信软件、浏览器等提供商都需要在欧盟派驻一名代表。

  

**3\. E-privacy Regulation的基本原则之一——保障电子通信保密**  
 

  

在E-privacy Regulation中，欧盟委员会提出：

  

Respect for private life首先要求保障自然人电子通信的保密性。而电子通信的保密性包含两个方面——内容和元数据：Confidentiality of electronic communications ensures that **information exchanged** between parties and **the external elements of such communication**, including when the information has been sent, from where, to whom, is not to be revealed to anyone other than to the parties involved in a communication. 

  

其中，保护电子通信的内容的保密性，自不用说：The content of electronic communications may reveal highly sensitive information about the natural persons involved in the communication, from personal experiences and emotions to medical conditions, sexual preferences and political views, the disclosure of which could result in personal and social harm, economic loss or embarrassment. 

  

而关于电子通信的元数据（meta data）也应当保障其保密性：Similarly, metadata derived from electronic communications may also reveal very sensitive and personal information. These metadata includes the numbers called, the websites visited, geographical location, the time, date and duration when an  
individual made a call etc., allowing precise conclusions to be drawn regarding the private lives of the persons involved in the electronic communication, such as their social relationships, their habits and activities of everyday life, their interests, tastes etc.   

  

**保障通信保密的首要原则是，未经同意（consent），不能干扰电子通信，不得截取、存储、使用****电子通信的数据。**值得注意的是，与GDPR只保护自然人不同，E-privacy Regulation同样保护法人（legal person）的通信保密。因此当电子通信的终端用户为法人时，GDPR的部分规定，特别是关于“同意”的限定，同样适用。

  

另外一点，既然自然人浏览网站或网页是电子通信行为中的一种，那在此过程中涉及到的浏览历史记录，就属于电子通信的元数据。其保密性应当得到尊重。

  

**4\. E-privacy Regulation的基本原则之二——保护个人数据**  
 

  

由于电子通信的数据（包括内容和元数据），在很大程度上为个人数据。因此，E-privacy Regulation在电子通信领域，对GDPR是做了具体化和补充。The provisions of this Regulation particularise and complement the general rules on the protection of personal data laid down in Regulation (EU) 2016/679 as regards electronic communications data that qualify as personal data.
