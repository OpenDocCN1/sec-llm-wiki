实验室留学生James Adu Ansere获得2020年度优秀来华留学生奖学金


CSC Excellence Scholarship


实验室留学生James Adu Ansere获得2020年度优秀来华留学生奖学金（CSC Excellence Scholarship），祝贺！

International student James Adu Ansere in the laboratory won the 2020 CSC Excellence Scholarship. Congratulations!



James Adu Ansere，男，加纳籍，2017级信息与通信工程专业博士研究生。James是一个勤奋刻苦的学生，他积极投身于学术研究，具有灵活的独立研究能力。在博士学习期间，James累计在IEEE Internet of Things Journal、IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology等国际顶级期刊上发表了4篇SCI论文和许多合著论文，同时他还担任了International Telecommunication System, IEEE Access, IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology, IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems, IEEE Communications Surveys and Tutorials等期刊的审稿人。James已经完成了他的博士毕业答辩，值此之际，河海大学网络与安全实验室全体成员祝愿James能够在今后的人生道路上一帆风顺，越来越好！

James Adu Ansere, male, Ghanaian, a doctoral student in Information and Communication Engineering of the year of 2017. James is a hardworking student who is actively involved in academic research and has resilient independent research ability. During his doctoral studies, James has published 4 SCI papers and many co-authored papers in reputable journals such as IEEE Internet of Things Journal and IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology. At the same time, he serves as an academic reviewer for many top SCI journals, such as International Telecommunication System, IEEE Access, IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology, IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems, IEEE Communications Surveys and Tutorials. James has completed his Ph.D. graduation defense. On this occasion, all members of the Network and Security Laboratory of Hohai University wish James all the best and better in his future life!
