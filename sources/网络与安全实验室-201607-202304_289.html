  专刊主题

Software-Defined Networking: Establishing Security, Privacy, and Trust in the Network.

2

 投稿截止日期

November 10, 2017

3

  Topics

  • Potential topics include but are not limited to the following:

  • 1、Security modelling and threat in SDN

  • 2、Software-defined security for SDN architecture

  • 3、Trust models between third party applications and the controller in SDN

  • 4、Traffic and resource isolation protocols for SDN

  • 5、Privacy protection in SDN

  • 6、Privacy policy framework for SDN

  • 7、Establishment of trust models and trust boundaries in SDN

  • 8、Interface Security protection on Application-Control Programming Interface A-CPI and I-CPI Identity management protocols for SDN users and components

  • 9、Access control mechanisms for all components and users of SDN

  • 10、Effective protocols for building authentication, authorization and accounting implementations in SDN

  • 11、Security architectures for SDN

  • 12、Security protocols for SDN

  • 13、Privacy-preserving data mining for SDN

  • 14、Information sharing and data protection in SDN

  • 15、Cryptographic protocols against internal attacks in SDN

  • 16、Risk management in SDN environments

  • 17、Mechanisms to enforce privacy and trust in SDN

  • 18、Energy/cost/efficiency of security in SDNs

  • 19、Trust, reputation and policy management in SDNs

  • 20、Privacy-preserving data access in SDNs
