> **实验室韩光洁教授等人在IEEE Communications Magazine(IF=5.125)期刊上申请的专刊获批**

![](../Images/f386040bf60369863eddb94799631e65.png)

网络与安全实验室  

实验室韩光洁教授等人在IEEE Communications Magazine(IF=5.125)期刊上申请的专刊获批，专刊主题：**EMERGING TRENDS, ISSUES AND CHALLENGES IN BIG DATA AND ITS IMPLEMENTATION TOWARDS FUTURE SMART CITIES**, 投稿截至日期: April 1, 2017，祝贺！

![](../Images/6dfe8ed4226e59debf36d48f5264ab9f.png)

Topic Include

**Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:**

1、Distributed and parallel algorithms for big data in smart cities

2、Big data analytics in data processing center for smart cities

3、Cloud/fog computing in data processing center for smart cities

4、The application of mobile cloud/fog computing for smart cities

5、Fault tolerance, reliability and survivability in smart cities

6、E-health and connected healthcare systems in smart cities

7、Cyber-physical and social computing and networks in smart cities

8、Environmental and urban monitoring in smart cities

9、QoS and QoE of systems, applications, and services for smart cities

10、Safety, security, privacy and trust in applications and services for smart cities

11、Other topics related to big data and its implementations for smart cities
