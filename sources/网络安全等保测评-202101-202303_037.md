## We often hear how important security is,but we don’t always understand why. Security is important because it helps to ensure that an organization is able to continue to exist and operate in spite of any attempts to steal its data or compromise its physical or logicalelements. Security should be viewed as an element ofbusiness management rather than an IT concern. In fact, IT and security are different. *Information technology (IT)* or even *information systems (IS)* is the hardware and software that support the operations or functions of a business. **Security is the business management tool that ensures the reliable and protected operation of IT/IS****. Security exists to support the objectives, mission, and goals of the organization. **

| 我们经常听到安全是多么重要，但我们并不总是了解为什么。安全是重要的，因为它有助于确保一个组织能够继续存在和运作，尽管有任何企图窃取其数据或损害其物理或逻辑元素的行为。安全应该被看作是企业管理的一个要素，而不是一个IT问题。事实上，IT和安全是不同的。信息技术（IT）或甚至信息系统（IS）是支持企业运作或功能的硬件和软件。安全是确保IT/IS可靠和受保护运行的业务管理工具。安全的存在是为了支持组织的目标、任务和目的。 |

Generally, a security framework should be adopted that provides a starting point for how to implement security. Once an initiation of security has been accomplished, then finetuning that security is accomplished through evaluation. There are three common types of security evaluation: **risk assessment, vulnerability assessment, and penetration testing **(these are covered in detail in Chapter 2 and Chapter 15,“Security Assessment and Testing”) .

*   **Risk assessment **is a process of identifying assets, threats, and vulnerabilities, and then using that information to calculate risk. Once risk is understood, it is used to guide the improvement of the existing security infrastructure.
    
*   **Vulnerability assessment** uses automated tools to locate known security weaknesses, which can be addressed by adding in more defenses or adjusting the existing protections.
    
*   **Penetration testing **uses trusted individuals to stress test the security infrastructure to find issues that may not be discovered by the prior two means, with the goal of finding those concerns before an adversary takes advantage of them.
    

|  一般来说，应该采用一个安全框架，为如何实施安全提供一个起点。一旦完成了安全的启动，就可以通过评估来完成安全的微调。有三种常见的安全评估类型：风险评估、漏洞评估和渗透测试（这些将在第2章和第15章 "安全评估和测试 "中详细介绍）。风险评估是一个识别资产、威胁和漏洞的过程，然后使用这些信息来计算风险。一旦了解了风险，它就被用来指导改进现有的安全基础设施。漏洞评估使用自动工具来定位已知的安全弱点，这些弱点可以通过增加更多的防御措施或调整现有的保护措施来解决。渗透测试使用受信任的人对安全基础设施进行压力测试，以发现前两种方法可能无法发现的问题，其目的是在对手利用这些问题之前发现这些问题。 |

Security should be **cost-effective**. Organizations do not have infinite budgets and thus must allocate their funds appropriately. Additionally, an organizational budget includes a percentage of monies dedicated to security, just as most other business tasks and processes require capital, not to mention payments to employees, insurance, retirement, and so on. You should select security controls that provide the greatest protection for the lowest resource cost. 

| 安全应该是具有成本效益的。组织没有无限的预算，因此必须适当地分配他们的资金。此外，一个组织的预算包括一定比例的资金用于安全，就像大多数其他业务任务和流程需要资金一样，更不用说支付给员工、保险、退休等等。你应该选择那些以最低资源成本提供最大保护的安全控制措施。 |
| --- |

  
Security should be **legally defensible.** The laws of your jurisdiction are the backstop of organizational security. When someone intrudes into your environment and breaches security, especially when such activities are illegal, then prosecution in court may be the only available response for compensation or closure. Also, many decisions made by an organization will have legal liability issues. If required to defend a security action in the courtroom, legally supported security will go a long way toward protecting your organization from facing large fines, penalties, or charges of negligence. 

| 安全应该在法律上是站得住脚的。你的管辖区的法律是组织安全的后盾。当有人侵入你的环境并破坏了安全，特别是当这种活动是非法的，那么在法庭上的起诉可能是唯一可用于赔偿或关闭的反应。另外，一个组织所做的许多决定会有法律责任问题。如果需要在法庭上为安全行动辩护，法律支持的安全将大大有助于保护你的组织不面临巨额罚款、惩罚或疏忽的指控。 |

**Security is a journey, not a finish line. **It is not a process that will ever be concluded. It is not possible to fully secure something, because security issues are always changing. Our deployed technology is changing by the passage of time, by the users, and by the adversaries discovering flaws and developing exploits. The defenses that were sufficient yesterday may not be sufficient tomorrow. As new vulnerabilities are discovered, as new means of attack are crafted and new exploits are built, we have to respond by reassessing our security infrastructure and responding appropriately.

|  安全是一个旅程，而不是一个终点线。它不是一个将永远结束的过程。不可能完全保证某件事情的安全，因为安全问题总是在变化。我们部署的技术随着时间的推移、用户的变化以及对手发现缺陷和开发漏洞而变化。昨天还足够的防御措施，明天可能就不足够了。当新的漏洞被发现，当新的攻击手段被制作出来，当新的漏洞被制造出来，我们必须通过重新评估我们的安全基础设施并做出适当的反应。 |
