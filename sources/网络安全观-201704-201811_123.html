One approach which I wanted to explore is homomorphic encryption. From Wikipedia: 

 一种我想展示的解决方案是有关于同态加密的。以下是来自于维基百科的解释：


Homomorphic encryption is a form of encryption that allows computations to be carried out on ciphertext, thus generating an encrypted result which, when decrypted, matches the result of operations performed on the plaintext. 

同态加密是一种加密形式，它允许人们对密文进行特定的代数运算得到仍然是加密的结果，将其解密所得到的结果与对明文进行同样的运算结果一样。

The "holy grail" of homomorphic encryption is Fully Homomorphic Encryption, with which you can get results of arbitrary computations on encrypted data, not just their sum or product. One crypto-system which provides you with additive homomorphic encryption is the Paillier Crypto- system. Additive here means that you can get the sum of two encrypted numbers. 

同态加密的最高境界是完全同态加密，通过它你可以得到加密数据的随机计算记过，而不仅仅是他们的和或者积。一种可以提供加和同态加密的数字系统叫做Paillier数字系统。加和在这里表示你可以获得两个加密数字的和。

Every day, employees submit their expenses to the contract. They don\\\\'t wan the other employees to know their expenses, so they encrypt it and send it to the contract. 

每天，雇员提交他们的报销到这个只能合约。他们不希望其他的同事能看到他们的开销，所以他们对它进行了加密并发送到了合约。

                                                               

The contract adds the encrypted expense to the current total expenses amount.                      

合约会加密开销并增加到目前的总报销额中。                  


At the end of every month, the company wants to know the total expenses amount, but doesn\\\\'t want anyone else to know it. They take the encrypted total expenses amount from the contract and decrypt it locally. 

到每个月的最后，公司希望知道总报销额，但又不希望其他人看到这个数字。他们会将这个加密的报销额从合约中提取出来，然后在本地解密。

The encryption and decryption would be client side, using a python paillier library. That means that the company first encrypts the initial balance, and whenever the employees want to report the expense, they use the homomorphicAdd function. This function uses the homomorphic addition property to store the result of the sum of the previous balance and the new expense. 

加密和解密的过程都是在客户端进行，使用Python的Paillier库。这表示公司一开始会加密初始总报销额，同时不论员工合适希望提交报销，他们都使用同态的Add方法。这个方法使用了同态的加法属性来保存之前总报销额和新总报销额的和。

                                                

In this manner, the contract has only one storage variable for the balance while employees can report an unlimited number of expenses. 

通过这样，这个合约可以仅只用一个变来来储存总报销额，同时员工可以无限次添加报销。

    

                                            

Conclusion 

总结

                                                

To summarize, this shows a method with which we can preserve privacy while still have some auditing properties. In this example, people can know that they can trust that the final encrypted balance represents the sum of all of the expenses reliably. 

让我们总结一下，这篇文章展示了一个即拥有加密隐私又拥有一定审查属性的方法。在这个例子中，人们知道自己可以相信最后通过每次报销添加后被加密的总报销额。
