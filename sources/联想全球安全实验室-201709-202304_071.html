Analysis and comments about APT-C-36

Some months ago, I found this on internet.

The image shows that there is an APT targeting Colombian Government institution, using malicious word documents to install malware.

The APT was discovered by a security company in April, 2018, the targets of the campaign were Colombian Oil companies, government institutions, and some other Colombia-based companies.

According to the APT report, the target aim to install a backdoor on specific companies, to access internal networks to collect strategic information and intellectual property (the researchers believe, the target of the APT campaign is to collect key information or intellectual property, due to the type of attacked companies).  


The APT detected targets were

  • IXXX (Government institution to assist disabled people)

  • EcoXXXXXX (Oil company)

  • HoXXX (Oil Company)

  • ImXX (Manufacturer of rough casters and wheelbarrows)

  • ExXXXXX(Commercial Information Services)

  • AlmXXXXX (Logistic Solutions)

  • Banco XXXXXXX (Bank)

  • Banco XXXXXX (Bank)

  • AXX (Interbank network)

  • SuXXXXXX (producer of printing inks, coatings, pigments, polymers etc.)


The penetration methods are the same as always, phishing and social engineering (using scarcity and curiosity). The report indicates, the APT uses spear phishing, but I bet it is ordinary phishing, because the attack surface is too big and the target companies are from different industries; I only see an apparent pattern among three of these companies (one bank, a logistic solution company and the interbank network, they belong to the same owner), but if the target were just the same person, why the other seven companies have been involved in the attack?

The impersonated organizations in the phishing emails were (the alleged senders of malicious emails):

  • Registraduría nacional del estado civil (Colombia civil registry)

  • Dane (Colombia National Administrative Department of Statistics)

  • Dian (ColombiaNational Directorate of Taxes and Customs)

  • Cyber división de la policía nacional (Colombia Cybercrime police unit)

  • Migración Colombia  (Colombia Migration)

  • Fiscalía general de la nación (Office of the Attorney General of Colombia)

The infection method was an email with a RAR file attached, the file was encrypted to protect the confidentiality of the information but the password was in plain text in the email content. The encrypted file technique was used to evade the email anti-malware functionality. 

After decrypt the file, there is a malicious word document, that use social engineering to trick the victim in to activate the macro execution to be able to see the contentsof the file and thus view the details of the cancellation of your citizenshipcard.

After the victim enable the macro execution, the file1.exe is downloaded and saved automatically. According with the report, this file is a variation of the Imminet Monitor RAT; also, the report supposes the attacker use the infected machine to do lateral movements in the infected network, looking for confidential information to exfiltrate(I wrote supposes,because in the report I didn’t see any proof of that).

Later, the report intents to do attribution profiling the attacker using 3 key points:

  1. Hours in which the files were generated.

  2. Language in which the email was written

  3. Character set in which the email was written, HTML code

According with the report, the attacker can be a group within South America and can have government support. 

The report came to that conclusion based on:

  • The attacker is located in South America, by the time zone found in the files.

  • The attacker is located in South America, because the emails are written in Spanish.

  • The APT is probably carried out by a neighboring country (In the report I did not see any evidence to say that).

  • The background of the victims and the duration of the attack indicates that the attacker is someone who is strategically leading the attack and looking for something long-term.

Broadly speaking, that is all the information I found about APT-C-36.

Now, after gathering and analyzing all the information about this APT, I want to point some discrepancies.

First, I don’t believe the attacker or group of attackers has the support of a neighboring country. The target companies do not seem too valuable, I can’t imagine a neighboring country with which Colombia was fighting in 2018, attacking a company that manufactures wheelbarrows and treadmills, or another company that is related to printer inks; nor do I see that high-level information can be obtained from a national institute for disabled people. With regard to banks, they are always subject to cyber-attacks so it does not seem strange to me that they receive attacks.

Second, the report says, the infected machines are used to make lateral movements within the network to find valuable information to exfiltrate. I did not find any support for this statement; I think it is more feasible that the threat actor uses these machines to spread ransomware on the compromised network.

Third, exist different inconsistencies in the attacker’s content of the emails, for example, they say that it is the last call for interrogation, but further down in the email it is said that it is the first citation. I don’t know if in law exist last call for interrogation in the first situation, I am not a lawyer, but sounds buggy and inconsistent, also I don’t think you can see these mistakes in one APT.

Another thing that I would like to highlight and this is why, I think this threat is not an APT, is the following mistake.

The malicious email enunciates article 12 of the immigration law; according to them, the non-compliance with this article, the target will be prohibited from leaving the country. When I check for this article, I found the article 12 is not even related with leaving or entering country.

When I was performing the research for this article, one thing I notice was, the Google predictive search show me that search as a common search, that means many people were looking for that, but the strange thing in here is this article is not important at all, then my question is Why many people search for this article?

The only answer I can think is, many people fall in this APT and was worried about the legal consequences then they want to understand what happened. If my assumption is correct that means this is NOT an APT, this was a phishing campaign but not an APT.
