Introduction

This is an introduction to social engineering and how to use human relationships, rules and instincts to deceive it. The article includes social engineering key concept, the 6 basic instincts and how to prevent a social engineering attack.

 

Prior to define social engineering is better to understand the concept of society.

 

Society: Is a group of individuals (in ourcase humans) that shares different kind of relationships like language,territory, culture, hobbies. Also, share different rules, for example don’tkill another person, don’t robe, etc. Also, in many cases, the society rules are non-written rules for example: help the other society individuals when you can.

 

Now, social engineering is a set of techniques that use the society rules, relationships and instincts to manipulate (deceive) the human to do something.

 

The keystone of social Engineering appliedto Information security is the next assumption:

 

A chain is as strong as its weakest link and the weakest link is the HUMAN

Based on that assumption, we can say that doesn’t matter how well protected is one system, how many firewalls, antivirus,antispyware and technical mechanism the company use to protect them self; if these technological protections depend or interact with humans then the social engineer can bypass the security.

 

In short, Attack the human to penetrate the target, bypassing all the security.


The Instincts

 Instinct is a fixed pattern or behavior as a response to certain stimuli. One example of that is the reaction of one babyto bitter food, another example is, the reaction of one person to fire.

 

Most of our instincts born with us but while we grow up, we change some of them and create new ones. (change instincts are difficult but not impossible).

 

The instincts are important in social engineering because: First, are like reactions, that means the individuals don’t think about what he is going to do, just act without thinking about the consequences of the action. Second, instincts reactions usually are the same (predictable). That means the social engineer can plan what to do to deceive the target.

 

Next, wewill study the social engineering most used instincts: 

 

(1) Like

The first situation are when the attacker makes the target feel they both are similar (like). The second situation is when the attacker talks about some topic that the targets like.

 

(2) Scarcity

One situation for this instinct is when the attacker makes the victim feel he is unique or better than the other individuals. The other situation is when the attacker gives limitations in terms of time or availability on some resource or service.

 

(3) Authority

Is when one authority figure, give you an order. Since we are kids, we learn to follow the authority figures' orders(usually without thinking-- instinctively); the key point here is, who is an authority figure for the target and what kind or orders the authority figure gives.


(4) Reciprocity

In our society exist a non-written rule that is, if I help you, you should help me. If I do something for you, you should do something for me. Also, since we are kids, we hear our parents talk about if we help others, others will help us when we need it.

 

Knowing that many people are willing to help; the social engineer just asks for help or offers some help to deceive the victim.

 

(5) Social prove

Most of us know this instinct as peer pressure. This happens because the human is a social being and has a natural fear to be excluded from his social group, then when a social group imposes some rule or standard, his members should follow it or will risk being expelled or excluded from the group. In social engineering, this instinct is used to influence a person to do something.

 

(6) Predictability

It is the most important instinct, because thanks to this, we can predict in advance what will be the reaction of one subject to one situation, then the social engineer can plan what to do.

 

Prevention

Because the social engineer needs information to understand the target and plan his attack. The easy way to prevent these attacks is reducing the target exposure (attack surface), less information in social media (Facebook, linked, WeChat moments, etc.) and internet.  

 

Another measure is building compartments, in each compartment use different emails, different phone numbers and don’tlink the compartments. For example, you can build two compartments, one for work and personal issues, other for public matters. In case your public profile is hacked your personal compartment won’t have problem. On the other side, If your personal compartment is hacked, I suggest you try to find the mole or change your friend because with friends like these, who needs enemies?

 

The last measure to prevent social engineer attacks is education, training, training, and training. With education, you will learn how the attacker acts and his techniques. With constant training and simulations the security awareness of the victims will increase, then when the times come, the deceiver will be deceived.
