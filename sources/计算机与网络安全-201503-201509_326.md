**1、阿里巴巴创始人马云妻子：张瑛**

  

![](../Images/4b356f5c0eeb18bc1af7f104da877089.png)

![](../Images/3577bb4a50de7213355ac20c9afc2700.png)

![](../Images/9a363343f00b4a825864d9a97ab9dfc9.png)
