**2\. 检查日志**

  
\# last（查看正常情况下登录到本机的所有用户的历史记录）  
注意”entered promiscuous mode”  
注意错误信息  
注 意Remote Procedure Call (rpc) programs with a log entry that includes a large number (> 20) strange characters(-^PM-^PM-^PM-^PM-^PM-^PM-^PM-^PM)

**3\. 检查进程**

| 123 || `# ps -aux（注意UID是``0``的）``# lsof -p pid（察看该进程所打开端口和文件）``# cat /etc/inetd.conf | grep -v “^#”（检查守护进程）` |
| --- | --- |

  
检查隐藏进程  

| 123 || `# ps -ef|awk ‘{print }’|sort -n|uniq >``1``# ls /porc |sort -n|uniq >``2``# diff ``1` `2` |

**4\. 检查文件**

| 123456 || `# find / -uid ``0` `–perm -``4000` `–print``# find / -size +10000k –print``# find / -name “…” –print``# find / -name “.. ” –print``# find / -name “. ” –print``# find / -name ” ” –print` |
