在[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)通信中涉及到一个重要方面，那就是如何定义要保护的数据流，因为这涉及到底要通过[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保护哪部分的数据流。而在定义保护数据流又涉及到其中一种基于隧道接口的方式，下面介绍[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保护数据流的定义和[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道接口。

  

**一、保护数据流的定义方式**

  

保护数据流的定义”就是指定哪些数据流要进入到[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道传输，其他的数据流不能进入[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道传输。[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)对需要保护的数据流的定义有基于“基于ACL”、“基于虚拟隧道接口”和“基于Efficient[ VPN](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124772&idx=2&sn=f8441a7025b8ef21d0ff7d5956badd1b&chksm=bc8660cb8bf1e9dd8f704347245727e5055e167f808a3cd8b2b8db6b74f4cb059d79ddafec61&scene=21#wechat_redirect)策略建立[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道”三种方式，下面分别予以介绍。

  

**1、基于ACL方式**

  

我们知道，高级IPACL可以基于源/目的IP地址、源/目的端口等信息对数据报文进行过滤，而这正可以用来对允许进入[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道的数据流进行过滤。

  

当采用ACL方式来定义需要保护的数据流时，手工方式和IKE动态协商方式建立的[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道是由高级IPACL来指定要保护的数据流范围，筛选出需要进入[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道的报文。ACL规则允许（permit）的报文将被保护，ACL规则拒绝（deny）的报文将不被保护。因为这里的ACL是高级IPACL，所以可以明确指定数据流中的源/目的IP地址、源/目的传输层端口、协议类型等参数。但这里的源/目的IP地址是指数据发送方和数据接收方主机的IP地址，通常是两端内部网络中的私网IP地址。

  

这种基于ACL来定义数据流的方式的优点是可以利用ACL配置的灵活性，根据IP地址、传输层端口、协议类型（如IP、ICMP、TCP、UDP等）等对报文进行过滤进而灵活制定[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的保护方法。

  

**2、基于虚拟隧道接口方式**

  

基于虚拟隧道接口来定义需要保护的数据流，首先就要在两端的[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)设备创建一个虚拟的隧道接口Tunnel，然后通过配置以该Tunnel接口为出接口的静态路由，以限定到达哪个目的子网的数据流可以通过[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道进行转发。因为Tunnel接口是点对点类型的接口，是运行PPP链路层协议的，所以以该接口为出接口的静态路由是可不指定下一跳IP地址的。

  

[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)虚拟隧道接口是一种三层逻辑接口，采用这种方式时所有路由到[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)虚拟隧道接口的报文都将进行[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保护，不再对数据流类型进行区分。但使用[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)虚拟隧道接口建立[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道仍具有以下优点：

  

（1）简化配置

  

只需将需要[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保护的数据流引到虚拟隧道接口，无需使用ACL定义待加/解密的流量特征。使得IPSec的配置不会受到网络规划的影响，增强了网络规划的可扩展性，降低了网络维护成本。

  

（2）减少开销

  

在保护远程接入用户流量的组网应用中，只需在[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)虚拟隧道接口处进行[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)报文封装，与[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect) over GRE或者[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect) over L2TP方式的隧道封装相比，无需额外为进入隧道的流量加封装GRE头或者L2TP头，减少了报文封装的层次，节省了带宽。

  

（3）支持范围更广

  

点对点[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)虚拟隧道接口可以支持动态路由协议，同时还可以支持对组播流量的保护。另外，[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)虚拟隧道接口在实施过程中明确地区分出“加密前”和“加密后”两个阶段，用户可以根据不同的组网需求灵活选择其他业务（例如NAT、QoS）实施的阶段。例如，如果用户希望对[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)封装前的报文应用QoS，则可以在[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)虚拟隧道接口上应用QoS策略；如果希望对[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)封装后的报文应用QoS，则可以在进入对端内部网络的物理接口上应用QoS策略。

  

**3、基于Efficient VPN策略建立IPSec隧道**

  

Efficient [VPN](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124772&idx=2&sn=f8441a7025b8ef21d0ff7d5956badd1b&chksm=bc8660cb8bf1e9dd8f704347245727e5055e167f808a3cd8b2b8db6b74f4cb059d79ddafec61&scene=21#wechat_redirect)采用C/S结构，其主要特点是它将[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)及其他相应配置都集中在Server端（总部网关），当Remote端（分支网关）配置好基本参数后，Remote端即可向Server端发起协商并与建立[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道，然后Server端将[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的其他相关属性及其他网络资源“推送”给Remote端，Remote端和Server端就直接定义了哪部分数据流是需要保护的，简化了分支网关的[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和其他网络资源的配置和维护。另外，Efficient [VPN](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124772&idx=2&sn=f8441a7025b8ef21d0ff7d5956badd1b&chksm=bc8660cb8bf1e9dd8f704347245727e5055e167f808a3cd8b2b8db6b74f4cb059d79ddafec61&scene=21#wechat_redirect)还支持远程站点设备的自动升级。

**二、IPSec虚拟隧道接口**

  

[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)虚拟隧道接口（即Tunnel接口）是一种支持路由的三层逻辑接口，它可以支持动态路由协议，所有路由到[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)虚拟隧道接口的报文都将进行[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保护，同时还可以支持对组播流量的保护。

  

通过之前的学习我们已经了解到，用户数据一方面要通过[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)虚拟隧道传输，必须首先要进行各种协议封装（如AH、ESP协议封装）和加密，才能按照[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道建立时定下的安全策略对用户数据进行安全传输，并且可以传输到隧道对端所连接的另一个私有网络设备上。另一方面，当通过[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)传输的数据安全到达隧道对端接口时，为了能使目的设备识别源设备发送的数据内容，必须把原来经过封装和加密的数据进行解封装和解密。这些对数据的封装/解封装、加密/解密的过程都是发生在虚拟的隧道接口上的。下面简单介绍[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道两端虚拟隧道接口的数据处理基本流程。

  

**1、IPSec隧道接口的数据封装和加密基本流程**

  

用户数据到达[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)设备（如路由器）后，需要[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保护的报文（即兴趣流）会被转发到[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)虚拟隧道接口上进行封装和加密，如图1所示。

![](../Images/f42ef9c6f6a8a85cbff9a9386345de7b.png)

图1  IPSec隧道接口对报文封装和加密的过程示意

（1）Router将从入接口接收到明文IP报文后送到转发模块进行处理；

  

（2）转发模块依据路由查询结果，发现如果是要保护的数据流，则将其发送到[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)虚拟隧道接口进行AH或ESP封装，具体的封装方法参见《[IPSec VPN 基本工作原理](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125497&idx=2&sn=132efe420be5ab133790e488dfb2d9c6&chksm=bc8662168bf1eb00162771aa31a8f8b022c26ffd789bc2554a80dc98d1b1a10eab20749f89e7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)》；

  

（3）[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)虚拟隧道接口完成对明文IP报文的封装处理后，根据建立的[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect) SA安全策略再将封装后的报文进行加密，然后再将加密后的密文转发模块进行处理；

  

（4）转发模块再通过第二次路由查询后，将已封装和加密的IP报文通过隧道接口对应的实际物理接口转发出去，直到对端的[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)设备Tunnel接口。

  

**2、IPSec隧道接口的数据解封装和解密基本流程**

  

数据经过[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道传输到达对端[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)设备时，需要对数据进行解封装和解密，其基本过程如图2所示。

![](../Images/def8b4442379f4bbc972b785857bae2f.png)

图2  IPSec隧道接口的数据解封装和解密基本流程

（1）Router将从入接口接收到已加密的IP报文后送到转发模块进行处理；

  

（2）转发模块识别到此密文的目的IP地址为本设备的隧道接口IP地址，且IP协议号为AH或ESP时，会将IP密文送到相应的[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)虚拟隧道接口进行相应的解封装处理；

  

（3）[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)虚拟隧道接口完成对IP密文的解封装处理之后，再进行相应的解封装处理，然后再将IP明文重新送回转发模块处理；

  

（4）转发模块再进行第二次路由查询后，将IP明文从隧道的实际物理接口转发出去，根据内网路由到达目的设备上。
