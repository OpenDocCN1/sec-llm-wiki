**一、僵尸网络传播**

  

botnet或蠕虫，一个重要的特征就是自动传播能力，这里介绍2类常见的传播方式。

  

[**漏洞**](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)**传播**——为了快速获取大量被控[僵尸](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[肉鸡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116724&idx=3&sn=b2218bc876e86e13ddb7777efe05f614&chksm=bc86405b8bf1c94ddfbfcb81e2242e81e48f557de20c187a113d4b4ab1ed78fec1c166028b81&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122652&idx=2&sn=257e5e110a89596431d5b96963608001&chksm=bc8669338bf1e0254f6a8b1ec27185c310a0994a042c846327f6e5d6ff3c7eb52d21190f9c60&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者会及时更新[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116765&idx=3&sn=4c2ba7e8d739c1904c4cdff9aadb646b&chksm=bc8640328bf1c9241002e67f3c5b3826b3bb2718f776ae85d8fc21d8c691e3837448eace1f25&scene=21#wechat_redirect)模块。每次在检测到有新一波[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)手法时，去查查最近公开的[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)或许就能看到。同时作为[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)大范围的[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c5335 RCE、JBoss DeploymentFileRepository WAR Deployment、CVE-1823（PHP-CGI RCE）等。

  

**自动化攻击工具**——[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)常有，而装有存在[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的软件的[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)不常有，但人的弱点总是避免不了的。比如弱口令问题，现如今最古老的弱口令猜解[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)依然存在，ssh\\ftp\\rdp\\telnet等等，加之tomcat管理后台等各种口令。现在越来越快的网速，越来越强的硬件性能使之效率越来越高。
