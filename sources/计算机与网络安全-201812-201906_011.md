[法国情报机构](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655120936&idx=3&sn=eaecd9d171da61b121707a8aef2223b6&chksm=bc8673c78bf1fad183400ab4fb9d95d9be1a478b496687b67165cae6bac4ea7ab507dd79c27a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)包括：部际[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)委员会、对外安全总局、领土监护局、普通[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)局、电话侦控部、国防部[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)局、军事[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)局和电[子情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)旅等等。

  

部际[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)委员会是法国最高的[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)协调机构，受总理直接控制。它的主要任务是确定各[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)机构的主要目标、任务，协调各[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)机关的行动。该委员会由6名成员组成：总统办公室主任、总理的[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)协调人、对外安全局局长、领土监护局局长、国防秘书处秘书长、国防部[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)局局长等。

  

对外安全总局又称第七局、法国国外安全总局、法国国外[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)局。总部设在巴黎第20区图尔威尔游泳池附近的莫蒂埃旧兵营里，故有“游泳池”的代称。

  

领土监护局又称本土警戒局、国内外间谍局、领土安全保卫局、领土监护司等，缩写为DSF。是法国内政部国家警察总局所辖的反间谍机构，也是国家警察的一部分。其性质和职能大体上相当于[美国联邦调查局](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117052&idx=3&sn=4c006911860dd406e1ab6779c9380c20&chksm=bc8643138bf1ca055316524247cfa3865b7446e76a6339dd2078b3477886152b77c0a3d6f225&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，主要任务是负责国内的反间谍、反颠覆和反恐怖。

  

普通[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)局是内政部下属的司局级机构。它的前身最早是管理铁路的治安机构，成立于1846年。在第三共和国时期，治安机构的职能不断扩大，于1911年成为特种警察，两年后改名为行政警察，后来又演变成为国家服务的政治警察。1941年4月，法国政府以法律条文确定该局机构的正式名称为普通[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)局。

  

电话侦控部归总理直接管辖，其任务是专门窃听各种各样的电话。它设有电话倒查号码设备，能及时查出打电话的人所用电话的号码。据统计90年代，巴黎就有3400部电话被电话侦控部窃听。

  

国防部[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)局又称法国总参二局。下设7个处：军事[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)处、科技[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)处、经济[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)处、武官处、总务处、联络通信处、档案资料处。总部设在巴黎国防部大楼，有工作人员1000余人。主要任务是搜集军事[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和与军事有关系的经济科技[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

军事[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)局是1993年新建的军事[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)机关。1990～1991年的海湾战争证明了法国的军事[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)部门工作不力，对[美国情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121000&idx=3&sn=86357a8935327a4a57d1f7fadead070d&chksm=bc8673878bf1fa91a0a27020b2ec308b55183805814faac543da6fbbc8223fc2c0d762785624&scene=21#wechat_redirect)有很大的依赖性，为此，法国于1993年正式组建了一个新的[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)机关——军事[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)局。该局把所有的军事[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)机构集中统一起来，从而成为法国的军事[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中心。

  

电子[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)旅是法国于1993年成立的电子部队，人员编制为4000人，基本由步兵组成。它是由分散在法国各武装力量中的有关人员和搜集处理[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的单位合并而成的，统一由陆军指挥部调动指挥。
