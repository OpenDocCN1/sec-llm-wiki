**1、“我们的”战场**

  

说起[攻防](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127030&idx=3&sn=2adb039c3bf3066eb9ebe819fe2f3310&chksm=bc8598198bf2110fc9fd93ef57c9165eb6776a4da15d16fa810f7dd1d13222dfba6f98876135&scene=21#wechat_redirect)对抗，大家很熟悉的一句话是：“[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)只需一点即可得手，而防守必须全面设防”。可见多数时候，防守方很被动。防守方其实是有优势的。无论坏人多么狡猾，他也是在我们的主场作战。我的系统我做主，孙猴子再怎么折腾也逃不出如来佛的五指山。

**2、高维防守**

  

当[攻防](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127174&idx=3&sn=b0c84359114c49067f3728971b0f3233&chksm=bc859b698bf2127f130740c2ede1cf2f75fdf7c7d2dcaa4200f1055d9094c6b6d81b03b36fbc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)双方在同一纬度时，[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122652&idx=2&sn=257e5e110a89596431d5b96963608001&chksm=bc8669338bf1e0254f6a8b1ec27185c310a0994a042c846327f6e5d6ff3c7eb52d21190f9c60&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者会有很多途径实现[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，而防守策略必须不断地叠加来封堵，往往疲于奔命。像杀毒软件或各色[IPS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116999&idx=3&sn=366c450cbcb68f1be62fa41b6b40b472&chksm=bc8643288bf1ca3e49e8b1412e2e849e883a45f0b7cda9c791746f6beaa7f4de9852030d436d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)\\[IDS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123686&idx=2&sn=23c35bb4d7c1b0414f175ae821ddc950&chksm=bc8665098bf1ec1f63b16681546b1ab596a1350b753f6e7f6c28fde6d0085fe3c15d04977d9e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)那样不断累加[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的方式，在[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)公司安全团队有限的人力资源条件下，去做这么一件需长年累月投入人力且收效未必很好的事是非常不可取的。

  

如何让策略足够完备呢，基于系统的机制、CGI和协议规范是较为靠谱的，比我们不断用新规则、新策略去为之前的策略打“补丁”来靠谱的多。因为一切[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122652&idx=2&sn=257e5e110a89596431d5b96963608001&chksm=bc8669338bf1e0254f6a8b1ec27185c310a0994a042c846327f6e5d6ff3c7eb52d21190f9c60&scene=21#wechat_redirect)与防守所需的基本功能与基本逻辑基于此。

  

无论什么[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130986&idx=3&sn=0e68bbe46f5ac759f2f830ef451e367f&chksm=bc8588858bf201938d2fcd040e8778de84f17db6e9dc8f47cdbfdce073da9c2dbaaeedaa6aa0&scene=21#wechat_redirect)行为，都对应着更高一纬度的系统功能和能力支持。在我们的主场，为什么放着这些有利的条件不用呢。与[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者针尖对麦芒地在低纬度对抗，犹如遭受了《三体》中的“降维[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122652&idx=2&sn=257e5e110a89596431d5b96963608001&chksm=bc8669338bf1e0254f6a8b1ec27185c310a0994a042c846327f6e5d6ff3c7eb52d21190f9c60&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”，是不明智的做法。

**3、策略实践**

  

梳理清楚了“webshell上传”这个场景在各纬度的技术细节，如何制定不易绕过的策略就相对容易了。

  

网络层，不用去考虑添加N个规则去应对诸如“XXeditor上传[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”、“XXCMS上传[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”，要知道无论什么[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和哪种猥琐的[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122652&idx=2&sn=257e5e110a89596431d5b96963608001&chksm=bc8669338bf1e0254f6a8b1ec27185c310a0994a042c846327f6e5d6ff3c7eb52d21190f9c60&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，总是要符合[HTTP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129052&idx=3&sn=6aa03cee097fa59206f385629b151800&chksm=bc8590338bf219253db54d9678c7924707d1e7d1195f8709817128e1ad501546618ac2f91cc7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)协议，那从高纬度的[HTTP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129052&idx=3&sn=6aa03cee097fa59206f385629b151800&chksm=bc8590338bf219253db54d9678c7924707d1e7d1195f8709817128e1ad501546618ac2f91cc7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)协议入手，“检测符合RFC1867标准的上传CGI行为”：

**4、近身防守**

  

通常供[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者施展的[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)利用或[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122652&idx=2&sn=257e5e110a89596431d5b96963608001&chksm=bc8669338bf1e0254f6a8b1ec27185c310a0994a042c846327f6e5d6ff3c7eb52d21190f9c60&scene=21#wechat_redirect)渠道能做的事情毕竟有限，防守方在高一纬度对事件的观察与检测让[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)方很难逃逸。但也要注意一点，对[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130986&idx=3&sn=0e68bbe46f5ac759f2f830ef451e367f&chksm=bc8588858bf201938d2fcd040e8778de84f17db6e9dc8f47cdbfdce073da9c2dbaaeedaa6aa0&scene=21#wechat_redirect)\\[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)场景的细致分析，以及[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130986&idx=3&sn=0e68bbe46f5ac759f2f830ef451e367f&chksm=bc8588858bf201938d2fcd040e8778de84f17db6e9dc8f47cdbfdce073da9c2dbaaeedaa6aa0&scene=21#wechat_redirect)场景关键环节的提炼也是策略制定的关键，否则也会陷入不断为旧策略打补丁的尴尬境地。

  

要解决一个入侵场景，在制定策略之前做好足够的分析并提炼其最核心的技术点，贴近此特征制定策略效果就非常好，且不易被绕过。下面选择两个[Linux](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126956&idx=3&sn=237ee71fb1fc21ca3822e3d9d0202ed1&chksm=bc8598438bf21155d298f8b99f358cc572e21b627d967d5350aadc7bb838bd9d6689aca8fdb3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统常见的[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)场景和案例来说明。

熟悉[恶意代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122949&idx=2&sn=1ee3feea8b48a3aabc92d8f452344783&chksm=bc866bea8bf1e2fca3e416016050213dbaabe4e46e51667cf279e03efad0de48f7d571db209f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)取证的朋友应该清楚常见的反连[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，‘核心’代码部分一般是这样的：

  

connect(sockfd, (struct sockaddr \*)&cliaddr, sizeof(struct sockaddr));

dup2(sockfd, 1);

dup2(sockfd, 2);

execve("/bin/sh","sh",envp);

  

那我们检测十分简单，通常一个bash进程是不会有网络连接的，所以检测策略是bash的STDIN STDOUT绑定了socket则为[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，如图4所示。

但有一些老练的[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者的代码则不会那么偷懒，譬如mod\_rootme这个[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，为了复用Apache的监听端口以及获得root权限的[shell](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127748&idx=3&sn=c1a3d178c1155c7613708f4f6384eb64&chksm=bc85952b8bf21c3d2d44139d072702367155b51c86221ea83618a580515205fc6dd139e1496c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，在代码逻辑上做了很多精巧的变换组合，使得检测不可能象前述那类普通[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)一样方便。TSRC官微曾经有分享过如何发现mod\_rootme这类极为隐蔽的[后门](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129244&idx=3&sn=6df349d4b772425777487a77091f0f4d&chksm=bc8593738bf21a652bfc7213ea58a506986d7b1e6812c5132b33c598b7a8a210c10f13d7f900&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，如图5所示。再狡猾的[后门](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129244&idx=3&sn=6df349d4b772425777487a77091f0f4d&chksm=bc8593738bf21a652bfc7213ea58a506986d7b1e6812c5132b33c598b7a8a210c10f13d7f900&scene=21#wechat_redirect)也有异于系统正常行为的地方，根据异常行为建立策略是相比那类签名特征更为靠谱的检测手段。

甲方安全团队尽量避免做看似大而全，针对每一类[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=3&sn=c82f59076adf4123590301c9577f3c59&chksm=bc859b348bf21222ef281bc9aebb654a06582273fd7d9eb73ab84b56adbf383f74305efe3c3c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122652&idx=2&sn=257e5e110a89596431d5b96963608001&chksm=bc8669338bf1e0254f6a8b1ec27185c310a0994a042c846327f6e5d6ff3c7eb52d21190f9c60&scene=21#wechat_redirect)手法制定策略的思路。而要针对自身业务特点，分析其主要风险，针对性的制定策略。将有限的资源用于对抗清晰的风险场景，是更为可取且有效率的事。
