近日，法学博士许先生起诉“[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)浏览器”[APP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130459&idx=3&sn=f8b64d61307c28f9f3265b4eb1eda12a&chksm=bc858eb48bf207a2b2fd53128cd92797950f71b4e141abfb9c60970b6a662bc1849e85912dff&scene=21#wechat_redirect)运营方腾讯。许先生称，“[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)浏览器”[APP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130459&idx=3&sn=f8b64d61307c28f9f3265b4eb1eda12a&chksm=bc858eb48bf207a2b2fd53128cd92797950f71b4e141abfb9c60970b6a662bc1849e85912dff&scene=21#wechat_redirect)在其不知情的情况下，窃取了大量隐私，不仅包括[微信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127698&idx=3&sn=5101f6a80628d7caf6bc8ed7eec325c5&chksm=bc85957d8bf21c6bad5b83f1dcf2124043fa2e6a19eee9f604e23db0211b7ddcf8d94ecf97b8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的头像、性别、地区、生日等[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，还包括[微信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127698&idx=3&sn=5101f6a80628d7caf6bc8ed7eec325c5&chksm=bc85957d8bf21c6bad5b83f1dcf2124043fa2e6a19eee9f604e23db0211b7ddcf8d94ecf97b8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的好友关系信息。近日，法院裁定腾讯立即停止获取许先生[微信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127698&idx=3&sn=5101f6a80628d7caf6bc8ed7eec325c5&chksm=bc85957d8bf21c6bad5b83f1dcf2124043fa2e6a19eee9f604e23db0211b7ddcf8d94ecf97b8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)用户、[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)用户账号信息的行为。

![](../Images/90e9d68b19b3992d7c58a6b037f802e5.png)

许先生告诉记者，他经常使用“[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)浏览器”浏览网页。因为“[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)浏览器”的一些功能在登录之后用起来会更便捷，所以他偶尔会用自己的[微信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127698&idx=3&sn=5101f6a80628d7caf6bc8ed7eec325c5&chksm=bc85957d8bf21c6bad5b83f1dcf2124043fa2e6a19eee9f604e23db0211b7ddcf8d94ecf97b8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)账号、[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)账号登录“[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)浏览器”，“[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)浏览器”目前也仅支持这两种登录方式。

  

一次使用中，许先生发现使用[微信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127698&idx=3&sn=5101f6a80628d7caf6bc8ed7eec325c5&chksm=bc85957d8bf21c6bad5b83f1dcf2124043fa2e6a19eee9f604e23db0211b7ddcf8d94ecf97b8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)账号登录“[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)浏览器”之后，“[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)浏览器”在没有提示、告知的情况下，未经许先生授权同意，就获取了许先生的[微信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127698&idx=3&sn=5101f6a80628d7caf6bc8ed7eec325c5&chksm=bc85957d8bf21c6bad5b83f1dcf2124043fa2e6a19eee9f604e23db0211b7ddcf8d94ecf97b8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)好友关系，并展示在好友页面上。不仅如此，“[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)浏览器”还同步了许先生[微信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127698&idx=3&sn=5101f6a80628d7caf6bc8ed7eec325c5&chksm=bc85957d8bf21c6bad5b83f1dcf2124043fa2e6a19eee9f604e23db0211b7ddcf8d94ecf97b8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)账号中的性别、地区等[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

许先生又使用[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)登录“[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)浏览器”，发现一样在没有被提示和告知的情况下，未经授权同意就被获取了[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)好友关系，以及性别、地区甚至生日信息。而这些个人隐私信息的收集、获取过程，许先生表示一无所知，从未授权过“[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)浏览器”收集[微信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127698&idx=3&sn=5101f6a80628d7caf6bc8ed7eec325c5&chksm=bc85957d8bf21c6bad5b83f1dcf2124043fa2e6a19eee9f604e23db0211b7ddcf8d94ecf97b8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上的个人隐私信息。

许先生很难理解，为什么仅用[微信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127698&idx=3&sn=5101f6a80628d7caf6bc8ed7eec325c5&chksm=bc85957d8bf21c6bad5b83f1dcf2124043fa2e6a19eee9f604e23db0211b7ddcf8d94ecf97b8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)登录“[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)浏览器”，就被收集了个人隐私信息。他认为“[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)浏览器”这是在公然窃取隐私。担心之余，许先生就想在“[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)浏览器”中删掉这些被收集的个人隐私信息，但让许先生惊讶的是，[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)浏览器未提供任何方式来取消[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的授权，在“[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)浏览器”中，也找不到任何能够删除个人隐私信息的地方。
