## 1990年，早期的[无线网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127878&idx=3&sn=b8b6c3abeac9c1094bbd26ffad57d6c9&chksm=bc8594a98bf21dbfb02de90b480c45015566dfe4da4becd2c2cf15ff7634baa404e612c3a546&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品Wireless LAN在[美国](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128142&idx=3&sn=7b8de69bbda3be653df725fdec9fc24a&chksm=bc8597a18bf21eb7f39f365987d171669e5868d0a43659b4fa35b9053cfc346b30c80e7c5e8a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)出现，1997年IEEE802.11[无线网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127878&idx=3&sn=b8b6c3abeac9c1094bbd26ffad57d6c9&chksm=bc8594a98bf21dbfb02de90b480c45015566dfe4da4becd2c2cf15ff7634baa404e612c3a546&scene=21#wechat_redirect)标准颁布，对[无线网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127878&idx=3&sn=b8b6c3abeac9c1094bbd26ffad57d6c9&chksm=bc8594a98bf21dbfb02de90b480c45015566dfe4da4becd2c2cf15ff7634baa404e612c3a546&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术的发展和[无线网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127878&idx=3&sn=b8b6c3abeac9c1094bbd26ffad57d6c9&chksm=bc8594a98bf21dbfb02de90b480c45015566dfe4da4becd2c2cf15ff7634baa404e612c3a546&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的应用起到了重要的推动作用，促进了不同厂家的[无线网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127878&idx=3&sn=b8b6c3abeac9c1094bbd26ffad57d6c9&chksm=bc8594a98bf21dbfb02de90b480c45015566dfe4da4becd2c2cf15ff7634baa404e612c3a546&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品的互通互联。1999年[无线网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127878&idx=3&sn=b8b6c3abeac9c1094bbd26ffad57d6c9&chksm=bc8594a98bf21dbfb02de90b480c45015566dfe4da4becd2c2cf15ff7634baa404e612c3a546&scene=21#wechat_redirect)国际标准的更新及完善，进一步规范了不同频点的产品及更高网络速度产品的开发和应用。

  

建设符合IEEE802.11标准的[无线网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127878&idx=3&sn=b8b6c3abeac9c1094bbd26ffad57d6c9&chksm=bc8594a98bf21dbfb02de90b480c45015566dfe4da4becd2c2cf15ff7634baa404e612c3a546&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，不仅可以满足需要，而且日后网络还可以平滑升级，可以有效地保护投资。IEEE802.11 工作小组已成立了新的研究小组，对大信息流量及多工作组同时工作、流量控制及更安全的保密编码、安全认证等技术问题进行研究，随着[无线网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127878&idx=3&sn=b8b6c3abeac9c1094bbd26ffad57d6c9&chksm=bc8594a98bf21dbfb02de90b480c45015566dfe4da4becd2c2cf15ff7634baa404e612c3a546&scene=21#wechat_redirect)成本的不断下调、配套技术的不断完善、覆盖范围的不断增大，[无线网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127878&idx=3&sn=b8b6c3abeac9c1094bbd26ffad57d6c9&chksm=bc8594a98bf21dbfb02de90b480c45015566dfe4da4becd2c2cf15ff7634baa404e612c3a546&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的应用将会成为未来网络的技术主流。
