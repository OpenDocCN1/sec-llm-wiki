**1、破解Windows用户密码**

  

[Windows](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127496&idx=3&sn=21acddfe6a98fe94ae346db72555e138&chksm=bc859a278bf21331d775fdb2224b50832a618a13d3a8fa76c41786186e846f5126daf96682bc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统的用户名和[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126686&idx=3&sn=c588c739341ee07b2b1d6a84c5e1a6ec&chksm=bc8599718bf210670bfe501723741133e318ca7abcc697f1d1fd5236a9490f3bd6b555342dd9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保存在SAM（安全账号管理器）文件中。在基于NT内核的[Windows](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127496&idx=3&sn=21acddfe6a98fe94ae346db72555e138&chksm=bc859a278bf21331d775fdb2224b50832a618a13d3a8fa76c41786186e846f5126daf96682bc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统中，包括[Windows](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127496&idx=3&sn=21acddfe6a98fe94ae346db72555e138&chksm=bc859a278bf21331d775fdb2224b50832a618a13d3a8fa76c41786186e846f5126daf96682bc&scene=21#wechat_redirect) 2000及后续版本，这个文件保存在“C:\\Windows\\System32\\Config”目录下。出于安全原因，微软特定添加了一些额外的安全措施将该文件保护了起来。首先，[操作系统](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122728&idx=2&sn=6b263bf85d6b8bd8bf1d3744a491a40c&chksm=bc8668c78bf1e1d1a0e813fcd7b41c7bc300c2c98eb269b77f92854c62f1fe5127a0e01c5574&scene=21#wechat_redirect)启动之后，SAM文件将同时被锁定。这意味着[操作系统](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122728&idx=2&sn=6b263bf85d6b8bd8bf1d3744a491a40c&chksm=bc8668c78bf1e1d1a0e813fcd7b41c7bc300c2c98eb269b77f92854c62f1fe5127a0e01c5574&scene=21#wechat_redirect)运行之时，用户无法打开或复制SAM文件。除了锁定，整个SAM文件还经过加密，且不可见。
