在网络空间中，[大数据](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128534&idx=3&sn=e679f67ac39ab11dbf5f8cae74032564&chksm=bc8596398bf21f2ffb6d133b630409ede8176bcaaa3d12565e956b45bfd287978ab050e9230d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是更容易被“发现”的大目标，承载着越来越多的关注度。一方面，[大数据](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128608&idx=3&sn=f7597e9aca98808d9c1aa019290ea11b&chksm=bc8591cf8bf218d959e08078f5309adfeffd6ae2c94cf34da4dbc781b6bdfbe0406b1ceaaace&scene=21#wechat_redirect)不仅意味着海量的数据，也意味着更复杂、更敏感的数据，这些数据会吸引更多的潜在攻击者，成为更具吸引力的目标；另一方面，数据的大量聚集，使[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=3&sn=c82f59076adf4123590301c9577f3c59&chksm=bc859b348bf21222ef281bc9aebb654a06582273fd7d9eb73ab84b56adbf383f74305efe3c3c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)一次成功的攻击能够获得更多的数据，无形中降低了[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127290&idx=3&sn=04aaac7d1e20fd446929032cb45b6b38&chksm=bc859b158bf212032e5e2121f6066cd1ec171afc141be62e32ad671879a403ade7b3435495bb&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的进攻成本，增加了“收益率”。

  

从基础技术角度看，Hadoop对数据的聚合增加了数据泄露的风险。作为一个分布式系统架构，Hadoop可以用来应对PB甚至ZB级的海量数据存储；作为一个云化的平台，Hadoop自身存在[云计算](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129406&idx=3&sn=83a9a82717306dd30435caf358542d3f&chksm=bc8592d18bf21bc7511d53a4676fd1fb053751a75f0bb0343db152b0f90c39a0ea71e00176aa&scene=21#wechat_redirect)面临的安全风险，企业需要实施安全访问机制和数据保护机制。同样，[大数据](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128534&idx=3&sn=e679f67ac39ab11dbf5f8cae74032564&chksm=bc8596398bf21f2ffb6d133b630409ede8176bcaaa3d12565e956b45bfd287978ab050e9230d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)依托的基础技术——NoSQL（非关系型[数据库](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129320&idx=3&sn=e5b2f0721ffd54f8891e633fbc280f4b&chksm=bc8593078bf21a1184c1596a84ce0604e00b716de6081c5b27ea0311d1df5e2b5024ba752979&scene=21#wechat_redirect)）与当前广泛应用的SQL（关系型[数据库](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129320&idx=3&sn=e5b2f0721ffd54f8891e633fbc280f4b&chksm=bc8593078bf21a1184c1596a84ce0604e00b716de6081c5b27ea0311d1df5e2b5024ba752979&scene=21#wechat_redirect)）技术不同，没有经过长期改进和完善，在维护[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)方面也未设置严格的访问控制和隐私管理机制。NoSQL技术还因[大数据](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128608&idx=3&sn=f7597e9aca98808d9c1aa019290ea11b&chksm=bc8591cf8bf218d959e08078f5309adfeffd6ae2c94cf34da4dbc781b6bdfbe0406b1ceaaace&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中数据来源和承载方式的多样性，使企业很难定位和保护其中的机密信息，这是NoSQL内在安全机制的不完善，即缺乏机密性和完整性。另外，NoSQL对来自不同系统、不同应用程序及不同活动的数据进行关联，也加大了隐私泄露的风险。此外，NoSQL还允许不断对数据记录添加属性，这也对[数据库](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129320&idx=3&sn=e5b2f0721ffd54f8891e633fbc280f4b&chksm=bc8593078bf21a1184c1596a84ce0604e00b716de6081c5b27ea0311d1df5e2b5024ba752979&scene=21#wechat_redirect)管理员的安全性预见能力提出了更高的要求。从核心价值角度看，[大数据](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128534&idx=3&sn=e679f67ac39ab11dbf5f8cae74032564&chksm=bc8596398bf21f2ffb6d133b630409ede8176bcaaa3d12565e956b45bfd287978ab050e9230d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的技术关键在于[数据分析](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130350&idx=3&sn=9164f5b5a6b1b3e413689caac8c2babb&chksm=bc858f018bf206171a3cc1e08e1b4f85cee39996daf49ae024369447f252ca2bfe533d7faaa7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和利用，但[数据分析](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130350&idx=3&sn=9164f5b5a6b1b3e413689caac8c2babb&chksm=bc858f018bf206171a3cc1e08e1b4f85cee39996daf49ae024369447f252ca2bfe533d7faaa7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术的发展，势必对用户隐私产生极大威胁。

  

在企业用[数据挖掘](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116815&idx=3&sn=d9a679a08f4478e0fa09576cb479567b&chksm=bc8643e08bf1caf668c66b2dd78732344afdb7e72ab88d36102e949d8ba079573b19c014e972&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和[数据分析](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130350&idx=3&sn=9164f5b5a6b1b3e413689caac8c2babb&chksm=bc858f018bf206171a3cc1e08e1b4f85cee39996daf49ae024369447f252ca2bfe533d7faaa7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)等[大数据](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128608&idx=3&sn=f7597e9aca98808d9c1aa019290ea11b&chksm=bc8591cf8bf218d959e08078f5309adfeffd6ae2c94cf34da4dbc781b6bdfbe0406b1ceaaace&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术获取商业价值的同时，[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=3&sn=c82f59076adf4123590301c9577f3c59&chksm=bc859b348bf21222ef281bc9aebb654a06582273fd7d9eb73ab84b56adbf383f74305efe3c3c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)也正在利用这些[大数据](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128534&idx=3&sn=e679f67ac39ab11dbf5f8cae74032564&chksm=bc8596398bf21f2ffb6d133b630409ede8176bcaaa3d12565e956b45bfd287978ab050e9230d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术向企业发起攻击。[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127290&idx=3&sn=04aaac7d1e20fd446929032cb45b6b38&chksm=bc859b158bf212032e5e2121f6066cd1ec171afc141be62e32ad671879a403ade7b3435495bb&scene=21#wechat_redirect)最大限度地收集更多有用信息，如[社交网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123516&idx=3&sn=71d77d0f1dbf3bdb1383e630e83828b6&chksm=bc8665d38bf1ecc51b91be0f4058cb6807a474269c2926c71ab19a2de217d874a2024df045ad&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、邮件、微博、电子商务、电话和家庭住址等，为发起攻击做准备，[大数据](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128608&idx=3&sn=f7597e9aca98808d9c1aa019290ea11b&chksm=bc8591cf8bf218d959e08078f5309adfeffd6ae2c94cf34da4dbc781b6bdfbe0406b1ceaaace&scene=21#wechat_redirect)分析让[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=3&sn=c82f59076adf4123590301c9577f3c59&chksm=bc859b348bf21222ef281bc9aebb654a06582273fd7d9eb73ab84b56adbf383f74305efe3c3c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的攻击更精准。此外，[大数据](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128534&idx=3&sn=e679f67ac39ab11dbf5f8cae74032564&chksm=bc8596398bf21f2ffb6d133b630409ede8176bcaaa3d12565e956b45bfd287978ab050e9230d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)为[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127290&idx=3&sn=04aaac7d1e20fd446929032cb45b6b38&chksm=bc859b158bf212032e5e2121f6066cd1ec171afc141be62e32ad671879a403ade7b3435495bb&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发起攻击提供了更多机会。[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=3&sn=c82f59076adf4123590301c9577f3c59&chksm=bc859b348bf21222ef281bc9aebb654a06582273fd7d9eb73ab84b56adbf383f74305efe3c3c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)利用[大数据](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128608&idx=3&sn=f7597e9aca98808d9c1aa019290ea11b&chksm=bc8591cf8bf218d959e08078f5309adfeffd6ae2c94cf34da4dbc781b6bdfbe0406b1ceaaace&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发起僵尸[网络攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，可能会同时控制上百万台傀儡机并发起攻击，这个数量级是传统单点攻击不具备的。

[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127290&idx=3&sn=04aaac7d1e20fd446929032cb45b6b38&chksm=bc859b158bf212032e5e2121f6066cd1ec171afc141be62e32ad671879a403ade7b3435495bb&scene=21#wechat_redirect)利用[大数据](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128534&idx=3&sn=e679f67ac39ab11dbf5f8cae74032564&chksm=bc8596398bf21f2ffb6d133b630409ede8176bcaaa3d12565e956b45bfd287978ab050e9230d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)将攻击很好地隐藏起来，传统的防护策略难以检测出来。传统的检测是基于单个时间点进行的基于威胁特征的实时匹配检测，而高级可持续攻击（APT）是一个实施过程，并不具备能够被实时检测出来的明显特征，无法被实时检测。同时，APT攻击代码隐藏在大量数据中，让其很难被发现。此外，[大数据](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128608&idx=3&sn=f7597e9aca98808d9c1aa019290ea11b&chksm=bc8591cf8bf218d959e08078f5309adfeffd6ae2c94cf34da4dbc781b6bdfbe0406b1ceaaace&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的价值低密度性，让安全分析工具很难聚焦在价值点上，[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=3&sn=c82f59076adf4123590301c9577f3c59&chksm=bc859b348bf21222ef281bc9aebb654a06582273fd7d9eb73ab84b56adbf383f74305efe3c3c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)可以将攻击隐藏在[大数据](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128534&idx=3&sn=e679f67ac39ab11dbf5f8cae74032564&chksm=bc8596398bf21f2ffb6d133b630409ede8176bcaaa3d12565e956b45bfd287978ab050e9230d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中，给安全服务提供商的分析制造了很大困难。[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127290&idx=3&sn=04aaac7d1e20fd446929032cb45b6b38&chksm=bc859b158bf212032e5e2121f6066cd1ec171afc141be62e32ad671879a403ade7b3435495bb&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发起的任何一个会误导安全厂商目标信息提取和检索的攻击，都会导致安全监测偏离应有的方向。
