**一、Nessus**

  

Nessus号称是世界上最流行的[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)扫描程序，全世界有超过75000个组织在使用它。该工具提供完整的电脑[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)扫描服务，并随时更新其[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[数据库](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129320&idx=3&sn=e5b2f0721ffd54f8891e633fbc280f4b&chksm=bc8593078bf21a1184c1596a84ce0604e00b716de6081c5b27ea0311d1df5e2b5024ba752979&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。Nessus不同于传统的[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)扫描软件，Nessus可同时在本机或远端上遥控，进行系统的[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)分析扫描。Nessus也是[渗透测试](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127440&idx=2&sn=c223aa7241748e66d4ec2490bbe3018e&chksm=bc859a7f8bf213690ae817fe10cf9b5e3a794f74c7650f5fb229284933de86f2f735d6b0bc2c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)重要工具之一。

  

**1、安装和配置Nessus**

  

为了[定位](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126780&idx=3&sn=6c65767c93a0f3917d8992301cf94292&chksm=bc8599138bf210055e67c6ddc2f4e5f25980802975066830b0aea7073f7d8a495e4274265d78&scene=21#wechat_redirect)在目标系统上的[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，Nessus依赖feeds的格式实现[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)检查。Nessus官网提供了两种版本：家庭版和专业版。

  

● 家庭版：家庭版是供非商业性或个人使用。家庭版比较适合个人使用，可以用于非专业的环境。

● 专业版：专业版是供商业性使用。它包括支持或附加功能，如无线并发连接等。

Config、创建扫描目标及新建扫描任务才可以进行各种[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)扫描。设置好这些信息用户就可以进行各种[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)扫描了，如本地[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)扫描、网络[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)扫描和指定[操作系统](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122728&idx=2&sn=6b263bf85d6b8bd8bf1d3744a491a40c&chksm=bc8668c78bf1e1d1a0e813fcd7b41c7bc300c2c98eb269b77f92854c62f1fe5127a0e01c5574&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)扫描等。进行这些[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)扫描之前，都必须要创建Scan
