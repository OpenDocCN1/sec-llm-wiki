> **预防技术**

  

计算机[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=2&sn=5b5d7e7a936eb7f7aec16bddd6c3f0b2&chksm=bc859b468bf2125084447624ecb6da59672b17585f652343747238fd1b3bcf35ded30d6fc680&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的预防技术就是通过一定的技术手段防止计算机[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)对系统的传染和破坏。实际上这是一种动态判定技术，即一种行为规则判定技术。也就是说，计算机[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127030&idx=2&sn=cb843183873e4e206b8160d2dfb98e5c&chksm=bc8598198bf2110f132c45befd4571d47b8059b9651cae0fc9e734c692f7ac022d62a2830928&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的预防是采用对[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127156&idx=2&sn=47d8815448e2605609399f0ee3d59863&chksm=bc859b9b8bf2128d090a3ac47007b357d7037fd5858dc366dddb617de8dd30aded65b9c225c7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的规则进行分类处理，而后在程序运作中凡有类似的规则出现则认定是计算机[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=2&sn=5b5d7e7a936eb7f7aec16bddd6c3f0b2&chksm=bc859b468bf2125084447624ecb6da59672b17585f652343747238fd1b3bcf35ded30d6fc680&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。具体来说，计算机[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的预防是通过阻止计算机[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127030&idx=2&sn=cb843183873e4e206b8160d2dfb98e5c&chksm=bc8598198bf2110f132c45befd4571d47b8059b9651cae0fc9e734c692f7ac022d62a2830928&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进入系统内存或阻止计算机[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127156&idx=2&sn=47d8815448e2605609399f0ee3d59863&chksm=bc859b9b8bf2128d090a3ac47007b357d7037fd5858dc366dddb617de8dd30aded65b9c225c7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)对磁盘的操作，尤其是写操作。预防[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=2&sn=5b5d7e7a936eb7f7aec16bddd6c3f0b2&chksm=bc859b468bf2125084447624ecb6da59672b17585f652343747238fd1b3bcf35ded30d6fc680&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术包括：磁盘引导区保护、加密可执行程序、读写控制技术、系统监控技术等。例如，大家所熟悉的防[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)卡，其主要功能是对磁盘提供写保护，监视在计算机和驱动器之间产生的信号。以及可能造成危害的写命令，并且判断磁盘当前所处的状态：哪一个磁盘将要进行写操作，是否正在进行写操作，磁盘是否处于写保护等，来确定[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127030&idx=2&sn=cb843183873e4e206b8160d2dfb98e5c&chksm=bc8598198bf2110f132c45befd4571d47b8059b9651cae0fc9e734c692f7ac022d62a2830928&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是否将要发作。计算机[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127156&idx=2&sn=47d8815448e2605609399f0ee3d59863&chksm=bc859b9b8bf2128d090a3ac47007b357d7037fd5858dc366dddb617de8dd30aded65b9c225c7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的预防应用包括对已知[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=2&sn=5b5d7e7a936eb7f7aec16bddd6c3f0b2&chksm=bc859b468bf2125084447624ecb6da59672b17585f652343747238fd1b3bcf35ded30d6fc680&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的预防和对未知[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的预防两个部分。目前，对已知[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127030&idx=2&sn=cb843183873e4e206b8160d2dfb98e5c&chksm=bc8598198bf2110f132c45befd4571d47b8059b9651cae0fc9e734c692f7ac022d62a2830928&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的预防可以采用特征判定技术或静态判定技术，而对未知[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127156&idx=2&sn=47d8815448e2605609399f0ee3d59863&chksm=bc859b9b8bf2128d090a3ac47007b357d7037fd5858dc366dddb617de8dd30aded65b9c225c7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的预防则是一种行为规则的判定技术，即动态判定技术。

  

> **检测病毒技术**

  

计算机[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=2&sn=5b5d7e7a936eb7f7aec16bddd6c3f0b2&chksm=bc859b468bf2125084447624ecb6da59672b17585f652343747238fd1b3bcf35ded30d6fc680&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的检测技术是指通过一定的技术手段判定出特定计算机[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的一种技术。它有两种：一种是根据计算机[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127030&idx=2&sn=cb843183873e4e206b8160d2dfb98e5c&chksm=bc8598198bf2110f132c45befd4571d47b8059b9651cae0fc9e734c692f7ac022d62a2830928&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的关键字、特征程序段内容、[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127156&idx=2&sn=47d8815448e2605609399f0ee3d59863&chksm=bc859b9b8bf2128d090a3ac47007b357d7037fd5858dc366dddb617de8dd30aded65b9c225c7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)特征及传染方式、文件长度的变化，在特征分类的基础上建立的[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=2&sn=5b5d7e7a936eb7f7aec16bddd6c3f0b2&chksm=bc859b468bf2125084447624ecb6da59672b17585f652343747238fd1b3bcf35ded30d6fc680&scene=21#wechat_redirect)检测技术。另一种是不针对具体[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序的自身校验技术。即对某个文件或数据段进行检验和计算并保存其结果，以后定期或不定期地以保存的结果对该文件或数据段进行检验，若出现差异，即表示该文件或数据段完整性已遭到破坏，感染上了[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127030&idx=2&sn=cb843183873e4e206b8160d2dfb98e5c&chksm=bc8598198bf2110f132c45befd4571d47b8059b9651cae0fc9e734c692f7ac022d62a2830928&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，从而检测到[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127156&idx=2&sn=47d8815448e2605609399f0ee3d59863&chksm=bc859b9b8bf2128d090a3ac47007b357d7037fd5858dc366dddb617de8dd30aded65b9c225c7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的存在。

  

> ### **清除病毒技术**

  

计算机[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=2&sn=5b5d7e7a936eb7f7aec16bddd6c3f0b2&chksm=bc859b468bf2125084447624ecb6da59672b17585f652343747238fd1b3bcf35ded30d6fc680&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的清除技术是计算机[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)检测技术发展的必然结果，是计算机[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127030&idx=2&sn=cb843183873e4e206b8160d2dfb98e5c&chksm=bc8598198bf2110f132c45befd4571d47b8059b9651cae0fc9e734c692f7ac022d62a2830928&scene=21#wechat_redirect)传染程序的一种逆过程。目前，清除[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127156&idx=2&sn=47d8815448e2605609399f0ee3d59863&chksm=bc859b9b8bf2128d090a3ac47007b357d7037fd5858dc366dddb617de8dd30aded65b9c225c7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)大都是在某种[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=2&sn=5b5d7e7a936eb7f7aec16bddd6c3f0b2&chksm=bc859b468bf2125084447624ecb6da59672b17585f652343747238fd1b3bcf35ded30d6fc680&scene=21#wechat_redirect)出现后，通过对其进行分析研究而研制出来的具有相应解毒功能的软件。这类软件技术发展往往是被动的，带有滞后性。而且由于计算机软件所要求的精确性，解毒软件有其局限性，对有些变种[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的清除无能为力。目前市场上流行的Intel公司的PC\_CILLIN、CentralPoint公司的CPAV，及我国的LANClear和Kill89等产品均采用上述三种防[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127030&idx=2&sn=cb843183873e4e206b8160d2dfb98e5c&chksm=bc8598198bf2110f132c45befd4571d47b8059b9651cae0fc9e734c692f7ac022d62a2830928&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术。

**1、特征码（值）扫描**

  

特征码是由反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127030&idx=2&sn=cb843183873e4e206b8160d2dfb98e5c&chksm=bc8598198bf2110f132c45befd4571d47b8059b9651cae0fc9e734c692f7ac022d62a2830928&scene=21#wechat_redirect)分析人员从[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127156&idx=2&sn=47d8815448e2605609399f0ee3d59863&chksm=bc859b9b8bf2128d090a3ac47007b357d7037fd5858dc366dddb617de8dd30aded65b9c225c7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)样本中的不同位置提取的一系列字节，也就是带有[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=2&sn=5b5d7e7a936eb7f7aec16bddd6c3f0b2&chksm=bc859b468bf2125084447624ecb6da59672b17585f652343747238fd1b3bcf35ded30d6fc680&scene=21#wechat_redirect)特征的一系列代码。随后这些代码经过严格的实验及比对，最终将符合条件的特征码整合在一起，[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)库（又名[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)库）由此诞生。

  

[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)扫描则是使用自身引擎反编译文件代码，并且代码内搜索匹配是否含有[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)库中的[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)偏移的位置，进而判断文件是否为[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127030&idx=2&sn=cb843183873e4e206b8160d2dfb98e5c&chksm=bc8598198bf2110f132c45befd4571d47b8059b9651cae0fc9e734c692f7ac022d62a2830928&scene=21#wechat_redirect)文件。在[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127156&idx=2&sn=47d8815448e2605609399f0ee3d59863&chksm=bc859b9b8bf2128d090a3ac47007b357d7037fd5858dc366dddb617de8dd30aded65b9c225c7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)与杀毒软件的斗争中，[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=2&sn=5b5d7e7a936eb7f7aec16bddd6c3f0b2&chksm=bc859b468bf2125084447624ecb6da59672b17585f652343747238fd1b3bcf35ded30d6fc680&scene=21#wechat_redirect)库显然无法及时更新跟上[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的开发步伐，同时也会因为[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127030&idx=2&sn=cb843183873e4e206b8160d2dfb98e5c&chksm=bc8598198bf2110f132c45befd4571d47b8059b9651cae0fc9e734c692f7ac022d62a2830928&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[加壳](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123600&idx=3&sn=75e03920427adf750a5c2dfbcc2ba584&chksm=bc86657f8bf1ec69a8b1315c0351d3bf3dbd07de5347ea5c5883a418c78ca0fbe421e5e9250d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、加花[免杀](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127072&idx=3&sn=171c03ea4f8581a39802ad3e03a0081a&chksm=bc859bcf8bf212d94dd4e98657882ca6a63c427f9694883dd4925acdcfbf6349fe873b5ca782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)从而难以扫描出符合的[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，虽然其后增加了复杂[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)扫描以及隐藏[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)扫描，但[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)扫描始终无法做到“快毒一步”，因此启发式扫描作为弥补[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)扫描的缺陷而被开发出来。

  

**2、启发式扫描**

  

启发式扫描分为静态启发式扫描与动态启发式扫描。静态启发式扫描以[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)扫描为基础，通过反[汇编](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124760&idx=3&sn=01e26b282af797472eb6a0c3155eede7&chksm=bc8660f78bf1e9e1c2436dc4d11fbcd5385e395c94d61dcdcb4c4f503840beaf9351feb434d2&scene=21#wechat_redirect)在不运行的条件下对目标文件进行特征指令扫描，如果其使用的指令组合与原先由专家经验分析得出的[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)指令（通常是带有[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127030&idx=2&sn=cb843183873e4e206b8160d2dfb98e5c&chksm=bc8598198bf2110f132c45befd4571d47b8059b9651cae0fc9e734c692f7ac022d62a2830928&scene=21#wechat_redirect)性质的API函数的组合）相似度高，就会被判断为[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序。动态启发式扫描则是结合杀毒软件内置的虚拟机技术（软件模拟CPU等仿真环境），将[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127030&idx=2&sn=cb843183873e4e206b8160d2dfb98e5c&chksm=bc8598198bf2110f132c45befd4571d47b8059b9651cae0fc9e734c692f7ac022d62a2830928&scene=21#wechat_redirect)动态运行在虚拟机中并检测其行为，若发现类似[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127156&idx=2&sn=47d8815448e2605609399f0ee3d59863&chksm=bc859b9b8bf2128d090a3ac47007b357d7037fd5858dc366dddb617de8dd30aded65b9c225c7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的可疑行为，则判定为[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=2&sn=5b5d7e7a936eb7f7aec16bddd6c3f0b2&chksm=bc859b468bf2125084447624ecb6da59672b17585f652343747238fd1b3bcf35ded30d6fc680&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序。

  

启发式扫描在未知[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的检测方面起到了关键性的作用，弥补了[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)扫描无法第一时间检测最新[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的缺陷，当然启发式扫描存在误报的情况，但很明显是利大于弊了。

  

**3、主动防御技术**

  

为了起到实时、主动防护[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127030&idx=2&sn=cb843183873e4e206b8160d2dfb98e5c&chksm=bc8598198bf2110f132c45befd4571d47b8059b9651cae0fc9e734c692f7ac022d62a2830928&scene=21#wechat_redirect)入侵的作用，主动防御技术打破了传统杀毒软件检测[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的思路。首先它在驱动内核层次加载自身，从而避免自身进程在用户层面被[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127156&idx=2&sn=47d8815448e2605609399f0ee3d59863&chksm=bc859b9b8bf2128d090a3ac47007b357d7037fd5858dc366dddb617de8dd30aded65b9c225c7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)终结；其次，就像键盘钩子HOOK，主动防御技术在[操作系统](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122728&idx=2&sn=6b263bf85d6b8bd8bf1d3744a491a40c&chksm=bc8668c78bf1e1d1a0e813fcd7b41c7bc300c2c98eb269b77f92854c62f1fe5127a0e01c5574&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中HOOK了一些[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=2&sn=5b5d7e7a936eb7f7aec16bddd6c3f0b2&chksm=bc859b468bf2125084447624ecb6da59672b17585f652343747238fd1b3bcf35ded30d6fc680&scene=21#wechat_redirect)常用的API函数，每当有程序使用这些API函数的时候，它会根据程序使用的API函数以及伴随这些API函数做出的行为推测该程序是否为[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，进而做到实时监控。

  

主动防御技术避免了用户在不知情的情况下运行[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127030&idx=2&sn=cb843183873e4e206b8160d2dfb98e5c&chksm=bc8598198bf2110f132c45befd4571d47b8059b9651cae0fc9e734c692f7ac022d62a2830928&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，并且对未知[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127156&idx=2&sn=47d8815448e2605609399f0ee3d59863&chksm=bc859b9b8bf2128d090a3ac47007b357d7037fd5858dc366dddb617de8dd30aded65b9c225c7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)也起到了一定的防范作用。同时，杀毒软件在主动防御技术中也增添了[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126078&idx=3&sn=d35caf044cb5875a19b1b28ff13eb559&chksm=bc859fd18bf216c7526229d54675da8b4adc6ac9998f7dff265d1b503ae114cbc0a549fc64dc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)实时修补功能，杜绝了[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127156&idx=2&sn=47d8815448e2605609399f0ee3d59863&chksm=bc859b9b8bf2128d090a3ac47007b357d7037fd5858dc366dddb617de8dd30aded65b9c225c7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)通过[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126078&idx=3&sn=d35caf044cb5875a19b1b28ff13eb559&chksm=bc859fd18bf216c7526229d54675da8b4adc6ac9998f7dff265d1b503ae114cbc0a549fc64dc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)入侵计算机。

  

**4、云查杀**

  

由于传统杀毒方法的[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=2&sn=5b5d7e7a936eb7f7aec16bddd6c3f0b2&chksm=bc859b468bf2125084447624ecb6da59672b17585f652343747238fd1b3bcf35ded30d6fc680&scene=21#wechat_redirect)库巨大以及杀毒内存开销大等缺点，云查杀将成为未来杀毒软件的主流趋势。云查杀将[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)库存于服务器端，在查杀时通过安全公司的“云计算”手段，实现了在短时间内迅速查杀以及云端[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)库快速更新，但其缺点是需要保证一个畅通无阻的网络环境，并且有隐私泄露的危险。
