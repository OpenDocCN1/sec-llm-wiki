本文用调试API针对CrackMe来编写一个显示[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126686&idx=3&sn=c588c739341ee07b2b1d6a84c5e1a6ec&chksm=bc8599718bf210670bfe501723741133e318ca7abcc697f1d1fd5236a9490f3bd6b555342dd9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的程序。

  

在编写关于CrackMe的[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126686&idx=3&sn=c588c739341ee07b2b1d6a84c5e1a6ec&chksm=bc8599718bf210670bfe501723741133e318ca7abcc697f1d1fd5236a9490f3bd6b555342dd9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)显示程序以前需要准备两项工作，第一个工作是知道要在什么地方合理地下断点，第二个工作是从哪里能读取到[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126686&idx=3&sn=c588c739341ee07b2b1d6a84c5e1a6ec&chksm=bc8599718bf210670bfe501723741133e318ca7abcc697f1d1fd5236a9490f3bd6b555342dd9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。带着这两个问题重新来进行思考一下。在我们的程序中，要对两个字符串进行比较，而比较的函数是strcmp()，该函数有两个参数，分别是输入的[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126686&idx=3&sn=c588c739341ee07b2b1d6a84c5e1a6ec&chksm=bc8599718bf210670bfe501723741133e318ca7abcc697f1d1fd5236a9490f3bd6b555342dd9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和真正的[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126686&idx=3&sn=c588c739341ee07b2b1d6a84c5e1a6ec&chksm=bc8599718bf210670bfe501723741133e318ca7abcc697f1d1fd5236a9490f3bd6b555342dd9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。也就是说在调用strcmp()这个函数的位置下断点通过查看它的参数是可以获取到正确的[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126686&idx=3&sn=c588c739341ee07b2b1d6a84c5e1a6ec&chksm=bc8599718bf210670bfe501723741133e318ca7abcc697f1d1fd5236a9490f3bd6b555342dd9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的。就在调用strcmp()这个函数的位置设置INT3断点，也就是将0xCC机器码写入这个地址。用OD看一下调用strcmp()函数的地址，如图1所示。

![](../Images/36af7eaccb0ce6d2f90ba3e9936029a3.png)

图1  调用strcmp()函数的地址

从图1中可以看出，调用strcmp()函数的地址为004024CE。有了这个地址只要找到该函数的两个参数，就可以找到输入的错误的[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126686&idx=3&sn=c588c739341ee07b2b1d6a84c5e1a6ec&chksm=bc8599718bf210670bfe501723741133e318ca7abcc697f1d1fd5236a9490f3bd6b555342dd9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)及正确的[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126686&idx=3&sn=c588c739341ee07b2b1d6a84c5e1a6ec&chksm=bc8599718bf210670bfe501723741133e318ca7abcc697f1d1fd5236a9490f3bd6b555342dd9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。正确的[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126686&idx=3&sn=c588c739341ee07b2b1d6a84c5e1a6ec&chksm=bc8599718bf210670bfe501723741133e318ca7abcc697f1d1fd5236a9490f3bd6b555342dd9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的起始地址保存在EDX中，错误的[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126686&idx=3&sn=c588c739341ee07b2b1d6a84c5e1a6ec&chksm=bc8599718bf210670bfe501723741133e318ca7abcc697f1d1fd5236a9490f3bd6b555342dd9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的起始地址保存在ECX中，只要在004024CE这个地址下断，并通过线程环境读取EDX和ECX寄存器值就可以得到两个[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126686&idx=3&sn=c588c739341ee07b2b1d6a84c5e1a6ec&chksm=bc8599718bf210670bfe501723741133e318ca7abcc697f1d1fd5236a9490f3bd6b555342dd9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的起始地址。
