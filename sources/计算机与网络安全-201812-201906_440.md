一套优秀的反[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)定位策略可以将大部分骇客挡在外面，因此反[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)定位应该是每个反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品的必备功能，但实际上并非如此。

  

下面从基本原理及思想上粗略阐述一下反[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)定位的相应策略。

  

**一、释放干扰码**

  

所谓的释放干扰码就是指隐藏真正的[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)并随机释放一个假的[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，这样做可以很容易地将正在执行[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)定位的骇客引向其他方向，使其永远也定位不出真正的[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

释放干扰码需要有两个前提：一个是一定要能判断骇客的定位行为；另一个就是要确定此文件确实是恶意程序。

  

判定骇客[免杀](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123875&idx=2&sn=13c3a97389656175c280e4b327d56601&chksm=bc86644c8bf1ed5aa41eb0db082759183aed97e81fcad56c9892e1552a0efa70220c02665273&scene=21#wechat_redirect)行为其实很容易，国内某反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件的策略就是检查文件中的0x00区域的大小及分布规律，并由此判断当前反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件是否正在受到[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)定位攻击。由于骇客们在定位[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)时总体上来讲都是使用二分法查找[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的，这种查找算法有些类似于二叉树，因此它的填充规律是有一定特点的。

  

另外，释放干扰码的前提是要求反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件要有一个缓存机制，也就是要能将部分历史扫描细节保存下来，并将这次扫描细节与历史扫描结果相比对，只有这样才能根据两次或多次扫描的变化来判断当前反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件是否正在受到[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)定位攻击。

  

释放干扰码的策略有两种：一种是以干扰为目的，也就是[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)每隔一段时间变换一次，使得骇客们无法用常规方式找到[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)；另一种是以欺骗为目的，也就是在本机中此干扰码会被存储到临时[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)库中，因此只针对本机的反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件有效，这个恶意软件即便是在本机完成[免杀](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123875&idx=2&sn=13c3a97389656175c280e4b327d56601&chksm=bc86644c8bf1ed5aa41eb0db082759183aed97e81fcad56c9892e1552a0efa70220c02665273&scene=21#wechat_redirect)操作，到其他的计算机上运行后仍然会被查杀。

**二、定位行为的判定**

  

一般情况下，我们可以根据以下几点来检查文件特征，进而判定反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件是否正在遭受[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)定位攻击。

  

> 文件数量：每次扫描文件的数量最多不会超过5万个，而且每次扫描都呈递减趋势。
> 
>   
> 
> 文件类型：每次扫描的文件100%都是可执行文件（或是同一种类型文件）。
> 
>   
> 
> 文件大小：每次扫描的所有可执行文件的体积都是一样的。
> 
>   
> 
> 文件异同：由于特征码定位时每个文件被填充的位置都不同，但是每个文件未填充的总区域大小都是一样的，因此每个文件的异同率都是一样的。

  

通过以上几点进行综合判定，一般情况下即可分辨出当前反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件是否正在遭受[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)定位攻击，进而作出下一步的应对策略。

  

**三、设定“靶特征码”**

  

所谓的“靶[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”就是专门用来被[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)定位攻击的一个[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，这个[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)应该尽可能以无限接近100%的几率与其他的某个[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)同时出现在某一个文件中。

  

由于骇客们定位[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)时是分段破坏的，在整个[免杀](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123875&idx=2&sn=13c3a97389656175c280e4b327d56601&chksm=bc86644c8bf1ed5aa41eb0db082759183aed97e81fcad56c9892e1552a0efa70220c02665273&scene=21#wechat_redirect)操作过程中“靶[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”与真实[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)始终同时被破坏的几率非常小，因此如果我们的反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件中有“靶[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”，就可以通过判定“靶[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”和与其配对的[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是否全部存在，来检测当前反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=3&sn=af9c601966e87b775c0e27318ff319a8&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27f809083f6f0c6f5151b4f5f6fab96eb040793f31f391dafe3a14d57cc7b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件是否正在受到[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)定位攻击，这样会节省大部分系统资源。
