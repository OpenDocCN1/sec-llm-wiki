在[IPSec VPN](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117516&idx=1&sn=28f0c7df31da4f48eeab81c73c6485a8&chksm=bc867d238bf1f435936a414c0d6a78d81a775be2b2fa5459f2c449383073152bc02e2472cb8e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的应用方案配置中，无论采用哪种具体的[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道建立方式，都主要存在两种可能的故障情形：一是[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道建立不成功，二是虽然[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道建立成功了，但两端仍不能通信。本文仅针对基于ACL方式的手工建立[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道的配置方案中，以上两种典型故障介绍具体的排除思路。

  

**一、IPSec隧道建立不成功的故障排除**

  

这种故障现象最常见，但由于在手工方式建立[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道的配置方案中，所有用于建立最终的[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect) SA参数都是手工配置的，所以如果一切按照要求配置了，[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道是肯定可以建立起来的。下面介绍这种故障的排除方法。

  

**1、两端或其中一端没有成功接入Internet**

  

因为[IPSec VPN](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117516&idx=1&sn=28f0c7df31da4f48eeab81c73c6485a8&chksm=bc867d238bf1f435936a414c0d6a78d81a775be2b2fa5459f2c449383073152bc02e2472cb8e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)通常是在公网——Internet上构建虚拟隧道来实现远程网络连接的，所以其前提就是两端必须已成功接入到了Internet，并且[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道两端所连接的公网、私网路由都是通的。

  

排除方法是首先从一端内网主机ping另一端[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)设备的公网侧接口IP地址，能ping通则证明两端都已成功接入Internet，且两端的路由配置也是正确的，否则检查Internet接入和两端到达对端公网、私网的路由配置（包括源/目的主机的网关配置）。

  

**2、两端的IPSec配置不正确**

  

排除了线路和路由的问题后，再来检查两端的[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)配置，这里最容易出问题，因为有许多地方在配置时要特别注意。以下任何其中一条配置不符合要求都可能造成[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect) SA建立不了，也就使[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道建立不成功。

  

（1）看两端的[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)安全提议配置是否一致

  

可在两端的[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)设备上执行display ipsec proposal命令（输出类似如下所示），要求两端最终显示出的封装模式（Encapsulation mode）、所使用的安全协议（Transform），以及所选安全协议所使用的认证或[加密算法](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122060&idx=2&sn=9783dd961efb288c95e65fa5a895a3aa&chksm=bc866f638bf1e6751c94279fc53bbc81e569177fd0442bd1f816a16c15b42f44708e627ed7de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)（加密算法仅在选择ESP协议时有）配置必须完全一致。[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)安全提议名称（IPsec proposal name）和序号（Number of proposals）可不一样。

  

> <> display ipsec proposal
> 
> Number of proposals: 1
> 
> IPsec proposal name: pro1
> 
> Encapsulation mode: Tunnel
> 
> Transform　　　　:esp-new
> 
> ESPprotocol　　 :Authentication SHA1-HMAC-96
> 
> Encryption　　DES

  

不同产品不同版本的[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)安全提议中的[认证算法](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=2&sn=86e6005447386ba0be0e422570c3ffe6&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27ce011700752a395d56a3f2b866fc19ba243e5b1c8d2bd2db0c3ff98ab1d9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和[加密算法](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122060&idx=2&sn=9783dd961efb288c95e65fa5a895a3aa&chksm=bc866f638bf1e6751c94279fc53bbc81e569177fd0442bd1f816a16c15b42f44708e627ed7de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的缺省值是不一样的，要认真核查两端设备运行的VRP系统版本，确保两端的[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)安全提议参数配置一致。

  

另外，站点到站点的连接必须使用隧道（Tunnel）封装模式，而不能使用传输（Transport）封装模式。在手工方式建立[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道的配置方案中，[认证算法](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125214&idx=2&sn=86e6005447386ba0be0e422570c3ffe6&chksm=bc8663318bf1ea27ce011700752a395d56a3f2b866fc19ba243e5b1c8d2bd2db0c3ff98ab1d9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)也不能采用SM3算法。

  

（2）安全策略中的配置没按要求配置成镜像，或保持一致

  

在手工方式建立[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道时，安全参数必须手工一条条配置，而且大多数配置两端必须是镜像配置（但所选安全协议必须一致，安全策略名称和序号两端可以不一致），如一端的本端/对端IP地址必须与对端配置的对端/本端IP地址对应一致，SA出/入方面的SPI、密钥参数配置也是一样的。

  

另外，隧道两端的IP地址配置必须是[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)设备连接公网侧接口的公网IP地址，出/入方向SA的SPI值不能设置成相同值，即不同的SA必须对应于不同的SPI。

（3）在安全策略中没有引入所配置的[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)安全提议

  

在手工方式建立[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道中的安全策略配置中，必须要引用所创建的[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)安全提议（[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)安全提议也必须先创建好，因为缺省情况下，系统没有配置[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)安全提议，但各项参数配置可以直接采用缺省配置），否则[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect) SA也是无法建立成功的。可在[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)设备上执行display ipsec proposal命令查看所配置的[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)安全提议信息，如果显示为空，或者没见到你在安全策略中所引用的安全提议，则要重新配置了。

  

（4）两端定义的需要保护数据流的不匹配

  

要使两端能成功建立SA，两端有ACL配置最好是镜像的，也就是两端所配置的源/目的IP地址等信息是直接互换的。当对等体间ACL规则非镜像配置时，仅当协商发起方的ACL规则定义的范围小于响应方ACL规定定义的范围时， SA才能成功建立。

  

可以通过在两端[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)设备上进入对应的ACL视图后再执行display this命令查看其中各条ACL规则的配置，看是否符合以上要求。还要注意的是，只有与permit规则匹配的数据流才会进入到[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect) 隧道被[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保护。

  

（5）安全策略应用的接口错误，或者接口有问题

  

在手工方式建立[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道方案中，安全策略通常都是在[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)设备连接公网的物理接口上应用的，但这个接口必须是三层接口，且配置了IP地址，当然还必须是已是Up状态的。如有疑问可在对应设备上执行display interface xxxx（xxxx代表接口类型的编号）查看。

  

**二、IPSec隧道建立成功，但两端仍不能通信的故障排除**

  

在手工方式建立[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道中其实很难确定[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道是否建立成功，因为参数都是手工配置的，通过执行display ipsec sa命令所能查看到的都是手工配置的这些参数，包括策略应用的接口、[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)安全策略（包括各项已配置的参数值或所采用的缺省值），以及在安全策略中所引用的[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)安全提议。

如果出现两端不能通信时仍可以从以下两方面来考虑。

  

（1）两端私网通信的数据流没有进入[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道

  

这主要是因为两端所创建的用于定义需要保护数据流的高ACL配置不正确，没有把两端所连接的私网IP网段的数据流允许进入[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隧道，这时这些数据流就会通过Internet来转发了，自然到达不到对端的私网。

  

（2）两端[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)设备上所配置的到达对端公网、私网路由不正确

  

许多人喜欢用缺省路由来指定一端到达对端的静态路由，这是很不合理的。因为如果你没有为数据指定明确路由的话，所有进入设备的数据包都将采用这一条缺省路由来转发，不管是从哪个接口进来的数据包。这样会导致[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)设备上本来是从对端接收的数据包又可能被从指定的缺省路由转发出去了，这时自然就不通了。所以建议为到达对端公网、私网配置明确的路由。

  

另外，两端主机的网关配置也要正确，否则发到对端的数据就不能正确地转发到[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)设备上，再由[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)设备转发到目的主机了。

  

如果源或目的主机不是直接与[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)设备的LAN接口在同一IP网段（即中间隔了其他三层设备），这时就需要在[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)设备和其他三层设备上配置好路由，使得来自源主机IP数据包在到达对端[IPSec](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123043&idx=2&sn=b51aa0717f5554d7f330fc3b16ba4d58&chksm=bc866b8c8bf1e29a5144f5fe3a12db3ed7d5bcaec12843deefadf075a3567d4b75706be9765c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)设备时能正确通过路由转发到目的主机上。
