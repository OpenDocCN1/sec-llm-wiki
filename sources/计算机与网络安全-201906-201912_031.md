微信公众号：计算机与网络安全

ID：Computer-network

  

海外[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131366&idx=2&sn=18215226672d73083f399efc8e61ab76&chksm=bc858b098bf2021f4ba79a264088a3761321619396e24fc817ec636744973de380bacdcd88b6&scene=21#wechat_redirect)事故频发，也引起了国内对于[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131340&idx=2&sn=f0a410fd4ac4d8564cf40c2f1284a3bf&chksm=bc858b238bf20235f42eff940d48927ae2b76820b953edb729e99bb5c9cd4d7938554025f20a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)重视。[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132059&idx=2&sn=59415d501c548f6795570f9b6b4f114c&chksm=bc8584748bf20d624ec9a84be5f53cef4a206290202e01409ebe0bd44d93064a41e28cbc18ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)行业也获得更多的发展和机会。

  

网络上，有一群人被称为“白帽子”。他们通过自己专业能力，识别[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统或网络系统中的安全[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，但并不会恶意去利用，而是通知企业、厂商及时修复，避免公众利益损失。而未修复前，绝不公开，这是白帽子们自觉维护一种较为广泛接受的文化和道德，维持网络的安全有序。

  

专家介绍，这些大家广为接受的机制包括：技术[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的上报通报机制、技术比武大会的保密机制、悬赏[漏洞挖掘](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133432&idx=3&sn=53e40327ee43f6b25613608d21172017&chksm=bc8583178bf20a011e1dfa4217c756e6aac20c8bcf3f04ca1442270c2e25cb546278c5ab6867&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的激励机制、有偿[漏洞挖掘](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133432&idx=3&sn=53e40327ee43f6b25613608d21172017&chksm=bc8583178bf20a011e1dfa4217c756e6aac20c8bcf3f04ca1442270c2e25cb546278c5ab6867&scene=21#wechat_redirect)服务的协议机制、白帽子论坛的适度文化。

  

简单来说，白帽子们，发现[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)后，会及时通知给涉及企业、厂家，在他们修复[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)前，不会擅自公开[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，以免造成公众利益损失。

虽然有竞争关系，但在安全面前公众利益第一

  

然而，在利益驱动下，有个别组织和个人违反了约定俗成的“行规”，破坏安全市场整体秩序的行为，严重的甚至危害到社会公众的利益。例如：

  

● 未将[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上报及通知相关厂商，即公开进行产品破解；

  

● 无现实危害发生时，模拟[网络攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)并滥发预警信息；

  

● 在厂商还未完成[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)修复时，披露[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)利用技术细节和受影响的用户[隐私](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127519&idx=3&sn=a846e840b4ac021e01ba4a64c9228817&chksm=bc859a308bf2132693545d19fdb92d481e63a9d92d18960c1e4c198747b628d2bad763105175&scene=21#wechat_redirect)数据；  

  

● 以公开发布会的方式公开竞争对手的安全[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)或系统风险，打击竞争对手产品的安全性，意图阻碍竞争对手业务发展；

  

● 为显示能力，发布公开[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、勒索软件等[恶意程序](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127399&idx=2&sn=e7605669abc54947803b3cd1b19aa9db&chksm=bc859a888bf2139e9e8f91b9db4e21e52039157964132cbbf79d41504f8deff65826665e4a4b&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的源[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和制作方法；

  

● 夸大评价某些[网络安全攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、事件、风险、脆弱性状况，引发恐慌；

  

● 公开其它网络运营者的重要技术信息如系统架构、网络规划设计、拓扑结构、资产信息、软件[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)等。

专家认为，上述行为的目的已偏离了正常的技术交流，其本质属于不正当的市场竞争行为，严重损害了安全技术领域的原有秩序和文化，甚而进一步危及到整个国家的[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129537&idx=3&sn=1c8a2b85ec3a063ce7a132992d32a3be&chksm=bc85922e8bf21b3810cf89c2dba2b7041b8a93bf3df7d28e4ac4a87791862e41bf791bc21304&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

针对这一行业乱象，国家[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)信息办公室近日发布[《网络安全威胁信息发布管理办法（征求意见稿）》](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134103&idx=3&sn=a5734591782a5cd7efe404ea8e2d3783&chksm=bc85bc788bf2356eddc8d99172ed488b9b0e0bde0384f2cd98577dced997c75e0ab77dcdcacd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，明确规定不得利用[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127174&idx=3&sn=b0c84359114c49067f3728971b0f3233&chksm=bc859b698bf2127f130740c2ede1cf2f75fdf7c7d2dcaa4200f1055d9094c6b6d81b03b36fbc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)威胁信息进行炒作或从事不正当竞争，要求坚持“客观、真实、审慎、负责”的原则发布[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126536&idx=2&sn=39c4220685f09a248d786269d734f524&chksm=bc8599e78bf210f1dc14c61a61a3df29707dc40e245ea220752d07d28b9aeb3b63631678ef3c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)威胁信息。办法明确，报刊、广播电视、出版物、互联网站、论坛、博客、微博、公众账号、即时通信工具、[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)直播、[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)视听节目、应用程序、网络硬盘等、公开举行的会议、论坛、讲座、公开举办的[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124760&idx=2&sn=96fb5d7fa9e05ea83b59cd7329fb4736&chksm=bc8660f78bf1e9e1acbd641806329c0233f135b238ef7a3395117a7aa455c2c59ffa555202eb&scene=21#wechat_redirect)竞赛等作为信息发布平台，在发现违法发布行为和发布内容时，也应立即停止发布、采取消除等处置措施。

[《网络安全威胁信息发布管理办法（征求意见稿）》](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134103&idx=3&sn=a5734591782a5cd7efe404ea8e2d3783&chksm=bc85bc788bf2356eddc8d99172ed488b9b0e0bde0384f2cd98577dced997c75e0ab77dcdcacd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)对可能威胁网络正常运行的行为，用于描述其意图、方法、工具、过程、结果等的信息（如[网络攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、[网络安全事件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132557&idx=3&sn=0dab660075e0f09f21efe4a6675678e2&chksm=bc8586628bf20f74f027354e810c343911c4dc5e86f13275475aa5828ed08e375befe66c81b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)等）和可能暴露网络脆弱性的信息（如系统[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、网络规划设计）的发布进行了全面规范，从内容管理、前置上报及平台处置等角度进行管控。

  

此前，工业和信息化部曾在今年6月发布[《网络安全漏洞管理规定（征求意见稿）》](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131135&idx=3&sn=b0205bdd6a8be3665c0aa3195ba5e8d8&chksm=bc8588108bf20106ad2cb847d044a05f3fd6e35ed91decea5b2923e93e1d96fbdb252a815b25&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，要求第三方组织或个人通过网站、媒体、会议等方式向社会发布[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)信息的行为遵循“必要、真实、客观、有利于防范和应对[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126563&idx=3&sn=14023df84f67afb48342aae5f5fe71f7&chksm=bc8599cc8bf210da3250510eb9d50c84270e15141f1eac9d3f98a726114f5d3e51263495ecf9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)风险”的原则，不得在发布[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)修补或防范措施之前发布相关[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)信息、不得刻意夸大[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的危害和风险、不得发布和提供专门用于利用[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)从事危害[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655120585&idx=2&sn=9622d3fd3041997ec13b8f8e385a717d&chksm=bc8671268bf1f830b295b492d4537709131dec78eb7e709d8a6b7d4bcbafd155c70245f182fb&scene=21#wechat_redirect)活动的方法、程序和工具，并同步发布[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=21
