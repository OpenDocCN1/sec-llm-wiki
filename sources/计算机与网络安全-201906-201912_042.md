 

浙江省公安机关在“净网2019”专项行动中，针对非法生产、销售、使用联网型针孔[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的相关线索深挖经营，成功查明了一起从设计制作专用芯片、搭建[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)控制平台，到加工组装伪装配件，并线上线下同步销售针孔[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的非法制售利益链条。

  

在侦查过程中，警方发现，制成的微型[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)大多安装在像[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、时钟、水杯这些商品内，原本几十元的普通商品立马升值成几百元的[偷拍](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127790&idx=3&sn=62a5ac394be74c4bec137904ff6082c0&chksm=bc8595018bf21c172946998a5ba5b6a55c949786779346d17ce973b64abb4bca540f7e16f154&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工具，再经各批发商代理，通过[微信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127698&idx=3&sn=5101f6a80628d7caf6bc8ed7eec325c5&chksm=bc85957d8bf21c6bad5b83f1dcf2124043fa2e6a19eee9f604e23db0211b7ddcf8d94ecf97b8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、网商平台等渠道对外销售。

  

记者仔细比对后发现，嫌疑人在[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上打孔安装了[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)后，还特意在这个孔洞上方标注了字母T伪装成某种指示灯，非专业人士根本不会注意到这个多出来的小孔后却隐藏着偷窥的眼睛。 

  

除了无线[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和时钟，常被改造伪装的日常用品还有烟雾报警器、电源开关插座等屋内常备装置。此外，便携手持的常用设备，如充电宝、手表、打火机，甚至饮水杯，也常被改造成[偷拍](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127790&idx=3&sn=62a5ac394be74c4bec137904ff6082c0&chksm=bc8595018bf21c172946998a5ba5b6a55c949786779346d17ce973b64abb4bca540f7e16f154&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工具。 

  

此次净网专项行动，浙江省公安部门历经2个月的侦查追捕，现已抓获并刑拘非法生产、销售窃照专用器材犯罪人员26名，查扣针孔[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)成品及半成品5000多个。

  

从这些改造伪装的[偷拍](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127790&idx=3&sn=62a5ac394be74c4bec137904ff6082c0&chksm=bc8595018bf21c172946998a5ba5b6a55c949786779346d17ce973b64abb4bca540f7e16f154&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工具不难发现，利用这一类最常见、不起眼的日常物品进行改装就是不想被发现。那么这些伪装后的偷窥偷拍设备都被安装在哪里，又是被用来做什么呢？

  

据警方查实，杭州某男子在对外出租的房屋内安装针孔[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，长期窥探租客的生活[隐私](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127519&idx=3&sn=a846e840b4ac021e01ba4a64c9228817&chksm=bc859a308bf2132693545d19fdb92d481e63a9d92d18960c1e4c198747b628d2bad763105175&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

福州某男子借住宿之机，先后在28家宾馆偷偷安装[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，偷拍录制了其他住宿客人多达180G的[隐私](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127519&idx=3&sn=a846e840b4ac021e01ba4a64c9228817&chksm=bc859a308bf2132693545d19fdb92d481e63a9d92d18960c1e4c198747b628d2bad763105175&scene=21#wechat_redirect)视频。 

  

宁波一男子使用伪装在球鞋上的针孔[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，在车站、商场等公共场所用于[偷拍](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127790&idx=3&sn=62a5ac394be74c4bec137904ff6082c0&chksm=bc8595018bf21c172946998a5ba5b6a55c949786779346d17ce973b64abb4bca540f7e16f154&scene=21#wechat_redirect)女性裙底，共累计[偷拍](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127790&idx=3&sn=62a5ac394be74c4bec137904ff6082c0&chksm=bc8595018bf21c172946998a5ba5b6a55c949786779346d17ce973b64abb4bca540f7e16f154&scene=21#wechat_redirect)视频90多部，并准备将这些视频上传网络牟利。 

  

浙江警方通过追踪针孔[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)非法使用者，目前已治安拘留偷窥他人[隐私](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127519&idx=3&sn=a846e840b4ac021e01ba4a64c9228817&chksm=bc859a308bf2132693545d19fdb92d481e63a9d92d18960c1e4c198747b628d2bad763105175&scene=21#wechat_redirect)人员20名，发现其他违法犯罪线索13条，治安拘留卖淫嫖娼等违法人员25名。
