近日，谷歌、三星等制造商生产的安卓手机，被曝出系统存在严重安全[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=3&sn=c82f59076adf4123590301c9577f3c59&chksm=bc859b348bf21222ef281bc9aebb654a06582273fd7d9eb73ab84b56adbf383f74305efe3c3c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)能够在未经手机机主许可的情况下，操控安卓手机秘密拍摄[照片](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128912&idx=3&sn=ba2c0b428e17d9fec29608d96e86984d&chksm=bc8590bf8bf219a9fdc650848d21a1b5f4facbf61cd091ebc242a3b9f38d3dbe62290b7fe129&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、录制音视频并上传。

  

据今日俄罗斯网站报道，研究人员发现，由于安卓系统存在严重的安全[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，某些“恶意应用程序”能够在未经机主许可、不被机主发现的情况下使用手机[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和麦克风。

  

测试发现，即使手机屏幕或相关应用软件处于关闭状态，[恶意程序](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127399&idx=2&sn=e7605669abc54947803b3cd1b19aa9db&chksm=bc859a888bf2139e9e8f91b9db4e21e52039157964132cbbf79d41504f8deff65826665e4a4b&scene=21#wechat_redirect)也可以启动相机和麦克风拍摄[照片](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128912&idx=3&sn=ba2c0b428e17d9fec29608d96e86984d&chksm=bc8590bf8bf219a9fdc650848d21a1b5f4facbf61cd091ebc242a3b9f38d3dbe62290b7fe129&scene=21#wechat_redirect)或录制音视频和电话通话。此外，这些[恶意程序](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127399&idx=2&sn=e7605669abc54947803b3cd1b19aa9db&chksm=bc859a888bf2139e9e8f91b9db4e21e52039157964132cbbf79d41504f8deff65826665e4a4b&scene=21#wechat_redirect)还可以访问[照片](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128912&idx=3&sn=ba2c0b428e17d9fec29608d96e86984d&chksm=bc8590bf8bf219a9fdc650848d21a1b5f4facbf61cd091ebc242a3b9f38d3dbe62290b7fe129&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中的位置数据，并上传到[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

系统安全[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)还允许[恶意程序](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127399&idx=2&sn=e7605669abc54947803b3cd1b19aa9db&chksm=bc859a888bf2139e9e8f91b9db4e21e52039157964132cbbf79d41504f8deff65826665e4a4b&scene=21#wechat_redirect)消除相机快门的声音并访问手机的距离传感器。距离传感器的作用是当感应到有大面积物体接近手机屏幕时，自动进入黑屏，使触屏等功能暂时失效。

  

研究人员表示，虽然在手机遭到[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)时，手机屏幕会在录制视频或拍照时显示[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)标识，可一旦[恶意程序](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127399&idx=2&sn=e7605669abc54947803b3cd1b19aa9db&chksm=bc859a888bf2139e9e8f91b9db4e21e52039157964132cbbf79d41504f8deff65826665e4a4b&scene=21#wechat_redirect)访问距离传感器，[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127290&idx=3&sn=04aaac7d1e20fd446929032cb45b6b38&chksm=bc859b158bf212032e5e2121f6066cd1ec171afc141be62e32ad671879a403ade7b3435495bb&scene=21#wechat_redirect)便可以在机主接打电话，或是手机屏幕朝下时再发动[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，这样就能在机主无法察觉的情况下窃取图片以及音视频了。
