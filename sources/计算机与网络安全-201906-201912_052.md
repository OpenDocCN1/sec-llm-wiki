1988年11月2日17时1分59秒，[美国](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128142&idx=3&sn=7b8de69bbda3be653df725fdec9fc24a&chksm=bc8597a18bf21eb7f39f365987d171669e5868d0a43659b4fa35b9053cfc346b30c80e7c5e8a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)康奈尔大学的[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)科学系研究生，23岁的莫里斯（Morris）将其编写的蠕虫程序输入[计算机网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132497&idx=2&sn=142429c138b601edbc0eaae618f1ef14&chksm=bc8586be8bf20fa8c4303f7f7e30b0313d2d9bff51f9da91326fd3c829473bffcc0ac770d5d9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，这个网络连接着大学、研究机关的155000多台[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，在几小时内导致了[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)堵塞。这件事就像是[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)界的一次大地震，产生了巨大反响，震惊了全世界，引起了人们对[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的恐慌，也使更多的[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)专家重视和致力于[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c3  

随着[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的日益普及，[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)已经成为了当今信息社会的一大顽症，借助于[计算机网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132497&idx=2&sn=142429c138b601edbc0eaae618f1ef14&chksm=bc8586be8bf20fa8c4303f7f7e30b0313d2d9bff51f9da91326fd3c829473bffcc0ac770d5d9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)可以传播到[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)世界的每一个角落，并大肆破坏[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)数据、更改操作程序、干扰正常显示、摧毁系统，甚至对硬件系统都能产生一定的破坏作用。[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的侵袭，会使[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统速度降低、运行失常、可靠性降低，有的系统被破坏后可能无法工作。从第一个[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)问世以来，在世界范围内由于一些致命[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，已经消耗了[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)用户大量的人力和财力，甚至对人们正常工作、企业正常生产以及国家的安全都造成了巨大的影响。因此，网络防[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术已成为[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131366&idx=2&sn=18215226672d73083f399efc8e61ab76&chksm=bc858b098bf2021f4ba79a264088a3761321619396e24fc817ec636744973de380bacdcd88b6&scene=21#wechat_redirect)研究的重要课题。

  

**1、计算机病毒**

  

**（1）计算机病毒的定义**

  

[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)借用了生物[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的概念。众所周知，生物[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是能侵入人体和其他生物体内的病原体，并能在人群及生物群体中传播，潜入人体或生物体内的细胞后就会大量繁殖与其本身相仿的复制品，这些复制品又去感染其他健康的细胞，造成[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进一步扩散。[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和生物病毒一样，是一种能侵入[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统和网络、危害其正常工作的“病原体”，能够对[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统进行各种破坏，同时能自我复制，具有传染性和潜伏性。

  

早在1949年，[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的先驱者[冯·诺依曼](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655113537&idx=2&sn=35c0037dd251a582bd2bd26c702b1b6c&chksm=bc864cae8bf1c5b8579e819e61293acd2a0c07361a82e8a2519d23dab17c3bf0ecd5d25ae9ef&scene=21#wechat_redirect)（Von Neumann）在一篇名为《复杂自动装置的理论及组织的进行》的论文中就已勾画出了[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序的蓝图：[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)实际上就是一种可以自我复制、传播的具有一定破坏性或干扰性的[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序，或是一段可执行的程序[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)可以把自己附着在各种类型的正常文件中，使用户很难察觉和根除。

 

**（2）计算机病毒的特点**

  

无论是哪一种[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，都是人为制造的、具有一定破坏性的程序，有别于医学上所说的传染[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)（[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)不会传染给人），然而，两者又有一些相似的地方。[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)具有以下特征。

  

**传染性。**传染性是[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)最基本的特征。在生物界，[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)通过传染从一个生物体扩散到另一个生物体。在适当的条件下，可得到大量繁殖，并使被感染的生物体表现出病症甚至死亡。同样，[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)也会通过各种渠道从已被感染的[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)扩散到未被感染的[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，在某些情况下造成被感染的[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工作失常甚至瘫痪。与生物[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)不同的是，[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是一段人为编制的[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，这段程序[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)一旦进入[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)并得以执行，就会搜寻其他符合其传染条件的程序或存储介质，确定目标后再将自身[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)插入其中，达到自我繁殖的目的。一台[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)染毒，如果不及时处理，[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)就会在这台机器上迅速扩散，其中的大量文件（一般是可执行文件）会被感染。而被感染的文件又成了新的传染源，再与其他机器进行[数据交换](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132640&idx=2&sn=e0954d46598a9843960629ed35cd09fe&chksm=bc85860f8bf20f191b39c13121eee685fd040dbfebb58997240e496106a10685a742c044aa80&scene=21#wechat_redirect)或通过网络接触，[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&sce  

**隐蔽性。**[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)往往是短小精悍的程序，非常容易隐藏在可执行程序或数据文件当中。当用户运行正常程序时，[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)伺机窃取到系统控制权，限制正常程序的执行，而这些对于用户来说都是未知的。不经过[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)分析，[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序和普通程序是不容易区分开的。正是由于[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序的隐蔽性才使其在发现之前已进行了广泛的传播，造成较大的破坏。

  

**潜伏性。**[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的潜伏性是指[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)具有依附于其他媒体而寄生的能力。一个编制精巧的[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序进入系统之后一般不会马上发作，可以在几周或者几个月内，甚至几年内隐藏在合法文件中，传染其他系统，而不被人发现。例如，在每年4月26日发作的CIH[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、每逢13号的星期五发作的“黑色星期五”[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)等。[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的潜伏性越好，其在系统中的存在时间就会越长，[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的传染范围就会越大。潜伏性的第一种表现是：[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序不用专用检测程序是检查不出来的，因此[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)可以静静地在磁盘或磁带里躲上几天，甚至几年，一旦时机成熟，得到运行机会，就又要四处繁殖、扩散，继续为害。潜伏性的第二种表现是：[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的内部往往有一种触发机制，不满足触发条件时，[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)除了传染外不进行什么破坏。触发条件一旦得到满足，有的在屏幕上显示信息、图形或特殊标识，有的则执行破坏系统的操作，如格式化磁盘、删除磁盘文件、加密数据文件、封锁键盘以及使系统死锁等。

  

**触发性。**[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的触发性是指[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)在一定的条件下通过外界的刺激而被激活，发生破坏作用。触发[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序的条件是[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)设计者安排、设计的，这些触发条件可能是时间/日期触发、计数器触发、输入特定符号触发、启动触发等。[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)运行时，触发机制检查预定条件是否满足，如果满足，就启动感染或破坏动作，使[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进行感染或[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)；如果不满足，则[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)继续潜伏。

  

**破坏性。**[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的最终目的是破坏用户程序及数据，[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的破坏行为体现了[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的杀伤能力。[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)破坏行为的激烈程度取决于[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)制作者的主观愿望和所具有的技术能量。如果[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)设计者的目的在于彻底破坏系统的正常运行，那么这种[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)对于[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统造成的后果是难以设想的，可以破坏磁盘文件的内容、删除数据、抢占内存空间，甚至对硬盘进行格式化，造成整个系统崩溃。有时几种本没有多大破坏作用的[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)交叉感染，也会导致系统崩溃等。

  

**衍生性。**由于[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)本身是一段可执行程序，同时又由于[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)本身是由几部分组成的，所以可以被恶作剧者或恶意[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者模仿，甚至修改[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的几个模块，使之成为一种不同于原[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。例如，曾经在[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上影响颇大的“震荡波”[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，其变种[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)就有A、B、C等好几种。

**（3）计算机病毒的分类**

  

以前，大多数[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)主要通过软盘传播，但是当[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)成为人们的主要通信方式以后，网络又为[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的传播提供了新的传播机制，[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的产生速度大大加快，数量也不断增加。据国外统计，[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)以10种/周的速度递增；另据我国公安部统计，国内[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)以4～6种/月的速度递增。目前，全球的[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)有几万种，对[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的分类方法也存在多种，常见的分类有以下几种。

  

**1）按病毒存在的媒体分类**

  

**① 引导型**[**病毒**](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)**。**引导型[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是在系统引导时出现的[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，依托的环境是BIOS中断服务程序。引导型[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是利用了操作系统的引导模块放在某个固定的位置，并且控制权的转交方式是以物理地址为依据，而不是以操作系统引导区的内容为依据，因而[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)占据该物理位置后即可获得控制权，而将真正的引导区内容转移或替换。待[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序被执行后，再将控制权交给真正的引导区内容，使这个带[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的系统表面上看似正常运转，但实际上[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)已经隐藏在了系统中，伺机传染、发作。引导型[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)主要感染软盘、硬盘上的引导扇区（Boot Sector）上的内容，使用户在启动[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)或对软盘等存储介质进行读、写操作时进行感染和破坏活动，而且还会破坏硬盘上的文件分区表（File Allocation Table,FAT）。此类[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)有Anti-CMOS、Stone等。

**2）按病毒的破坏能力分类**

  

**① KB的文件变成约90 KB，就是被几种[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)反复感染了多次。这不仅消耗了大量宝贵的磁盘存储空间，而且整个[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统也由于多种[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)寄生于其中而无法正常工作。典型的良性[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)有小球[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、救护车[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、Dabi[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)等。

  

**② 恶性[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。**恶性[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是指那些一旦发作，就会破坏系统或数据，造成[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统瘫痪的一类[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。这类[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)危害性极大，一旦发作，给用户造成的损失可能是不可挽回的。例如，黑色星期五[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、CIH[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、米开朗基罗[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)（也叫米氏[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a777  

**3）按病毒传染的方法分类**

  

**① 驻留型[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。**驻留型[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)感染[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)后把自身驻留在内存（Random Access Memory，RAM）中，这一部分程序挂接系统调用并合并到操作系统中，并一直处于激活状态。

  

**② 非驻留型[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。**非驻留型[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是一种立即传染的[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，每执行一次带毒程序，就自动在当前路径中搜索，查到满足要求的可执行文件即进行传染。该类[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)不修改中断向量，不改动系统的任何状态，因而很难区分当前运行的是一个[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)还是一个正常的程序。典型的[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)有Vienna/648。

  

**4）按照计算机病毒的链接方式分类**

  

**① 源码型[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。**这类[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)较为少见，主要[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)高级语言编写的源程序。源码型[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)在源程序编译之前插入其中，并随源程序一起编译、连接成可执行文件。最终所生成的可执行文件便已经感染了[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

**④ 操作系统型[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。**这种[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)用自己的程序意图加入或取代部分操作系统进行工作，具有很强的破坏力，可以导致整个系统的瘫痪。圆点[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和大麻[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)就是典型的操作系统型[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。这种[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)在运行时，用自己的逻辑部分取代操作系统的合法程序模块，对操作系统进行破坏。

**使用单机防[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)卡。**单机防[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)卡的核心实际上是一个软件，事先固化在ROM中。单机防[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)卡通过动态驻留内存来监视[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的运行情况，根据总结出来的[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)行为规则和经验来判断是否有[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)活动，并可以通过截获中断控制权来使内存中的[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)瘫痪，使其失去传染其他文件和破坏信息资料的能力。装有单机防[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)卡的工作站对[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&ch
