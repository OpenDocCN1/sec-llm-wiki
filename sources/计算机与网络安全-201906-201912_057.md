我们通常将试图破坏信息系统的完整性、机密性、可信性的任何网络活动都称为[网络入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)（Intrusion Detection，ID）。防范[网络入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)最常用的方法就是[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)具有简单实用的特点，并且透明度高，可以在不修改原有网络应用系统的情况下达到一定的安全要求。但是，[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)只是一种被动防御性的[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126563&idx=3&sn=14023df84f67afb48342aae5f5fe71f7&chksm=bc8599cc8bf210da3250510eb9d50c84270e15141f1eac9d3f98a726114f5d3e51263495ecf9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工具，仅仅使用[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是不够的。首先，[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者可以找到[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，绕过[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进行[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。其次，[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)对来自内部的[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)无能为力。[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)提供的服务方式是要么都拒绝，要么都通过，而这远远不能满足用户复杂的应用要求，于是产生了[入侵检测](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132051&idx=3&sn=6b7a3f3dc684d1464a532c7417b03b91&chksm=bc85847c8bf20d6a77567fc8d4ffb9dcf61d14b87241d0bb734507b597e395212e759fdaddd1&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术。

  

**1、入侵检测的基本概念**

  

[入侵检测](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132051&idx=3&sn=6b7a3f3dc684d1464a532c7417b03b91&chksm=bc85847c8bf20d6a77567fc8d4ffb9dcf61d14b87241d0bb734507b597e395212e759fdaddd1&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是识别针对[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)或网络资源的恶意企图和行为，并对此做出反应的过程。

  

[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)被检测出来的过程包括监控在[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统或者网络中发生的事件，再分析处理这些事件，检测出[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)事件。[入侵检测](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132051&idx=3&sn=6b7a3f3dc684d1464a532c7417b03b91&chksm=bc85847c8bf20d6a77567fc8d4ffb9dcf61d14b87241d0bb734507b597e395212e759fdaddd1&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统（Intrusion Detection System，[IDS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123686&idx=2&sn=23c35bb4d7c1b0414f175ae821ddc950&chksm=bc8665098bf1ec1f63b16681546b1ab596a1350b753f6e7f6c28fde6d0085fe3c15d04977d9e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)）就是使这种监控和分析过程自动化的独立系统，它既可以是一种安全软件，也可以是硬件。

  

[IDS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123065&idx=3&sn=817bed2e3f7733ac1ca3fe22793b6767&chksm=bc866b968bf1e280b26f1073407f2636ba8f20c0b83fd80e8a652dbffd1154f9807929c45e08&scene=21#wechat_redirect)能够检测未授权对象（人或程序）针对系统的[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)企图或行为，同时监控授权对象对系统资源的非法操作。[IDS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123686&idx=2&sn=23c35bb4d7c1b0414f175ae821ddc950&chksm=bc8665098bf1ec1f63b16681546b1ab596a1350b753f6e7f6c28fde6d0085fe3c15d04977d9e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)对[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的检测通常包括以下几个部分。

  

1）对系统的不同环节收集信息。

2）分析该信息，试图寻找[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)活动的特征。

3）自动对检测到的行为做出响应。

4）记录并报告检测过程结果。

  

[入侵检测](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132051&idx=3&sn=6b7a3f3dc684d1464a532c7417b03b91&chksm=bc85847c8bf20d6a77567fc8d4ffb9dcf61d14b87241d0bb734507b597e395212e759fdaddd1&scene=21#wechat_redirect)作为一种积极主动的[安全防护](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132591&idx=3&sn=0e73014aa2b38745a8259c348e71680c&chksm=bc8586408bf20f564af8b79e6b9e006803065e2aa2f69af8d33554c7622c6ce7198559c23ced&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术，提供了对内部[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、外部[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和误操作的实时保护，在网络系统受到危害之前拦截和响应[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。[入侵检测](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132051&idx=3&sn=6b7a3f3dc684d1464a532c7417b03b91&chksm=bc85847c8bf20d6a77567fc8d4ffb9dcf61d14b87241d0bb734507b597e395212e759fdaddd1&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统能很好地弥补[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的不足，从某种意义上说是[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的补充。

  

**2、入侵检测的分类**

  

现有的[入侵检测](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132051&idx=3&sn=6b7a3f3dc684d1464a532c7417b03b91&chksm=bc85847c8bf20d6a77567fc8d4ffb9dcf61d14b87241d0bb734507b597e395212e759fdaddd1&scene=21#wechat_redirect)分类大多都是基于信息源和分析方法来进行的。

  

**（1）根据信息源的不同，分为基于主机型和基于网络型两大类**

  

1）基于[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的[入侵检测](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132051&idx=3&sn=6b7a3f3dc684d1464a532c7417b03b91&chksm=bc85847c8bf20d6a77567fc8d4ffb9dcf61d14b87241d0bb734507b597e395212e759fdaddd1&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统（Host-based Intrusion Detection System，HIDS）。HIDS可监测系统、事件和[Windows](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127496&idx=3&sn=21acddfe6a98fe94ae346db72555e138&chksm=bc859a278bf21331d775fdb2224b50832a618a13d3a8fa76c41786186e846f5126daf96682bc&scene=21#wechat_redirect) NT下的安全记录以及UNIX环境下的系统记录。当有文件被修改时，HIDS将新的记录条目与已知的[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)特征相比较，查看是否匹配。如果匹配，就会向系统管理员报警或者做出适当的响应。

  

基于[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的[入侵检测](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132051&idx=3&sn=6b7a3f3dc684d1464a532c7417b03b91&chksm=bc85847c8bf20d6a77567fc8d4ffb9dcf61d14b87241d0bb734507b597e395212e759fdaddd1&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统在发展过程中还融入了其他技术。检测关键的系统文件和可执行文件[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的常用方法是定期检查文件的校验和，以便发现异常的变化。对异常情况反应的快慢取决于轮询间隔时间的长短。许多产品都是监听端口的活动，并在特定端口被访问时向管理员报警。这类检测方法将基于网络的[入侵检测](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132051&idx=3&sn=6b7a3f3dc684d1464a532c7417b03b91&chksm=bc85847c8bf20d6a77567fc8d4ffb9dcf61d14b87241d0bb734507b597e395212e759fdaddd1&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的基本方法融入基于[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的检测环境中。

2）基于网络的[入侵检测](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132051&idx=3&sn=6b7a3f3dc684d1464a532c7417b03b91&chksm=bc85847c8bf20d6a77567fc8d4ffb9dcf61d14b87241d0bb734507b597e395212e759fdaddd1&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统（Network-based Intrusion Detection System，NIDS）。NIDS以数据包作为分析的数据源，通常利用一个工作在混杂模式下的网卡来实时监视并分析通过网络的数据流。分析模块通常使用模式匹配、统计分析等技术来识别[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)行为。一旦检测到了[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)行为，[IDS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123686&idx=2&sn=23c35bb4d7c1b0414f175ae821ddc950&chksm=bc8665098bf1ec1f63b16681546b1ab596a1350b753f6e7f6c28fde6d0085fe3c15d04977d9e&scene=21#wechat_redirect) 的响应模块就做出适当的响应，如报警、切断相关用户的网络连接等。不同[入侵检测](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132051&idx=3&sn=6b7a3f3dc684d1464a532c7417b03b91&chksm=bc85847c8bf20d6a77567fc8d4ffb9dcf61d14b87241d0bb734507b597e395212e759fdaddd1&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统在实现时采用的响应方式也可能不同，但通常都包括通知管理员、切断连接、记录相关的信息以提供必要的法律依据等。

  

目前，许多机构的网络安全解决方案都同时采用了基于[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和基于网络的两种[入侵检测](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132051&idx=3&sn=6b7a3f3dc684d1464a532c7417b03b91&chksm=bc85847c8bf20d6a77567fc8d4ffb9dcf61d14b87241d0bb734507b597e395212e759fdaddd1&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统，因为这两种系统在很大程度上是互补的。实际上，许多客户在使用[IDS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123065&idx=3&sn=817bed2e3f7733ac1ca3fe22793b6767&chksm=bc866b968bf1e280b26f1073407f2636ba8f20c0b83fd80e8a652dbffd1154f9807929c45e08&scene=21#wechat_redirect)时都配置了基于网络的[入侵检测](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132051&idx=3&sn=6b7a3f3dc684d1464a532c7417b03b91&chksm=bc85847c8bf20d6a77567fc8d4ffb9dcf61d14b87241d0bb734507b597e395212e759fdaddd1&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。在[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)之外的检测器可以用来检测来自外部[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。[DNS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127816&idx=3&sn=2b8d5dfef1801f990c8b7cb25fc65d73&chksm=bc8594e78bf21df19918b7e41f11ca714d5f530030014dd6a48e278b45385d835489385f1cd8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、E-mail和[Web](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130618&idx=3&sn=449f93385285290a85de33579f5e55bf&chksm=bc858e158bf20703b709bfb7ddf2adee4149765451b8281f45a741aee2c621c396b330f67af2&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)经常是[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的目标，但是又必须与外部网络交互，不可能全部将其屏蔽，所以应当在各个[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上安装基于[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的[入侵检测](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132051&idx=3&sn=6b7a3f3dc684d1464a532c7417b03b91&chksm=bc85847c8bf20d6a77567fc8d4ffb9dcf61d14b87241d0bb734507b597e395212e759fdaddd1&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统，其检测结果也要向分析员控制台报告。因此，即便是小规模的网络结构，也常常需要基于[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和基于网络的两种[入侵检测](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132051&idx=3&sn=6b7a3f3dc684d1464a532c7417b03b91&chksm=bc85847c8bf20d6a77567fc8d4ffb9dcf61d14b87241d0bb734507b597e395212e759fdaddd1&scene=21#wechat_redirect)能力。

  

**（2）根据检测所用分析方法的不同，分为误用检测和异常检测**

  

1）误用检测（Misuse Detection）。大部分现有的[入侵检测](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132051&idx=3&sn=6b7a3f3dc684d1464a532c7417b03b91&chksm=bc85847c8bf20d6a77567fc8d4ffb9dcf61d14b87241d0bb734507b597e395212e759fdaddd1&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工具都是使用误用检测方法。误用检测方法应用了系统缺陷和特殊[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的累计知识。该[入侵检测](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132051&idx=3&sn=6b7a3f3dc684d1464a532c7417b03b91&chksm=bc85847c8bf20d6a77567fc8d4ffb9dcf61d14b87241d0bb734507b597e395212e759fdaddd1&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统包含一个缺陷库并且检测出利用这些缺陷[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的行为。每当检测到[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)时，系统就会报警。因为凡是不符合正常规则的所有行为都被认为是不合法的，所以误用检测的准确度很高，但是其查全度（检测所有[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的能力）与[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)规则的更新程度有密切关系。

  

误用检测的优点是误报率很低，并且对每一种[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)都能提供详细资料，使用者能够更方便地做出响应。缺点是[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)信息的收集和更新比较困难，需要做大量的工作，花费很多时间。另外，这种方法难以检测本地[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)（如权限滥用等），因为没有确定的规则来描述这类[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)事件，因此误用检测一般是适用于特殊环境下的检测工具。

  

2）异常检测（Anomaly Detection）。异常检测假设[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者活动异常于正常的活动。为实现该类检测，[IDS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123065&idx=3&sn=817bed2e3f7733ac1ca3fe22793b6767&chksm=bc866b968bf1e280b26f1073407f2636ba8f20c0b83fd80e8a652dbffd1154f9807929c45e08&scene=21#wechat_redirect)建立了正常活动的“规范集”，只要主体的活动违反其统计规律，就被认为可能是“[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”行为。异常检测最大的优点是具有抽象系统正常行为，从而具备检测系统异常行为的能力。因为这种能力不受系统以前是否知道这种[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的限制，所以能够检测出新的[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)或者从未发生过的[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。大多数正常行为的模型使用一种矩阵的数学模型，矩阵的数量来自于系统的各种指标，如CPU使用率、内存使用率、登录的时间和次数、网络活动、文件的改动等。异常检测的缺点是若[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者了解了检测规律，就可以小心地避免系统指标突变，从而使用逐渐改变系统指标的方法来逃避检测。另外，异常检测的查准率也不高，检测时间较长。最糟糕的是，异常检测是一种“事后”的检测，当检测到[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)行为时，破坏早已发生了。
