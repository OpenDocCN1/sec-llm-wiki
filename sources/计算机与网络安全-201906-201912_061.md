

** 文件级别rootkit **

  

文件级别的[rootkit](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126137&idx=2&sn=5a2ba6293cad5d2770be28ee9230a101&chksm=bc859f968bf2168060cd28db17aafb3653a0aab4aabad54c3c5fe1a3c04dd30beebbcd40a22b&scene=21#wechat_redirect)一般是通过程序[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)或者系统[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进入系统后，通过修改系统的重要文件来达到隐藏自己的目的。在系统遭受[rootkit](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126251&idx=2&sn=e323208cf25cc13d57b182e6d2e186e2&chksm=bc859f048bf216124cd5cc0566238b314d2d326012696add50995c1cf0be136c574029334d5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)后，合法的文件被[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序替代，变成了外壳程序，而其内部是隐藏着的[后门](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129244&idx=3&sn=6df349d4b772425777487a77091f0f4d&chksm=bc8593738bf21a652bfc7213ea58a506986d7b1e6812c5132b33c598b7a8a210c10f13d7f900&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序。通常容易被[rootkit](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126376&idx=2&sn=eb0bd0f0a46894b95d30aa1b30011b7e&chksm=bc859e878bf217915a873e57c2440d63258665f195b78b7d2e6d7e0695f35c9ad4c5a7c3ae60&scene=21#wechat_redirect)替换的系统程序有login、ls、ps、ifconfig、du、find、netstat等，其中login程序是最经常被替换的，因为当访问[Linux](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133968&idx=2&sn=2454fe58d155dec7738888c75a963aeb&chksm=bc85bcff8bf235e97db0692fa805d2eeecdcaaa1e2a26f03ab5112230e873a342fcaca0bdf4f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)时，无论是通过本地登录还是[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)登录，/bin/login程序都会运行，系统将通过/bin/login来收集并核对用户的账号和[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，而[rootkit](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126137&idx=2&sn=5a2ba6293cad5d2770be28ee9230a101&chksm=bc859f968bf2168060cd28db17aafb3653a0aab4aabad54c3c5fe1a3c04dd30beebbcd40a22b&scene=21#wechat_redirect)就是利用这个程序的特点，使用一个带有根权限[后门](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129244&idx=3&sn=6df349d4b772425777487a77091f0f4d&chksm=bc8593738bf21a652bfc7213ea58a506986d7b1e6812c5132b33c598b7a8a210c10f13d7f900&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的/bin/login来替换系统的/bin/login，这样[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者通过输入设定好的[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)就能轻松进入系统。此时，即使系统管理员修改root[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)或者清除root[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者还是一样能通过root用户登录系统。通常[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者在进入[Linux](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133968&idx=2&sn=2454fe58d155dec7738888c75a963aeb&chksm=bc85bcff8bf235e97db0692fa805d2eeecdcaaa1e2a26f03ab5112230e873a342fcaca0bdf4f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统后，会进行一系列的[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)动作，最常见的是安装嗅探器收集本机或者网络中其他[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的重要数据。在默认情况下，[Linux](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133968&idx=2&sn=2454fe58d155dec7738888c75a963aeb&chksm=bc85bcff8bf235e97db0692fa805d2eeecdcaaa1e2a26f03ab5112230e873a342fcaca0bdf4f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中也有一些系统文件会监控这些工具动作，例如ifconfig命令，所以，[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者为了避免被发现，会想方设法替换其他系统文件，常见的就是ls、ps、ifconfig、du、find、netstat等。如果这些文件都被替换，那么在系  

这就是文件级别的[rootkit](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126376&idx=2&sn=eb0bd0f0a46894b95d30aa1b30011b7e&chksm=bc859e878bf217915a873e57c2440d63258665f195b78b7d2e6d7e0695f35c9ad4c5a7c3ae60&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，对系统维护威胁很大，目前最有效的防御方法是定期对系统重要文件的完整性进行检查，如果发现文件被修改或者替换，那么很可能系统已经遭受了[rootkit](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126137&idx=2&sn=5a2ba6293cad5d2770be28ee9230a101&chksm=bc859f968bf2168060cd28db17aafb3653a0aab4aabad54c3c5fe1a3c04dd30beebbcd40a22b&scene=21#wechat_redirect)入侵。检查文件完整性的工具很多，常见的有Tripwire、aide等，可以通过这些工具定期检查文件系统的完整性，以检测系统是否被[rootkit](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126251&idx=2&sn=e323208cf25cc13d57b182e6d2e186e2&chksm=bc859f048bf216124cd5cc0566238b314d2d326012696add50995c1cf0be136c574029334d5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

** 内核级别的rootkit **

  

内核级别[rootkit](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126376&idx=2&sn=eb0bd0f0a46894b95d30aa1b30011b7e&chksm=bc859e878bf217915a873e57c2440d63258665f195b78b7d2e6d7e0695f35c9ad4c5a7c3ae60&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是比文件级别[rootkit](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126137&idx=2&sn=5a2ba6293cad5d2770be28ee9230a101&chksm=bc859f968bf2168060cd28db17aafb3653a0aab4aabad54c3c5fe1a3c04dd30beebbcd40a22b&scene=21#wechat_redirect)更高级的一种[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)方式，它可以使[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者获得对系统底层的完全控制权，此时[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者可以修改系统内核，进而截获运行程序向内核提交的命令，并将其重定向到[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者所选择的程序并运行此程序，也就是说，当用户要运行程序A时，被[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者修改过的内核会假装执行A程序，而实际上却执行了程序B。

  

内核级别[rootkit](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126251&idx=2&sn=e323208cf25cc13d57b182e6d2e186e2&chksm=bc859f048bf216124cd5cc0566238b314d2d326012696add50995c1cf0be136c574029334d5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)主要依附在内核上，它并不对系统文件做任何修改，因此一般的检测工具很难检测到它的存在，这样一旦系统内核被植入[rootkit](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126376&idx=2&sn=eb0bd0f0a46894b95d30aa1b30011b7e&chksm=bc859e878bf217915a873e57c2440d63258665f195b78b7d2e6d7e0695f35c9ad4c5a7c3ae60&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者就可以对系统为所欲为而不被发现。目前针对内核级的[rootkit](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126137&idx=2&sn=5a2ba6293cad5d2770be28ee9230a101&chksm=bc859f968bf2168060cd28db17aafb3653a0aab4aabad54c3c5fe1a3c04dd30beebbcd40a22b&scene=21#wechat_redirect)还没有很好的防御工具，因此，做好系统安全防范就非常重要，将系统维持在最小权限内工作，只要[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者不能获取root权限，就无法在内核中植入[rootkit](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126251&idx=2&sn=e323208cf25cc13d57b182e6d2e186e2&chksm=bc859f048bf216124cd5cc0566238b314d2d326012696add50995c1cf0be136c574029334d5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

**1、rootkit后门检测工具chkrootkit**

  

chkrootkit是[Linux](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133968&idx=2&sn=2454fe58d155dec7738888c75a963aeb&chksm=bc85bcff8bf235e97db0692fa805d2eeecdcaaa1e2a26f03ab5112230e873a342fcaca0bdf4f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统下查找并检测[rootkit](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126376&idx=2&sn=eb0bd0f0a46894b95d30aa1b30011b7e&chksm=bc859e878bf217915a873e57c2440d63258665f195b78b7d2e6d7e0695f35c9ad4c5a7c3ae60&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[后门](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129244&idx=3&sn=6df349d4b772425777487a77091f0f4d&chksm=bc8593738bf21a652bfc7213ea58a506986d7b1e6812c5132b33c598b7a8a210c10f13d7f900&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的一个工具。chkrootkit没有包含在官方的CentOS源中，因此要采取手动编译的方法来安装，不过这种安装方法也更加安全。下面简单介绍下chkrootkit的安装过程。

  

**（1）准备gcc编译环境**

  

对于CentOS系统，需要安装gcc编译环境，执行下述三个命令：

  

\[root@server ~\]# yum -y install gcc

\[root@server ~\]# yum -y install gcc-c++

**（2）安装chkrootkit**

  

为了安全起见，建议直接从官方网站下载chkrootkit源码，然后进行安装，操作如下：

  

\[root@server~\]# tar zxvf chkrootkit.tar.gz

\[root@server~\]# cd chkrootkit-\*

\[root@server~\]# make sense

  

注意：上面的编译命令为make sense。

  

\[root@server ~\]# cd ..

\[root@server ~\]# cp -r chkrootkit-\* /usr/local/chkrootkit

\[root@server ~\]# rm -rf chkrootkit-\*

  

**（3）使用chkrootkit**

  

安装完的chkrootkit程序位于/usr/local/chkrootkit目录下，执行如下命令即可显示chkrootkit的详细用法：

**（4）chkrootkit的缺点**

  

chkrootkit在检查[rootkit](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126251&idx=2&sn=e323208cf25cc13d57b182e6d2e186e2&chksm=bc859f048bf216124cd5cc0566238b314d2d326012696add50995c1cf0be136c574029334d5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的过程中使用了部分系统命令，因此，如果[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)被[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=3&sn=c82f59076adf4123590301c9577f3c59&chksm=bc859b348bf21222ef281bc9aebb654a06582273fd7d9eb73ab84b56adbf383f74305efe3c3c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，那么依赖的系统命令可能也已经被[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者替换，此时chkrootkit的检测结果将变得完全不可信。为了避免chkrootkit的这个问题，可以在[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)对外开放前，事先将chkrootkit使用的系统命令进行备份，在需要的时候使用备份的原始系统命令让chkrootkit对[rootkit](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126376&idx=2&sn=eb0bd0f0a46894b95d30aa1b30011b7e&chksm=bc859e878bf217915a873e57c2440d63258665f195b78b7d2e6d7e0695f35c9ad4c5a7c3ae60&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进行检测。这个过程可以通过下面的操作实现：

  

\[root@server ~\]# mkdir /usr/share/.commands

\[root@server ~\]# cp \`which --skip-alias awk cut echo find egrep id head ls netstat ps strings sed uname\` /usr/share/.commands

\[root@server ~\]# /usr/local/chkrootkit/chkrootkit -p /usr/share/.commands/

\[root@server share\]# cd /usr/share/

\[root@server share\]# tar zcvf commands.tar.gz .commands

\[root@server share\]#  rm -rf commands.tar.gz

  

上面这段操作是在/usr/share/下建立了一个.commands隐藏文件，然后将chkrootkit使用的系统命令进行备份到这个目录下。为了安全起见，可以将.commands目录压缩打包，然后下载到一个安全的地方进行备份，以后如果[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)遭受[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，就可以将这个备份上传到[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)任意路径下，然后通过chkrootkit命令的“-p”参数指定这个路径进行检测即可。

\[root@server ~\]# /usr/local/bin/rkhunter –-check –-skip-keypress

30 09 \* \* \* root /usr/local/bin/rkhunter –-check –-cronjob

  

这样，RKHunter检测程序就会在每天的9：30运行一次。
