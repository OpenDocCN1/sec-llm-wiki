**1、防火墙的基本概念**  
古时候，人们常在寓所之间砌起一道砖墙，一旦火灾发生，就能够防止火势蔓延到其他寓所。现在，如果一个网络连接到了[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，用户就可以访问外部世界并与之通信。同时，外部世界同样可以访问该网络并与之交互。为安全起见，可以在该网络和[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)之间插入一个中介系统，竖起一道安全屏障。这道屏障的作用是阻断来自外部通过网络对本网络的威胁和[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，提供扼守本网络的安全和审计的唯一关卡，其作用与古时候的防火砖墙有类似之处，因此把这个屏障称为[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)（Fire wall）。  
在网络中，[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是指在两个网络之间实现控制策略的系统（软件、硬件或者是两者并用），用来保护内部的网络不易受到来自[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的侵害。因此，[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是一种安全策略的体现。如果内部网络的用户要连接[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，就必须首先连接到[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上，从那儿使用[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。同样，[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)要访问内部网络，也必须先通过[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)通过监控内网和[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)之间的任何活动，控制进出网络的信息流和信息包，尽可能地对外部屏蔽内部网络的信息、结构和运行状况，以实现对内部网络的保护。这种做法能有效防止来自[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，如图1所示。

 
目前的[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术一般都可以起到以下安全作用。  
**集中的网络安全。**[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)允许网络管理员定义一个中心（阻塞点）来防止非法用户（如[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=3&sn=c82f59076adf4123590301c9577f3c59&chksm=bc859b348bf21222ef281bc9aebb654a06582273fd7d9eb73ab84b56adbf383f74305efe3c3c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、网络破坏者等）进入内部网络，禁止存在不安全因素的访问进出网络，并抗击来自各种线路的[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术能够简化网络的安全管理、提高网络的安全性。  
**安全警报。**通过[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)可以方便地监视网络的安全性，并产生报警信号。网络管理员必须审查并记录所有通过[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的重要信息。  
**重新部署网络地址转换。**[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的迅速发展使有效的、未被申请的IP地址越来越少，这就意味着想进入[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的机构可能申请不到足够的IP地址来满足内部网络用户的需要。为了接入[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，可以通过网络地址转换（Network Address Translator，[NAT](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130230&idx=3&sn=34c8306972117fa8eb4237336eb50969&chksm=bc858f998bf2068f4c3817a5a73b2ba53728af9afb96e3ecfd881cf0c00d05a22ff939dd37e5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)）来完成内部私有地址到外部注册地址的映射。[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是部署[NAT](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130230&idx=3&sn=34c8306972117fa8eb4237336eb50969&chksm=bc858f998bf2068f4c3817a5a73b2ba53728af9afb96e3ecfd881cf0c00d05a22ff939dd37e5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的理想位置。  
**监视Internet的使用。**[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)也是审查和记录内部人员使用[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的最佳位置，可以在此记录内部访问[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的情况。  
**向外发布信息。**[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)除了起到安全屏障作用外，也是部署WWW[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和FTP[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的理想位置。允许[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)访问上述[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，而禁止访问内部受保护的其他系统。  
但是，[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)也有其自身的局限性，无法防范来自[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)以外的其他途径所进行的[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。有许多机构购买了价格昂贵的[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，却忽视了通往其网络中的其他几扇[后门](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129244&idx=3&sn=6df349d4b772425777487a77091f0f4d&chksm=bc8593738bf21a652bfc7213ea58a506986d7b1e6812c5132b33c598b7a8a210c10f13d7f900&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。例如，在一个被保护的网络上有一个没有限制的拨号访问存在，这样就为[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127290&idx=3&sn=04aaac7d1e20fd446929032cb45b6b38&chksm=bc859b158bf212032e5e2121f6066cd1ec171afc141be62e32ad671879a403ade7b3435495bb&scene=21#wechat_redirect)从[后门](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129244&idx=3&sn=6df349d4b772425777487a77091f0f4d&chksm=bc8593738bf21a652bfc7213ea58a506986d7b1e6812c5132b33c598b7a8a210c10f13d7f900&scene=21#wechat_redirect)攻击创造了机会。  
另外，由于[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)依赖于口令，所以[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)不能防范[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=3&sn=c82f59076adf4123590301c9577f3c59&chksm=bc859b348bf21222ef281bc9aebb654a06582273fd7d9eb73ab84b56adbf383f74305efe3c3c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)对口令的[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。曾经两个在校学生编写了一个简单的程序，通过对波音公司的口令字的排列组合，试出了开启其内部网的密钥，从网中得到了一张授权的波音公司的口令表，然后将口令一一出售。因此，有人说[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)不过是一道矮小的篱笆墙，[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127290&idx=3&sn=04aaac7d1e20fd446929032cb45b6b38&chksm=bc859b158bf212032e5e2121f6066cd1ec171afc141be62e32ad671879a403ade7b3435495bb&scene=21#wechat_redirect)就像老鼠一样能从这道篱笆墙的窟窿中进进出出。同时，[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)也不能防止来自内部用户带来的威胁，不能解决进入[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的数据带来的所有安全问题，如果用户在本地运行了一个包含恶意[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的程序，就很可能导致敏感[信息泄露](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127361&idx=3&sn=4c76bb9efc3b43bd56fbff3d96a594a3&chksm=bc859aae8bf213b88ea93d3438b436317a1aabf7b69bb53366f148ae9ac7f9c613365268779d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和破坏。  
因此，要使[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发挥作用，[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的策略必须现实，能够反映出整个[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131340&idx=2&sn=f0a410fd4ac4d8564cf40c2f1284a3bf&chksm=bc858b238bf20235f42eff940d48927ae2b76820b953edb729e99bb5c9cd4d7938554025f20a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的水平。例如，一个保存着超级机密或保密数据的站点根本不需要[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，因为这个站点根本不应该被接入[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，或者保存着真正秘密数据的系统应与企业的其余网络隔离开。

**2、防火墙的主要类型**  
典型的[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统通常由一个或多个构件组成，相应地，实现[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的技术包括五大类：包过滤型[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)（也称网络级[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)）、应用级网关、电路级网关、代理服务[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和复合型[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。这些技术各有所长，具体使用哪一种或是否混合使用，要根据具体情况而定。  
**（1）包过滤防火墙（Packet Filtering Firewall）**  
一个[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)便是一个传统的包过滤[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)可以对IP地址、[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)或UDP分组头信息进行检查与过滤，以确定是否与设备的过滤规则匹配，继而决定此数据包按照路由表中的信息被转发或被丢弃。  
大多数[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)都能通过检查这些信息来决定是否转发收到的数据包，但是不能判断出一个数据包来自何方、去向何处。有些先进的网络级[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)则可以判断这一点，可以提供内部信息，以说明所通过的连接状态和一些数据流的内容，把判断的信息同[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)内部的规则表进行比较，在规则表中定义了各种规则来表明是否同意或拒绝包通过。包过滤[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)检查每一条规则直至发现包中的信息与某规则相符。如果没有一条规则符合，[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)就会使用默认规则，一般情况下，默认规则就是要求[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)丢弃该数据包。另外，通过定义基于[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)或UDP数据包的端口号，[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)能够判断是否允许建立特定的连接，如Telnet、FTP连接等。其功能如图2所示。

包过滤[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)对用户来说是全透明的，其最大的优点是：只需在一个关键位置设置一个包过滤[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)就可以保护整个网络。如果在内部网络与外界之间已经有了一个独立的[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，那么可以简单地加一个包过滤软件进去，一步就可以实现对全网的保护，而不必在用户机上再安装其他特定的软件。使用起来非常简洁、方便，并且速度快、费用低。  
包过滤[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)也有其自身的缺点和局限性，具体如下。  
1）包过滤规则配置比较复杂，而且几乎没有什么工具能够对过滤规则的正确性进行测试。  
2）由于包过滤[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)只检查地址和端口，对网络更高协议层的信息无理解能力，因而对网络的保护十分有限。  
3）包过滤没法检测具有数据驱动[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)这一类潜在危险的数据包。  
4）随着过滤次数的增加，[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的吞吐量会明显下降，从而影响整个网络的性能。  
**（2）应用级网关（Application Level Gateway）**  
应用级网关主要控制对应用程序的访问，能够检查进出的数据包，通过网关复制、传递数据来防止在受信任的[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)与不受信任的[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)间直接建立联系。应用级网关不仅能够理解应用层上的协议，而且提供一种监督控制机制，使网络内、外部的访问请求在监督机制下得到保护。同时，应用级网关还能对数据包进行分析、统计并详细记录。其功能如图3所示。

为了消除这一安全[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，应用级网关可以通过重写所有主要的应用程序来提供[访问控制](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122451&idx=2&sn=b3bd63905b75fab54ae990f848ed9c98&chksm=bc8669fc8bf1e0eada1a65ac8464c19c9306751a59b47f6794164dcc64e804425b31e71cef77&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。新的应用程序驻留在所有人都要使用的集中式[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中，这个集中式[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)称为堡垒[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)（Bastion Host）。由于堡垒[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上其他站点所能到达的唯一站点，即是[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上的[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)能连接到的唯一的内部网络上的系统，任何外部的系统试图访问内部的系统或[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)都必须连接到这台[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上，因此堡垒[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)被认为是最重要的安全点，必须具有全面的安全措施。  
应用级网关的优点是：具有较强的[访问控制](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122451&idx=2&sn=b3bd63905b75fab54ae990f848ed9c98&chksm=bc8669fc8bf1e0eada1a65ac8464c19c9306751a59b47f6794164dcc64e804425b31e71cef77&scene=21#wechat_redirect)功能，是目前最安全的[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术之一。缺点是：每一种协议都需要相应的代理软件，实现起来比较困难，使用时工作量大，效率不如网络级[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)高，而且对用户缺乏“透明度”。在实际使用过程中，用户在受信任的网络上通过[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)访问[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)时，经常会发现存在较大的延迟并且有时必须进行多次登录才能访问[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)或[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。  
**（3）电路级网关（Circuit Level Gateway）**  
电路级网关是一种特殊的[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，通常工作在[OSI参考模型](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132810&idx=2&sn=ee7f1720a26e1b82f9fb49a3657bc698&chksm=bc8581658bf2087393c30977fcac770581ab0565ccbb20dc781cc0caf6da8284d33405244c10&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中的会话层上。电路级网关只依赖于[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)连接，而并不关心任何应用协议，也不进行任何的包处理或过滤。电路级网关只根据规则建立从一个网络到另一个网络的连接，并只在内部连接和外部连接之间来回复制字节，不进行任何审查、过滤或协议管理。但是电路级网关可以隐藏受保护网络的有关信息。  
实际上，电路级网关并非作为一个独立的产品存在，一般要和其他应用级网关结合在一起使用，如Trust Information Systems公司的Gauntlet [Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect) Firewall、DEC公司的Alta Vista Firewall等。另外，电路级网关还可在代理[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上运行“地址转移”进程，将所有内部的IP地址映射到一个“安全”的IP地址，这个地址是[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)专用的。  
电路级网关最大的优点是[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)可以被设置成混合网关。这样，整个[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统对于要访问[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的内部用户来说使用起来很方便，还能提供完善的保护内部网络免于外部[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)功能。  
**（4）代理服务防火墙（Proxy Server Firewall）**  
代理服务[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工作在[OSI参考模型](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132810&idx=2&sn=ee7f1720a26e1b82f9fb49a3657bc698&chksm=bc8581658bf2087393c30977fcac770581ab0565ccbb20dc781cc0caf6da8284d33405244c10&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的最高层——应用层，有时也将其归为应用级网关一类。代理[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)（Proxy Server）通常运行在Intranet和[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)之间，是内部网与外部网的隔离点，起着监视和隔绝应用层通信流的作用。当代理[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)收到用户对某站点的访问请求后，便会立即检查该请求是否符合规则。若规则允许用户访问该站点，代理[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)便会以客户的身份登录目的站点，取回所需的信息再发给客户，如图4所示。由此可以看出，代理[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)像一堵墙一样挡在内部用户和外界之间，从外部只能看到该代理[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)而无法获知任何内部资料，如用户的IP地址等。
