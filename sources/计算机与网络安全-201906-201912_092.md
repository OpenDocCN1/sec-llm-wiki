**黑客团伙非法控制5万多台计算机**

  

2018年5月，江苏徐州睢宁[网警](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129790&idx=3&sn=11e84e6198ac348684c43f8a59b2cdad&chksm=bc858d518bf2044777bab43f98099d5ea2fcf713a9a53e5a23371226572415f5584be6b436f9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)在网络巡查中发现，徐州电信机房的一台网络[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)有些异常，疑似被人上传了“[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”控制程序，用于[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)控制他人的[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。警方根据这一线索，随即展开了侦查。 

  

警方对租用[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的黄某，围绕他展开侦查，发现了利用控制的[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进行[网络攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，还有非法控制网站的一个网络[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=3&sn=c82f59076adf4123590301c9577f3c59&chksm=bc859b348bf21222ef281bc9aebb654a06582273fd7d9eb73ab84b56adbf383f74305efe3c3c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)犯罪团伙。 

  

嫌疑人在好多的[微信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127698&idx=3&sn=5101f6a80628d7caf6bc8ed7eec325c5&chksm=bc85957d8bf21c6bad5b83f1dcf2124043fa2e6a19eee9f604e23db0211b7ddcf8d94ecf97b8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)群发布、出售“[肉鸡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116724&idx=3&sn=b2218bc876e86e13ddb7777efe05f614&chksm=bc86405b8bf1c94ddfbfcb81e2242e81e48f557de20c187a113d4b4ab1ed78fec1c166028b81&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”的广告，圈内有需要的人跟他联系。黄某就是按照控制台数，一台几角钱或者一元钱这样的价格，租售给有需求的犯罪嫌疑人，有需求控制“[肉鸡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116724&idx=3&sn=b2218bc876e86e13ddb7777efe05f614&chksm=bc86405b8bf1c94ddfbfcb81e2242e81e48f557de20c187a113d4b4ab1ed78fec1c166028b81&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”，购买“[肉鸡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116724&idx=3&sn=b2218bc876e86e13ddb7777efe05f614&chksm=bc86405b8bf1c94ddfbfcb81e2242e81e48f557de20c187a113d4b4ab1ed78fec1c166028b81&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”的犯罪嫌疑人，主要是通过“[肉鸡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116724&idx=3&sn=b2218bc876e86e13ddb7777efe05f614&chksm=bc86405b8bf1c94ddfbfcb81e2242e81e48f557de20c187a113d4b4ab1ed78fec1c166028b81&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”来实施[DDoS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126795&idx=3&sn=9038ab35960660611a2d94ac3eb31681&chksm=bc8598e48bf211f22b26629df6dfe1cbf0a08bcf8acfa47740b1ce6afce1f9ab817d994e437a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，实施这样一种犯罪行为。

  

嫌疑人利用网络存在的一些[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，可以[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)控制别人的[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。这些被控制的[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，被一些圈内人士称为“[肉鸡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116724&idx=3&sn=b2218bc876e86e13ddb7777efe05f614&chksm=bc86405b8bf1c94ddfbfcb81e2242e81e48f557de20c187a113d4b4ab1ed78fec1c166028b81&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”或“傀儡机”。而[DDoS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126795&idx=3&sn=9038ab35960660611a2d94ac3eb31681&chksm=bc8598e48bf211f22b26629df6dfe1cbf0a08bcf8acfa47740b1ce6afce1f9ab817d994e437a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，就是让这些大量受非法控制的[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，同时集中访问某个网站，或发送一些攻击性指令，致使目标网站瘫痪或无法使用。

  

一般来讲，他们都是按小时收费，就是对目标网站实施[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，令目标网站瘫痪，一个小时几百元钱，根据网站的防御能力，有些防御能力比较强，收费就高一点。

  

随着进一步调查，警方发现这是一个庞大的[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127290&idx=3&sn=04aaac7d1e20fd446929032cb45b6b38&chksm=bc859b158bf212032e5e2121f6066cd1ec171afc141be62e32ad671879a403ade7b3435495bb&scene=21#wechat_redirect)犯罪团伙，他们非法控制的[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)多达5万多台，他们还有一个专门进行[DDoS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126795&idx=3&sn=9038ab35960660611a2d94ac3eb31681&chksm=bc8598e48bf211f22b26629df6dfe1cbf0a08bcf8acfa47740b1ce6afce1f9ab817d994e437a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的QQ群，通过网上接单，然后[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，从中获利。那么，这些购买“傀儡机”实施 [DDoS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126795&idx=3&sn=9038ab35960660611a2d94ac3eb31681&chksm=bc8598e48bf211f22b26629df6dfe1cbf0a08bcf8acfa47740b1ce6afce1f9ab817d994e437a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的，一般都是什么人呢？ 

  

第一种是行业的竞争，同类型网站之间排名的竞争，我把你的网站打瘫痪了，其他的网站可能人家关注度更高一些，同行网站之间的竞争，这个占一定比重；第二种是纯粹的[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、敲诈的犯罪行为，我把你的网站[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)瘫痪了，如果你要想恢复，不受我这个干扰，支付一定的费用，就相当于收取网上的“保护费”。

**警方打掉黑客犯罪链条，41名嫌疑人落网**

  

由于案情重大、涉案人员众多，江苏睢宁警方成立了专案组，经过9个多月的调查取证，渐渐梳理出了一个涉及发单人、“[肉鸡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116724&idx=3&sn=b2218bc876e86e13ddb7777efe05f614&chksm=bc86405b8bf1c94ddfbfcb81e2242e81e48f557de20c187a113d4b4ab1ed78fec1c166028b81&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”商、[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)实施人、买卖[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)权限等具备多个犯罪角色的[网络黑产](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126580&idx=3&sn=c8b22ba96245b8899740c6028d3676be&chksm=bc8599db8bf210cd3d548bd8f108fb7756f53ed04726398699fc03c5d96aef142d6b77755a8a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)链条。

  

聂某从黄某手上租用了大量“[肉鸡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116724&idx=3&sn=b2218bc876e86e13ddb7777efe05f614&chksm=bc86405b8bf1c94ddfbfcb81e2242e81e48f557de20c187a113d4b4ab1ed78fec1c166028b81&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”，他主要是利用非法网站控制权限，[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)其他做排名的人，这个网站非法控制权限的来源主要是从杨某处获得的，杨某就是扫描网站的[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，去上传[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，获取这个网站的非法控制权限，给这个聂某提供技术支持。 

  

在调查过程中，警方发现，除了实施[DDoS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126795&idx=3&sn=9038ab35960660611a2d94ac3eb31681&chksm=bc8598e48bf211f22b26629df6dfe1cbf0a08bcf8acfa47740b1ce6afce1f9ab817d994e437a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)犯罪之外，还有一批技术更好的[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=3&sn=c82f59076adf4123590301c9577f3c59&chksm=bc859b348bf21222ef281bc9aebb654a06582273fd7d9eb73ab84b56adbf383f74305efe3c3c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，像聂某等人，是通过“[肉鸡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116724&idx=3&sn=b2218bc876e86e13ddb7777efe05f614&chksm=bc86405b8bf1c94ddfbfcb81e2242e81e48f557de20c187a113d4b4ab1ed78fec1c166028b81&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”来抢占网站控制权，再进行倒卖牟利的。据[网警](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129790&idx=3&sn=11e84e6198ac348684c43f8a59b2cdad&chksm=bc858d518bf2044777bab43f98099d5ea2fcf713a9a53e5a23371226572415f5584be6b436f9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)介绍，所谓网站控制权，就相当于后门，嫌疑人通过[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127290&idx=3&sn=04aaac7d1e20fd446929032cb45b6b38&chksm=bc859b158bf212032e5e2121f6066cd1ec171afc141be62e32ad671879a403ade7b3435495bb&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术，在这些网站植入[木马病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116716&idx=3&sn=6979add977615d21d7ca4ef9fbee6d55&chksm=bc8640438bf1c955956fe58cb713239ee06d4848176e5fe9409c95a025e3c08cefd8d1d49e90&scene=21#wechat_redirect)后，就可以对网站相关信息进行修改。

  

这两个软件就是嫌疑人用于对其他用户的[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进行控制的，每一个IP地址对应就是一台[电脑](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130768&idx=3&sn=cc77ccdffb6cba3d89fe41c4790efba2&chksm=bc85897f8bf20069f87c5f381951d451265eaa6dbbff136ddf97b06d3261f5106d15f272dab3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，可以看到嫌疑人控制[电脑](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130768&idx=3&sn=cc77ccdffb6cba3d89fe41c4790efba2&chksm=bc85897f8bf20069f87c5f381951d451265eaa6dbbff136ddf97b06d3261f5106d15f272dab3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的数量，其中一个软件控制的是1500多台，而另外一个软件控制的是800多台。

  

在掌握整个团伙犯罪事实和证据后，专案组抽调大量警力，在今年的1月份和3月份，奔赴广东、河南、云南等16个省，20个地级市，先后两次展开了抓捕行动。成功打掉以黄某为核心的[DDoS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126795&idx=3&sn=9038ab35960660611a2d94ac3eb31681&chksm=bc8598e48bf211f22b26629df6dfe1cbf0a08bcf8acfa47740b1ce6afce1f9ab817d994e437a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)团伙，和以聂某、杨某为首的获取、买卖网站[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)控制权限的犯罪团伙。 

  

抓获41人，查获各类网站非法控制权限20余万个，关闭涉案[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)30余台，涉案金额1000多万元。 

  

警方提醒，网络并非法外之地，各相关行业部门一定要做好相关网站的安全管理工作，及时修复网站相关[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，定期升级系统安全方案策略，一旦发现系统被[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，应及时报警。
