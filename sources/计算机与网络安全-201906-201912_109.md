**一、什么是密码？**

  

现实生活中提到“[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”一词，人们通常以为就是每天接触的手机开机“[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”、电子邮箱登录“[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”、微信“[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”、[银行卡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131390&idx=3&sn=e9896516cb490015825f5dd195f7e8ee&chksm=bc858b118bf202074f7da87e08ee1ff48ef80656b5ac7578c43910ba3363d5d632dcef743c1d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)支付“[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”等。实际上，生活中的这些“[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”只是进入个人手机、[电子邮箱](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125751&idx=3&sn=28e2d7d9b8377adde031ce19f5c3ba9a&chksm=bc859d188bf2140e95e2bd0c5f6268a6075563cce4a41ebceff90114e9fa32e14a9029acf5f0&scene=21#wechat_redirect)或者个人银行账户的口令、“通行证”，是一种简单、初级的身份认证手段，是最简易的[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

[密码法](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133703&idx=3&sn=f5049d043227a586bdb95621bd3a1c80&chksm=bc85bde88bf234fec79d33cce0eebfbf2ed103c6af720e879ec4c7f3035561fb2bc4a4e612a7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中的[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，是指采用特定变换的方法对信息等进行加密保护、安全认证的技术、产品和服务。它的主要功能有两个：一个是加密保护，另一个是安全认证。前者是指将原来可读的信息变成不能识别的符号序列，后者是指确认主体和信息的真实可靠性。

  

[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)可以按照不同的标准进行分类。按照保护信息的种类，可以将密码分为核心[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、普通[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和商用[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

**二、核心密码、普通密码和商用密码分别指什么？**

  

核心[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、普通[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和商用[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是按照要保护信息的种类来区分的。

  

核心[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、普通[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)属于国家秘密，用于保护国家秘密信息。核心[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保护信息的最高密级为绝密级，普通[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保护信息的最高密级为机密级，两种[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)都由[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)管理部门依法实行严格统一管理。在有线、无线通信中传递的国家秘密信息，以及存储、处理国家秘密信息的信息系统，应当依照法律、行政法规和国家有关规定使用核心[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、普通[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进行加密保护、安全认证。

  

商用[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)用于保护不属于国家秘密的信息，公民、法人和其他组织可以依法使用商用[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保护网络与[信息安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132187&idx=3&sn=3b147d0bca9c133367b3ef73c799e210&chksm=bc8587f48bf20ee261ea55d8a31c7165f0316955d828185b7433ea21d6649ff9b58f2bc532cf&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。国家鼓励商用[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术的研究开发和应用，健全商用[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)市场体系，鼓励和促进商用[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产业发展，鼓励从业单位自愿接受商用[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)检测认证。

  

三类[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保护的对象不同，对其进行明确划分，有利于确保[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)安全保密，有利于[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)管理部门根据不同信息等级和使用对象对[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)实行科学管理，充分发挥三类[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)在保护网络与[信息安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132187&idx=3&sn=3b147d0bca9c133367b3ef73c799e210&chksm=bc8587f48bf20ee261ea55d8a31c7165f0316955d828185b7433ea21d6649ff9b58f2bc532cf&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中的核心支撑作用。

  

**三、为啥说密码就像网络空间的DNA**

  

[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是保障网络与[信息安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132187&idx=3&sn=3b147d0bca9c133367b3ef73c799e210&chksm=bc8587f48bf20ee261ea55d8a31c7165f0316955d828185b7433ea21d6649ff9b58f2bc532cf&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的核心技术和基础支撑，是解决网络与[信息安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132187&idx=3&sn=3b147d0bca9c133367b3ef73c799e210&chksm=bc8587f48bf20ee261ea55d8a31c7165f0316955d828185b7433ea21d6649ff9b58f2bc532cf&scene=21#wechat_redirect)问题最有效、最可靠、最经济的手段。
