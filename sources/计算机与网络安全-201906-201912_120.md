IP地址是Internet上[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的唯一标识，数字型IP地址对[计算机网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132497&idx=2&sn=142429c138b601edbc0eaae618f1ef14&chksm=bc8586be8bf20fa8c4303f7f7e30b0313d2d9bff51f9da91326fd3c829473bffcc0ac770d5d9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)来讲自然是最有效的，但是对使用网络的用户来说有不便记忆的缺点。与IP地址相比，人们更喜欢使用具有一定含义的字符串来标识Internet上的[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。因此，在Internet中，用户可以用各种各样的方式来命名自己的[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。但是这样就可能在Internet上出现重名，如提供WWW服务的[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)都命名为WWW，提供E-mail服务的[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)都命名为MAIL等，不能唯一地标识Internet上的[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)位置。为了避免重复，Internet网络协会采取了在[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)名后加上后缀名的方法，这个后缀名称为域名，用来标识[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的区域位置，域名是通过申请合法得到的。

  

域名系统就是一种帮助人们在Internet上用名字来唯一标识自己的[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，并保证[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)名和IP地址一一对应的网络服务。

  

**1、域名系统的层次命名机构**

  

所谓层次域名机制，就是按层次结构依次为[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)命名。在Internet中，首先由中央管理机构（Network Information Center，NIC，又称为顶级域）将第一级域名划分为若干部分，包括一些国家代码，如中国用“CN”表示、英国用“UK”表示、日本用“JP”表示等；又由于Internet的形成有其历史的特殊性，主要是在[美国](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128142&idx=3&sn=7b8de69bbda3be653df725fdec9fc24a&chksm=bc8597a18bf21eb7f39f365987d171669e5868d0a43659b4fa35b9053cfc346b30c80e7c5e8a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发展壮大的，Internet的主干网都在[美国](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128142&idx=3&sn=7b8de69bbda3be653df725fdec9fc24a&chksm=bc8597a18bf21eb7f39f365987d171669e5868d0a43659b4fa35b9053cfc346b30c80e7c5e8a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，因此在第一级域名中还包括[美国](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128142&idx=3&sn=7b8de69bbda3be653df725fdec9fc24a&chksm=bc8597a18bf21eb7f39f365987d171669e5868d0a43659b4fa35b9053cfc346b30c80e7c5e8a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的各种组织机构的域名，与其他国家的国家代码同级，都作为一级域名。

  

[美国](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128142&idx=3&sn=7b8de69bbda3be653df725fdec9fc24a&chksm=bc8597a18bf21eb7f39f365987d171669e5868d0a43659b4fa35b9053cfc346b30c80e7c5e8a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中第一级域名一般直接说明其[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的性质，而不是国家代码。如果用户见到某[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的第一级域名由COM或EDU等构成，一般可以判断这台[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)在[美国](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128142&idx=3&sn=7b8de69bbda3be653df725fdec9fc24a&chksm=bc8597a18bf21eb7f39f365987d171669e5868d0a43659b4fa35b9053cfc346b30c80e7c5e8a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)（也有[美国](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128142&idx=3&sn=7b8de69bbda3be653df725fdec9fc24a&chksm=bc8597a18bf21eb7f39f365987d171669e5868d0a43659b4fa35b9053cfc346b30c80e7c5e8a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)第一级域名为US的情况）。其他国家的第一级域名一般都是其国家代码。

  

第一级域名将其各部分的管理权授予相应的机构，如中国域CN授权给国务院信息办，国务院信息办再负责分配第二级域名。第二级域名往往表示[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)所属的网络性质，如是属于教育界还是政府部门等。中国地区的用户第二级域名有教育网（EDU）、邮电网（NET）、科研网（AC）、团体（ORG）、政府（GOV）、商业（COM）、军队（MIL）等。

**2、域名的表示方式**

  

Internet的域名结构是由[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132849&idx=2&sn=ef83e17a64981d39a9c24e568a8b704d&chksm=bc85815e8bf20848e37dfd677f426b6ce368140bcd7048d19d73efe55a8474d47d2081dbee0c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)集的域名系统定义的。域名结构也和IP地址一样，采用典型的层次结构，其通用的格式如图2所示。

![](../Images/174051a83c97631e7c5c8cbe322b8e86.png)

例如，在www.scu.edu.cn这个名字中，www为[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)名，由[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)管理员命名；scu.edu.cn为域名，由[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)管理员合法申请后使用。其中，scu表示四川大学，edu表示国家教育机构部门，cn表示中国。www.scu.edu.cn就表示中国教育机构四川大学的www[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

域名地址是比IP地址更高级、更直观的一种地址表示形式，因此实际使用时人们通常采用域名地址。应该注意的是，在实际使用中，有人将IP地址称为IP号，而将域名地址称为IP地址或者直接称之为地址。但是，Internet中的地址还是应该分成IP地址和域名地址两种，叫法上也要严格区分，但域名地址可以直接称为地址。

**3、域名服务器和域名的解析过程**

  

**（1）域名服务器的功能**

  

Internet上的[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)之间是通过IP地址来进行通信的，而为了用户使用和记忆方便，通常习惯使用域名来表示一台[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。因此，在网络通信过程中，[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的域名必须转换成IP地址，实现这种转换的[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)称为域名[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)（DNS Server）。域名[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是一个基于客户机/[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的[数据库](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129320&idx=3&sn=e5b2f0721ffd54f8891e633fbc280f4b&chksm=bc8593078bf21a1184c1596a84ce0604e00b716de6081c5b27ea0311d1df5e2b5024ba752979&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，在这个[数据库](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129320&idx=3&sn=e5b2f0721ffd54f8891e633fbc280f4b&chksm=bc8593078bf21a1184c1596a84ce0604e00b716de6081c5b27ea0311d1df5e2b5024ba752979&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中，每个[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的域名和IP地址是一一对应的。域名[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的主要功能是回答有关域名、地址、域名到地址或地址到域名的映射的询问以及维护关于询问类型、分类或域名的所有资源记录的列表。

  

为了快速响应询问，域名[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)一般对以下两种类型的域名信息进行管理。

  

1）区域支持的或被授权的本地数据。本地数据中可包含指向其他域名[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的指针，而这些域名[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)可能提供需要的其他域名信息。

  

2）包含有从其他[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的解决方案或回答中采集的信息。

  

**（2）域名的解析过程**

  

域名与IP地址之间的转换，具体可分为两种情况。一种是当目标[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)（要访问的[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)）在本地网络时，由于本地域名[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中含有本地[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)域名与IP地址的对应表，因此这种情况下的解析过程比较简单。首先客户机向本地域名[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发出请求，请求将目标[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的域名解析成IP地址，本地域名[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)检查其管理范围内[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的域名，查出目标[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的域名对应的IP地址，并将解析出的IP地址返回给客户机。另一种是目标[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)不在本地网络，这种情况下的解析过程稍微复杂一些。

  

例如，当某个客户机发出一个请求，要求[DNS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127816&idx=3&sn=2b8d5dfef1801f990c8b7cb25fc65d73&chksm=bc8594e78bf21df19918b7e41f11ca714d5f530030014dd6a48e278b45385d835489385f1cd8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)解析www.sina.com.cn的地址时，具体的解析过程如下。

  

1）客户机先向自身指定的本地[DNS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127816&idx=3&sn=2b8d5dfef1801f990c8b7cb25fc65d73&chksm=bc8594e78bf21df19918b7e41f11ca714d5f530030014dd6a48e278b45385d835489385f1cd8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发送一个查询请求，请求得到www.sina.com.cn的IP地址。

  

2）收到查询请求的本地[DNS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127816&idx=3&sn=2b8d5dfef1801f990c8b7cb25fc65d73&chksm=bc8594e78bf21df19918b7e41f11ca714d5f530030014dd6a48e278b45385d835489385f1cd8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)若未能在[数据库](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129320&idx=3&sn=e5b2f0721ffd54f8891e633fbc280f4b&chksm=bc8593078bf21a1184c1596a84ce0604e00b716de6081c5b27ea0311d1df5e2b5024ba752979&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中找到对应www.sina.com.cn的IP地址，就从根域层的域名[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)开始自上而下地逐层查询，直到找到对应该域名的IP地址为止。

  

3）sina.com.cn域名[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)给本地[DNS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127816&idx=3&sn=2b8d5dfef1801f990c8b7cb25fc65d73&chksm=bc8594e78bf21df19918b7e41f11ca714d5f530030014dd6a48e278b45385d835489385f1cd8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)返回www.sina.com.cn对应的IP地址。

  

4）本地[DNS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127816&idx=3&sn=2b8d5dfef1801f990c8b7cb25fc65d73&chksm=bc8594e78bf21df19918b7e41f11ca714d5f530030014dd6a48e278b45385d835489385f1cd8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)向客户机发送一个回复，其中包含www.sina.com.cn的IP地址。
