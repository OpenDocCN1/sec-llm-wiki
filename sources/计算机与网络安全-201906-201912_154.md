**1、路由器的接口**

  

[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)具有非常强大的网络连接和路由功能，可以与各种不同类型的网络进行物理连接。这就决定了[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的接口技术非常复杂，越是高档的[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)其接口种类也就越多，因为[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)越高档所能连接的网络类型就越多。[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的接口主要分为配置接口、[局域网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133196&idx=3&sn=fced3cdaddc933dbd9589c7cbfb41c3d&chksm=bc8583e38bf20af512424bf8e3328b45e53324d55ba487f38c118ae17454713ce6962aec0da9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)接口和广域网接口3类。

  

**（1）配置接口**

  

[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的配置端口有两个，分别是“CONSOLE”和“AUX”。“CONSOLE”通常用于在进行[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的基本配置时通过专用连线与[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)连接；“AUX”则是用于[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)配置连接。

  

1）CONSOLE 端口。CONSOLE 端口使用配置专用连线直接连接至[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的串口，利用终端仿真程序（如[Windows](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127496&idx=3&sn=21acddfe6a98fe94ae346db72555e138&chksm=bc859a278bf21331d775fdb2224b50832a618a13d3a8fa76c41786186e846f5126daf96682bc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)下的“超级终端”）进行[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)本地配置。[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的CONSOLE端口多为RJ-45端口。

  

2）AUX 端口。AUX 端口为异步端口，主要用于[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)配置，也可用于拨号连接，还可通过收发器与Modem连接。由于AUX与CONSOLE端口用途各不相同，因此[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)通常同时提供这两个端口，如图1所示。

图1  路由器配置接口情况

  

**（2）局域网接口**

  

常见的以太网接口有AUI、BNC和RJ-45接口，另外FDDI、ATM、吉比特以太网等都有相应的网络接口，下面分别介绍几种主要的[局域网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133196&idx=3&sn=fced3cdaddc933dbd9589c7cbfb41c3d&chksm=bc8583e38bf20af512424bf8e3328b45e53324d55ba487f38c118ae17454713ce6962aec0da9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)接口。

  

1）AUI端口。AUI端口是用来与粗同轴电缆连接的接口，是一种“D”型15针接口，在令牌环网或总线形网络中比较常见。[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)可通过粗同轴电缆收发器实现与10 Base-5网络的连接，更多地则是借助于外接的收发转发器（AUI-to-RJ-45）实现与10 Base-T以太网络的连接。当然，也可借助于其他类型的收发转发器实现与细同轴电缆（10 Base-2）或光缆（10 Base-F）的连接。AUI接口情况如图2所示。

![](../Images/2571a00b977ca4a62b2abc4aaf8554f0.png)

图2  AUI接口情况

  

2）RJ-45端口。RJ-45端口是最常见的端口，是人们常见的双绞线以太网端口。因为在快速以太网中也主要采用双绞线作为[传输介质](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132582&idx=2&sn=cc2be4fa4ab260bcfee0310063f901e1&chksm=bc8586498bf20f5f40fe2be3d132b5ffef2151d12397aa5190c477cec69394e8f61e7aea3edd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，所以根据端口的通信速率不同，RJ-45端口又可分为10 Base-T网RJ-45端口和100 Base-TX网RJ-45端口两类。其中，10 Base-T网的RJ-45端口在[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中通常标识为“ETH”，如图3所示；100 Base-TX网的RJ-45端口则通常标识为“10/100b TX”，如图4所示。

![](../Images/77230bcbd3d368a2af175085a8badb3b.png)

图3  10 Base-T网的RJ-45端口

![](../Images/a78dfe67da3925bf8969df7b07f0a098.png)

图4  100 Base-TX 网的RJ-45端口

  

两种RJ-45端口仅就端口本身而言是完全一样的，但端口中对应的网络电路结构是不同的，所以两个端口也不能随便交换。

  

3）SC端口。SC端口是人们常说的光纤端口，主要用于与光纤的连接。光纤通常不直接与工作站相连，而是连接到具有光纤端口的快速以太网或吉比特以太网[交换机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128257&idx=3&sn=4b46cf6a7be7396a95c7d64493cc81c9&chksm=bc85972e8bf21e389f60513d23a5a5fa4af5807a1b4532d03eda374d4890a5f5f13479f5a34e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上。这种端口一般只有在高档[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上才有，都以“100b FX”标注，如图5所示。

![](../Images/df5480cf8d0ef6fdd6396c587475051c.png)

图5  光纤端口

  

**（3）广域网接口**

  

[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)不仅能实现局域网之间的连接，还能应用于[局域网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133196&idx=3&sn=fced3cdaddc933dbd9589c7cbfb41c3d&chksm=bc8583e38bf20af512424bf8e3328b45e53324d55ba487f38c118ae17454713ce6962aec0da9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)与广域网、广域网与广域网之间的互连。广域网规模大，网络环境复杂，因此决定了用于连接广域网的[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)端口速率较高，一般都要求在100Mbit/s的快速以太网以上。下面介绍几种常见的广域网接口。

1）RJ-45端口。利用RJ-45端口也可以建立[局域网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133196&idx=3&sn=fced3cdaddc933dbd9589c7cbfb41c3d&chksm=bc8583e38bf20af512424bf8e3328b45e53324d55ba487f38c118ae17454713ce6962aec0da9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)与广域网之间的[VLAN](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133068&idx=2&sn=0b8205613bd5ca15e35bc0b083f6650b&chksm=bc8580638bf20975ec4c70bddf2af3fd17bb532f718bbe9092304819b4937256c1e0800d6827&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，以及[局域网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133196&idx=3&sn=fced3cdaddc933dbd9589c7cbfb41c3d&chksm=bc8583e38bf20af512424bf8e3328b45e53324d55ba487f38c118ae17454713ce6962aec0da9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)与Internet的连接。如果使用[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)为不同[VLAN](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133068&idx=2&sn=0b8205613bd5ca15e35bc0b083f6650b&chksm=bc8580638bf20975ec4c70bddf2af3fd17bb532f718bbe9092304819b4937256c1e0800d6827&scene=21#wechat_redirect)提供路由，则可以直接利用双绞线连接至不同的[VLAN](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133068&idx=2&sn=0b8205613bd5ca15e35bc0b083f6650b&chksm=bc8580638bf20975ec4c70bddf2af3fd17bb532f718bbe9092304819b4937256c1e0800d6827&scene=21#wechat_redirect)端口。但要注意，这里的RJ-45端口所连接的网络一般不是10 Base-T，而是速率为100Mbit/s的快速以太网或吉比特以太网。图6所示为连接快速以太网的RJ-45端口。

![](../Images/fda9c2c8655a2970774a07ab6bca36d9.png)

图6  快速以太网RJ-45端口

  

2）AUI端口。AUI端口是用于与粗同轴电缆连接的网络接口，也经常用于与广域网的连接。在Cisco 2600系列[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上就提供了AUI与RJ-45两个广域网连接端口，如图7所示。用户可以根据自己的需要选择适当的类型。

![](../Images/b8642dbecb01acf0485f436669c6c7f3.png)

图7  AUI端口

  

3）高速同步串口。在[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的广域网连接中，应用最多的端口是“高速同步串口”（SERIAL），如图8所示。这种端口主要用于连接DDN、ISDN、帧中继、X.25和PSTN等专线网络。这种同步端口对速率的要求非常高，因为该端口连接的网络要求两端的[数据传输](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132673&idx=2&sn=4ebed9559e94616637f5bce407cdd9a5&chksm=bc8581ee8bf208f815cb8279d3c10de158484886ffa52d39680ad373bf945014a4ac69635b03&scene=21#wechat_redirect)必须实时同步。

![](../Images/470f6e54dd67a894c79a13e7e13e9ff2.png)

图8  高速同步串口

  

4）异步串口。异步串口（ASYNC）主要应用于Modem或Modem池的连接，主要用于实现[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)通过公用电话网拨入网络，如图9所示。这种异步端口相对于上面介绍的同步端口而言，在速率要求上没有那么苛刻，并不要求网络的两端数据保持实时同步，只要能连续即可。

![](../Images/aa6fdb5a3d48167f2d6f1074550e3f33.png)

图9  异步串口

  

5）ISDN BRI端口。由于ISDN接入方式在连接速度上有其独特的一面，因而在当时ISDN刚兴起时得到了充分的应用。ISDN有两种速率连接端口：一种是ISDN BRI（基本速率接口），另一种是ISDN PRI（基群速率接口）。

  

ISDNBRI端口用于实现ISDN线路通过[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)与Internet或其他[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)网络的连接，连接速率可达到128kbit/s。ISBN BRI采用RJ-45标准，在与ISDN NT1的连接时使用RJ-45-to-RJ-45的直通双绞线。ISDN BRI端口如图10所示。

**2、路由器的配置方法**

  

以Quidway（华为）系列[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)为例，可以通过5种方法来配置Quidway[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)：① CONSOLE配置；② AUX端口[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)配置；③ [远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)Telnet配置；④ 哑终端配置；⑤ FTP下载配置文件。由于Telnet视图和FTP视图都需要预先对[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进行相应的配置才能生效，而通过AUX端口视图需要连接Modem。所以，第一次配置[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)时，通过CONSOLE端口（配置口）配置是必然的选择。只有先通过CONSOLE端口配置[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，才能使用其他视图。

  

**（1）通过CONSOLE端口配置**

  

通过CONSOLE端口来配置[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是一种最常用的配置方法，也是最基本的[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)视图。用户可以通过控制台端口，使用各种通信程序来访问[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。下面简单介绍如何使用CONSOLE端口来配置[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

1）硬件环境搭配。使用[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)随机附带的配置电缆，将电缆 RJ-45 接头一端接在[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的 CONSOLE端口上，另一端9针（或25针）的RS 232接口接在PC的串行口上。

进入[操作系统](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122728&idx=2&sn=6b263bf85d6b8bd8bf1d3744a491a40c&chksm=bc8668c78bf1e1d1a0e813fcd7b41c7bc300c2c98eb269b77f92854c62f1fe5127a0e01c5574&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，利用“开始”→“程序”→“附件”→“通信”菜单命令，运行“超级终端”，进入如图11所示的界面。用户可以为创建的“超级终端”取一个名字，如“Quidway”。还要为其任意选择一个图标。设置完毕后，单击“确定”按钮，进入下一步。

**（2）通过AUX端口搭建远程配置环境**

  

当需要配置远端的[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)时，可以通过拨号线路连接到需要配置的[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上，可以通过CONSOLE端口、AUX端口或异步串口。

  

1）首先在本地[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的串口上连接Modem，确保能与其他[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)建立拨号连接。

  

2）将另一端的Modem连接到[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的CONSOLE端口、AUX端口或异步串口上，并将Modem加电。

  

3）如果是连接在[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的 CONSOLE 端口或 AUX 端口上，则无须在[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上做任何配置，只需要通知对方先将Modem加电，然后再对[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)加电即可。

  

如果是接在[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的异步串口上，则需要设置串口工作在交互方式下，配置命令如下。

  

\[Quidway-Serial0/0\] physical-mode async；设置为异步模式

\[Quidway-Serial0/0\] async mode flow；设置为交互模式

  

4）设置好[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的相关属性并建立好连接后，即可通过“超级终端”建立与[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的拨号连接，如图15和图16所示。
