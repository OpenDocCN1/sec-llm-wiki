 

**一、总体要求**

  

**（一）指导思想**

  

以习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想为指导，深入贯彻习近平总书记关于[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132051&idx=3&sn=6b7a3f3dc684d1464a532c7417b03b91&chksm=bc85847c8bf20d6a77567fc8d4ffb9dcf61d14b87241d0bb734507b597e395212e759fdaddd1&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的系列重要讲话精神，坚持新发展理念，树立正确的[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132067&idx=3&sn=bc78826629c1a380b70cbf304ab57a73&chksm=bc85844c8bf20d5a78ab1410dccb317f44eaf355727afaaf9937239165e233c49caa7a589a2d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)观，贯彻落实[《网络安全法》](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655113238&idx=4&sn=7a6aeba5889cedfd8cd21ca949ce6dbb&chksm=bc864df98bf1c4efdc987d135c7571718de3351fdd3c22ba266e90e0f2162ee811ee420143f4&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，以服务国家网络空间安全战略需求为导向，主动应对[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、[大数据](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128534&idx=3&sn=e679f67ac39ab11dbf5f8cae74032564&chksm=bc8596398bf21f2ffb6d133b630409ede8176bcaaa3d12565e956b45bfd287978ab050e9230d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、[人工智能](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127156&idx=3&sn=a7f6d0290ce96e83acb46ff63fe8acfd&chksm=bc859b9b8bf2128da489de501e4853f4a09bd469a2c28214f4f7e5da3267e3413469f7e4e124&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和实体经济深度融合伴生的新风险，积极应对5G、工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、下一代[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、物联网等新技术新应用带来的新挑战，坚持市场主导、政府引导，着力突破关键技术、构建产业生态、优化发展环境，推动我国[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid 需求引领。推动各行业各领域持续加大[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132224&idx=3&sn=7e64086a48eb3860ec31e33c8dfb4a02&chksm=bc8587af8bf20eb9a21bd846b2419c75ede4cddfede8cf5630d367bb5ee73d71eae6d46d7535&scene=21#wechat_redirect)投入，坚持问题导向，加强供需对接，促使产业更好满足金融、能源、通信、交通、电子政务等重点领域[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132257&idx=3&sn=48dc055037ffeb6b822a7873e3261cbc&chksm=bc85878e8bf20e98065e89299b9582511cd454eeaeece0c82d54975db5f7f685a7d501ceafe9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)需求。
