**1、TCP/IP概述**  
说到[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122664&idx=2&sn=217886990c4b23d220863fb26a6181ea&chksm=bc8669078bf1e011c6885022439c157b068a0203761967900beb8c6f5a61080da19dbb470140&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的历史，不得不谈到Internet的历史。20世纪60年代初期，[美国](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128142&idx=3&sn=7b8de69bbda3be653df725fdec9fc24a&chksm=bc8597a18bf21eb7f39f365987d171669e5868d0a43659b4fa35b9053cfc346b30c80e7c5e8a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)国防部委托高级研究计划局（Advanced Research Pojects Agency，ARPA）研制广域网络互连课题，并建立了ARPANET实验网络，这就是Internet的起源。ARPANET的初期运行情况表明，[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)广域网络应该有一种标准化的通信协议，于是在1973年[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122664&idx=2&sn=217886990c4b23d220863fb26a6181ea&chksm=bc8669078bf1e011c6885022439c157b068a0203761967900beb8c6f5a61080da19dbb470140&scene=21#wechat_redirect)诞生了。虽然ARPANET并未发展成为公众可以使用的Internet，但是ARPANET的运行经验表明，[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122664&idx=2&sn=217886990c4b23d220863fb26a6181ea&chksm=bc8669078bf1e011c6885022439c157b068a0203761967900beb8c6f5a61080da19dbb470140&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是一个非常可靠且实用的[网络协议](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122060&idx=3&sn=430f3ac5d4a2946fdf391ee9c07b0a13&chksm=bc866f638bf1e6758a9237a97ec3e782f1f074bace9fe04f5ffcaf0fbc2614e6fafef05239a0&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。当现代Internet的雏形——[美国](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128142&idx=3&sn=7b8de69bbda3be653df725fdec9fc24a&chksm=bc8597a18bf21eb7f39f365987d171669e5868d0a43659b4fa35b9053cfc346b30c80e7c5e8a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)国家科学基础网（NSFNET）于20世纪80年代末出现时，借鉴了ARPANET的[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122664&idx=2&sn=217886990c4b23d220863fb26a6181ea&chksm=bc8669078bf1e011c6885022439c157b068a0203761967900beb8c6f5a61080da19dbb470140&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术。借助于[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122664&idx=2&sn=217886990c4b23d220863fb26a6181ea&chksm=bc8669078bf1e011c6885022439c157b068a0203761967900beb8c6f5a61080da19dbb470140&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术，NSFNET使越来越多的网络互连在一起，最终形成了今天的Internet。[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122664&idx=2&sn=217886990c4b23d220863fb26a6181ea&chksm=bc8669078bf1e011c6885022439c157b068a0203761967900beb8c6f5a61080da19dbb470140&scene=21#wechat_redirect)也因此成为了Internet上广泛使用的标准网络通信协议。  
[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122664&idx=2&sn=217886990c4b23d220863fb26a6181ea&chksm=bc8669078bf1e011c6885022439c157b068a0203761967900beb8c6f5a61080da19dbb470140&scene=21#wechat_redirect)标准由一系列的文档定义组成，这些文档定义描述了Internet的内部实现机制，以及各种网络服务或服务的定义。[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122664&idx=2&sn=217886990c4b23d220863fb26a6181ea&chksm=bc8669078bf1e011c6885022439c157b068a0203761967900beb8c6f5a61080da19dbb470140&scene=21#wechat_redirect)标准并不是由某个特定组织开发的，实际上是由一些团体共同开发的，任何人都可以把自己的意见作为文档发布，但只有被认可的文档才能最终成为Internet标准。  
作为一套完整的网络通信协议，[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122664&idx=2&sn=217886990c4b23d220863fb26a6181ea&chksm=bc8669078bf1e011c6885022439c157b068a0203761967900beb8c6f5a61080da19dbb470140&scene=21#wechat_redirect)实际上是一个协议簇。除了其核心协议——[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和IP之外，[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122664&idx=2&sn=217886990c4b23d220863fb26a6181ea&chksm=bc8669078bf1e011c6885022439c157b068a0203761967900beb8c6f5a61080da19dbb470140&scene=21#wechat_redirect)簇还包括一系列其他协议，包含在[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122664&idx=2&sn=217886990c4b23d220863fb26a6181ea&chksm=bc8669078bf1e011c6885022439c157b068a0203761967900beb8c6f5a61080da19dbb470140&scene=21#wechat_redirect)簇的4个层次中，形成了[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122664&idx=2&sn=217886990c4b23d220863fb26a6181ea&chksm=bc8669078bf1e011c6885022439c157b068a0203761967900beb8c6f5a61080da19dbb470140&scene=21#wechat_redirect)栈，如图1所示。

**2、TCP/IP参考模型各层的功能**

 
根据[OSI模型](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132810&idx=2&sn=ee7f1720a26e1b82f9fb49a3657bc698&chksm=bc8581658bf2087393c30977fcac770581ab0565ccbb20dc781cc0caf6da8284d33405244c10&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的经验，会话层和表示层对大多数应用程序没有用处，所以[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122664&idx=2&sn=217886990c4b23d220863fb26a6181ea&chksm=bc8669078bf1e011c6885022439c157b068a0203761967900beb8c6f5a61080da19dbb470140&scene=21#wechat_redirect)参考模型将其排除在外。[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122664&idx=2&sn=217886990c4b23d220863fb26a6181ea&chksm=bc8669078bf1e011c6885022439c157b068a0203761967900beb8c6f5a61080da19dbb470140&scene=21#wechat_redirect)参考模型各层次的功能如下。  
**（1）主机—网络层**  
[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)—网络层（Host to Network Layer）是[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122664&idx=2&sn=217886990c4b23d220863fb26a6181ea&chksm=bc8669078bf1e011c6885022439c157b068a0203761967900beb8c6f5a61080da19dbb470140&scene=21#wechat_redirect)参考模型中的最低层。事实上，[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122664&idx=2&sn=217886990c4b23d220863fb26a6181ea&chksm=bc8669078bf1e011c6885022439c157b068a0203761967900beb8c6f5a61080da19dbb470140&scene=21#wechat_redirect)参考模型并没有真正定义这一部分，只是指出在这一层上必须具有物理层和数据链路层的功能，以实现从网络层传送下来的数据发送到目的[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的网络层。至于在这一层上使用哪些标准，则不是[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122664&idx=2&sn=217886990c4b23d220863fb26a6181ea&chksm=bc8669078bf1e011c6885022439c157b068a0203761967900beb8c6f5a61080da19dbb470140&scene=21#wechat_redirect)参考模型关心的。  
在[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)—网络层中包含了多种网络层协议，如以太网协议（Ethernet）、令牌环网协议（Token Ring）、分组交换网协议（X.25）等。

**（2）互连层**  
互连层（Internet Layer）是[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122664&idx=2&sn=217886990c4b23d220863fb26a6181ea&chksm=bc8669078bf1e011c6885022439c157b068a0203761967900beb8c6f5a61080da19dbb470140&scene=21#wechat_redirect)参考模型中的第2层，是整个[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122664&idx=2&sn=217886990c4b23d220863fb26a6181ea&chksm=bc8669078bf1e011c6885022439c157b068a0203761967900beb8c6f5a61080da19dbb470140&scene=21#wechat_redirect)参考模型的关键部分。互连层提供的是无连接的服务，主要负责将源[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的数据分组（Packet）发送到目的[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。源[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)与目的[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)既可以在同一个物理网内，也可以不在一个物理网内。  
互连层上定义了正式的数据分组格式和协议，即网际协议（Internet Protocol，IP）。除了IP之外，还包括一些用于互连层的控制协议，如Internet控制报文协议（Internet Control Message Protocol，ICMP）、地址解析协议（Address Resolution Protocol，ARP）、反向地址解析协议（Reverse Address Resolution Protocol，RARP）等。  
互连层的主要功能包括以下几点。  
1）处理来自传输层的分组发送请求。在接收到分组发送请求之后，将分组装入IP数据报，填充报头，选择发送路径，然后将数据报发送到相应的网络。  
2）处理接收到的数据报。在接收到其他[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发送的数据报之后，检查目的地址，若需要转发，则选择发送路径，转发出去；如果目的地址为节点IP地址，则除去报头，将分组交送到传输层处理。  
3）进行流量控制与拥塞控制。  
**（3）传输层**  
传输层（Transport Layer）是[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122664&idx=2&sn=217886990c4b23d220863fb26a6181ea&chksm=bc8669078bf1e011c6885022439c157b068a0203761967900beb8c6f5a61080da19dbb470140&scene=21#wechat_redirect)参考模型中的第3层。传输层的主要功能是使发送方[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和接收方[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上的对等实体可以进行会话。从这一点上看，[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122664&idx=2&sn=217886990c4b23d220863fb26a6181ea&chksm=bc8669078bf1e011c6885022439c157b068a0203761967900beb8c6f5a61080da19dbb470140&scene=21#wechat_redirect)参考模型的传输层和[OSI参考模型](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132810&idx=2&sn=ee7f1720a26e1b82f9fb49a3657bc698&chksm=bc8581658bf2087393c30977fcac770581ab0565ccbb20dc781cc0caf6da8284d33405244c10&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的传输层功能类似。  
在传输层上定义了以下两个端到端的协议。  
1）传输控制协议。传输控制协议（Transmission Control Protocol，[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)）是一个面向连接的协议，允许从源[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发出的字节流无差错地传送到网络上的其他[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上。在发送端，[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)把应用层的字节流分成多个报文段并传给互连层。在接收端，[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)把收到的报文段再封装成字节流，送往应用层。[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)同时还要处理流量控制，以避免高速发送方[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)向低速接收方[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发送的报文过多而造成接收方[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)无法处理的情况。  
2）用户数据报协议。用户数据报协议（User Datagram Protocol，UDP）是一个不可靠的、无连接的协议。UDP主要用于不需要数据分组顺序到达的传输环境中，同时也被广泛应用于只有一次的、客户/[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)（Client/Server，C/S）模式的请求应答查询，以及快速传送比准确传送更重要的应用程序（如传输语音或影像）中。  
**（4）应用层**  
应用层（Application Layer）是[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122664&idx=2&sn=217886990c4b23d220863fb26a6181ea&chksm=bc8669078bf1e011c6885022439c157b068a0203761967900beb8c6f5a61080da19dbb470140&scene=21#wechat_redirect)参考模型的最高层。应用层负责向用户提供一组常用的应用程序，如电子邮件、[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127682&idx=3&sn=22db7784dea4d838745bde91f23e55e6&chksm=bc85956d8bf21c7bab2ee1082cf453865066b19d1a92332b2d70915297dbfe8e438fd6ada1c0&scene=21#wechat_redirect)登录、文件传输等。应用层包含了[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122664&idx=2&sn=217886990c4b23d220863fb26a6181ea&chksm=bc8669078bf1e011c6885022439c157b068a0203761967900beb8c6f5a61080da19dbb470140&scene=21#wechat_redirect)簇中的所有高层协议，如文件传输协议（File Transfer Protocol，FTP）、电子邮件协议（Simple Mail Transfer Protocol，SMTP）、超文本传输协议（Hyper Text Transfer Protocol，HTTP）、简单网络管理协议（Simple Network Management Protocol，SNMP）和域名系统协议（Domain Name System，[DNS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127816&idx=3&sn=2b8d5dfef1801f990c8b7cb25fc65d73&chksm=bc8594e78bf21df19918b7e41f11ca714d5f530030014dd6a48e278b45385d835489385f1cd8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)）等。  
应用层协议一般可以分为3类：一类是依赖于面向连接的[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，如文件传输协议、电子邮件协议等；一类是依赖于无连接的UDP，如简单网络管理协议；还有一类则既依赖于[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，又依赖于UDP，如域名系统协议。
