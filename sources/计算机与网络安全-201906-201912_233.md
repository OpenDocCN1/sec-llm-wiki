近日，中共中央总书记、国家主席、中央军委主席习近平对国家[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129537&idx=3&sn=1c8a2b85ec3a063ce7a132992d32a3be&chksm=bc85922e8bf21b3810cf89c2dba2b7041b8a93bf3df7d28e4ac4a87791862e41bf791bc21304&scene=21#wechat_redirect)宣传周作出重要指示强调，举办[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131366&idx=2&sn=18215226672d73083f399efc8e61ab76&chksm=bc858b098bf2021f4ba79a264088a3761321619396e24fc817ec636744973de380bacdcd88b6&scene=21#wechat_redirect)宣传周、提升全民[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131340&idx=2&sn=f0a410fd4ac4d8564cf40c2f1284a3bf&chksm=bc858b238bf20235f42eff940d48927ae2b76820b953edb729e99bb5c9cd4d7938554025f20a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)意识和技能，是国家[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132059&idx=2&sn=59415d501c548f6795570f9b6b4f114c&chksm=bc8584748bf20d624ec9a84be5f53cef4a206290202e01409ebe0bd44d93064a41e28cbc18ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工作的重要内容。国家[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132359&idx=3&sn=16601adf8f240ad2b39c01b0fa08109f&chksm=bc8587288bf20e3e0c79c577de4ae338f8b00fbcd0ca5dbf66b65bff5f61805d1d27a30c0a2d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工作要坚持[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132224&idx=3&sn=7e64086a48eb3860ec31e33c8dfb4a02&chksm=bc8587af8bf20eb9a21bd846b2419c75ede4cddfede8cf5630d367bb5ee73d71eae6d46d7535&scene=21#wechat_redirect)为人民、[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132102&idx=2&sn=db1f82b8b573158d7aa22e99c24ddcab&chksm=bc8584298bf20d3f0a8214632899e99f347519865d85bff3c6784126bfe4e0a3e8fefd17fd12&scene=21#wechat_redirect)靠人民，保障个人[信息安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132187&idx=3&sn=3b147d0bca9c133367b3ef73c799e210&chksm=bc8587f48bf20ee261ea55d8a31c7165f0316955d828185b7433ea21d6649ff9b58f2bc532cf&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，维护公民在网络空间的合法权益。要坚持[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132051&idx=3&sn=6b7a3f3dc684d1464a532c7417b03b91&chksm=bc85847c  

2019年国家[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131886&idx=3&sn=8b6e208a5ee199d96e6a55687aaff452&chksm=bc8585018bf20c17b19265ceba54cce7740430716f7b435ea99ce8b3aca187a9b90af76976ce&scene=21#wechat_redirect)宣传周开幕式16日在天津举行。中共中央政治局委员、中宣部部长黄坤明在开幕式上宣读习近平的重要指示并讲话。他说，要认真学习贯彻习近平总书记重要指示精神，深刻把握信息化发展大势，积极应对[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131857&idx=3&sn=f05704b1a33a118f5974dc1e0e3d8fde&chksm=bc85853e8bf20c282a388c76d4472ec1e0c54b97c6f96ce0179e0c75f9d0922a988125c9881d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)挑战，充分发挥广大人民在维护[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中的主体作用，把“四个坚持”的原则要求落到实处，有力维护人民群众在网络空间的切身利益。

  

国家[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655120585&idx=2&sn=9622d3fd3041997ec13b8f8e385a717d&chksm=bc8671268bf1f830b295b492d4537709131dec78eb7e709d8a6b7d4bcbafd155c70245f182fb&scene=21#wechat_redirect)宣传周从2014年开始每年举办一届。本届宣传周以“[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127174&idx=3&sn=b0c84359114c49067f3728971b0f3233&chksm=bc859b698bf2127f130740c2ede1cf2f75fdf7c7d2dcaa4200f1055d9094c6b6d81b03b36fbc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)为人民，[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127030&idx=3&sn=2adb039c3bf3066eb9ebe819fe2f3310&chksm=bc8598198bf2110fc9fd93ef57c9165eb6776a4da15d16fa810f7dd1d13222dfba6f98876135&scene=21#wechat_redirect)靠人民”为主题，9月16日至22日期间将举办[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126563&idx=3&sn=14023df84f67afb48342aae5f5fe71f7&chksm=bc8599cc8bf210da3250510eb9d50c84270e15141f1eac9d3f98a726114f5d3e51263495ecf9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)博览会、网络安全技术高峰论坛、[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126536&idx=2&sn=39c4220685f09a248d786269d734f524&chksm=bc8599e78bf210f1dc14c61a61a3df29707dc40e245ea220752d07d28b9aeb3b63631678ef3c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)主题日等活动。
