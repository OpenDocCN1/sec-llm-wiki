2018年12月1日，多名用户称其[电脑](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130768&idx=3&sn=cc77ccdffb6cba3d89fe41c4790efba2&chksm=bc85897f8bf20069f87c5f381951d451265eaa6dbbff136ddf97b06d3261f5106d15f272dab3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)感染一款需要使用手机扫描[微信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127698&idx=3&sn=5101f6a80628d7caf6bc8ed7eec325c5&chksm=bc85957d8bf21c6bad5b83f1dcf2124043fa2e6a19eee9f604e23db0211b7ddcf8d94ecf97b8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)支付二维码支付110元赎金的[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。据悉，这是国内首次出现要求使用[微信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127698&idx=3&sn=5101f6a80628d7caf6bc8ed7eec325c5&chksm=bc85957d8bf21c6bad5b83f1dcf2124043fa2e6a19eee9f604e23db0211b7ddcf8d94ecf97b8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)支付的勒索[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，该[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)采用“供应链感染”方式通过论坛传播，同时还窃取用户淘宝、支付宝、[QQ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127653&idx=3&sn=e54fd5479003f9480f8a00bce7023731&chksm=bc85958a8bf21c9c42abd595f1b2f8d28b4f05eb71155c9ae1c126f19ea67b4196f1b4fbca5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)等账号[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126686&idx=3&sn=c588c739341ee07b2b1d6a84c5e1a6ec&chksm=bc8599718bf210670bfe501723741133e318ca7abcc697f1d1fd5236a9490f3bd6b555342dd9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

2018年12月5日，公安机关将制作这款[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=3&sn=c82f59076adf4123590301c9577f3c59&chksm=bc859b348bf21222ef281bc9aebb654a06582273fd7d9eb73ab84b56adbf383f74305efe3c3c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)罗某抓获，并在其住处查获[电脑](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130768&idx=3&sn=cc77ccdffb6cba3d89fe41c4790efba2&chksm=bc85897f8bf20069f87c5f381951d451265eaa6dbbff136ddf97b06d3261f5106d15f272dab3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、手机等作案工具。

  

据了解，罗某是一名高中辍学的“95后”，虽文化程度不高，但对[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)兴趣浓厚。被告人罗某2018年1月在东莞东坑镇一间出租屋研发“cheat”[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，预谋盗取他人支付宝等网络账号和[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126686&idx=3&sn=c588c739341ee07b2b1d6a84c5e1a6ec&chksm=bc8599718bf210670bfe501723741133e318ca7abcc697f1d1fd5236a9490f3bd6b555342dd9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。2018年11月，他租用河南郑州一家网络公司[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，将[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)植入一个软件模块并在论坛发布，[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)会对下载该软件的[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进行感染。该[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)运行后，对[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的文件进行加密，并弹出附有[微信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127698&idx=3&sn=5101f6a80628d7caf6bc8ed7eec325c5&chksm=bc85957d8bf21c6bad5b83f1dcf2124043fa2e6a19eee9f604e23db0211b7ddcf8d94ecf97b8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)收款二维码的勒索窗口，向每台感染[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)用户敲诈勒索110元，共收款37笔，其中一笔30元，非法所得3990元。

  

经司法鉴定，该[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序会对用户[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中存储的数据进行加密，为未授权地修改、干扰，故为破坏性程序，共感染[电脑](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130768&idx=3&sn=cc77ccdffb6cba3d89fe41c4790efba2&chksm=bc85897f8bf20069f87c5f381951d451265eaa6dbbff136ddf97b06d3261f5106d15f272dab3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)数量为27939台；该[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)具有通过监控用户键鼠操作来获取用户网络账号、[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126686&idx=3&sn=c588c739341ee07b2b1d6a84c5e1a6ec&chksm=bc8599718bf210670bfe501723741133e318ca7abcc697f1d1fd5236a9490f3bd6b555342dd9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)信息的功能，共记录键盘鼠标操作记录有21546条。检察机关认为，被告人罗某违反国家规定，故意制造、传播[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)等破坏性程序，影响[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统正常运行，应当以破坏[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)信息系统罪追究其刑事责任。

  

法院认为，罗某故意制造、传播[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)等破坏性程序，影响[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统正常运行，后果特别严重，其行为已构成破坏[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)信息系统罪，其间，还进行盗窃、敲诈勒索，同时也触犯敲诈勒索罪、盗窃罪。根据被告人罗某的犯罪情节及悔罪表现，法院判处其有期徒刑六年六个月。
