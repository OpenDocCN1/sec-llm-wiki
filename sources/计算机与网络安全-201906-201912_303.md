8月14，柬埔寨警方与中国[公安部](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129746&idx=3&sn=d5040d38e863cab36a670a36b632f1b4&chksm=bc858d7d8bf2046b0048fdda6c78fb73329334added1f9f4f673a5671a567a35d92005831098&scene=21#wechat_redirect)联手打击[电信诈骗](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127440&idx=3&sn=6b3824a940faea24e75e485835aa3cce&chksm=bc859a7f8bf2136965b01e762237c066d239933d838a14c1c4ea5990186ca3a9bc7e9fe5f74d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，西港127名中国籍[电信诈骗](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127440&idx=3&sn=6b3824a940faea24e75e485835aa3cce&chksm=bc859a7f8bf2136965b01e762237c066d239933d838a14c1c4ea5990186ca3a9bc7e9fe5f74d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)嫌犯被抓，其中女性11人。

  

据悉，警方在西港突袭了两个地点，抓获了上述的127名[电信诈骗](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127440&idx=3&sn=6b3824a940faea24e75e485835aa3cce&chksm=bc859a7f8bf2136965b01e762237c066d239933d838a14c1c4ea5990186ca3a9bc7e9fe5f74d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)嫌犯。据透露，此次行动由柬埔寨反恐和跨境犯罪局、反商业赌博局和中国[公安部](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129746&idx=3&sn=d5040d38e863cab36a670a36b632f1b4&chksm=bc858d7d8bf2046b0048fdda6c78fb73329334added1f9f4f673a5671a567a35d92005831098&scene=21#wechat_redirect)联合开展。

  

据警方透露的消息称，这127名嫌疑人中还包含通缉犯。据了解，他们涉嫌利用[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)等对中国公民实施[电信诈骗](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127440&idx=3&sn=6b3824a940faea24e75e485835aa3cce&chksm=bc859a7f8bf2136965b01e762237c066d239933d838a14c1c4ea5990186ca3a9bc7e9fe5f74d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，进行敲诈勒索。

  

![](../Images/dcd920d1439ca36388049482d4524b72.png)

  

西港127名被捕的[网络诈骗](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128457&idx=3&sn=e1a19005f3cc770f694f95bf08f596b0&chksm=bc8596668bf21f7071fe1691b16851873db81144dae1da0edb5214f8330722b4c87eb4f96e59&scene=21#wechat_redirect)嫌犯已被带至金边，等候专机遣返回国。这些嫌犯因涉嫌使用网络对中国公民进行诈骗而被逮捕。

  

被逮捕后，上述127名中国籍[网络诈骗](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128457&idx=3&sn=e1a19005f3cc770f694f95bf08f596b0&chksm=bc8596668bf21f7071fe1691b16851873db81144dae1da0edb5214f8330722b4c87eb4f96e59&scene=21#wechat_redirect)嫌犯接受了反恐怖和跨境犯罪警方的盘问，同日晚上被带到金边移民总局，等候从中国飞来的专机，以将他们遣返回国。
