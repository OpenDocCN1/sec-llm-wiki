随着智能手机的普及，一些手机[App](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130459&idx=3&sn=f8b64d61307c28f9f3265b4eb1eda12a&chksm=bc858eb48bf207a2b2fd53128cd92797950f71b4e141abfb9c60970b6a662bc1849e85912dff&scene=21#wechat_redirect)过度收集用户信息、侵犯用户[隐私](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127519&idx=3&sn=a846e840b4ac021e01ba4a64c9228817&chksm=bc859a308bf2132693545d19fdb92d481e63a9d92d18960c1e4c198747b628d2bad763105175&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的问题屡见不鲜。

  

全国信息安全标准化技术委员会日前发布了有关“[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)应用收集[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)基本规范”草案，面向社会公开征求意见，这意味着[App](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130459&idx=3&sn=f8b64d61307c28f9f3265b4eb1eda12a&chksm=bc858eb48bf207a2b2fd53128cd92797950f71b4e141abfb9c60970b6a662bc1849e85912dff&scene=21#wechat_redirect)收集[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)有了“国标”。  
此次《规范》对网贷[App](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130459&idx=3&sn=f8b64d61307c28f9f3265b4eb1eda12a&chksm=bc858eb48bf207a2b2fd53128cd92797950f71b4e141abfb9c60970b6a662bc1849e85912dff&scene=21#wechat_redirect)做出了要求，不应强制读取用户通讯录。  
《规范》明确了[App](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130459&idx=3&sn=f8b64d61307c28f9f3265b4eb1eda12a&chksm=bc858eb48bf207a2b2fd53128cd92797950f71b4e141abfb9c60970b6a662bc1849e85912dff&scene=21#wechat_redirect)收集[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)应满足的管理要求和技术要求。  

**也就是说[App](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130459&idx=3&sn=f8b64d61307c28f9f3265b4eb1eda12a&chksm=bc858eb48bf207a2b2fd53128cd92797950f71b4e141abfb9c60970b6a662bc1849e85912dff&scene=21#wechat_redirect)不能再强制索要用户信息，哪怕用户拒绝提供额外[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，仍然可以使用[App](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130459&idx=3&sn=f8b64d61307c28f9f3265b4eb1eda12a&chksm=bc858eb48bf207a2b2fd53128cd92797950f71b4e141abfb9c60970b6a662bc1849e85912dff&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。此外，《规范》还要求，对外共享、转让[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)前，[App](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130459&idx=3&sn=f8b64d61307c28f9f3265b4eb1eda12a&chksm=bc858eb48bf207a2b2fd53128cd92797950f71b4e141abfb9c60970b6a662bc1849e85912dff&scene=21#wechat_redirect)应事先征得用户明示同意。**
