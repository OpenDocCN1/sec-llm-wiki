读写文件是最常见的IO操作。[Python](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126409&idx=3&sn=7880b5cffc9b669e5548a0b522ee0fbd&chksm=bc859e668bf2177034e513527cc10ed27435a3f3aa7f7d862a98149f140d8cd21a179c1d017d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)内置了读写文件的函数，用法和[C语言](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130890&idx=3&sn=0dd3fcf40471b5fa90403f9b82dfdf9d&chksm=bc8588e58bf201f3e5a4c7c8ec4b234eb3aa5cac0487ad4a573473783e437e3da29809f2ecca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的读写文件非常类似。在磁盘上读写文件的功能都是由[操作系统](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122728&idx=2&sn=6b263bf85d6b8bd8bf1d3744a491a40c&chksm=bc8668c78bf1e1d1a0e813fcd7b41c7bc300c2c98eb269b77f92854c62f1fe5127a0e01c5574&scene=21#wechat_redirect)提供的，现代[操作系统](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122728&idx=2&sn=6b263bf85d6b8bd8bf1d3744a491a40c&chksm=bc8668c78bf1e1d1a0e813fcd7b41c7bc300c2c98eb269b77f92854c62f1fe5127a0e01c5574&scene=21#wechat_redirect)不允许普通的程序直接操作磁盘，所以，读写文件就是请求[操作系统](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122728&idx=2&sn=6b263bf85d6b8bd8bf1d3744a491a40c&chksm=bc8668c78bf1e1d1a0e813fcd7b41c7bc300c2c98eb269b77f92854c62f1fe5127a0e01c5574&scene=21#wechat_redirect)打开一个文件对象（通常称为文件描述符），然后，通过[操作系统](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122728&idx=2&sn=6b263bf85d6b8bd8bf1d3744a491a40c&chksm=bc8668c78bf1e1d1a0e813fcd7b41c7bc300c2c98eb269b77f92854c62f1fe5127a0e01c5574&scene=21#wechat_redirect)提供的接口从这个文件对象中读取数据（读文件），或者把数据写入这个文件对象（写文件）。
