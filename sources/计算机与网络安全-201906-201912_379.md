[汇编语言](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130595&idx=3&sn=286d73c539d290710890ea96a73a0bdb&chksm=bc858e0c8bf2071a56c52df98623092c5b5defa5cda26c862d737070f8e38b0a7dd3cfa0c42e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)（assembly language）是一种用于电子[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130694&idx=3&sn=832595c69e0c2095eeaf458d4cd628bb&chksm=bc8589a98bf200bfdbccd6fd1c9f3531adb05e73556c5bbd7fb0a3a82aa9009b2cedddf83028&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、微处理器、微控制器或其他可[编程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128726&idx=3&sn=6bbfafcc4786966456c6ec9fd0ef37a8&chksm=bc8591798bf2186f7df0c0558b10945b9c71cf8dae01be5a80844bd7e6480ff5febe4a7ecfe6&scene=21#wechat_redirect)器件的低级语言，亦称为符号语言。在[汇编语言](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130595&idx=3&sn=286d73c539d290710890ea96a73a0bdb&chksm=bc858e0c8bf2071a56c52df98623092c5b5defa5cda26c862d737070f8e38b0a7dd3cfa0c42e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中，用助记符（Mnemonics）代替机器指令的操作码，用地址符号（Symbol）或标号（Label）代替指令或操作数的地址。在不同的设备中，[汇编语言](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130595&idx=3&sn=286d73c539d290710890ea96a73a0bdb&chksm=bc858e0c8bf2071a56c52df98623092c5b5defa5cda26c862d737070f8e38b0a7dd3cfa0c42e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)对应着不同的机器语言指令集，通过[汇编](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130595&idx=3&sn=286d73c539d290710890ea96a73a0bdb&chksm=bc858e0c8bf2071a56c52df98623092c5b5defa5cda26c862d737070f8e38b0a7dd3cfa0c42e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)过程转换成机器指令。普遍地说，特定的[汇编语言](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130595&idx=3&sn=286d73c539d290710890ea96a73a0bdb&chksm=bc858e0c8bf2071a56c52df98623092c5b5defa5cda26c862d737070f8e38b0a7dd3cfa0c42e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和特定的机器语言指令集是一一对应的，不同平台之间不可直接移植。
