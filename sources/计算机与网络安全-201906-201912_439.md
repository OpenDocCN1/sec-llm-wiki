计算机网络从20世纪60年代发展至今，已经形成从小型的办公[局域网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127596&idx=3&sn=2b17fff793ef78538de02f7efa722d07&chksm=bc8595c38bf21cd534d05234f6b7fcf6ca8686efce3e93ae458e888c959af3de713dced11ad6&scene=21#wechat_redirect)络到全球性[广域网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123194&idx=2&sn=2ffb753a732f09751f891f376a72f37e&chksm=bc866b158bf1e20335c05d9945c40485a1da8c5c75b6a43994c92eb5a7ee02224f15ecd43232&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的规模，对现代人类的生产、经济、生活等方方面面都产生了巨大的影响。1962年，由[美国](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128142&idx=3&sn=7b8de69bbda3be653df725fdec9fc24a&chksm=bc8597a18bf21eb7f39f365987d171669e5868d0a43659b4fa35b9053cfc346b30c80e7c5e8a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)国防部（DOD）资助、国防部高级研究计划局（ARPA）主持研究建立了数据包交换计算机网络ARPANET。ARPANET利用租用的通信线路，将[美国](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128142&idx=3&sn=7b8de69bbda3be653df725fdec9fc24a&chksm=bc8597a18bf21eb7f39f365987d171669e5868d0a43659b4fa35b9053cfc346b30c80e7c5e8a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)加州大学洛杉矶分校、加州大学圣巴巴拉分校、斯坦福大学和犹他大学四个节点的计算机连接起来，构成了专门完成主机间通信任务的通信子网。该网络采用分组交换技术传送信息，这种技术能够保证四所大学间的网络不会因为某条线路被切断，而影响其他线路间的通信。当时的人们根本想不到，20年后计算机网络在现代信息社会中会扮演如此重要的角色。ARPANET已从最初四个节点发展成为横跨全世界一百多个国家和地区，挂接数万个网络、数千万台计算机、数亿用户的[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)（Internet）。由ARPANET发展而来的[Internet](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655113947&idx=1&sn=60e75dcb0981a35b6e270817c4427d69&chksm=bc864f348bf1c622a00442be7cbe3b7a87fce68faa7ee2dcf369b49a071fc2f8947773cf009d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，是目前全世界最大的国际型计算机[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)络，目前仍在快速发展中。

  

**1、恶意代码**

  

网络的发展给人们带来了诸多便利和好处，然而也带来了恶意行为的肆虐。

  

对于2010年席卷全球工业界的“震网”[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)事件，相信大家多少有所耳闻。这是一款专门定向[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)真实世界中基础（能源）设施的“蠕虫”[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，该[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)具有超强的破坏性和自我复制能力，已感染全球超过45000个网络，曾造成伊朗核电站1/5的离心机报废、约3万终端被感染、监控录像被篡改、放射性物质被泄漏，危害不亚于切尔诺贝利核电站事故。这款[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)以其强大的破坏性，使得伊朗被迫关闭核电站，让[美国](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128142&idx=3&sn=7b8de69bbda3be653df725fdec9fc24a&chksm=bc8597a18bf21eb7f39f365987d171669e5868d0a43659b4fa35b9053cfc346b30c80e7c5e8a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)不废一兵一卒就将其[工业控制](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123660&idx=2&sn=6a4a3f432f923b6a03c9e0e996d1e09f&chksm=bc8665238bf1ec3575efec08afde53c5e6594e0c697a545230e62b0f859d0a90dcc82a04a1d8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统摧毁。该恶意代码能够成功的关键是它同时调用了几个所谓的“零日[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”，即新发现的还未被人恶意利用过的软件缺陷，这几个[漏洞 9afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)电力、运输、石油、化工、汽车等重要工业和民用基础设施。这还只是影响力较大的[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)之一，事实上，全球每天都在上演着各式各样的恶意代码进行破坏的“戏码”。

  

为什么恶意代码有如此大的破坏力呢？因为计算机由硬件和软件两部分组成，前者决定了它的“体力”，后者决定了它的“智力”。软件不过就是指令和数据的集合，表现形式是代码。人类把做“好事”的代码称为善意代码，把做“坏事”的代码称为恶意代码，但从计算机角度看，它们都是代码，没有任何区别。在信息时代到处都有计算机的身影，它可以大至一间屋子（如超算中心），小到一个微型器件（如嵌入式芯片），并与人们的生活密切相关。凡是计算机能做的事情，即（善意）代码可做的“善事”，也都可以由恶意代码转换成“恶事”，从而影响人们的工作生活，这就是恶意代码“邪恶无边”的原因。而且从制造难度上看，恶意代码比善意代码更容易编写，因为普遍的规律是“败事容易成事难”。恶意代码以其制造的容易性、破坏的强大性，已经形成了一个个“黑色部落”，演化出了一个庞大的“家族”。这个“家族”里比较典型的有四种：[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、蠕虫（简称为“毒僵木蠕”）。此外，还有[后门](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129244&idx=3&sn=6df349d4b772425777487a77091f0f4d&chksm=bc8593738bf21a652bfc7213ea58a506986d7b1e6812c5132b33c598b7a8a210c10f13d7f900&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、下载器、间谍软件、内核套件、勒索软件等其他类型。

  

**（1）病毒**

  

[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)（这里指[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)），从其名字就可以感受到它的威力，就像生物[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)一样，感染者非病即死。世界上第一款[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)雏形出现在20世纪60年代初的[美国](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128142&idx=3&sn=7b8de69bbda3be653df725fdec9fc24a&chksm=bc8597a18bf21eb7f39f365987d171669e5868d0a43659b4fa35b9053cfc346b30c80e7c5e8a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)贝尔实验室里，三个年轻的[程序员](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129762&idx=3&sn=ff93f3c7ab9c4f118cc540ca9613e9f8&chksm=bc858d4d8bf2045b98ec58aca816d0bdc223b0600f9808e34f7257c8c420c3b7ef9006a02814&scene=21#wechat_redirect)编写了一个名为“磁芯大战”的游戏，游戏中可通过复制自身来摆脱对方的控制。20世纪70年代，[美国](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128142&idx=3&sn=7b8de69bbda3be653df725fdec9fc24a&chksm=bc8597a18bf21eb7f39f365987d171669e5868d0a43659b4fa35b9053cfc346b30c80e7c5e8a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)作家雷恩在其出版的《P1的青春》一书中构思了一种能够自我复制的计算机程序，并第一次称之为[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。1983年11月，在国际计算机安全学术研讨会上[美国](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128142&idx=3&sn=7b8de69bbda3be653df725fdec9fc24a&chksm=bc8597a18bf21eb7f39f365987d171669e5868d0a43659b4fa35b9053cfc346b30c80e7c5e8a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)计算机专家首次将[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序在vax/750计算机上进行了实验，世界上第一个[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)就这样出现了。但真正意义上在世界上流行的第一个[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)出现在20世纪80年代后期，在巴基斯坦，两个以[编程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128726&idx=3&sn=6bbfafcc4786966456c6ec9fd0ef37a8&chksm=bc8591798bf2186f7df0c0558b10945b9c71cf8dae01be5a80844bd7e6480ff5febe4a7ecfe6&scene=21#wechat_redirect)为生的兄弟为了打击那些盗版软件的使用者，设计出了一款名为“巴基斯坦智囊”的[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，该[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)在全世界广为传播。虽然其形状不像生物[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，但在行为特征方面，[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)比生物[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)有过之而无不及，都具有感染性、传播性、隐蔽性、可激发性等破坏性，而且还能自我繁殖、互相传染和激活再生。按照感染策略，[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)可分为非常驻型[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和常驻型[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。顾名思义，前者短暂停留，一旦摸清被[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者的情况就快速展开感染、复制、繁殖；后者则长期隐藏在受害者体内，一旦时机成熟就会像癌细胞一样不断分裂，复制自身，消耗系统资源，不断作恶。当然，与生物分类的多样性类似，[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)还有其他许多分类方法，在此不一一列举，只需要知道它的基本行为特征和破坏力就够了。

**（2）僵尸网络**

  

[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，听上去也是一个让人犯怵的名字。[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者通过各种途径传播僵尸程序（虽然本质上是[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，但它只是充当了一个[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)平台的角色）以感染[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上的大量主机，而被感染的主机通过一个控制信道接收[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者的指令，组成一个受控的“[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”，众多计算机就在不知不觉中成为被人利用的一种工具。“[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”是一种由引擎驱动的恶意行为，常与之一起出现的词还有[DDoS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126795&idx=3&sn=9038ab35960660611a2d94ac3eb31681&chksm=bc8598e48bf211f22b26629df6dfe1cbf0a08bcf8acfa47740b1ce6afce1f9ab817d994e437a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)（Distributed Denial of Service，分布式拒绝服务攻击），后者是利用服务请求来耗尽被[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)网络的系统资源，从而使被[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)网络无法处理合法用户的请求。[DDoS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126795&idx=3&sn=9038ab35960660611a2d94ac3eb31681&chksm=bc8598e48bf211f22b26629df6dfe1cbf0a08bcf8acfa47740b1ce6afce1f9ab817d994e437a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)形式多样，但最常见的是流量溢出，它可以消耗大量带宽，却不消耗[应用程序](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122664&idx=3&sn=7121cc60a98dc61779bca7ae7dc36bdd&chksm=bc8669078bf1e011b3a485e4beb1bac0778b4263e0839fe576cc166b21d65ee58448a953205b&scene=21#wechat_redirect)资源。正是“[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”的兴起，使得[DDoS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126795&idx=3&sn=9038ab35960660611a2d94ac3eb31681&chksm=bc8598e48bf211f22b26629df6dfe1cbf0a08bcf8acfa47740b1ce6afce1f9ab817d994e437a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)迅速壮大和普及，因为“[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”为[DDoS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126795&idx=3&sn=9038ab35960660611a2d94ac3eb31681&chksm=bc8598e48bf211f22b26629df6dfe1cbf0a08bcf8acfa47740b1ce6afce1f9ab817d994e437a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)提供了所需的“火力”带宽和计算机以及管理[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad41  

**（3）木马**

  

[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)也称[木马病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116716&idx=3&sn=6979add977615d21d7ca4ef9fbee6d55&chksm=bc8640438bf1c955956fe58cb713239ee06d4848176e5fe9409c95a025e3c08cefd8d1d49e90&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，名字来源于古希腊传说（《荷马史诗》中“[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)计”的故事），但它与一般的[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)不同，它不会自我繁殖，也不会刻意感染其他文件，而是通过将自身伪装起来以吸引用户下载执行。正如它的全名“特洛伊[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”，意思是“害人的礼物”，比喻在敌方阵营里埋下伏兵，等待命令开始行动。[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者通过特定的[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序控制另一台计算机，等到合适的时机，[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者在控制端发出命令，于是隐藏的[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序就开始进行破坏性行动了，比如窃取文件、修改注册表和计算机配置、复制、移动、删除等。从行为模式上看，[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序与普通的[远程控制](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655125497&idx=3&sn=0403881020241a12fc0d156a88b56926&chksm=bc8662168bf1eb00d0ea4ab77edeab0badf0a9a01d0d190e374dfc77d85cb0adce04f548eee3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件相似，但后者进行维护、升级或遥控等正当行动，而[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序则从事着非法活动，且因有着很好的隐蔽性而不容易被发现。也正因为[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序的隐蔽性，普通杀毒软件难以发现它的行踪，而且它一旦启动就很难被阻止。它会将自己加载到核心软件中，当系统启用时就自动运行。[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的种类也是异常繁多，挂载在不同应用上就表现出不同的功能，不一而足。

**（4）蠕虫**

  

蠕虫也是一种[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，其利用网络进行复制和传播。最初的蠕虫[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)定义源于在[DOS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116930&idx=2&sn=93b8d2ec73d56ef6f703f802fa91b8ce&chksm=bc86436d8bf1ca7bbaeecf45f40467333bc427afe1eeff330704af0a88ce2eefccd7419998ad&scene=21#wechat_redirect)环境下，该[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发作时会在屏幕上出现一条类似虫子的东西，胡乱吞吃屏幕上的字母并将其改形。蠕虫[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是自包含的程序，它能将自身功能的拷贝或自身的某些部分传播到其他计算机系统中。与普通[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)不同的是，蠕虫不需要将其自身附着在宿主程序上就能干坏事。蠕虫主要包括主机蠕虫和网络蠕虫，前者完全包含在其运行的主机中，并且通过网络将自身拷贝到其他计算机终端。一旦完成拷贝动作就会自毁，而让其“克隆物”继续作恶，因此在任何时刻都只有一个“蠕虫拷贝”在运行。蠕虫会“游荡”在[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中，尝试一个又一个[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wech 有[网络攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)就一定有[网络防御](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130803&idx=2&sn=86b4c7953e5788c8455c342c0e959b49&chksm=bc85895c8bf2004adb8d18ecdfba65e5778689ec5a12534ab5ac1863bdd63de846ae094f13ff&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。曾经有一款蠕虫[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的出现改变了[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)界对安全的认识，那就是1988年11月Morris蠕虫[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的发布，其引起了羽翼未满的[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)对安全性的广泛关注，该[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)还推进了第一种[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655120585&idx=2&sn=9622d3fd3041997ec13b8f8e385a717d&chksm=bc8671268bf1f830b295b492d4537709131dec78eb7e709d8a6b7d4bcbafd155c70245f182fb&scene=21#wechat_redirect)设施——[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e **（1）防火墙**

  

自存在网络互联以来，[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)就是保护[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127030&idx=3&sn=2adb039c3bf3066eb9ebe819fe2f3310&chksm=bc8598198bf2110fc9fd93ef57c9165eb6776a4da15d16fa810f7dd1d13222dfba6f98876135&scene=21#wechat_redirect)所使用的最流行和最重要的工具之一。[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是一种采用隔离技术的[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127030&idx=3&sn=2adb039c3bf3066eb9ebe819fe2f3310&chksm=bc8598198bf2110fc9fd93ef57c9165eb6776a4da15d16fa810f7dd1d13222dfba6f98876135&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统（可以是硬件形式也可是软件形式），常常部署在内部网与外部网、专用网与公共网之间，以保护内部网络免受非法用户的侵入。[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的核心工作原理就是包过滤机制，通过在两个网络通信时执行一种访问控制策略，允许“同意”的人和数据进入网络，同时将“不同意”的人和数据拒之门外，最大限度地阻止网络中[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=3&sn=c82f59076adf4123590301c9577f3c59&chksm=bc859b348bf21222ef281bc9aebb654a06582273fd7d9eb73ab84b56adbf383f74305efe3c3c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的访问。

  

在20世纪80年代，最早的[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)几乎与[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)同时出现，第一代[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)主要基于包过滤技术，是依附于[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的包过滤功能实现的，随着[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126795&idx=1&sn=bba9fa628e171c9b90404b58b1d2edf0&chksm=bc8598e48bf211f29038ee1a02893a3544c4e0ab892b23f9b752f036afc26a0d89f949c1d58b&scene=21#wechat_redirect)重要性和对性能要求的提升，[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)才逐渐发展成为一个具有独立结构和专门功能的设备。到了1989年，贝尔实验室推出了第二代[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，即电路层[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。到20世纪90年代初开始推出第三代[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，即应用层[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)（又称代理[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)）。1992年，USC（南加州大学）信息科学院的Bob Braden开发出了基于动态包过滤的技术，后来演变为目前所说的状态监视技术，1994年市面上出现了第四代[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，即以色列的Check Point公司推出的基于该技术的商业化产品，它是具有安全[操作系统](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122728&idx=2&sn=6b263bf85d6b8bd8bf1d3744a491a40c&chksm=bc8668c78bf1e1d1a0e813fcd7b41c7bc300c2c98eb269b77f92854c62f1fe5127a0e01c5574&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。到了1998年，NAI公司推出了自适应代理技术并在其产品中实现，给代理类型的[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)赋予了全新的意义，可以称之为第五代[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。虽然经历了升级换代，但[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的基本功能仍是根据包的起点和终点来判断是否允许其通过，而这种方式并不能筛选出所有具有风险的包，因为[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)只关心包的起点和终点，对于包中含有的恶意内容，[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)无法发现。这也是[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的局限所在。

  

**（2）入侵检测系统**

  

[入侵检测](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127637&idx=3&sn=eb354c81aeee91431416469bf645d668&chksm=bc8595ba8bf21cac3017017560467081303be6de1228d4bfe211760a4c38031bd63229927ab9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统（Intrusion Detection System，IDS）是一种对网络传输进行即时监视，在发现可疑传输时发出警报或者采取主动反应措施的[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126756&idx=3&sn=bf4bd0ce3a8dea14b3648ccdaa01c24a&chksm=bc85990b8bf2101d074cbb5ec1465dbd8304a763e46247771dbf856ee888188b9064808b220c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统，对各种事件进行分析并从中发现违反安全策略的行为是其核心功能。与其他[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126610&idx=3&sn=e491908dbd39be5ec9f9f589575dcb9a&chksm=bc8599bd8bf210ab03cb24f3b363ea926d3eb75f64b9296c80bf223785e0090461200d685ee6&scene=21#wechat_redirect)设施的不同之处在于，[IDS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123686&idx=2&sn=23c35bb4d7c1b0414f175ae821ddc950&chksm=bc8665098bf1ec1f63b16681546b1ab596a1350b753f6e7f6c28fde6d0085fe3c15d04977d9e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是一种积极主动的[安全防护](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130646&idx=3&sn=97c1053f37242978660fcb12785d609b&chksm=bc8589f98bf200ef420ca1982463e0f0330d45d404e05db58c784600274ed2b3f93adfca0450&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术。[IDS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124196&idx=1&sn=21bd9cb4cd4a9f98ceea3a1de1ab6ea9&chksm=bc86670b8bf1ee1df62790f57355d1ff9168d5051e371eb5c4f6cde8cace2ffdf4c2900caf8f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)最早出现在1980年4月，20世纪80年代中期[IDS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124239&idx=1&sn=d1bdfc11e32b8a8f8344a5e564227aee&chksm=bc8666e08bf1eff60875d5c998ab2f6b74b7943bfd31dda278b7d09a61afed5e3295c3379f54&scene=21#wechat_redirect)逐渐发展成为[入侵检测](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127637&idx=3&sn=eb354c81aeee91431416469bf645d668&chksm=bc8595ba8bf21cac3017017560467081303be6de1228d4bfe211760a4c38031bd63229927ab9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)专家系统。1990年，根据信息来源的不同，[IDS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123065&idx=3&sn=817bed2e3f7733ac1ca3fe22793b6767&chksm=bc866b968bf1e280b26f1073407f2636ba8f20c0b83fd80e8a652dbffd1154f9807929c45e08&scene=21#wechat_redirect)分化为基于网络的[IDS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123686&idx=2&sn=23c35bb4d7c1b0414f175ae821ddc950&chksm=bc8665098bf1ec1f63b16681546b1ab596a1350b753f6e7f6c28fde6d0085fe3c15d04977d9e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和基于[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的[IDS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124196&idx=1&sn=21bd9cb4cd4a9f98ceea3a1de1ab6ea9&chksm=bc86670b8bf1ee1df62790f57355d1ff9168d5051e371eb5c4f6cde8cace2ffdf4c2900caf8f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，后来又出现了分布式[IDS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124239&idx=1&sn=d1bdfc11e32b8a8f8344a5e564227aee&chksm=bc8666e08bf1eff60875d5c998ab2f6b74b7943bfd31dda278b7d09a61afed5e3295c3379f54&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

根据检测方法和安全策略的差异，[IDS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123065&idx=3&sn=817bed2e3f7733ac1ca3fe22793b6767&chksm=bc866b968bf1e280b26f1073407f2636ba8f20c0b83fd80e8a652dbffd1154f9807929c45e08&scene=21#wechat_redirect)分为异常[入侵检测](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127637&idx=3&sn=eb354c81aeee91431416469bf645d668&chksm=bc8595ba8bf21cac3017017560467081303be6de1228d4bfe211760a4c38031bd63229927ab9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和误用[入侵检测](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127637&idx=3&sn=eb354c81aeee91431416469bf645d668&chksm=bc8595ba8bf21cac3017017560467081303be6de1228d4bfe211760a4c38031bd63229927ab9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，前者通过建立正常行为模型来阻挡不符合该模型的行为[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130986&idx=3&sn=0e68bbe46f5ac759f2f830ef451e367f&chksm=bc8588858bf201938d2fcd040e8778de84f17db6e9dc8f47cdbfdce073da9c2dbaaeedaa6aa0&scene=21#wechat_redirect)；后者则是建立不可接受的行为模型，凡是符合该模型的即被断定为[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130986&idx=3&sn=0e68bbe46f5ac759f2f830ef451e367f&chksm=bc8588858bf201938d2fcd040e8778de84f17db6e9dc8f47cdbfdce073da9c2dbaaeedaa6aa0&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。这两种策略各有长短，前者漏报率低、误报率高，后者则误报率低、漏报率高。从技术手段上，[IDS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123686&idx=2&sn=23c35bb4d7c1b0414f175ae821ddc950&chksm=bc8665098bf1ec1f63b16681546b1ab596a1350b753f6e7f6c28fde6d0085fe3c15d04977d9e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)可分成基于标志和基于异常情况的[入侵检测](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127637&idx=3&sn=eb354c81aeee91431416469bf645d668&chksm=bc8595ba8bf21cac3017017560467081303be6de1228d4bfe211760a4c38031bd63229927ab9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，前者通过选取并定义违背安全策略的事件的特征来判别[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，重在维护一个特征知识库；后者思路同异常[入侵检测](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127637&idx=3&sn=eb354c81aeee91431416469bf645d668&chksm=bc8595ba8bf21cac3017017560467081303be6de1228d4bfe211760a4c38031bd63229927ab9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，先定义一组正常情况数值以比对是否符合正常，进而阻挡恶意[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

不同于[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，[IDS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124196&idx=1&sn=21bd9cb4cd4a9f98ceea3a1de1ab6ea9&chksm=bc86670b8bf1ee1df62790f57355d1ff9168d5051e371eb5c4f6cde8cace2ffdf4c2900caf8f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是一个监听设备，不需要跨接在任何链路上，无需网络流量流经即可工作。[IDS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124239&idx=1&sn=d1bdfc11e32b8a8f8344a5e564227aee&chksm=bc8666e08bf1eff60875d5c998ab2f6b74b7943bfd31dda278b7d09a61afed5e3295c3379f54&scene=21#wechat_redirect)一般部署在所有关注流量必须流经的链路上，也就是来自高危网络区域的访问流量和需要进行统计、监视的网络报文所经过的链路。在现实中，[IDS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123065&idx=3&sn=817bed2e3f7733ac1ca3fe22793b6767&chksm=bc866b968bf1e280b26f1073407f2636ba8f20c0b83fd80e8a652dbffd1154f9807929c45e08&scene=21#wechat_redirect)在网络中的位置一般选择在尽可能靠近[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)源或者尽可能靠近受保护资源的位置，如[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)区域的[交换机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128257&idx=3&sn=4b46cf6a7be7396a95c7d64493cc81c9&chksm=bc85972e8bf21e389f60513d23a5a5fa4af5807a1b4532d03eda374d4890a5f5f13479f5a34e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上。与[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)一样，[入侵检测](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127637&idx=3&sn=eb354c81aeee91431416469bf645d668&chksm=bc8595ba8bf21cac3017017560467081303be6de1228d4bfe211760a4c38031bd63229927ab9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统也有它的局限性：由于当代网络发展迅速，网络传输速率大大提升，[IDS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123686&idx=2&sn=23c35bb4d7c1b0414f175ae821ddc950&chksm=bc8665098bf1ec1f63b16681546b1ab596a1350b753f6e7f6c28fde6d0085fe3c15d04977d9e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工作负担越来越重，对[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)活动检测的可靠性较低，且由于模式识别技术的不完善，[IDS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123065&idx=3&sn=817bed2e3f7733ac1ca3fe22793b6767&chksm=bc866b968bf1e280b26f1073407f2636ba8f20c0b83fd80e8a652dbffd1154f9807929c45e08&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的虚警率高也是一大问题。

  

**（3）反病毒软件**

  

为了检测并采取行动来解除或删除一切已知的[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)等恶意代码，人们制造了[反病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=2&sn=a8e76542cfd1541046477370dbf884cc&chksm=bc859b348bf212226789cdafb4e418915d4d6407c8f85c9d1132a64119b0ff51f3107883a577&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件。[反病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126399&idx=2&sn=bf753a81f7b7b8d2873b4c9e041a153a&chksm=bc859e908bf2178665c154c1e9616fd47ef0e90532f4062f708d96faadc5136e0b7efcd4a213&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件通常集成监控识别、[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)扫描和清除、自动升级等功能，有的还具有[数据恢复](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124725&idx=2&sn=519b5d9111a1f33ab2163ff3df75060c&chksm=bc86611a8bf1e80cc13f2c1f7266d8d247ec4a447af51ff25a552b1a8a1dda8721a7c139963c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的功能，是计算机防御系统的重要组成部分。1987年，在第一个PC[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)出现之后的几个月内，就已经有公司成立和销售[反病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=2&sn=a8e76542cfd1541046477370dbf884cc&chksm=bc859b348bf212226789cdafb4e418915d4d6407c8f85c9d1132a64119b0ff51f3107883a577&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件，并导致了一场竞赛。早期的[反病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126399&idx=2&sn=bf753a81f7b7b8d2873b4c9e041a153a&chksm=bc859e908bf2178665c154c1e9616fd47ef0e90532f4062f708d96faadc5136e0b7efcd4a213&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件有两种基本形态，一是扫描器，二是校验和检查器。扫描器是一种用于对可执行文件进行搜索的程序，目的是寻找已被识别的[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中已知的字符串。[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)编写者采用了多种方法来防止[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)被扫描出来，比如利用多态化技术或对代码进行加密，这使得扫描器时常难以识别[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。校验和检查器是将系统中所有授权的可执行程序及其原始版本的校验和信息保存至一个列表中，通常校验和是采用散列函数计算得出的，对这些校验和进行检查以识别[恶意程序](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127399&idx=2&sn=e7605669abc54947803b3cd1b19aa9db&chksm=bc859a888bf2139e9e8f91b9db4e21e52039157964132cbbf79d41504f8deff65826665e4a4b&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。随着[网络攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的商业化，恶意代码的编写者有越来越好的工具并接受过越来越专业的培训，很多新的恶意代码在初次使用时都不会被当前的[反病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=2&sn=a8e76542cfd1541046477370dbf884cc&chksm=bc859b348bf212226789cdafb4e418915d4d6407c8f85c9d1132a64119b0ff51f3107883a577&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件识别出来，因为编写者对其进行了完全测试。从效果上看，在21世纪的头几年[反病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126399&idx=2&sn=bf753a81f7b7b8d2873b4c9e041a153a&chksm=bc859e908bf2178665c154c1e9616fd47ef0e90532f4062f708d96faadc5136e0b7efcd4a213&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件可以检测几乎所有[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，而到了2007年，典型的[反病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=2&sn=a8e76542cfd1541046477370dbf884cc&chksm=bc859b348bf212226789cdafb4e418915d4d6407c8f85c9d1132a64119b0ff51f3107883a577&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件只能检测三分之一的[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)与其他[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126517&idx=3&sn=9aebb5885fd770004aa1ad413e7c2629&chksm=bc859e1a8bf2170c6f01aa98bec717f19009afd30bf327a673eea84c53356a0c87114d7712de&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工具。尽管[反病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126399&idx=2&sn=bf753a81f7b7b8d2873b4c9e041a153a&chksm=bc859e908bf2178665c154c1e9616fd47ef0e90532f4062f708d96faadc5136e0b7efcd4a213&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件也在不断地提升杀毒能力，但在智能识别未知[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和降低系统资源占用方面，[反病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=2&sn=a8e76542cfd1541046477370dbf884cc&chksm=bc859b348bf212226789cdafb4e418915d4d6407c8f85c9d1132a64119b0ff51f3107883a577&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件仍有待进一步改进。

  

**（4）统一威胁管理平台**

  

众多的[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126580&idx=2&sn=d3867b0e449ae311936f1fc7f835e833&chksm=bc8599db8bf210cda1a108f7dddd6c7d4f5b6608d2f0b1c81fd58b9ec6a7b8c464c6d523c670&scene=21#wechat_redirect)防御设施使得大型组织有时难以抉择，于是出现了一种集多种安全功能于一体和防范多种威胁的产品，即统一威胁管理平台（简称UTM平台）。UTM平台在具备单一管理面板的单一设施上组合了多种不同的安全功能，如[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、[IDS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123686&idx=2&sn=23c35bb4d7c1b0414f175ae821ddc950&chksm=bc8665098bf1ec1f63b16681546b1ab596a1350b753f6e7f6c28fde6d0085fe3c15d04977d9e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、邮件过滤、代理和[VPN](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124772&idx=2&sn=f8441a7025b8ef21d0ff7d5956badd1b&chksm=bc8660cb8bf1e9dd8f704347245727e5055e167f808a3cd8b2b8db6b74f4cb059d79ddafec61&scene=21#wechat_redirect)（虚拟专用网）、DLP（数据丢失防护）等，虽然每种安全功能不如单一安全设备强大，但胜在功能齐全，界面单一。UTM平台的优势显而易见，即减少了安全设备的种类，可以统一部署，简化了管理和修补流程，且费用相对购买多台设备要低得多。2012年，据Gartner公司报告称，UTM平台的市场规模超过10亿美元，在诸多[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126563&idx=3&sn=14023df84f67afb48342aae5f5fe71f7&chksm=bc8599cc8bf210da3250510eb9d50c84270e15141f1eac9d3f98a726114f5d3e51263495ecf9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)公司处于困境时，UTM市场能达到两位数的增长率还是能说明一些问题的。早期采用UTM技术的大多是小型公司，后来随着其性能提升，越来越多的大型组织开始采用UTM技术来保护自身[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124760&idx=2&sn=96fb5d7fa9e05ea83b59cd7329fb4736&chksm=bc8660f78bf1e9e1acbd641806329c0233f135b238ef7a3395117a7aa455c2c59ffa555202eb&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。然而，UTM平台功能的多样性和供货商的单一也恰恰是其缺陷所在：每种功能可能都不强大，单一供货商更多依赖一家供货商提供安全解决方案来满足所有安全性要求，而这唯一的一家供货商不一定在所有方面都能做到领先，容易导致对威胁的识别能力不如采用混合供应商的组织。
