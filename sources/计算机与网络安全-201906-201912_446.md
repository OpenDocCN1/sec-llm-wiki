**电信和互联网行业**

**提升网络数据安全保护能力专项行动方案**

  

近年来，随着国家[大数据](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128534&idx=3&sn=e679f67ac39ab11dbf5f8cae74032564&chksm=bc8596398bf21f2ffb6d133b630409ede8176bcaaa3d12565e956b45bfd287978ab050e9230d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发展战略加快实施，[大数据](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128608&idx=3&sn=f7597e9aca98808d9c1aa019290ea11b&chksm=bc8591cf8bf218d959e08078f5309adfeffd6ae2c94cf34da4dbc781b6bdfbe0406b1ceaaace&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术创新与应用日趋活跃，产生和集聚了类型丰富多样、应用价值不断提升的海量网络数据，成为数字经济发展的关键生产要素。与此同时，数据过度采集滥用、非法交易及用户数据泄露等[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)问题日益凸显，做好电信和[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)行业（以下简称行业）网络[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)管理尤为迫切。为积极应对新形势新情况新问题，切实做好新中国成立70周年网络[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保障工作，全面提升行业网络[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保护能力，制定本方案。  
　　

**一、总体要求**  
　　

以习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想为指导，全面贯彻党的十九大和十九届二中、三中全会精神，严格落实[《网络安全法》](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655113238&idx=4&sn=7a6aeba5889cedfd8cd21ca949ce6dbb&chksm=bc864df98bf1c4efdc987d135c7571718de3351fdd3c22ba266e90e0f2162ee811ee420143f4&scene=21#wechat_redirect)《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于加强网络信息保护的决定》《[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)信息服务管理办法》等[法律](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130851&idx=3&sn=aa093ab1c4c39158a726624b13d4f90c&chksm=bc85890c8bf2001a8b9e6c714674fe6596ec767fea832f0746c9b89126913cb49abae16f8515&scene=21#wechat_redirect)法规，坚持维护[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)与促进数据开发利用并重，坚持数据分类分级保护，坚持充分发挥政府引导作用、企业主体作用和社会监督作用，立足我部行业网络[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)监管职责，开展为期一年的行业提升网络[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保护能力专项行动（以下简称专项行动），加快推动构建行业网络[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)综合保障体系，为建设网络强国、助力数字经济发展提供有力保障和重要支撑。  
　　

**二、工作目标**  
　　

（一）通过集中开展[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)合规性评估、专项治理和监督检查，督促基础电信企业和重点[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)企业强化网络[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)全流程管理，及时整改消除重大数据泄露、滥用等安全隐患，2019年10月底前完成全部基础电信企业（含专业公司）、50家重点[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)企业以及200款主流[App](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130459&idx=3&sn=f8b64d61307c28f9f3265b4eb1eda12a&chksm=bc858eb48bf207a2b2fd53128cd92797950f71b4e141abfb9c60970b6a662bc1849e85912dff&scene=21#wechat_redirect)数据安全检查，圆满完成新中国成立70周年等重大活动网络[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保障工作。  
　

（二）基本建立行业网络[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保障体系。网络[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)制度标准体系进一步完善，形成行业网络数据保护目录，制定15项以上行业网络[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)标准规范，贯标试点企业不少于20家；行业网络[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)管理和技术支撑平台基本建成，遴选网络[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术能力创新示范项目不少于30个；基础电信企业和重点[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)企业网络[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)管理体系有效建立。  
　　

**三、重点任务**  
　　

（一）加快完善网络[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)制度标准  
　　

1.强化网络[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)管理制度设计。梳理对标[《网络安全法》](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655113238&idx=4&sn=7a6aeba5889cedfd8cd21ca949ce6dbb&chksm=bc864df98bf1c4efdc987d135c7571718de3351fdd3c22ba266e90e0f2162ee811ee420143f4&scene=21#wechat_redirect)《电信和[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)用户[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保护规定》等[法律](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130851&idx=3&sn=aa093ab1c4c39158a726624b13d4f90c&chksm=bc85890c8bf2001a8b9e6c714674fe6596ec767fea832f0746c9b89126913cb49abae16f8515&scene=21#wechat_redirect)法规要求，加快建立网络数据分类分级保护、[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)风险评估、[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)事件通报处置、数据对外提供使用报告等制度。部署电信和[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)企业按照[法律](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130851&idx=3&sn=aa093ab1c4c39158a726624b13d4f90c&chksm=bc85890c8bf2001a8b9e6c714674fe65
