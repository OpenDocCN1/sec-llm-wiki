间谍既指被间谍[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)机构秘密派遣到对象国（地区）从事以窃密为主的各种非法谍报活动的特工人员，又指被对方间谍[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)机构暗地招募而为其服务的本国公民。广义来说，间谍是指从事秘密侦探工作的人员，从敌对方或竞争对手那里刺探机密[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)或是进行破坏活动，以此来使其所效力的一方有利。

  

[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)人员，即间谍，也就是专门被雇来做这种事的人。他代表军队、警察或[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)机构里面其中一员。他们专门去收集、融合和分析取回来的[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，以方便给政府提供对策。大体来说，[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)人员往往会潜入对方的国家，然后收集和运行[情报](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127209&idx=3&sn=8da38e2c74971d6e0dd7e03f20d557d8&chksm=bc859b468bf21250edcfa31d5bd8d90c914f539effa773d749667f9f4a3b3d0cf5efd159f19d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。这就成为了他们自己国家的间谍。人们称这类间谍为“鼹鼠”（mole）或“投诚者”（defector）。
