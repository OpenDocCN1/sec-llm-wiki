王女士通过网络搜索该东西的商标logo，证实其是[偷拍](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127790&idx=3&sn=62a5ac394be74c4bec137904ff6082c0&chksm=bc8595018bf21c172946998a5ba5b6a55c949786779346d17ce973b64abb4bca540f7e16f154&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，“隐蔽性强”“夜视高清”等是其文字介绍。在公安局，王女士证实看到该设备中保存了的当天的视频中，除了自己，还有男性租客在自己房间自拍、在卫生间调试角度，另一位男性租客洗漱的画面。庆幸的是，该设备中只有当天视频，但王女士还是十分担心之前就被[偷拍](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127790&idx=3&sn=62a5ac394be74c4bec137904ff6082c0&chksm=bc8595018bf21c172946998a5ba5b6a55c949786779346d17ce973b64abb4bca540f7e16f154&scene=21#wechat_redirect)过。据悉，[偷拍](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127790&idx=3&sn=62a5ac394be74c4bec137904ff6082c0&chksm=bc8595018bf21c172946998a5ba5b6a55c949786779346d17ce973b64abb4bca540f7e16f154&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的租客已经被拘留。

**如何找到隐藏摄像头保隐私**

**手机手电筒得会用**

  

微型[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)如此容易购得，而[偷拍](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127790&idx=3&sn=62a5ac394be74c4bec137904ff6082c0&chksm=bc8595018bf21c172946998a5ba5b6a55c949786779346d17ce973b64abb4bca540f7e16f154&scene=21#wechat_redirect)事件屡屡发生，那么我们要如何自保[隐私](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127519&idx=3&sn=a846e840b4ac021e01ba4a64c9228817&chksm=bc859a308bf2132693545d19fdb92d481e63a9d92d18960c1e4c198747b628d2bad763105175&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，出门在外或者与他人合租如何找到隐藏[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)呢？

  

首先，我们要知道[偷拍](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127790&idx=3&sn=62a5ac394be74c4bec137904ff6082c0&chksm=bc8595018bf21c172946998a5ba5b6a55c949786779346d17ce973b64abb4bca540f7e16f154&scene=21#wechat_redirect)设备容易伪装成哪些物品。除了上文提到的网上销售的，还有卡通挂件、挂钩、烟雾报警器、饮料罐、牙刷、沐浴露、[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、钢笔、餐巾盒、吹风机、电视机、开关等。

  

其次，一一检查可疑物品。当您到达酒店房间、试衣间、合租屋等会暴露隐私的地方后 ，一一检查上方易被装入[偷拍](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127790&idx=3&sn=62a5ac394be74c4bec137904ff6082c0&chksm=bc8595018bf21c172946998a5ba5b6a55c949786779346d17ce973b64abb4bca540f7e16f154&scene=21#wechat_redirect)设备的物品，值得注意的是，不仅仅是这些物品要检查，任何有孔的物品都要一一检查。

  

然后，要运用一些科学手段再做检查。比如，针对有红外线补光灯的[偷拍](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127790&idx=3&sn=62a5ac394be74c4bec137904ff6082c0&chksm=bc8595018bf21c172946998a5ba5b6a55c949786779346d17ce973b64abb4bca540f7e16f154&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，可关闭房间内的灯，拉上窗帘，使房间保持黑暗状态，打开手机照相机，把房间内可能装有[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的地方仔细扫一圈，如果手机屏幕上出现光斑，则可能是有[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。也可用手电筒将房间内所有外露孔的地方照射一遍，如果有反光，则可能是有[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。当然，大家也可以网购一些专业的查[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的器材，可能效果更佳。

  

最后，一定要在特定角度再检查一遍。比如在酒店，站在床边围绕自己头的高度检查一遍、按照膝盖高度检查一遍。在洗澡间，则在沐浴位置检查一遍。
