**网络安全漏洞管理规定**

（征求意见稿）

  

**第一条** 为规范[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124760&idx=2&sn=96fb5d7fa9e05ea83b59cd7329fb4736&chksm=bc8660f78bf1e9e1acbd641806329c0233f135b238ef7a3395117a7aa455c2c59ffa555202eb&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)（以下简称[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)）报告和信息发布等行为，保证网络产品、服务、系统的[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)得到及时修补，提高网络[安全防护](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130646&idx=3&sn=97c1053f37242978660fcb12785d609b&chksm=bc8589f98bf200ef420ca1982463e0f0330d45d404e05db58c784600274ed2b3f93adfca0450&scene=21#wechat_redirect)水平，根据《国家安全法》[《网络安全法》](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655113238&idx=4&sn=7a6aeba5889cedfd8cd21ca949ce6dbb&chksm=bc864df98bf1c4efdc987d135c7571718de3351fdd3c22ba266e90e0f2162ee811ee420143f4&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，制定本规定。

**第二条** 中华人民共和国境内网络产品、服务提供者和网络运营者，以及开展[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)检测、评估、收集、发布及相关竞赛等活动的组织（以下简称第三方组织）或个人，应当遵守本规定。

**第三条** 网络产品、服务提供者和网络运营者发现或获知其网络产品、服务、系统存在[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)后，应当遵守以下规定：

（一）立即对[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进行验证，对相关网络产品应当在90日内采取[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)修补或防范措施，对相关网络服务或系统应当在10日内采取[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)修补或防范措施；

（二）需要用户或相关技术合作方采取[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)修补或防范措施的，应当在对相关网络产品、服务、系统采取[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)修补或防范措施后5日内，将[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)风险及用户或相关技术合作方需采取的修补或防范措施向社会发布或通过客服等方式告知所有可能受影响的用户和相关技术合作方，提供必要的技术支持，并向[工业和信息化部](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129869&idx=3&sn=b8bc5c3a1021ab9add954fb2f5c924ff&chksm=bc858ce28bf205f4892489e9824034cb1a2574d148ca6edbee237e317ff4c182a2031450a4d4&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124515&idx=1&sn=d9a0d24ac2aad47f1662eea1f31888f5&chksm=bc8661cc8bf1e8da12d82d7003a4a739ec7073272cd40717f90589440d6769954764add4e6f4&scene=21#wechat_redirect)威胁信息共享平台报送相关[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)情况。

**第四条** [工业和信息化部](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129869&idx=3&sn=b8bc5c3a1021ab9add954fb2f5c924ff&chksm=bc858ce28bf205f4892489e9824034cb1a2574d148ca6edbee237e317ff4c182a2031450a4d4&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、[公安部](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129746&idx=3&sn=d5040d38e863cab36a670a36b632f1b4&chksm=bc858d7d8bf2046b0048fdda6c78fb73329334added1f9f4f673a5671a567a35d92005831098&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和有关行业主管部门按照各自职责组织督促网络产品、服务提供者和网络运营者采取[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)修补或防范措施。

**第五条** [工业和信息化部](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129869&idx=3&sn=b8bc5c3a1021ab9add954fb2f5c924ff&chksm=bc858ce28bf205f4892489e9824034cb1a2574d148ca6edbee237e317ff4c182a2031450a4d4&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、[公安部](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129746&idx=3&sn=d5040d38e863cab36a670a36b632f1b4&chksm=bc858d7d8bf2046b0048fdda6c78fb73329334added1f9f4f673a5671a567a35d92005831098&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、国家互联网信息办公室等有关部门实现[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)信息实时共享。

**第六条** 第三方组织或个人通过网站、媒体、会议等方式向社会发布[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)信息，应当遵循必要、真实、客观、有利于防范和应对[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129537&idx=3&sn=1c8a2b85ec3a063ce7a132992d32a3be&chksm=bc85922e8bf21b3810cf89c2dba2b7041b8a93bf3df7d28e4ac4a87791862e41bf791bc21304&scene=21#wechat_redirect)风险的原则，并遵守以下规定：

（一）不得在网络产品、服务提供者和网络运营者向社会或用户发布[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)修补或防范措施之前发布相关[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)信息； 

（二）不得刻意夸大[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的危害和风险；

（三）不得发布和提供专门用于利用网络产品、服务、系统[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)从事危害[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127174&idx=3&sn=b0c84359114c49067f3728971b0f3233&chksm=bc859b698bf2127f130740c2ede1cf2f75fdf7c7d2dcaa4200f1055d9094c6b6d81b03b36fbc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)活动的方法、程序和工具；

（四）应当同步发布[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)修补或防范措施。

**第七条** 第三方组织应当加强内部管理，履行下列管理义务，防范[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[信息泄露](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127361&idx=3&sn=4c76bb9efc3b43bd56fbff3d96a594a3&chksm=bc859aae8bf213b88ea93d3438b436317a1aabf7b69bb53366f148ae9ac7f9c613365268779d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和内部人员违规发布[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)信息：

（一）明确[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)管理部门和责任人；

（二）建立[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)信息发布内部审核机制；

（四）定期对内部人员进行保密教育；

（五）制定内部问责制度。

**第八条** 网络产品、服务提供者和网络运营者未按本规定采取[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)修补或防范措施并向社会或用户发布的，由[工业和信息化部](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129869&idx=3&sn=b8bc5c3a1021ab9add954fb2f5c924ff&chksm=bc858ce28bf205f4892489e9824034cb1a2574d148ca6edbee237e317ff4c182a2031450a4d4&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、[公安部](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129746&idx=3&sn=d5040d38e863cab36a670a36b632f1b4&chksm=bc858d7d8bf2046b0048fdda6c78fb73329334added1f9f4f673a5671a567a35d92005831098&scene=21#wechat_redirect)等有关部门按职责依据[《网络安全法》](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655113238&idx=4&sn=7a6aeba5889cedfd8cd21ca949ce6dbb&chksm=bc864df98bf1c4efdc987d135c7571718de3351fdd3c22ba266e90e0f2162ee811ee420143f4&scene=21#wechat_redirect)第五十六条、第五十九条、第六十条等规定组织对其进行约谈或给予行政处罚。

**第九条** 第三方组织违反本规定向社会发布[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)信息，由[工业和信息化部](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129869&idx=3&sn=b8bc5c3a1021ab9add954fb2f5c924ff&chksm=bc858ce28bf205f4892489e9824034cb1a2574d148ca6edbee237e317ff4c182a2031450a4d4&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、[公安部](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129746&idx=3&sn=d5040d38e863cab36a670a36b632f1b4&chksm=bc858d7d8bf2046b0048fdda6c78fb73329334added1f9f4f673a5671a567a35d92005831098&scene=21#wechat_redirect)等有关部门组织对其进行约谈，或依据[《网络安全法》](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655113238&idx=4&sn=7a6aeba5889cedfd8cd21ca949ce6dbb&chksm=bc864df98bf1c4efdc987d135c7571718de3351fdd3c22ba266e90e0f2162ee811ee420143f4&scene=21#wechat_redirect)第六十二条、第六十三条等规定给予行政处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；给网络产品、服务提供者和网络运营者造成经济或名誉损害的，依法承担民事责任。

**第十条** 鼓励第三方组织和个人获知网络产品、服务、系统存在的[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)后，及时向国家[信息安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121200&idx=3&sn=70e6e459976c8517916785fddb79ff87&chksm=bc8672df8bf1fbc95e6d988f697861726578ce17ed2b061e09d3623e462a5695f73b22ce46fb&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)共享平台、国家[信息安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130851&idx=2&sn=1c24a2351e89c9b7653161a349cfc7b0&chksm=bc85890c8bf2001a87e60b19145bde4d5ae5f79f3223389fde458d0abc991cfe067719542517&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)库等[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)收集平台报送有关情况。[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127021&idx=3&sn=451c93a621ba2ced4a5ca0e1e63f43c1&chksm=bc8598028bf21114cebd8e7f27e0cf15b764fdef35358d8c62edc03d681d72382568fbadb991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)收集平台应当遵守本规定第六条、第七条规定。
