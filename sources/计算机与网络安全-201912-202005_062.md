当客户端与[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)不再进行通信时，都会以4次挥手的方式结束连接。本文介绍4次挥手的过程。

  

**1、第1次挥手**

  

客户端向[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)端发送断开[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)连接请求的\[FIN，ACK\]报文，在报文中随机生成一个序列号SEQ=x，表示要断开[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)连接，如图1所示。

![](../Images/2dbbe8be0c5bf58768af2e5278394560.png)

图1  第1次挥手

  

**2、第2次挥手**

  

当[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)端收到客户端发来的断开[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)连接的请求后，回复发送ACK报文，表示已经收到断开请求。回复时，随机生成一个序列号SEQ=y。由于回复的是客户端发来的请求，所以在客户端请求序列号SEQ=x的基础上加1，得到ACK=x+1，如图2所示。

![](../Images/3e6e7932b7ab59f9e7cdcc682f864034.png)

图2  第2次挥手

  

**3、第3次挥手**

  

[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)端在回复完客户端的[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)断开请求后，不会马上进行[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)连接的断开。[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)端会先确认断开前，所有传输到客户端的数据是否已经传输完毕。确认[数据传输](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132673&idx=2&sn=4ebed9559e94616637f5bce407cdd9a5&chksm=bc8581ee8bf208f815cb8279d3c10de158484886ffa52d39680ad373bf945014a4ac69635b03&scene=21#wechat_redirect)完毕后才进行断开，向客户端发送\[FIN，ACK\]报文，设置字段值为1。再次随机生成一个序列号SEQ=z。由于还是对客户端发来的[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)断开请求序列号SEQ=x进行回复，因此ACK依然为x+1，如图3所示。

![](../Images/b920cefdc2e59e2ef0b004a3fddcb7e2.png)

图3  第3次挥手

  

**4、第4次挥手**

  

客户端收到[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发来的[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)断开连接数据包后将进行回复，表示收到断开[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)连接数据包。向[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发送ACK报文，生成一个序列号SEQ=x+1。由于回复的是[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，所以ACK字段的值在[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发来断开[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)连接请求序列号SEQ=z的基础上加1，得到ACK=z+1，如图4所示。

![](../Images/684c29dde5b56242546f9af3d392c9bd.png)

图4  第4次挥手
