应用指纹，是[Web](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130618&idx=3&sn=449f93385285290a85de33579f5e55bf&chksm=bc858e158bf20703b709bfb7ddf2adee4149765451b8281f45a741aee2c621c396b330f67af2&scene=21#wechat_redirect)应用的一种身份标识，具有唯一性。在[Web](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130618&idx=3&sn=449f93385285290a85de33579f5e55bf&chksm=bc858e158bf20703b709bfb7ddf2adee4149765451b8281f45a741aee2c621c396b330f67af2&scene=21#wechat_redirect)应用的开发过程中，为了提高开发的效率和系统的稳定性，通常会用到一些成熟、稳定的第三方环境、程序、框架或服务等，而这些第三方内容的名称或标识就是这里所说的应用指纹。

  

**1、应用指纹种类及识别**

  

根据网络数据的流向，并结合分层思想，常见的应用指纹分成5类，如下：

  

● 网络层指纹

  

网关、[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、[VPN](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124772&idx=2&sn=f8441a7025b8ef21d0ff7d5956badd1b&chksm=bc8660cb8bf1e9dd8f704347245727e5055e167f808a3cd8b2b8db6b74f4cb059d79ddafec61&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、CDN、[DNS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127816&idx=3&sn=2b8d5dfef1801f990c8b7cb25fc65d73&chksm=bc8594e78bf21df19918b7e41f11ca714d5f530030014dd6a48e278b45385d835489385f1cd8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)等基础设施指纹。

  

● [主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)层指纹

  

[操作系统](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122728&idx=2&sn=6b263bf85d6b8bd8bf1d3744a491a40c&chksm=bc8668c78bf1e1d1a0e813fcd7b41c7bc300c2c98eb269b77f92854c62f1fe5127a0e01c5574&scene=21#wechat_redirect)信息、软件[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上各种对外提供服务的软件指纹。

  

● 服务层指纹

  

[Web](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130618&idx=3&sn=449f93385285290a85de33579f5e55bf&chksm=bc858e158bf20703b709bfb7ddf2adee4149765451b8281f45a741aee2c621c396b330f67af2&scene=21#wechat_redirect)服务、FTP服务、[SSH](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131079&idx=3&sn=794779c5c517f42260167e098ee06322&chksm=bc8588288bf2013ebd40fb2b944291585012bf1af14e1198e987a745b0ed2b95eb882984db5a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)服务等各种对外提供服务的指纹。

  

● 应用层指纹

  

各种建站程序、开源框架、前端框架等。

  

● 语前层指纹

  

各种脚本语前信息，如：ASP、ASPX、[PHP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130803&idx=3&sn=32cc58b76d89e2ba3f9d132628ea3200&chksm=bc85895c8bf2004a26b780597e0b78206119e6b311d936b856b95ae2dbfdf4574840e4068298&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和JSP等。

  

应用指纹识别是通过对目标进行分析和判断，知道它是由哪些应用指纹组成的。

  

在应用指纹识别中，通常需要建立一个[Web](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130618&idx=3&sn=449f93385285290a85de33579f5e55bf&chksm=bc858e158bf20703b709bfb7ddf2adee4149765451b8281f45a741aee2c621c396b330f67af2&scene=21#wechat_redirect)应用的指纹库，指纹库中记目着应用指纹信息和指纹特征的对应关系。在实际的指纹识别中，通过分析目标是否存在特定的指纹特征，就能够知道目标是否使用了这种应用。

  

**2、应用指纹识别的价值**

  

下面是应用指纹识别的价值。

  

**（1）快速检测目标的漏洞**

  

每一种应用都有自己的标识特征，通过对标识特征的探测，就能够推断出目标是否使用了该应用，而这对[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)检测是很有帮助的。每一种应用都会存在[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，因此当对目标进行应用指纹识别后，根据应用的[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)知识库，就可以对目标进行针对性的应用[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)检测，这样就能快速地找到目标的[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

**（2）极大丰富攻击的路径**

  

在对目标进行[渗透](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131857&idx=3&sn=f05704b1a33a118f5974dc1e0e3d8fde&chksm=bc85853e8bf20c282a388c76d4472ec1e0c54b97c6f96ce0179e0c75f9d0922a988125c9881d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)时，每一个应用指纹都可以看作是一个新的[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)入口。如果对目标的指纹识别越多，那么就越有可能从某个侧面完成[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。它可以极大丰富[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的路径。

  

从上面两个角度可以看出，应用指纹识别不仅可以快速检测目标是否存在[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，还可以对[信息收集](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133509&idx=3&sn=5ec6d2d116de0d6e817389b5c401fc01&chksm=bc8582aa8bf20bbcec2ee9f9fefdabe5a344ac785fa549a7e4cccbaf899ed4a1ed785c307f5f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的内容进行补充，增加[渗透测试](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131857&idx=3&sn=f05704b1a33a118f5974dc1e0e3d8fde&chksm=bc85853e8bf20c282a388c76d4472ec1e0c54b97c6f96ce0179e0c75f9d0922a988125c9881d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)路径，提高[扫描器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136430&idx=2&sn=fd390c0ee21800137c2ccef23ce846e1&chksm=bc85b7418bf23e57b2c293077f02d0c09a335c01f5b2f6531a1a939f2faa6ba75e823b854d41&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的安全[渗透](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131857&idx=3&sn=f05704b1a33a118f5974dc1e0e3d8fde&chksm=bc85853e8bf20c282a388c76d4472ec1e0c54b97c6f96ce0179e0c75f9d0922a988125c9881d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)能力。

  

**3、应用指纹识别技术**

  

既然应用指纹的价值如此之大，那么如何去识别和获取呢？首先需要获取指纹的特征，通常这些指纹特征会存在于特定页面的[HTTP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129052&idx=3&sn=6aa03cee097fa59206f385629b151800&chksm=bc8590338bf219253db54d9678c7924707d1e7d1195f8709817128e1ad501546618ac2f91cc7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)响应中，因此可以通过下面三种方式来处理：

  

● 内容特征

  

这类指纹的特征在 [HTTP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129052&idx=3&sn=6aa03cee097fa59206f385629b151800&chksm=bc8590338bf219253db54d9678c7924707d1e7d1195f8709817128e1ad501546618ac2f91cc7&scene=21#wechat_redirect) 响应的正文中，通过对响应正文中的内容进行特征匹配即可识别。

  

● 页面特征

  

这类指纹在[HTTP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129052&idx=3&sn=6aa03cee097fa59206f385629b151800&chksm=bc8590338bf219253db54d9678c7924707d1e7d1195f8709817128e1ad501546618ac2f91cc7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)请求中并没有明显的特征，而页面的内容却相对固定，因此可以根据页面内容的Hash值进行识别。

  

● Headers特征

  

这类指纹会在[HTTP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129052&idx=3&sn=6aa03cee097fa59206f385629b151800&chksm=bc8590338bf219253db54d9678c7924707d1e7d1195f8709817128e1ad501546618ac2f91cc7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)响应的消息报头中增加自己的报头信息，因此可以直接在消息报头中进行识别。
