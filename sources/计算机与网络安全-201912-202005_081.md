传输控制协议（Transmission Control Protocol，[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)）是一种面向连接的、可靠的、基于字节流的传输层通信协议。在[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)协议中，通过三次握手建立连接。通信结束后，还需要断开连接。如果在发送数据包时，没有正确被发送到目的地时，将会重新发送数据包。本文介绍[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)协议的工作机制。

  

**1、TCP协议作用**

  

[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)协议使用的是面向连接的方法进行通信的，其作用如下：

  

**面向流的处理：**[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)以流的方式处理数据。换句话说，[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)可以一个字节一个字节地接收数据，而不是一次接收一个预订格式的数据块。[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)把接收到的数据组成长度不等的段，再传递到网际层。

  

**重新排序：**如果数据以错误的顺序到达目的地，[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)模块能够对数据重新排序，来恢复原始数据。

  

**流量控制：**[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)能够确保数据传输不会超过目的[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)接收数据的能力。

  

**优先级与安全：**为[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)连接设置可选的优先级和安全级别。

  

**适当的关闭：**以确保所有的数据被发送或接收以后，再进行关闭连接。

  

**2、TCP工作模式**

  

[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)协议的数据包进行传输采用的是[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)端和客户端模式。发送[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)数据请求方为客户端，另一方则为[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)端。客户端要与[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)端进行通信，[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)端必须开启[监听](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655135867&idx=3&sn=e349ed4409292ff8fb45de78de2fc63b&chksm=bc85b5948bf23c82837949f72ab35e34841f4223f733a3aab8b2e696c38e6337e6e88d722996&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的端口，客户端才能通过端口连接到[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，然后进行通信。

  

[netwox](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136054&idx=2&sn=597b1336b8261f8c8e3c1b0dac907be9&chksm=bc85b4d98bf23dcf5d878649edfd3c32906c7ad556982769abcc0124d7b14d6f909fd6127fc2&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工具提供了相关模块，用于建立[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)端和[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)客户端。客户端连接[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)端后，可以进行[数据通信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133252&idx=3&sn=1a058de64d01ff31cd7cdfc9caf41739&chksm=bc8583ab8bf20abdfebce9f4a836452655cf46b5dc6309d0e003eb3f0dce5860cc70d9734b37&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。为了能够对[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)端进行[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)操控，用户也可以建立[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8 **（1）建立TCP服务器端和TCP客户端**

  

为了能够完成客户端与[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)端之间的通信，可以使用[netwox](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136054&idx=2&sn=597b1336b8261f8c8e3c1b0dac907be9&chksm=bc85b4d98bf23dcf5d878649edfd3c32906c7ad556982769abcc0124d7b14d6f909fd6127fc2&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工具编号为89的模块建立[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)端，然后使用编号为87的模块建立[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)客户端。

  

已知[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)A的[IP地址](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136180&idx=2&sn=904af978bb0594c8dcaba143cdaaef2a&chksm=bc85b45b8bf23d4d22582d999cd61d7dca3d2ab79c7b04655201d9bf24a60a29f404ce3c7255&scene=21#wechat_redirect)为192.168.59.131，[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)B的[IP地址](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136180&idx=2&sn=904af978bb0594c8dcaba143cdaaef2a&chksm=bc85b45b8bf23d4d22582d999cd61d7dca3d2ab79c7b04655201d9bf24a60a29f404ce3c7255&scene=21#wechat_redirect)为192.168.59.135。分别在这两个[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上建立[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)端和客户端，并进行连接，[监听](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655135867&idx=3&sn=e349ed4409292ff8fb45de78de2fc63b&chksm=bc85b5948bf23c82837949f72ab35e34841f4223f733a3aab8b2e696c38e6337e6e88d722996&scene=21#wechat_redirect)指定端口上的通信信息。具体步骤如下：

  

1）在[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)A上建立[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)端，[监听](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655135867&idx=3&sn=e349ed4409292ff8fb45de78de2fc63b&chksm=bc85b5948bf23c82837949f72ab35e34841f4223f733a3aab8b2e696c38e6337e6e88d722996&scene=21#wechat_redirect)端口为80，执行命令如下：

**（1）源端口和目的端口字段**

  

[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)源端口（Source Port）：源[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上的应用程序的端口号，占16位。

  

[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)目的端口（Destination Port）：目标[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的应用程序端口号，占16位。

  

**（2）序列号字段**

  

[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)序列号（Sequence Number）：占32位。它表示本报文段所发送数据的第一个字节的编号。在[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)连接中，所传送的字节流的每一个字节都会按顺序编号。当SYN标记不为1时，这是当前数据分段第一个字母的序列号；如果SYN的值是1时，这个字段的值就是初始序列值（ISN），用于对序列号进行同步。这时，第一个字节的序列号比这个字段的值大1，也就是ISN加1。

  

**（3）确认号字段**

  

[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)确认号（Acknowledgment Number，ACK Number）：占32位。它表示接收方期望收到发送方下一个报文段的第一个字节数据的编号。其值是接收[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)即将接收到的下一个序列号，也就是下一个接收到的字节的序列号加1。

  

**（4）数据偏移字段**

  

[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)首部长度（Header Length）：数据偏移是指数据段中的“数据”部分起始处距离[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)数据段起始处的字节偏移量，占4位。其实这里的“数据偏移”也是在确定[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)数据段头部分的长度，告诉接收端的应用程序，数据从何处开始。

  

**（5）保留字段**

  

保留（Reserved）：占4位。为[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)将来的发展预留空间，目前必须全部为0。

  

**（6）标志位字段**

  

CWR（Congestion Window Reduce）：拥塞窗口减少标志，用来表明它接收到了设置ECE标志的[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)包。并且，发送方收到消息之后，通过减小发送窗口的大小来降低发送速率。

  

ECE（ECN Echo）：用来在[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)三次握手时表明一个[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)端是具备ECN功能的。在[数据传输](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132673&idx=2&sn=4ebed9559e94616637f5bce407cdd9a5&chksm=bc8581ee8bf208f815cb8279d3c10de158484886ffa52d39680ad373bf945014a4ac69635b03&scene=21#wechat_redirect)过程中，它也用来表明接收到的[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)包的IP头部的ECN被设置为11，即网络线路拥堵。

  

URG（Urgent）：表示本报文段中发送的数据是否包含紧急数据。URG=1时表示有紧急数据。当URG=1时，后面的紧急指针字段才有效。

  

ACK：表示前面的确认号字段是否有效。ACK=1时表示有效。只有当ACK=1时，前面的确认号字段才有效。[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)规定，连接建立后，ACK必须为1。

  

PSH（Push）：告诉对方收到该报文段后是否立即把数据推送给上层。如果值为1，表示应当立即把数据提交给上层，而不是缓存起来。

  

RST：表示是否重置连接。如果RST=1，说明[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)连接出现了严重错误（如[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)崩溃），必须释放连接，然后再重新建立连接。

  

SYN：在建立连接时使用，用来同步序号。当SYN=1，ACK=0时，表示这是一个请求建立连接的报文段；当SYN=1，ACK=1时，表示对方同意建立连接。SYN=1时，说明这是一个请求建立连接或同意建立连接的报文。只有在前两次握手中SYN才为1。

  

FIN：标记数据是否发送完毕。如果FIN=1，表示数据已经发送完成，可以释放连接。

  

**（7）窗口大小字段**

  

窗口大小（Window Size）：占16位。它表示从Ack Number开始还可以接收多少字节的数据量，也表示当前接收端的接收窗口还有多少剩余空间。该字段可以用于[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的流量控制。

  

**（8）TCP校验和字段**  

  

校验位（[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect) Checksum）：占16位。它用于确认传输的数据是否有损坏。发送端基于数据内容校验生成一个数值，接收端根据接收的数据校验生成一个值。两个值必须相同，才能证明数据是有效的。如果两个值不同，则丢掉这个数据包。Checksum是根据伪头+[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)头+[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)数据三部分进行计算的。

  

**（9）紧急指针字段**  

  

紧急指针（Urgent Pointer）：仅当前面的URG控制位为1时才有意义。它指出本数据段中为紧急数据的字节数，占16位。当所有紧急数据处理完后，[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)就会告诉应用程序恢复到正常操作。即使当前窗口大小为0，也是可以发送紧急数据的，因为紧急数据无须缓存。

  

**（10）可选项字段**  

  

选项（Option）：长度不定，但长度必须是32bits的整数倍。
