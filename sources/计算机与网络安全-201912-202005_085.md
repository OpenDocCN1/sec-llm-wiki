曾经被广泛曝光的网络[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)偷窥[黑产](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126580&idx=3&sn=c8b22ba96245b8899740c6028d3676be&chksm=bc8599db8bf210cd3d548bd8f108fb7756f53ed04726398699fc03c5d96aef142d6b77755a8a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，似乎又有冒头之势。近日，有媒体报道称，有一些人花几百元，就可以轻易买到陌生人的监控账号，通过它们在偷偷地“看”着你。花200元可偷窥100个[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，卖家“**保证对着床**”。记者调查发现，网上售卖监控账号IP已成产业链条，检索输入相关关键词会弹出不少所谓行业交流群。

  

网络[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)监控早已不是新鲜事物，此前就有多个网络[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)品牌不顾公众[隐私](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127519&idx=3&sn=a846e840b4ac021e01ba4a64c9228817&chksm=bc859a308bf2132693545d19fdb92d481e63a9d92d18960c1e4c198747b628d2bad763105175&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，默认公开直播信号源或外泄[数据库](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129320&idx=3&sn=e5b2f0721ffd54f8891e633fbc280f4b&chksm=bc8593078bf21a1184c1596a84ce0604e00b716de6081c5b27ea0311d1df5e2b5024ba752979&scene=21#wechat_redirect)资料引发众怒。从媒体报道中，可以发现此次被曝光网络[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)偷窥[黑产](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126580&idx=3&sn=c8b22ba96245b8899740c6028d3676be&chksm=bc8599db8bf210cd3d548bd8f108fb7756f53ed04726398699fc03c5d96aef142d6b77755a8a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)依旧是熟悉的套路，还是利用安装在家庭居所或酒店房间内的[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，通过售卖账号IP来达到购买群体偷窥的目的。

  

在酒店客房安装网络[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)监控，是明眼人都知晓的违法行为，在这些年的反复报道中，公众对此类行为的警惕性相对较高。然而，此次案件中更严重的问题，应是那些网络[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的家庭用户。

  

网络[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)监控的安装，通常是用户自主行为。他们的目的为了家庭防盗、照看老人或小孩、监控宠物，会将[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)安放于客厅、卧室等位置，并且尽可能对家庭内进行全覆盖。

  

在这样的用户需求前提下，我们可以在类似的事件发生后，对网络[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品消费者建议，出于[隐私保护](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130605&idx=3&sn=f44f62563bc608af7c1f8492ae66d7bf&chksm=bc858e028bf207144824289f24de4ced59bc791099cbf81fdcde9413ef3038189784a26b84a4&scene=21#wechat_redirect)考虑，人在家中应及时将[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)断电、遮挡[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)镜头、加强产品用户[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)等。但这样的建议治标不治本，用户出于需求和信赖，使用网络[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品，但这一群体中的绝大部分不具备网络安防技术，因此不能将用户个人[隐私](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127519&idx=3&sn=a846e840b4ac021e01ba4a64c9228817&chksm=bc859a308bf2132693545d19fdb92d481e63a9d92d18960c1e4c198747b628d2bad763105175&scene=21#wechat_redirect)泄露的责任和第一重防范甩给用户自身承担。

  

在此次网络[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)偷窥[黑产](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126580&idx=3&sn=c8b22ba96245b8899740c6028d3676be&chksm=bc8599db8bf210cd3d548bd8f108fb7756f53ed04726398699fc03c5d96aef142d6b77755a8a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)曝光后，应该引起相关行业和有责部门重视的是，如何将用户个人[隐私保护](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130605&idx=3&sn=f44f62563bc608af7c1f8492ae66d7bf&chksm=bc858e028bf207144824289f24de4ced59bc791099cbf81fdcde9413ef3038189784a26b84a4&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的第一道防线在企业产品中建立起来。可以看出，企业的不作为往往很容易造成此类事件的频繁发生。此次案件涉及了一款名为云视通的[APP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130459&idx=3&sn=f8b64d61307c28f9f3265b4eb1eda12a&chksm=bc858eb48bf207a2b2fd53128cd92797950f71b4e141abfb9c60970b6a662bc1849e85912dff&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。检索资料可以发现，云视通是一个[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)网络视频监控平台，售卖的监控设备IP账号，便可以通过这一平台关联使用。

  

这样的平台产品，相信其设计初衷定然是好的，但企业不能因此而放松对产品和用户的保护。加强企业产品的[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)建设，防止[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=3&sn=c82f59076adf4123590301c9577f3c59&chksm=bc859b348bf21222ef281bc9aebb654a06582273fd7d9eb73ab84b56adbf383f74305efe3c3c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[拖库、撞库](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655119981&idx=3&sn=efd68422f1dc99bf07d8d136dd1477dc&chksm=bc8677828bf1fe94ffd446950397a61493ac87002cf9100e1b24e5fcc22e66eccf5d8810d0d1&scene=21#wechat_redirect)获取相关信息；完善网络监控账号IP的关联绑定认证，尽可能在流程上确保IP使用者是合法获得……当然，前置防范的要求还应施加在网络[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)厂商身上，必须要求提高其网络[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)视频传输信号的加密等级等。总之，必须在各个环节中提高安全防范，为用户、为消费者守住第一道[安全防护](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132591&idx=3&sn=0e73014aa2b38745a8259c348e71680c&chksm=bc8586408bf20f564af8b79e6b9e006803065e2aa2f69af8d33554c7622c6ce7198559c23ced&scene=21#wechat_redirect)关卡。

  

当下，保护消费者的个人[隐私](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127519&idx=3&sn=a846e840b4ac021e01ba4a64c9228817&chksm=bc859a308bf2132693545d19fdb92d481e63a9d92d18960c1e4c198747b628d2bad763105175&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是相关市场经济服务的核心，这一核心诉求是衡量一款产品好坏的最基础指标。现行法律法规已对侵犯个人[隐私](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127519&idx=3&sn=a846e840b4ac021e01ba4a64c9228817&chksm=bc859a308bf2132693545d19fdb92d481e63a9d92d18960c1e4c198747b628d2bad763105175&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的行为有了诸多明确规定，因此，行政监管也应该在行业准入标准上重做考量，重审用户[隐私](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127519&idx=3&sn=a846e840b4ac021e01ba4a64c9228817&chksm=bc859a308bf2132693545d19fdb92d481e63a9d92d18960c1e4c198747b628d2bad763105175&scene=21#wechat_redirect)重要性，不让此类网络[摄像头](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127887&idx=3&sn=21d88dcc1a39ed56ad716594ce52f69b&chksm=bc8594a08bf21db63e2bb434ffe246e5321d92004794990c30ea5ca560a18dc0f9a1934ef2ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)监控产业时不时就成为一个泄漏用户[隐私](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127519&idx=3&sn=a846e840b4ac021e01ba4a64c9228817&chksm=bc859a308bf2132693545d19fdb92d481e63a9d92d18960c1e4c198747b628d2bad763105175&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的“[直播](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127937&idx=3&sn=2e50c340981eec8f05b1106fba3c4c33&chksm=bc85946e8bf21d787ed676a4a6b74b46dffe3f2867de3d328f2a95b2e055f252ebfd77e020ec&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”平台。
