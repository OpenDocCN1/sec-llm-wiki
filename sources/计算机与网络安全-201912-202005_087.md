传输层（Transport Layer）是[OSI协议](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132810&idx=2&sn=ee7f1720a26e1b82f9fb49a3657bc698&chksm=bc8581658bf2087393c30977fcac770581ab0565ccbb20dc781cc0caf6da8284d33405244c10&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的第四层协议，是唯一负责总体的[数据传输](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132673&idx=2&sn=4ebed9559e94616637f5bce407cdd9a5&chksm=bc8581ee8bf208f815cb8279d3c10de158484886ffa52d39680ad373bf945014a4ac69635b03&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和数据控制传输层的一层协议。传输层提供端到端的交换数据机制，它不仅对会话层、表示层和应用层这高三层提供可靠的传输服务，还对网络层提供可靠的目的地站点信息。

  

**1、传输层的作用**

  

网际层提供了[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)之间的逻辑通道，即通过寻址的方式，把数据包从一个[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发到另一个[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上。如果一个[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)有多个[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)同时在使用网络连接，那么数据包到达[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)之后，如何区分它属于哪个[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)呢？为了区分数据包所属的[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，就需要使用到传输层。传输层提供了应用[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)之间的端到端连接，其作用如下：

  

● 为网络应用程序提供接口。

  

● 为端到端连接提供流量控制、[差错控制](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132731&idx=2&sn=8ecf2c9c24946b3198f13785f4c34d7d&chksm=bc8581d48bf208c29f7964aec04d2e28ebeeb41f6c189812e9b5d6f4ee30cdcdeff0c2bf99cb&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、服务质量等管理服务。

  

● 提供多路复用、多路分解机制。

  

**2、面向连接和无连接**

  

针对不同情况下的数据质量保证，传输层提供了两种[数据传输](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132673&idx=2&sn=4ebed9559e94616637f5bce407cdd9a5&chksm=bc8581ee8bf208f815cb8279d3c10de158484886ffa52d39680ad373bf945014a4ac69635b03&scene=21#wechat_redirect)协议类型，分别为面向连接与无连接。下面介绍这两种类型的概念和工作原理。

  

**（1）面向连接**

  

面向连接就是通信双方在通信时，要事先建立一条通信线路，然后进行通信。其过程分为三个阶段。第一阶段是建立连接。第二阶段是连接成功建立之后，进行[数据传输](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132673&idx=2&sn=4ebed9559e94616637f5bce407cdd9a5&chksm=bc8581ee8bf208f815cb8279d3c10de158484886ffa52d39680ad373bf945014a4ac69635b03&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。第三阶段是在数据传输完毕后，释放连接。

  

下面以李四与张三的对话来说明面向连接的工作原理，如图1所示。其中，李四向张三说的每一句话都要得到张三的回应，然后才会说下一句话，直到李四说完最后一句话。

![](../Images/5c55dd52ae2a71f1831767d8d5ac1e13.png)

图1  面向连接

  

**（2）无连接**

  

无连接是指通信双方不需要事先建立通信线路，而是把每个带有目的地址的包（报文分组）发送到线路上，由系统选定路线进行传输，不需要目标方进行回复。

  

下面以李四与张三的对话来说明无连接的工作原理，如图2所示。从图中可以看到，李四向张三说出了要说的话，而不需要得到张三的回复。

**3、端口和套接字**

  

为了区分同一个[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上不同应用程序的数据包，传输层提供了端口和套接字概念。下面介绍端口和套接字的作用。

  

**（1）端口的作用**

在数据链路层中，通过MAC地址来寻找[局域网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133196&idx=3&sn=fced3cdaddc933dbd9589c7cbfb41c3d&chksm=bc8583e38bf20af512424bf8e3328b45e53324d55ba487f38c118ae17454713ce6962aec0da9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中的[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)；在网际层中，通过[IP地址](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136180&idx=2&sn=904af978bb0594c8dcaba143cdaaef2a&chksm=bc85b45b8bf23d4d22582d999cd61d7dca3d2ab79c7b04655201d9bf24a60a29f404ce3c7255&scene=21#wechat_redirect)来寻找网络中互连的[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)或[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。在传输层中，需要通过端口进行寻址，来识别同一[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中同时通信的不同应用程序。

**（3）套接字**

  

应用层通过传输层进行数据通信时，[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和UDP会遇到需要同时为多个应用程序[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)提供并发服务的问题。多个[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)连接或多个应用程序[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)可能需要通过同一个[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)协议端口传输数据。为了区别不同的应用程序[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和连接，许多[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[操作系统](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122728&idx=2&sn=6b263bf85d6b8bd8bf1d3744a491a40c&chksm=bc8668c78bf1e1d1a0e813fcd7b41c7bc300c2c98eb269b77f92854c62f1fe5127a0e01c5574&scene=21#wechat_redirect)为应用程序与[TCP／IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132849&idx=2&sn=ef83e17a64981d39a9c24e568a8b704d&chksm=bc85815e8bf20848e37dfd677f426b6ce368140bcd7048d19d73efe55a8474d47d2081dbee0c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)协议交互提供了称为套接字（Socket）的接口，区分不同应用程序[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)间的网络通信和连接。

  

套接字是由[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的[IP地址](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136180&idx=2&sn=904af978bb0594c8dcaba143cdaaef2a&chksm=bc85b45b8bf23d4d22582d999cd61d7dca3d2ab79c7b04655201d9bf24a60a29f404ce3c7255&scene=21#wechat_redirect)加上[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上的端口号组成的地址。例如，套接字地址101.102.103.104：21，表示指向[IP地址](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136180&idx=2&sn=904af978bb0594c8dcaba143cdaaef2a&chksm=bc85b45b8bf23d4d22582d999cd61d7dca3d2ab79c7b04655201d9bf24a60a29f404ce3c7255&scene=21#wechat_redirect)为101.102.103.104的[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的21端口。

  

**4、多路复用和多路分解**

  

在网络上[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)与[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)之间的通信实质上是[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上运行的应用[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)之间的通信。在进行通信时，往往同时运行多个应用程序。为了能够让一个[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)同时支持多个网络程序，并且同时保持与多台[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进行连接，就需要使用多路复用和多路分解，其含义如下：

  

● 多路复用：从源[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的不同套接字中收集数据块，并为每个数据块封装首部信息，从而生成报文段，然后将报文段传递到网络层中。

  

● 多路分解：将传输层报文段中的数据交付到正确的套接字。
