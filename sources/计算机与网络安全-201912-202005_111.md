控制报文协议（Internet Control Message Protocol，ICMP）是[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132849&idx=2&sn=ef83e17a64981d39a9c24e568a8b704d&chksm=bc85815e8bf20848e37dfd677f426b6ce368140bcd7048d19d73efe55a8474d47d2081dbee0c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)协议族的一个子协议。ICMP协议用于在IP[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)之间传递控制消息，描述网络是否通畅、[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是否可达、[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是否可用等网络状态。

  

由于[IP协议](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136197&idx=2&sn=f8b46447bb319c91d8d25d916ce14958&chksm=bc85b42a8bf23d3c20a2ea316e9029009dfdf74bbe5fbcd1856a6f0d0e07cfdeea598c7c6f48&scene=21#wechat_redirect)简单，[数据传输](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132673&idx=2&sn=4ebed9559e94616637f5bce407cdd9a5&chksm=bc8581ee8bf208f815cb8279d3c10de158484886ffa52d39680ad373bf945014a4ac69635b03&scene=21#wechat_redirect)天然存在不可靠、无连接等特点，为了解决[数据传输](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132673&idx=2&sn=4ebed9559e94616637f5bce407cdd9a5&chksm=bc8581ee8bf208f815cb8279d3c10de158484886ffa52d39680ad373bf945014a4ac69635b03&scene=21#wechat_redirect)出现的问题，人们引入了ICMP协议。虽然ICMP协议的数据包并不传输用户数据，但是对于用户数据的传递起着重要的作用。

  

数据包在发送到目标[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的过程中，通常会经过一个或多个[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。而数据包在通过这些路由进行传输时，可能会遇到各种问题，导致数据包无法发送到目标[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上。为了了解数据包在传输的过程中在哪个环节出现了问题，就需要用到ICMP协议。它可以跟踪消息，把问题反馈给源[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

ICMP报文一般为8个字节，包括类型、[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、校验和扩展内容字段。ICMP报文基本结构如图1所示。

其中，类型表示ICMP的消息类型；[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)表示对类型的进一步说明；校验和表示对整个报文的报文信息的校验。在ICMP报文中，如果类型和[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)不同，ICMP数据包报告的消息含义也会不同。常见的类型和[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的ICMP含义如表1所示。
