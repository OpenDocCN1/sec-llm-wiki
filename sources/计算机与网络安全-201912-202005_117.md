**1、扫描单一主机**

  

如果用户想判断一个[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是否启用，可以使用[netwox](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136054&idx=2&sn=597b1336b8261f8c8e3c1b0dac907be9&chksm=bc85b4d98bf23dcf5d878649edfd3c32906c7ad556982769abcc0124d7b14d6f909fd6127fc2&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工具中编号为55的模块对目标[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进行[ARP协议](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136267&idx=2&sn=39b69117f4f796300be1563c5f18ef61&chksm=bc85b7e48bf23ef25b6f9dd514009ae3c20aaef7707d3befcd0359e35d8c3190ba83d1c98de9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)扫描。

**2、扫描多个主机**

  

有时用户需要判断多个[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的启用情况，使用上述的扫描方式需要分别对每个[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进行扫描，这样比较费时。这时，可以使用[netwox](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136054&idx=2&sn=597b1336b8261f8c8e3c1b0dac907be9&chksm=bc85b4d98bf23dcf5d878649edfd3c32906c7ad556982769abcc0124d7b14d6f909fd6127fc2&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工具中编号为71的模块进行扫描。如果[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)启用，则返回对应的MAC地址。

  

判断[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)192.168.59.133-192.168.59.140的启用情况。

**5、周期性发送ARP响应包**
