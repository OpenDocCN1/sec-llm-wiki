**1、协议包的结构**

  

[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136245&idx=3&sn=96e9cdde916746534d01ed2aca0754aa&chksm=bc85b41a8bf23d0cf4d75e845fdb34c34f709c94ee8fe343e3c3a505bedcd4641f8280bdde35&scene=21#wechat_redirect)协议是通过报文进行工作的，[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136245&idx=3&sn=96e9cdde916746534d01ed2aca0754aa&chksm=bc85b41a8bf23d0cf4d75e845fdb34c34f709c94ee8fe343e3c3a505bedcd4641f8280bdde35&scene=21#wechat_redirect)报文格式如图1所示。

![](../Images/4ff51fa66719310624c39c8ddf2dc581.png)

图1  ARP报文格式

  

[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136245&idx=3&sn=96e9cdde916746534d01ed2aca0754aa&chksm=bc85b41a8bf23d0cf4d75e845fdb34c34f709c94ee8fe343e3c3a505bedcd4641f8280bdde35&scene=21#wechat_redirect)报文总长度为28字节，MAC地址长度为6字节，[IP地址](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136180&idx=2&sn=904af978bb0594c8dcaba143cdaaef2a&chksm=bc85b45b8bf23d4d22582d999cd61d7dca3d2ab79c7b04655201d9bf24a60a29f404ce3c7255&scene=21#wechat_redirect)长度为4字节。

  

其中，每个字段的含义如下。

  

**硬件类型：**指明了发送方想知道的硬件接口类型，以太网的值为1。

  

**协议类型：**表示要映射的协议地址类型。它的值为0x0800，表示[IP地址](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136180&idx=2&sn=904af978bb0594c8dcaba143cdaaef2a&chksm=bc85b45b8bf23d4d22582d999cd61d7dca3d2ab79c7b04655201d9bf24a60a29f404ce3c7255&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

**硬件地址长度和协议长度：**分别指出硬件地址和协议的长度，以字节为单位。对于以太网上[IP地址](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136180&idx=2&sn=904af978bb0594c8dcaba143cdaaef2a&chksm=bc85b45b8bf23d4d22582d999cd61d7dca3d2ab79c7b04655201d9bf24a60a29f404ce3c7255&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136245&idx=3&sn=96e9cdde916746534d01ed2aca0754aa&chksm=bc85b41a8bf23d0cf4d75e845fdb34c34f709c94ee8fe343e3c3a505bedcd4641f8280bdde35&scene=21#wechat_redirect)请求或应答来说，它们的值分别为6和4。

  

**操作类型：**用来表示这个报文的类型，[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136245&idx=3&sn=96e9cdde916746534d01ed2aca0754aa&chksm=bc85b41a8bf23d0cf4d75e845fdb34c34f709c94ee8fe343e3c3a505bedcd4641f8280bdde35&scene=21#wechat_redirect)请求为1，[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136245&idx=3&sn=96e9cdde916746534d01ed2aca0754aa&chksm=bc85b41a8bf23d0cf4d75e845fdb34c34f709c94ee8fe343e3c3a505bedcd4641f8280bdde35&scene=21#wechat_redirect)响应为2，RARP请求为3，RARP响应为4。

  

**发送方MAC地址：**发送方设备的硬件地址。

  

**发送方IP地址：**发送方设备的[IP地址](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136180&idx=2&sn=904af978bb0594c8dcaba143cdaaef2a&chksm=bc85b45b8bf23d4d22582d999cd61d7dca3d2ab79c7b04655201d9bf24a60a29f404ce3c7255&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

**目标MAC地址：**接收方设备的硬件地址。

  

**目标IP地址：**接收方设备的[IP地址](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136180&idx=2&sn=904af978bb0594c8dcaba143cdaaef2a&chksm=bc85b45b8bf23d4d22582d999cd61d7dca3d2ab79c7b04655201d9bf24a60a29f404ce3c7255&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136245&idx=3&sn=96e9cdde916746534d01ed2aca0754aa&chksm=bc85b41a8bf23d0cf4d75e845fdb34c34f709c94ee8fe343e3c3a505bedcd4641f8280bdde35&scene=21#wechat_redirect)数据包分为请求包和响应包，对应报文中的某些字段值也有所不同。

  

1）[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136245&idx=3&sn=96e9cdde916746534d01ed2aca0754aa&chksm=bc85b41a8bf23d0cf4d75e845fdb34c34f709c94ee8fe343e3c3a505bedcd4641f8280bdde35&scene=21#wechat_redirect)请求包报文的操作类型（op）字段的值为request(1)，目标MAC地址字段的值为Target 00：00：00\_00：00：00(00：00：00：00：00：00)（广播地址）。

  

2）[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136245&idx=3&sn=96e9cdde916746534d01ed2aca0754aa&chksm=bc85b41a8bf23d0cf4d75e845fdb34c34f709c94ee8fe343e3c3a505bedcd4641f8280bdde35&scene=21#wechat_redirect)响应包报文中操作类型（op）字段的值为reply(2)，目标MAC地址字段的值为目标[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的硬件地址。

  

**2、构造ARP包**

  

[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136245&idx=3&sn=96e9cdde916746534d01ed2aca0754aa&chksm=bc85b41a8bf23d0cf4d75e845fdb34c34f709c94ee8fe343e3c3a505bedcd4641f8280bdde35&scene=21#wechat_redirect)数据包默认由[操作系统](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122728&idx=2&sn=6b263bf85d6b8bd8bf1d3744a491a40c&chksm=bc8668c78bf1e1d1a0e813fcd7b41c7bc300c2c98eb269b77f92854c62f1fe5127a0e01c5574&scene=21#wechat_redirect)自动发送。用户可以自己构造[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136245&idx=3&sn=96e9cdde916746534d01ed2aca0754aa&chksm=bc85b41a8bf23d0cf4d75e845fdb34c34f709c94ee8fe343e3c3a505bedcd4641f8280bdde35&scene=21#wechat_redirect)包，向目标[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发送请求，从而获取目标[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的MAC地址。这时，可以使用[netwox](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136054&idx=2&sn=597b1336b8261f8c8e3c1b0dac907be9&chksm=bc85b4d98bf23dcf5d878649edfd3c32906c7ad556982769abcc0124d7b14d6f909fd6127fc2&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工具提供的编号为33的模块。

  

使用[netwox](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136054&idx=2&sn=597b1336b8261f8c8e3c1b0dac907be9&chksm=bc85b4d98bf23dcf5d878649edfd3c32906c7ad556982769abcc0124d7b14d6f909fd6127fc2&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工具构造[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136245&idx=3&sn=96e9cdde916746534d01ed2aca0754aa&chksm=bc85b41a8bf23d0cf4d75e845fdb34c34f709c94ee8fe343e3c3a505bedcd4641f8280bdde35&scene=21#wechat_redirect)包。

  

1）查看[netwox](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136054&idx=2&sn=597b1336b8261f8c8e3c1b0dac907be9&chksm=bc85b4d98bf23dcf5d878649edfd3c32906c7ad556982769abcc0124d7b14d6f909fd6127fc2&scene=21#wechat_redirect)所在[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)默认的[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655136245&idx=3&sn=96e9cdde916746534d01ed2aca0754aa&chksm=bc85b41a8bf23d0cf4d75e845fdb34c34f709c94ee8fe343e3c3a505bedcd4641f8280bdde35&scene=21#wechat_redirect)包的相关信息，执行命令如下：
