地址解析协议（Address Resolution Protocol，[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117609&idx=3&sn=922328fdf363dca2bc330ae0b44aa782&chksm=bc867cc68bf1f5d0968afe8925d54fab8047315be8aa5039b7bd2b97912c244e925f0229de7f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)）是根据IP地址获取物理地址的一个[TCP/IP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132849&idx=2&sn=ef83e17a64981d39a9c24e568a8b704d&chksm=bc85815e8bf20848e37dfd677f426b6ce368140bcd7048d19d73efe55a8474d47d2081dbee0c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)协议。它通过IP地址向MAC地址的转换，解决网际层和网络访问层的衔接问题。

  

由于IP地址和MAC地址[定位](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132478&idx=3&sn=e1f269e93869552f9bc28106bc953c53&chksm=bc8586d18bf20fc71e48eab8d196d4495d034840574faf53e91fecf13ec05c86745bea80fbbe&scene=21#wechat_redirect)方式不同，[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117609&idx=3&sn=922328fdf363dca2bc330ae0b44aa782&chksm=bc867cc68bf1f5d0968afe8925d54fab8047315be8aa5039b7bd2b97912c244e925f0229de7f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)协议成为[数据传输](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132673&idx=2&sn=4ebed9559e94616637f5bce407cdd9a5&chksm=bc8581ee8bf208f815cb8279d3c10de158484886ffa52d39680ad373bf945014a4ac69635b03&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的必备协议。[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发送信息前，必须通过[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117609&idx=3&sn=922328fdf363dca2bc330ae0b44aa782&chksm=bc867cc68bf1f5d0968afe8925d54fab8047315be8aa5039b7bd2b97912c244e925f0229de7f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)协议获取目标IP地址对应的MAC地址，才能正确地发送数据包。本文介绍[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117609&idx=3&sn=922328fdf363dca2bc330ae0b44aa782&chksm=bc867cc68bf1f5d0968afe8925d54fab8047315be8aa5039b7bd2b97912c244e925f0229de7f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)协议的工作机制和结构。

  

**1、为什么需要ARP协议**

  

在网络访问层中，同一[局域网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133196&idx=3&sn=fced3cdaddc933dbd9589c7cbfb41c3d&chksm=bc8583e38bf20af512424bf8e3328b45e53324d55ba487f38c118ae17454713ce6962aec0da9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中的一台[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)要和另一台[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进行通信，需要通过MAC地址进行[定位](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132478&idx=3&sn=e1f269e93869552f9bc28106bc953c53&chksm=bc8586d18bf20fc71e48eab8d196d4495d034840574faf53e91fecf13ec05c86745bea80fbbe&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，然后才能进行数据包的发送。而在网络层和传输层中，[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)之间是通过IP地址[定位](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132478&idx=3&sn=e1f269e93869552f9bc28106bc953c53&chksm=bc8586d18bf20fc71e48eab8d196d4495d034840574faf53e91fecf13ec05c86745bea80fbbe&scene=21#wechat_redirect)目标[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，对应的数据报文只包含目标[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的IP地址，而没有MAC地址。因此，在发送之前需要根据IP地址获取MAC地址，然后才能将数据包发送到正确的目标[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，而这个获取过程是通过[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117609&idx=3&sn=922328fdf363dca2bc330ae0b44aa782&chksm=bc867cc68bf1f5d0968afe8925d54fab8047315be8aa5039b7bd2b97912c244e925f0229de7f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)协议完成的。

  

**2、基本流程**

  

[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117609&idx=3&sn=922328fdf363dca2bc330ae0b44aa782&chksm=bc867cc68bf1f5d0968afe8925d54fab8047315be8aa5039b7bd2b97912c244e925f0229de7f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工作流程分为两个阶段，一个是[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117609&idx=3&sn=922328fdf363dca2bc330ae0b44aa782&chksm=bc867cc68bf1f5d0968afe8925d54fab8047315be8aa5039b7bd2b97912c244e925f0229de7f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)请求过程，另一个是[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117609&idx=3&sn=922328fdf363dca2bc330ae0b44aa782&chksm=bc867cc68bf1f5d0968afe8925d54fab8047315be8aa5039b7bd2b97912c244e925f0229de7f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)响应过程。工作流程如图1和图2所示。

图1和图2中，[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)A的IP地址为192.168.1.1，[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)B的IP地址为192.168.1.2。[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)A与[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)B进行通信，需要获取其MAC地址，基本流程如下：

  

1）[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)A以广播形式向网络中所有[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发送[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117609&idx=3&sn=922328fdf363dca2bc330ae0b44aa782&chksm=bc867cc68bf1f5d0968afe8925d54fab8047315be8aa5039b7bd2b97912c244e925f0229de7f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)请求，请求包中包含了目标IP地址192.168.1.2。

  

2）[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)B接收到请求，发现自己就是[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)A要找的[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，返回响应。响应包中包含自己的MAC地址。

  

**3、ARP缓存**

  

在请求目标[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的MAC地址时，每次获取目标[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)MAC地址都需要发送一次[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117609&idx=3&sn=922328fdf363dca2bc330ae0b44aa782&chksm=bc867cc68bf1f5d0968afe8925d54fab8047315be8aa5039b7bd2b97912c244e925f0229de7f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)请求，然后根据响应获取到MAC地址。为了避免重复发送[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117609&idx=3&sn=922328fdf363dca2bc330ae0b44aa782&chksm=bc867cc68bf1f5d0968afe8925d54fab8047315be8aa5039b7bd2b97912c244e925f0229de7f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)请求，每台[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)都有一个[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117609&idx=3&sn=922328fdf363dca2bc330ae0b44aa782&chksm=bc867cc68bf1f5d0968afe8925d54fab8047315be8aa5039b7bd2b97912c244e925f0229de7f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)高速缓存。当[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)得到[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117609&idx=3&sn=922328fdf363dca2bc330ae0b44aa782&chksm=bc867cc68bf1f5d0968afe8925d54fab8047315be8aa5039b7bd2b97912c244e925f0229de7f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)响应后，将目标[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的IP地址和物理地址存入本机[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117609&idx=3&sn=922328fdf363dca2bc330ae0b44aa782&chksm=bc867cc68bf1f5d0968afe8925d54fab8047315be8aa5039b7bd2b97912c244e925f0229de7f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)缓存中，并保留一定时间。只要在这个时间范围内，下次请求MAC地址时，直接查询[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117609&idx=3&sn=922328fdf363dca2bc330ae0b44aa782&chksm=bc867cc68bf1f5d0968afe8925d54fab8047315be8aa5039b7bd2b97912c244e925f0229de7f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)缓存，而无须再发送[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117609&idx=3&sn=922328fdf363dca2bc330ae0b44aa782&chksm=bc867cc68bf1f5d0968afe8925d54fab8047315be8aa5039b7bd2b97912c244e925f0229de7f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)请求，从而节约了网络资源。

  

当有了[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117609&idx=3&sn=922328fdf363dca2bc330ae0b44aa782&chksm=bc867cc68bf1f5d0968afe8925d54fab8047315be8aa5039b7bd2b97912c244e925f0229de7f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)缓存以后，[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117609&idx=3&sn=922328fdf363dca2bc330ae0b44aa782&chksm=bc867cc68bf1f5d0968afe8925d54fab8047315be8aa5039b7bd2b97912c244e925f0229de7f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的工作流程如下：

  

1）[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)A在本机[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117609&idx=3&sn=922328fdf363dca2bc330ae0b44aa782&chksm=bc867cc68bf1f5d0968afe8925d54fab8047315be8aa5039b7bd2b97912c244e925f0229de7f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)缓存中检查[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)B的匹配MAC地址。

**4、查看ARP缓存**

  

每次成功得到[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117609&idx=3&sn=922328fdf363dca2bc330ae0b44aa782&chksm=bc867cc68bf1f5d0968afe8925d54fab8047315be8aa5039b7bd2b97912c244e925f0229de7f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)响应以后，就会将IP地址对应的MAC地址添加到[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117609&idx=3&sn=922328fdf363dca2bc330ae0b44aa782&chksm=bc867cc68bf1f5d0968afe8925d54fab8047315be8aa5039b7bd2b97912c244e925f0229de7f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)缓存中。用户可以通过arp命令查看[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117609&idx=3&sn=922328fdf363dca2bc330ae0b44aa782&chksm=bc867cc68bf1f5d0968afe8925d54fab8047315be8aa5039b7bd2b97912c244e925f0229de7f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)缓存中的信息，并验证是否会将目标IP地址和MAC地址添加到[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117609&idx=3&sn=922328fdf363dca2bc330ae0b44aa782&chksm=bc867cc68bf1f5d0968afe8925d54fab8047315be8aa5039b7bd2b97912c244e925f0229de7f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)缓存中。

  

查看[ARP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117609&idx=3&sn=922328fdf363dca2bc330ae0b44aa782&chksm=bc867cc68bf1f5d0968afe8925d54fab8047315be8aa5039b7bd2b97912c244e925f0229de7f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)缓存表并验证添加的IP地址和MAC地址。

  

1）使用arp命令查看当前[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)缓存信息，执行命令如下：

  

root@daxueba:~# arp -a

  

输出信息如下：

localhost (192.168.59.2) at 00:50:56:ea:f3:a1 \[ether\] on eth0

2）在当前[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上与[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)192.168.59.135进行通信。例如，可以使用ping命令探测该[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。执行命令如下：
