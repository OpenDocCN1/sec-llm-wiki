**一、清点拥有哪些资产**

  

如果没有完整的、详细的[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)资产清单，安全[运维](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655135970&idx=2&sn=00c1ac97803ed900c922b573f9dc0166&chksm=bc85b50d8bf23c1b1e365ea826a06b96b17ccade2ff3ed1b8912955743ef87e9c6b6af6ae17d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)团队将无法确保组织机构的安全，因为任何人都无法保护“未知”事物的安全性。本报告通过分析大量的企业级[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)核心资产情况，从而为各企业制定[安全防护](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132591&idx=3&sn=0e73014aa2b38745a8259c348e71680c&chksm=bc8586408bf20f564af8b79e6b9e006803065e2aa2f69af8d33554c7622c6ce7198559c23ced&scene=21#wechat_redirect)策略提供支持和帮助。

  

通过统计分析发现，在企业级客户中，超过81.45%的[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)使用都是[Linux](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133968&idx=2&sn=2454fe58d155dec7738888c75a963aeb&chksm=bc85bcff8bf235e97db0692fa805d2eeecdcaaa1e2a26f03ab5112230e873a342fcaca0bdf4f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[操作系统](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122728&idx=2&sn=6b263bf85d6b8bd8bf1d3744a491a40c&chksm=bc8668c78bf1e1d1a0e813fcd7b41c7bc300c2c98eb269b77f92854c62f1fe5127a0e01c5574&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，只有18.55%[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)使用的是[Windows](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127496&idx=3&sn=21acddfe6a98fe94ae346db72555e138&chksm=bc859a278bf21331d775fdb2224b50832a618a13d3a8fa76c41786186e846f5126daf96682bc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[操作系统](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122728&idx=2&sn=6b263bf85d6b8bd8bf1d3744a491a40c&chksm=bc8668c78bf1e1d1a0e813fcd7b41c7bc300c2c98eb269b77f92854c62f1fe5127a0e01c5574&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。这其中原因有很多，比如[Linux](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133968&idx=2&sn=2454fe58d155dec7738888c75a963aeb&chksm=bc85bcff8bf235e97db0692fa805d2eeecdcaaa1e2a26f03ab5112230e873a342fcaca0bdf4f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)兼容性更好、模块化、资源消耗少等等原因，让很多客户都会选择[Linux](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133968&idx=2&sn=2454fe58d155dec7738888c75a963aeb&chksm=bc85bcff8bf235e97db0692fa805d2eeecdcaaa1e2a26f03ab5112230e873a342fcaca0bdf4f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统。

当然，如果没有对[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)底层的[操作系统](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122728&idx=2&sn=6b263bf85d6b8bd8bf1d3744a491a40c&chksm=bc8668c78bf1e1d1a0e813fcd7b41c7bc300c2c98eb269b77f92854c62f1fe5127a0e01c5574&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进行适当配置，就会引发许多安全问题。建议[安全运维](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134279&idx=2&sn=98c1fbf140a3f0fbc70c7ff7fd1b0435&chksm=bc85bfa88bf236bee4fa96e94917bacca2186fc1ee19600acd70984dfac08f142dce7dee3b44&scene=21#wechat_redirect)人员能够谨慎配置[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)来满足组织机构的安全需求，并能够根据需求重新配置。通过研究分析样本数据，发现GRUB密码设置、UMASK值异常、未开启SYN COOKIE这三类问题是所有[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统风险中所占比例最多的三类。

**五、3个层面解读未来主机安全进化方向**

  

正如达尔文《进化论》说，进化来源于突变，而安全面对的正是“不可预知的未来”。[主机安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)作为[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131366&idx=2&sn=18215226672d73083f399efc8e61ab76&chksm=bc858b098bf2021f4ba79a264088a3761321619396e24fc817ec636744973de380bacdcd88b6&scene=21#wechat_redirect)领域中的重要分支，面对难以预测[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=3&sn=c82f59076adf4123590301c9577f3c59&chksm=bc859b348bf21222ef281bc9aebb654a06582273fd7d9eb73ab84b56adbf383f74305efe3c3c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)手段，传统的防范、阻止策略已经行不通。

  

一方面， [攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者和防守者处于天然不对等的地位，传统基于报警或已存在的威胁特征的检测技术，包括[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、[IPS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116999&idx=3&sn=366c450cbcb68f1be62fa41b6b40b472&chksm=bc8643288bf1ca3e49e8b1412e2e849e883a45f0b7cda9c791746f6beaa7f4de9852030d436d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、杀毒、沙箱等被动防御手段，更是让这种不平等愈发严重。很多被[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=3&sn=c82f59076adf4123590301c9577f3c59&chksm=bc859b348bf21222ef281bc9aebb654a06582273fd7d9eb73ab84b56adbf383f74305efe3c3c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)攻陷的企业组织，虽然已经构建了一定的[安全防御](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130803&idx=2&sn=86b4c7953e5788c8455c342c0e959b49&chksm=bc85895c8bf2004adb8d18ecdfba65e5778689ec5a12534ab5ac1863bdd63de846ae094f13ff&scene=21#wechat_redirect)体系，但仍然没能及时发现或阻止威胁，将损失降到最低。主要是因为当下检测体系在应对未知威胁过程中存在一些不足，表现为以下几个方面：

  

**检测技术单一：**基于签名检测技术无法检测未知威胁，更无法[定位](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132478&idx=3&sn=e1f269e93869552f9bc28106bc953c53&chksm=bc8586d18bf20fc71e48eab8d196d4495d034840574faf53e91fecf13ec05c86745bea80fbbe&scene=21#wechat_redirect)失陷[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130846&idx=2&sn=253621ac2583b0cbe513cfbc8e64e7bf&chksm=bc8589318bf200279e674718c80b9f00adf8c877418b8c13e3e5feeb1988f627c1712feb1ec8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

**缺乏持续检测：**只能做阶段性检测，无法覆盖威胁的全生命周期。

  

**无法进行联动：**各安全检测产品独立工作，[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)告警信息割裂，无法联动。

  

另一方面，当前[安全攻防](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134721&idx=1&sn=a140174680d55755f54c5e032b60b409&chksm=bc85b9ee8bf230f8f0a91858faade96c34728495cb056f93051607771126b49c75b570b2c6dd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)对抗日趋激烈，单纯指望通过防范和阻止的策略已行不通，必须更加注重检测与响应。企业组织要在已遭受[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的假定前提下，构建集防御、检测、响应和预防于一体的全新[安全防护](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132591&idx=3&sn=0e73014aa2b38745a8259c348e71680c&chksm=bc8586408bf20f564af8b79e6b9e006803065e2aa2f69af8d33554c7622c6ce7198559c23ced&scene=21#wechat_redirect)体系。这从2019年6月网络演习的规则也能看得出来，不强制要求系统不被[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，而是强调[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)之后的快速响应能力。

  

最后，随着[云计算](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655135309&idx=3&sn=2a8ada3354cc565a41e998a17cf04c31&chksm=bc85bba28bf232b46cbcf1b615f3bce5fb5792cd87c0f3e39c57e6d7887741dfb551eaed92b8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的快速发展，多云和云原生趋势渐渐成为主流，面对多云、云原生等新型架构也不断涌现，原有的[主机安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品如何适配新的架构，也成为了企业不得不考虑的话题。

  

为了应对外在环境的不断演进，[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[安全防护](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132591&idx=3&sn=0e73014aa2b38745a8259c348e71680c&chksm=bc8586408bf20f564af8b79e6b9e006803065e2aa2f69af8d33554c7622c6ce7198559c23ced&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件也在不断更新迭代，衍生出了一系列细分领域的[主机安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品。从[主机安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品发展级别来看，大体上可以概括为“基础性的[主机安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品”、“以应用为核心的[主机安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品”、“以检测响应为核心的[主机安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品”、“以主动防御为核心的[主机安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品”、“新形态下的[主机安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc
