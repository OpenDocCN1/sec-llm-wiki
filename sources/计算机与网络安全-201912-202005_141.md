近日，浙江绍兴新昌市民小朱用外婆的手机注册新用户时却发现无法收到[验证码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131672&idx=3&sn=5f25d006408aa11a3e4db5ce73993165&chksm=bc8585f78bf20ce1979903ba401093239253dd1008110b48504add50484d360b90705ba43e15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

“连着试了很多次，还是没有收到[验证码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131672&idx=3&sn=5f25d006408aa11a3e4db5ce73993165&chksm=bc8585f78bf20ce1979903ba401093239253dd1008110b48504add50484d360b90705ba43e15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，我怀疑我外婆的手机被人控制了。”当天，小朱选择了报警。

  

新昌公安网安大队对小朱外婆的手机进行了现场测试，又有奇怪的发现：除了无法收到[验证码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131672&idx=3&sn=5f25d006408aa11a3e4db5ce73993165&chksm=bc8585f78bf20ce1979903ba401093239253dd1008110b48504add50484d360b90705ba43e15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)类的[短信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131436&idx=3&sn=1f2181a408e8114cc1be4914a60503e9&chksm=bc858ac38bf203d54938a44c7d1bc03e63b1c674f583b59d9bf65d0900ac0d31ceb0886b591d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)之外，其他类型的[短信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131436&idx=3&sn=1f2181a408e8114cc1be4914a60503e9&chksm=bc858ac38bf203d54938a44c7d1bc03e63b1c674f583b59d9bf65d0900ac0d31ceb0886b591d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)均能正常收发，手机其他功能也都正常。

  

围绕涉案手机销售渠道，新昌[网警](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129790&idx=3&sn=11e84e6198ac348684c43f8a59b2cdad&chksm=bc858d518bf2044777bab43f98099d5ea2fcf713a9a53e5a23371226572415f5584be6b436f9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)先后询问了在本地购买同款“老年机”的37人，勘验手机25只，发现其中15只手机的[短信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131436&idx=3&sn=1f2181a408e8114cc1be4914a60503e9&chksm=bc858ac38bf203d54938a44c7d1bc03e63b1c674f583b59d9bf65d0900ac0d31ceb0886b591d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)收发不正常，情况雷同。

  

之后，民警确证手机主板被植入了特殊的[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序，能把特定的[短信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131436&idx=3&sn=1f2181a408e8114cc1be4914a60503e9&chksm=bc858ac38bf203d54938a44c7d1bc03e63b1c674f583b59d9bf65d0900ac0d31ceb0886b591d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上传到指定的[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

鉴于案情重大，绍兴、新昌两级公安机关成立了“2019.8.12”侵犯公民信息专案组，全力展开侦查。

  

专案组先找到了一个注册地在深圳的手机号码，专门用于接收被拦截的含[验证码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131672&idx=3&sn=5f25d006408aa11a3e4db5ce73993165&chksm=bc8585f78bf20ce1979903ba401093239253dd1008110b48504add50484d360b90705ba43e15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)类[短信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131436&idx=3&sn=1f2181a408e8114cc1be4914a60503e9&chksm=bc858ac38bf203d54938a44c7d1bc03e63b1c674f583b59d9bf65d0900ac0d31ceb0886b591d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。从这个号码切入，犯罪嫌疑人吴某和卢某进入警方视线，专案组最终确认这个犯罪团伙位于深圳市南山区。

  

2019年8月29日，绍兴警方赶到深圳开展第一轮抓捕，起获了大量的后台[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)数据以及犯罪团伙与上下游产业链的交易合同。 

  

经查，这个团伙以吴某为首，专门制作可以控制手机、识别和拦截[短信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131436&idx=3&sn=1f2181a408e8114cc1be4914a60503e9&chksm=bc858ac38bf203d54938a44c7d1bc03e63b1c674f583b59d9bf65d0900ac0d31ceb0886b591d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序，并与主板生产商合作，将[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序植入到手机主板中。

  

“被植入[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序激活的手机有500多万只，涉及到的手机型号有4500多种，受害者遍布全国，31个省、直辖市、自治区都有。”办案民警告诉记者。

  

之后，专案组民警顺藤摸瓜，在深圳抓获其中一个手机主板制造商，现场查获大量植入[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序的手机主板。

  

第三阶段，新昌警方又先后在厦门、杭州抓获利用非法购买的公民个人手机号和[验证码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131672&idx=3&sn=5f25d006408aa11a3e4db5ce73993165&chksm=bc8585f78bf20ce1979903ba401093239253dd1008110b48504add50484d360b90705ba43e15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、进行“[薅羊毛](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127729&idx=3&sn=7b16f3afe46e7580af00ee00f1651097&chksm=bc85955e8bf21c481b92de46790984a56d5cad957ecf941b1a9445146999ccbeabc71d82a69b&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”的嫌疑人14人。“[薅羊毛](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127729&idx=3&sn=7b16f3afe46e7580af00ee00f1651097&chksm=bc85955e8bf21c481b92de46790984a56d5cad957ecf941b1a9445146999ccbeabc71d82a69b&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”就是指注册新用户获得红包的行为。

  

通过公安部发起“2019净网行动”集群战役，新昌警方也对下游非法买卖手机号、[验证码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131672&idx=3&sn=5f25d006408aa11a3e4db5ce73993165&chksm=bc8585f78bf20ce1979903ba401093239253dd1008110b48504add50484d360b90705ba43e15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)等公民信息进行“[薅羊毛](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127729&idx=3&sn=7b16f3afe46e7580af00ee00f1651097&chksm=bc85955e8bf21c481b92de46790984a56d5cad957ecf941b1a9445146999ccbeabc71d82a69b&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”的[黑灰产业链](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126580&idx=3&sn=c8b22ba96245b8899740c6028d3676be&chksm=bc8599db8bf210cd3d548bd8f108fb7756f53ed04726398699fc03c5d96aef142d6b77755a8a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进行打击。

  

民警告诉记者，涉案的这种专用[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序主要针对的是老年人手机和儿童手机、电话手表等功能机，因为这两类用户对[短信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131436&idx=3&sn=1f2181a408e8114cc1be4914a60503e9&chksm=bc858ac38bf203d54938a44c7d1bc03e63b1c674f583b59d9bf65d0900ac0d31ceb0886b591d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的需求比较少，也不关注验证类信息，属于“闲置资源”。

  

“之前这个团伙也针对智能手机种植过‘[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)’，但由于智能手机使用的用户范围比较广，往往很快发现收不到[短信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131436&idx=3&sn=1f2181a408e8114cc1be4914a60503e9&chksm=bc858ac38bf203d54938a44c7d1bc03e63b1c674f583b59d9bf65d0900ac0d31ceb0886b591d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，然后发生投诉和纠纷，于是他们终止了智能机业务。”

  

民警介绍，被做了手脚的手机只要插入电话卡，主板里的[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序就会运行，向后台源源不断发送[短信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131436&idx=3&sn=1f2181a408e8114cc1be4914a60503e9&chksm=bc858ac38bf203d54938a44c7d1bc03e63b1c674f583b59d9bf65d0900ac0d31ceb0886b591d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，犯罪团伙就可以对这个手机进行实时控制。

  

在团伙中，犯罪嫌疑人吴某专门负责[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和对码平台的搭建。

  

犯罪嫌疑人邓某是一家手机主板生产厂家的技术负责人，他们把吴某提供的[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)嵌入到手机主板里，销售给手机生产商。

  

原先，厂家生产一块老年机的主板只有几毛钱的利润，但安装[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)后可以拿到三倍的利益，怪不得厂家纷纷下水。

  

最终，新昌警方打掉了这条“[薅羊毛](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127729&idx=3&sn=7b16f3afe46e7580af00ee00f1651097&chksm=bc85955e8bf21c481b92de46790984a56d5cad957ecf941b1a9445146999ccbeabc71d82a69b&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”的[黑色产业链](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126580&idx=3&sn=c8b22ba96245b8899740c6028d3676be&chksm=bc8599db8bf210cd3d548bd8f108fb7756f53ed04726398699fc03c5d96aef142d6b77755a8a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，近日吴某等20多位嫌疑人被提起公诉。
