2020年3月21日，针对媒体报道的新浪微博因用户查询接口被恶意调用导致[App](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130459&idx=3&sn=f8b64d61307c28f9f3265b4eb1eda12a&chksm=bc858eb48bf207a2b2fd53128cd92797950f71b4e141abfb9c60970b6a662bc1849e85912dff&scene=21#wechat_redirect)数据泄露问题，工业和信息化部[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131366&idx=2&sn=18215226672d73083f399efc8e61ab76&chksm=bc858b098bf2021f4ba79a264088a3761321619396e24fc817ec636744973de380bacdcd88b6&scene=21#wechat_redirect)管理局对新浪微博相关负责人进行了问询约谈，要求其按照[《网络安全法》](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655113238&idx=4&sn=7a6aeba5889cedfd8cd21ca949ce6dbb&chksm=bc864df98bf1c4efdc987d135c7571718de3351fdd3c22ba266e90e0f2162ee811ee420143f4&scene=21#wechat_redirect)《电信和[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133950&idx=3&sn=5d4ada814af5cb06eda826506cdd4dc7&chksm=bc85bd118bf2340750db425c3b156a50e68008c51b409f6b2d2428df0471fc61450d35d5e8b5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)用户[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保护规定》等法律法规要求，对照工业和信息化部等四部门制定的[《App违法违规收集使用个人信息行为认定方法》](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130081&idx=3&sn=dbd1fb866bd869356579605626f4bf21&chksm=bc858c0e8bf20518652ce27f79b1478afb92fdb3e80e41c9195046efcc06f237b674a9f4206a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，进一步采取有效措施，消除[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)隐患：

  

一是要尽快完善[隐私](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127519&idx=3&sn=a846e840b4ac021e01ba4a64c9228817&chksm=bc859a308bf2132693545d19fdb92d481e63a9d92d18960c1e4c198747b628d2bad763105175&scene=21#wechat_redirect)政策，规范用户个人[信息收集](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133509&idx=3&sn=5ec6d2d116de0d6e817389b5c401fc01&chksm=bc8582aa8bf20bbcec2ee9f9fefdabe5a344ac785fa549a7e4cccbaf899ed4a1ed785c307f5f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)使用行为；

  

二是要加强用户信息分类分级保护，强化用户查询接口风险控制等安全保护策略；

  

三是要加强企业内部[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)管理，定期及新业务上线前要开展[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)合规性自评估，及时防范[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)风险；

  

四是要在发生重大[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)事件时，及时告知用户并向主管部门报告。

 

新浪微博表示，公司高度重视[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保护，针对此次事件已采取了升级接口安全策略等措施，后续将按照工业和信息化部要求，落实企业[数据安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121771&idx=3&sn=3cae6a8ed0807be7fb35f25a59d8ce84&chksm=bc866c848bf1e592f5bf5bf031990b3df824cb7e7451aa2030d8f841370a4532b4bf685a0f3e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)主体责任，切实做好用户[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保护工作。
