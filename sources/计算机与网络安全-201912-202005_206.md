网站是指在[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上，根据一定的规则，使用HTML等工具制作的用于展示特定内容的相关网页的集合。简单地说，网站是一种通信工具，就像布告栏一样，人们可以通过网站发布自己想要公开的信息，或者利用网站提供相关的网络服务。人们可以通过网页浏览器访问网站，获取自己需要的资讯或者享受网络服务。通常，在一个网站上会搭建有[Web](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130618&idx=3&sn=449f93385285290a85de33579f5e55bf&chksm=bc858e158bf20703b709bfb7ddf2adee4149765451b8281f45a741aee2c621c396b330f67af2&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、[MySQL](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126890&idx=3&sn=1cdab8e3953ef5db6b337a3e91a3dafd&chksm=bc8598858bf21193106359c3a1855175328ce1420020eada002972457ce1145f91b55d5da09d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)服务、FTP服务等。如果这些服务中存在[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者即可利用这些[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。另外，网站是由多个网页构成，但是网页又是通过一些源[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)编写出来的，如果编写网页，则需要有对应的开发环境，如[Java](http:  

**1、渗透攻击Tomcat服务**

[Web](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130618&idx=3&sn=449f93385285290a85de33579f5e55bf&chksm=bc858e158bf20703b709bfb7ddf2adee4149765451b8281f45a741aee2c621c396b330f67af2&scene=21#wechat_redirect)等应用都运行在Tomcat[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上。Tomcat默认存在一个管理后台，默认的管理地址是http://IP或域名：端口/manager/html。通过此后台，可以在不重启Tomcat服务的情况下方便地部署、启动、停止或卸载[Web](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130618&idx=3&sn=449f93385285290a85de33579f5e55bf&chksm=bc858e158bf20703b709bfb7ddf2adee4149765451b8281f45a741aee2c621c396b330f67af2&scene=21#wechat_redirect)应用。但是如果配置不当的话则会存在很大的安全隐患。[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者利用这个[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，可以非常快速、轻松地[入侵](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132145&idx=3&sn=3a23fde20b5bda403b8bc61fa571ba7a&chksm=bc85841e8bf20d087eb627b3b43f5ac1e5737906c80b136c144a115e94a1977abcb8687810d3&scene=21#wechat_redirect)一台[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。下面介绍[渗透](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131857&idx=3&sn=f05704b1a33a118f5974dc1e0e3d8fde&chksm=bc85853e8bf20c282a388c76d4472ec1e0c54b97c6f96ce0179e0c75f9d0922a988125c9881d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230b msf5 auxiliary(tomcat\_mgr\_login) >
