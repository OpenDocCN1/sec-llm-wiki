**（1）判断数据库是否允许外链**

  

使用Metasploit的mysql\_version模块，可以判断目标[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的[MySQL](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126890&idx=3&sn=1cdab8e3953ef5db6b337a3e91a3dafd&chksm=bc8598858bf21193106359c3a1855175328ce1420020eada002972457ce1145f91b55d5da09d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[数据库](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129320&idx=3&sn=e5b2f0721ffd54f8891e633fbc280f4b&chksm=bc8593078bf21a1184c1596a84ce0604e00b716de6081c5b27ea0311d1df5e2b5024ba752979&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是否允许外链。如果允许外链，则可以显示该服务的版本信息；否则，不会显示其版本信息。

  

探测[MySQL](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126890&idx=3&sn=1cdab8e3953ef5db6b337a3e91a3dafd&chksm=bc8598858bf21193106359c3a1855175328ce1420020eada002972457ce1145f91b55d5da09d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[数据库](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129320&idx=3&sn=e5b2f0721ffd54f8891e633fbc280f4b&chksm=bc8593078bf21a1184c1596a84ce0604e00b716de6081c5b27ea0311d1df5e2b5024ba752979&scene=21#wechat_redirect)服务的版本。具体操作步骤如下：

**（2）实施暴力破解密码**

  

如果[数据库](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129320&idx=3&sn=e5b2f0721ffd54f8891e633fbc280f4b&chksm=bc8593078bf21a1184c1596a84ce0604e00b716de6081c5b27ea0311d1df5e2b5024ba752979&scene=21#wechat_redirect)允许外链，[渗透测试](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131857&idx=3&sn=f05704b1a33a118f5974dc1e0e3d8fde&chksm=bc85853e8bf20c282a388c76d4472ec1e0c54b97c6f96ce0179e0c75f9d0922a988125c9881d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者可以进行暴力[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)破解。下面使用mysql\_login模块实施暴力[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)破解。
