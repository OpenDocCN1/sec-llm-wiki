**1、多重编码**

  

多重编码就是对可执行文件进行多次编码。大多数杀毒软件使用[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)来识别[恶意代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122949&idx=2&sn=1ee3feea8b48a3aabc92d8f452344783&chksm=bc866bea8bf1e2fca3e416016050213dbaabe4e46e51667cf279e03efad0de48f7d571db209f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。这些[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)装载在杀毒引擎中，用来对磁盘和[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进行扫描，并寻找匹配对象。发现匹配对象后，杀毒软件会有相应的处理流程，大多数会将感染[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的二进制文件隔离，或杀掉正在运行的[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。通过多重编码对二进制文件内部机制进行修改，可以绕过杀毒软件的[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)检查。shikata\_ga\_nai编码是多态的，也就是说，每次生成的[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)载荷文件都不一样。所以生成的[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&

**2、自定义可执行文件模板**

  

通常情况下，运行msfvenom命令时，[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)载荷被嵌入式到默认的可执行文件模板中。[Kali Linux](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128965&idx=3&sn=b8b9a62359603244f96d47b39c0a094e&chksm=bc85906a8bf2197c862d73e7aa0b33d06465d5570713fd18493312f7c38da5c6b4b21f9895cf&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统的默认模板文件位于data/msfpayload/template.exe下。虽然这个模板文件有时会更新，但它永远是杀毒软件厂商在创建[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)库时的终端关注对象。恰好，msfvenom命令支持使用-x选项使用任意的[Windows](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127496&idx=3&sn=21acddfe6a98fe94ae346db72555e138&chksm=bc859a278bf21331d775fdb2224b50832a618a13d3a8fa76c41786186e846f5126daf96682bc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)可执行程序来代替默认模板文件。下面介绍使用自定义可执行文件模板来实现[免杀](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132117&idx=3&sn=26a57ab9923d0ee924413dadc07db9a4&chksm=bc85843a8bf20d2cb6d7900c5993d04501f24c73a361874067e38067334ac76b66a1c31c06d2&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术的方法。

**3、隐秘启动一个攻击载荷**

  

在大多数情况下，当被[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的用户运行类似前面生成的这种包含[后门](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129244&idx=3&sn=6df349d4b772425777487a77091f0f4d&chksm=bc8593738bf21a652bfc7213ea58a506986d7b1e6812c5132b33c598b7a8a210c10f13d7f900&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的可执行文件时，如果什么都没有发生，可能会引起用户的怀疑。为了避免被目标察觉，[渗透测试](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131857&idx=3&sn=f05704b1a33a118f5974dc1e0e3d8fde&chksm=bc85853e8bf20c282a388c76d4472ec1e0c54b97c6f96ce0179e0c75f9d0922a988125c9881d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者可以在启动[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)载荷的同时，让宿主程序也正常运行起来。下面介绍隐秘地启动一个[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)载荷的方法。

  

隐秘地启动[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)载荷。具体操作步骤如下：

  

1）本例中选择[Windows](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127496&idx=3&sn=21acddfe6a98fe94ae346db72555e138&chksm=bc859a278bf21331d775fdb2224b50832a618a13d3a8fa76c41786186e846f5126daf96682bc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)环境下的[SSH](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131079&idx=3&sn=794779c5c517f42260167e098ee06322&chksm=bc8588288bf2013ebd40fb2b944291585012bf1af14e1198e987a745b0ed2b95eb882984db5a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)客户端PuTTY程序，使[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)载荷隐秘地启动。首先下载PuTTY程序，执行命令如下：

\-k选项不一定能用在所有的可执行程序上，在实际[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)前请确保已经在实验环境中进行了测试。

**4、加壳软件**

  

[加壳](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123600&idx=3&sn=75e03920427adf750a5c2dfbcc2ba584&chksm=bc86657f8bf1ec69a8b1315c0351d3bf3dbd07de5347ea5c5883a418c78ca0fbe421e5e9250d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件是一类能够对可执行文件进行加密压缩并将解压[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)嵌入其中的工具。当加过壳的文件被执行后，解压[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)会从已压缩的数据中重建原始程序并运行。这些程序对用户是透明的，所以[加壳](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123600&idx=3&sn=75e03920427adf750a5c2dfbcc2ba584&chksm=bc86657f8bf1ec69a8b1315c0351d3bf3dbd07de5347ea5c5883a418c78ca0fbe421e5e9250d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)后的程序可以代替原始程序使用。[加壳](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123600&idx=3&sn=75e03920427adf750a5c2dfbcc2ba584&chksm=bc86657f8bf1ec69a8b1315c0351d3bf3dbd07de5347ea5c5883a418c78ca0fbe421e5e9250d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)后，可执行文件更小，而功能与原来的文件一样。

  

同MSF编码器一样，[加壳](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123600&idx=3&sn=75e03920427adf750a5c2dfbcc2ba584&chksm=bc86657f8bf1ec69a8b1315c0351d3bf3dbd07de5347ea5c5883a418c78ca0fbe421e5e9250d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件也可以改变可执行文件的结构。然后，MSF编码器通常会增加可执行文件的大小，而精心挑选的[加壳](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123600&idx=3&sn=75e03920427adf750a5c2dfbcc2ba584&chksm=bc86657f8bf1ec69a8b1315c0351d3bf3dbd07de5347ea5c5883a418c78ca0fbe421e5e9250d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件会使用不同的[算法](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133583&idx=3&sn=cbc3236f19fdefbf135c858e9b4795b2&chksm=bc8582608bf20b765b760eda0342717dd6aba7045f3967f624adb1af0ab183bceef19126bf9e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，一方面对可执行文件进行加密；另一方面还能对其体积进行压缩。下面使用在Kali中最受欢迎的UPX[加壳](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655123600&idx=3&sn=75e03920427adf750a5c2dfbcc2ba584&chksm=bc86657f8bf1ec69a8b1315c0351d3bf3dbd07de5347ea5c5883a418c78ca0fbe421e5e9250d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件对创建的[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)载荷文件payload.exe进行编码和压缩，以尝试对该文件进行[免杀](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132117&idx=3&sn=26a57ab9923d0ee924413dadc07db9a4&chksm=bc85843a8bf20d2cb6d7900c5993d04501f24c73a361874067e38067334ac76b66a1c31c06d2&scene=21#wechat_redirect)处理。
