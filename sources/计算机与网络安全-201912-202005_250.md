[数据库](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129320&idx=3&sn=e5b2f0721ffd54f8891e633fbc280f4b&chksm=bc8593078bf21a1184c1596a84ce0604e00b716de6081c5b27ea0311d1df5e2b5024ba752979&scene=21#wechat_redirect)作为最基础的数据存储服务之一，在存储系统中有着非常重要的地位，因此要求其具备高可用性无可厚非。能实现不同SLA（服务水平协定）的解决方案有很多种，这些方案可以保证[数据库](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129320&idx=3&sn=e5b2f0721ffd54f8891e633fbc280f4b&chksm=bc8593078bf21a1184c1596a84ce0604e00b716de6081c5b27ea0311d1df5e2b5024ba752979&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)在硬件或软件出现故障时服务继续可用。

  

高可用性需要解决的主要问题有两个，一个是如何实现数据共享或同步数据，另一个是如何处理failover。数据共享一般的解决方案是通过SAN（Storage Area Network）来实现，而数据同步可以通过rsync软件或DRBD技术来实现。failover的意思是指当[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)死机或出现错误时可以自动切换到其他备用[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，不影响[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上业务系统的运行。本文介绍目前比较成熟的[MySQL](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126890&idx=3&sn=1cdab8e3953ef5db6b337a3e91a3dafd&chksm=bc8598858bf21193106359c3a1855175328ce1420020eada002972457ce1145f91b55d5da09d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)高可用解决方案。

  

**1、主从复制解决方案**

  

这是[MySQL](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126890&idx=3&sn=1cdab8e3953ef5db6b337a3e91a3dafd&chksm=bc8598858bf21193106359c3a1855175328ce1420020eada002972457ce1145f91b55d5da09d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)自身提供的一种高可用解决方案，数据同步方法采用的是[MySQL](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126890&idx=3&sn=1cdab8e3953ef5db6b337a3e91a3dafd&chksm=bc8598858bf21193106359c3a1855175328ce1420020eada002972457ce1145f91b55d5da09d&scene=21#wechat_redirect) replication技术。[MySQL](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126890&idx=3&sn=1cdab8e3953ef5db6b337a3e91a3dafd&chksm=bc8598858bf21193106359c3a1855175328ce1420020eada002972457ce1145f91b55d5da09d&scene=21#wechat_redirect) replication技术就是一个日志的复制过程，在复制过程中一台[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)充当主[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，而一台或多台其他[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)充当从[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，简单说就是，从[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)到主[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)拉取二进制日志文件，然后再将日志文件解析成相应的SQL在从[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上重新执行一遍主[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的操作，通过这种方式保证数据的一致性。

  

[MySQL](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126890&idx=3&sn=1cdab8e3953ef5db6b337a3e91a3dafd&chksm=bc8598858bf21193106359c3a1855175328ce1420020eada002972457ce1145f91b55d5da09d&scene=21#wechat_redirect) replication技术仅仅提供了日志的同步执行功能，而从[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)只能提供读操作，并且当主[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发生故障时，必须手动处理failover，通常的做法是将一台从[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)更改为主[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。这种解决方案在一定程度上实现了[MySQL](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126890&idx=3&sn=1cdab8e3953ef5db6b337a3e91a3dafd&chksm=bc8598858bf21193106359c3a1855175328ce1420020eada002972457ce1145f91b55d5da09d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的高可用性，可以实现90.000%的SLA。

  

为了达到更高的可用性，在实际的应用环境中，一般都采用[MySQL](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126890&idx=3&sn=1cdab8e3953ef5db6b337a3e91a3dafd&chksm=bc8598858bf21193106359c3a1855175328ce1420020eada002972457ce1145f91b55d5da09d&scene=21#wechat_redirect) replication技术配合高可用集群软件来实现自动failover，这种方式可以实现95.000%的SLA。

  

**2、MMM高可用解决方案**

  

MMM（Master-Master Replication Manager for [MySQL](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126890&idx=3&sn=1cdab8e3953ef5db6b337a3e91a3dafd&chksm=bc8598858bf21193106359c3a1855175328ce1420020eada002972457ce1145f91b55d5da09d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)）[MySQL](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126890&idx=3&sn=1cdab8e3953ef5db6b337a3e91a3dafd&chksm=bc8598858bf21193106359c3a1855175328ce1420020eada002972457ce1145f91b55d5da09d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)主主复制管理器，提供了[MySQL](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126890&idx=3&sn=1cdab8e3953ef5db6b337a3e91a3dafd&chksm=bc8598858bf21193106359c3a1855175328ce1420020eada002972457ce1145f91b55d5da09d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)主主复制配置的监控、故障转移和管理的一套可伸缩的脚本套件。在MMM高可用解决方案中，典型的应用是双主多从架构，通过[MySQL](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126890&idx=3&sn=1cdab8e3953ef5db6b337a3e91a3dafd&chksm=bc8598858bf21193106359c3a1855175328ce1420020eada002972457ce1145f91b55d5da09d&scene=21#wechat_redirect) replication技术可以实现两台[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)互为主从，且在任何时候只有一个节点可以写入，避免多点写入的数据冲突。同时，当可写的主节点故障时，MMM套件可以立刻监控到，然后将服务自动切换到另一个主节点，继续提供服务，从而实现[MySQL](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126890&idx=3&sn=1cdab8e3953ef5db6b337a3e91a3dafd&chksm=bc8598858bf21193106359c3a1855175328ce1420020eada002972457ce1145f91b55d5da09d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的高可用。

  

MMM方案是目前比较成熟的[MySQL](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126890&idx=3&sn=1cdab8e3953ef5db6b337a3e91a3dafd&chksm=bc8598858bf21193106359c3a1855175328ce1420020eada002972457ce1145f91b55d5da09d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)高可用解决方案，可以实现99.000%的SLA。

  

**3、Heartbeat/SAN高可用解决方案**

  

此解决方案是借助第三方软硬件实现的，在这个方案中，处理failover的方式是高可用集群软件Heartbeat，它监控和管理各个节点间连接的网络，并监控集群服务，当节点出现故障或者服务不可用时，自动在其他节点启动集群服务。

  

在数据共享方面，通过SAN存储来共享数据，在正常状态下，集群主节点将挂载存储进行数据读写，而当集群发生故障时，Heartbeat会首先通过一个仲裁设备将主节点挂载的存储设备释放，然后在备用节点上挂载存储，接着启动服务，通过这种方式实现数据的共享和同步。这种数据共享方式实现简单，但是成本较高，并且存在脑裂的可能，需要根据实际应用环境来选择。这种方案可以实现99.990%的SLA。

  

**4、Heartbeat/DRBD高可用解决方案**

  

这种高可用解决方案也是借助第三方软硬件实现的，在处理failover的方式上依旧采用Heartbeat，不同的是，在数据共享方面，采用了基于块级别的数据同步软件DRBD来实现。

  

DRBD（Distributed Replicated Block Device）是一个用软件实现的、无共享的、[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)之间镜像块设备内容的存储复制解决方案。和SAN网络不同，它并不共享存储，而是通过[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)之间的网络复制数据。这种方案实现起来稍微复杂，同时也存在脑裂的问题，可以实现99.900%的SLA。

  

**5、MySQL Cluster高可用解决方案**

  

[MySQL](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126890&idx=3&sn=1cdab8e3953ef5db6b337a3e91a3dafd&chksm=bc8598858bf21193106359c3a1855175328ce1420020eada002972457ce1145f91b55d5da09d&scene=21#wechat_redirect) Cluster由一组服务节点构成，每个服务节点上均运行着多种进程，包括[MySQL](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126890&idx=3&sn=1cdab8e3953ef5db6b337a3e91a3dafd&chksm=bc8598858bf21193106359c3a1855175328ce1420020eada002972457ce1145f91b55d5da09d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、NDB Cluster的数据节点、管理[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，以及（可能）专门的数据访问程序。此解决方案是[MySQL](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126890&idx=3&sn=1cdab8e3953ef5db6b337a3e91a3dafd&chksm=bc8598858bf21193106359c3a1855175328ce1420020eada002972457ce1145f91b55d5da09d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)官方主推的技术方案，功能强大，但是由于实现较为繁琐，配置麻烦，企业实际应用并不多。[MySQL](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126890&idx=3&sn=1cdab8e3953ef5db6b337a3e91a3dafd&chksm=bc8598858bf21193106359c3a1855175328ce1420020eada002972457ce1145f91b55d5da09d&scene=21#wechat_redirect) Cluster的标准版和电信版可以达到99.999%的SLA。
