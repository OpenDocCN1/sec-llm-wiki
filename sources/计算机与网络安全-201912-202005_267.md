 

**1、HAProxy简介**

  

HAProxy是一个开源的、高性能的、基于[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)（第四层）和[HTTP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129052&idx=3&sn=6aa03cee097fa59206f385629b151800&chksm=bc8590338bf219253db54d9678c7924707d1e7d1195f8709817128e1ad501546618ac2f91cc7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)（第七层）应用的[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件，借助HAProxy可以快速、可靠地提供基于[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和[HTTP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129052&idx=3&sn=6aa03cee097fa59206f385629b151800&chksm=bc8590338bf219253db54d9678c7924707d1e7d1195f8709817128e1ad501546618ac2f91cc7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)应用的[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)解决方案。HAProxy作为一个专业的[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件，它的显著优点如下：

  

可靠性和稳定性非常好，可以与硬件级的F5[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)设备相媲美。

  

最高可以同时维护40000～50000个并发连接，单位时间内处理的最大请求数为20000个，最大数据处理能力可达10Gbps。作为软件级别的[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)来说，HAProxy的性能强大可见一斑。

  

支持多于8种[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[算法](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133583&idx=3&sn=cbc3236f19fdefbf135c858e9b4795b2&chksm=bc8582608bf20b765b760eda0342717dd6aba7045f3967f624adb1af0ab183bceef19126bf9e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，同时也支持session保持。

  

支持虚拟[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)功能，这样实现[Web](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130618&idx=3&sn=449f93385285290a85de33579f5e55bf&chksm=bc858e158bf20703b709bfb7ddf2adee4149765451b8281f45a741aee2c621c396b330f67af2&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)更加灵活。

  

从HAProxy1.3版本后开始支持连接拒绝、全透明代理等功能，这些功能是其他[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)器所不具备的。

  

HAProxy拥有一个功能强大的[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)状态监控页面，通过此页面可以实时了解系统的运行状况。

  

HAProxy拥有功能强大的ACL支持，能给使用带来很大方便。

  

HAProxy是借助于[操作系统](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122728&idx=2&sn=6b263bf85d6b8bd8bf1d3744a491a40c&chksm=bc8668c78bf1e1d1a0e813fcd7b41c7bc300c2c98eb269b77f92854c62f1fe5127a0e01c5574&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的技术特性来实现性能最大化的，因此，在使用HAProxy时，对[操作系统](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122728&idx=2&sn=6b263bf85d6b8bd8bf1d3744a491a40c&chksm=bc8668c78bf1e1d1a0e813fcd7b41c7bc300c2c98eb269b77f92854c62f1fe5127a0e01c5574&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进行性能调优是非常重要的。在业务系统方面，HAProxy非常适用于那些并发量特别大且需要持久连接或四层和七层处理机制的[Web](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130618&idx=3&sn=449f93385285290a85de33579f5e55bf&chksm=bc858e158bf20703b709bfb7ddf2adee4149765451b8281f45a741aee2c621c396b330f67af2&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统，例如门户网站或电商网站等。另外。HAProxy也可用于[MySQL](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126890&idx=3&sn=1cdab8e3953ef5db6b337a3e91a3dafd&chksm=bc8598858bf21193106359c3a1855175328ce1420020eada002972457ce1145f91b55d5da09d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[数据库](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129320&idx=3&sn=e5b2f0721ffd54f8891e633fbc280f4b&chksm=bc8593078bf21a1184c1596a84ce0604e00b716de6081c5b27ea0311d1df5e2b5024ba752979&scene=21#wechat_redirect)（读操作）的[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

**2、四层和七层负载均衡的区别**

  

HAProxy是一个四层和七层[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)器。下面简单介绍下四层和七层的概念与区别。

 

**3、HAProxy与LVS的异同**

  

下面就HAProxy[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)与LVS[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件的异同做一个简单总结。

  

1）两者都是软件[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品，但是LVS是基于[Linux](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133968&idx=2&sn=2454fe58d155dec7738888c75a963aeb&chksm=bc85bcff8bf235e97db0692fa805d2eeecdcaaa1e2a26f03ab5112230e873a342fcaca0bdf4f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[操作系统](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122728&idx=2&sn=6b263bf85d6b8bd8bf1d3744a491a40c&chksm=bc8668c78bf1e1d1a0e813fcd7b41c7bc300c2c98eb269b77f92854c62f1fe5127a0e01c5574&scene=21#wechat_redirect)实现的一种软[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，而HAProxy是基于第三应用实现的软[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

2）LVS是基于四层的IP[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术，而HAProxy是基于四层和七层技术、可提供[TCP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127719&idx=3&sn=aaeb3f0917400ca26f4f392793119e79&chksm=bc8595488bf21c5ed93c82b3d1b49b8a6fbe1493342ac2cfb2db8dddeae93b0d265db15a421f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和[HTTP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129052&idx=3&sn=6aa03cee097fa59206f385629b151800&chksm=bc8590338bf219253db54d9678c7924707d1e7d1195f8709817128e1ad501546618ac2f91cc7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)应用的[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)综合解决方案。

  

3）LVS工作在ISO模型的第四层，因此其状态监测功能单一，而HAProxy在状态监测方面功能强大，可支持端口、URL、脚本等多种状态检测方式。

  

4）虽然HAProxy功能强大，但是它的整体处理性能低于四层[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)模式的LVS，而LVS拥有接近硬件设备的网络吞吐和连接负载能力。

  

综上所述，HAProxy和LVS各有优缺点，没有好坏之分，要选择哪个作为[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)器，要以实际的应用环境来决定。
