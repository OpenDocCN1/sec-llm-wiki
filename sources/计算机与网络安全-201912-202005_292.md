为深入贯彻落实习近平总书记关于新型冠状病毒肺炎疫情防控工作的重要指示精神，切实做好疫情防控和经济社会运行的[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131366&idx=2&sn=18215226672d73083f399efc8e61ab76&chksm=bc858b098bf2021f4ba79a264088a3761321619396e24fc817ec636744973de380bacdcd88b6&scene=21#wechat_redirect)支撑保障工作，确保疫情防控期间网络基础设施安全，防止发生重大[网络安全事件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132557&idx=3&sn=0dab660075e0f09f21efe4a6675678e2&chksm=bc8586628bf20f74f027354e810c343911c4dc5e86f13275475aa5828ed08e375befe66c81b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，现就有关事项通知如下：  
 

**一、全力保障重点地区重点用户网络系统安全**

（二）加强重点地区网络基础设施[安全防护](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132591&idx=3&sn=0e73014aa2b38745a8259c348e71680c&chksm=bc8586408bf20f564af8b79e6b9e006803065e2aa2f69af8d33554c7622c6ce7198559c23ced&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。加强涉疫情重点保障地区网络基础设施、重要域名系统等[安全防护](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132591&idx=3&sn=0e73014aa2b38745a8259c348e71680c&chksm=bc8586408bf20f564af8b79e6b9e006803065e2aa2f69af8d33554c7622c6ce7198559c23ced&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，利用[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)检测等技术手段，强化对重点区域的[网络安全风险评估](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122231&idx=3&sn=57f3acd6608aceda77cc67ed87383b3b&chksm=bc866ed88bf1e7cedfcc7786853506652034c8e32ed2e4460dcdbcbf472a787121cbc5fe2519&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和隐患排查，为疫情防控指挥调度、医疗救助、[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)办公和人民群众生产生活提供安全可靠的基础网络服务。  
 

（三）加强涉疫情[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129537&idx=3&sn=1c8a2b85ec3a063ce7a132992d32a3be&chksm=bc85922e8bf21b3810cf89c2dba2b7041b8a93bf3df7d28e4ac4a87791862e41bf791bc21304&scene=21#wechat_redirect)威胁监测处置。按照公共[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655120585&idx=2&sn=9622d3fd3041997ec13b8f8e385a717d&chksm=bc8671268bf1f830b295b492d4537709131dec78eb7e709d8a6b7d4bcbafd155c70245f182fb&scene=21#wechat_redirect)威胁与处置工作机制，对伪装成疫情信息传播网络[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)或相关[钓鱼](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117090&idx=3&sn=11ab8e9e34661409ce2fd1dd6da729ae&chksm=bc8642cd8bf1cbdb072d8f039143e5bc5024e3f65c8195d91330ce15a72ea53e65405ed0a6fb&scene=21#wechat_redirect)网站、恶意邮件、[恶意程序](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127399&idx=2&sn=e7605669abc54947803b3cd1b19aa9db&chksm=bc859a888bf2139e9e8f91b9db4e21e52039157964132cbbf79d41504f8deff65826665e4a4b&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，以及医疗机构、疫情防控物资生产企业所属网络系统存在受控、[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)等情况加大监测力度，利用[网络安全威胁](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131969&idx=3&sn=dc792674b2482849adc2d4453346f19e&chksm=bc8584ae8bf20db8145db0130cc43a50921fc55ba8212587df94552cdce6fc1a129348616bcc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)信息共享平台及时通报有关情况，发布风险提示，协助相关单位采取有效处置措施，从源头上降低[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126536&idx=2&sn=39c4220685f09a248d786 （四）加强疫情期间电话用户入网服务保障。鼓励基础电信企业、移动通信转售企业通过网络渠道为用户办理电话入网实名登记手续，引导广大用户利用电信企业门户网站、手机[APP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130459&idx=3&sn=f8b64d61307c28f9f3265b4eb1eda12a&chksm=bc858eb48bf207a2b2fd53128cd92797950f71b4e141abfb9c60970b6a662bc1849e85912dff&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、掌上营业厅等线上方式办理电话入网手续及手机卡。基础电信企业、移动通信转售企业应加大对网络渠道销售手机卡业务及办理流程的宣传，做好相关业务系统和平台的保障，切实为广大用户提供便利。
