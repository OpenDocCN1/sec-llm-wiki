Nginx的[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)功能是通过upstream命令实现的，因此它的[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)机制实现比较简单，是一个基于内容和应用的7层交换[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的实现。Nginx[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)默认对后端[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)有健康检测能力，但是检测能力较弱，仅限于端口检测，在后端[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)比较少的情况下（10台以下）[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)能力表现突出。而对于有大量后端节点的负载应用，由于所有访问请求都从一台[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进出，容易发生请求堵塞进而引发连接失败，因此无法充分发挥后端[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的性能。

  

  

**1、Nginx的负载均衡算法**

  

Nginx的[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)模块目前支持4种调度[算法](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133583&idx=3&sn=cbc3236f19fdefbf135c858e9b4795b2&chksm=bc8582608bf20b765b760eda0342717dd6aba7045f3967f624adb1af0ab183bceef19126bf9e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，下面分别进行介绍，其中后两种属于第三方调度方法。

  

轮询（默认），每个请求按时间顺序逐一分配到不同的后端[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，如果后端某台[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)死机，自动剔除故障系统，使用户访问不受影响。

  

weight，指定轮询权值，weight值越大，分配到的访问概率越高，主要用于后端每台[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)性能不均衡的情况下。

  

ip\_hash，每个请求按访问IP的哈希结果分配，这样来自同一个IP的访客固定访问一台后端[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，有效解决动态网页存在的session共享问题。

  

fair，它是比上面两种更加智能的[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[算法](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133583&idx=3&sn=cbc3236f19fdefbf135c858e9b4795b2&chksm=bc8582608bf20b765b760eda0342717dd6aba7045f3967f624adb1af0ab183bceef19126bf9e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。此种[算法](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133583&idx=3&sn=cbc3236f19fdefbf135c858e9b4795b2&chksm=bc8582608bf20b765b760eda0342717dd6aba7045f3967f624adb1af0ab183bceef19126bf9e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)可以依据页面大小和加载时间长短智能地进行[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，也就是根据后端[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的响应时间来分配请求，响应时间短的优先分配。Nginx本身是不支持fair的，如果需要使用这种调度[算法](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133583&idx=3&sn=cbc3236f19fdefbf135c858e9b4795b2&chksm=bc8582608bf20b765b760eda0342717dd6aba7045f3967f624adb1af0ab183bceef19126bf9e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，必须下载Nginx的upstream\_fair模块。

  

url\_hash，按访问URL的哈希结果来分配请求，使每个URL定向到同一台后端[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，可以进一步提高后端缓存[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的效率。Nginx本身是不支持url\_hash的，如果需要使用这种调度[算法](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133583&idx=3&sn=cbc3236f19fdefbf135c858e9b4795b2&chksm=bc8582608bf20b765b760eda0342717dd6aba7045f3967f624adb1af0ab183bceef19126bf9e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，必须安装Nginx的hash软件包。

  

在HTTP Upstream模块中，可以通过server命令指定后端[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的IP地址和端口，同时还可以设定每台后端[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)在[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)调度中的状态。常用的状态有：

  

down，表示当前的server暂时不参与[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

backup，预留的备份机器。当其他所有的非backup机器出现故障或者忙的时候，才会请求backup机器，因此这台机器的访问压力最轻。

  

max\_fails，允许请求失败的次数，默认为1。当超过最大次数时，返回proxy\_next\_upstream模块定义的错误。

  

fail\_timeout，在经历了max\_fails次失败后，暂停服务的时间。max\_fails可以和fail\_timeout一起使用。

  

当负载调度算法为ip\_hash时，后端[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)在[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)调度中的状态不能是weight和backup。

  

**2、Nginx的负载均衡配置实例**

  

下面是一个Nginx的[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)配置实例，为了重点说明[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的配置，这里仅列出了Nginx配置文件中的http配置段，而省略了其他部分的配置，特此说明。关于[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的配置参数如下：

  

http  

{

upstream myserver {

server 192.168.12.181:80 weight=3 max\_fails=3 fail\_timeout=20s;

server 192.168.12.182:80 weight=1 max\_fails=3 fail\_timeout=20s;

server 192.168.12.183:80 weight=4 max\_fails=3 fail\_timeout=20s;

}

server

{

listen 80;

server\_name www.domain.com 192.168.12.189;

index index.htm index.html ;

root /ixdba/web/wwwroot;

location / {

proxy\_pass http://myserver;

proxy\_next\_upstream http\_500 http\_502 http\_503 error timeout invalid\_header;

include /opt/nginx/conf/proxy.conf;

}

}

}

  

在此段配置中，upstream关键字标识[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)配置开始，upstream是Nginx的[HTTP ](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129052&idx=3&sn=6aa03cee097fa59206f385629b151800&chksm=bc8590338bf219253db54d9678c7924707d1e7d1195f8709817128e1ad501546618ac2f91cc7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)Upstream模块，这个模块通过一个简单的调度[算法](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133583&idx=3&sn=cbc3236f19fdefbf135c858e9b4795b2&chksm=bc8582608bf20b765b760eda0342717dd6aba7045f3967f624adb1af0ab183bceef19126bf9e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)来实现客户端IP到后端[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。在上面的配置段中，通过upstream命令指定了一个[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)器的名称为myserver。这个名称可以任意指定，在后面需要的地方直接调用即可。

  

另外，proxy\_next\_upstream参数用来定义故障转移策略，当后端服务节点返回500、502、503、504和执行超时等错误时，自动将请求转发到upstream[负载均衡](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131608&idx=3&sn=bfe852604b69a994aaabfd5b2484def3&chksm=bc858a378bf2032165e74c15d6fe59f379723fb5c995c954ed5ae1b67413bfe839fac9e8b782&scene=21#wechat_redirect)组中的另一台[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134138&idx=2&sn=2da408a8f63d0e17aae10f56ee8bc3e3&chksm=bc85bc558bf23543bf9dd7d0df24588ada90c01b3b4f8d8d1d4404168ebb5a78ec3acaa6450e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，实现故障转移。
