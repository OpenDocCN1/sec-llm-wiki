近期，工业和信息化部[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131366&idx=2&sn=18215226672d73083f399efc8e61ab76&chksm=bc858b098bf2021f4ba79a264088a3761321619396e24fc817ec636744973de380bacdcd88b6&scene=21#wechat_redirect)威胁信息共享平台收到[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131340&idx=2&sn=f0a410fd4ac4d8564cf40c2f1284a3bf&chksm=bc858b238bf20235f42eff940d48927ae2b76820b953edb729e99bb5c9cd4d7938554025f20a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)企业及机构报告，发现多起利用新冠肺炎疫情实施[网络攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的行为。经研判分析，[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者利用疫情对公众的心理影响，将[计算机病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、移动[恶意程序](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127399&idx=2&sn=e7605669abc54947803b3cd1b19aa9db&chksm=bc859a888bf2139e9e8f91b9db4e21e52039157964132cbbf79d41504f8deff65826665e4a4b&scene=21#wechat_redirect)等伪装成包含“肺炎病例”“防护通知”等热门字样的信息，通过[钓鱼](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655117090&idx=3&sn=11ab8e9e34661409ce2fd1dd6da729ae&chksm=bc8642cd8bf1cbdb072d8f039143e5bc5024e3f65c8195d91330ce15a72ea53e65405ed0a6fb&scene=21#wechat_redirect)邮件、恶意链接等方式传播，一旦点击下载，可能导致[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&c 3bf3df7d28e4ac4a87791862e41bf791bc21304&scene=21#wechat_redirect)风险意识，采取以下措施进行防范：

 

一、不轻易打开来历不明的电子邮件及其附件。

二、不轻易点击[短信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131436&idx=3&sn=1f2181a408e8114cc1be4914a60503e9&chksm=bc858ac38bf203d54938a44c7d1bc03e63b1c674f583b59d9bf65d0900ac0d31ceb0886b591d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)或[微信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127698&idx=3&sn=5101f6a80628d7caf6bc8ed7eec325c5&chksm=bc85957d8bf21c6bad5b83f1dcf2124043fa2e6a19eee9f604e23db0211b7ddcf8d94ecf97b8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中不明来源的链接。

三、从正规应用商店或官方网站下载安装应用程序。

四、安装杀毒软件并及时更新。
