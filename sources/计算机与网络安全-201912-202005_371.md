不同的[rootkit](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130679&idx=3&sn=a22e5fd727a8e775029845ad9c1faf1d&chksm=bc8589d88bf200ce5a05db74595640e59318968fd176ea909d197e194d8b50f7e68eb5eb3aea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术将随着新设备的发展而不断发展，发现速度取决于[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)开发者学习新设备和[操作系统](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122728&idx=2&sn=6b263bf85d6b8bd8bf1d3744a491a40c&chksm=bc8668c78bf1e1d1a0e813fcd7b41c7bc300c2c98eb269b77f92854c62f1fe5127a0e01c5574&scene=21#wechat_redirect)内部机理的速度。

  

**3、僵尸网络的未来**
