mussh的全称是MUltihost [SSH](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131079&idx=3&sn=794779c5c517f42260167e098ee06322&chksm=bc8588288bf2013ebd40fb2b944291585012bf1af14e1198e987a745b0ed2b95eb882984db5a&scene=21#wechat_redirect) Wrapper，它其实是一个[SSH](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131079&idx=3&sn=794779c5c517f42260167e098ee06322&chksm=bc8588288bf2013ebd40fb2b944291585012bf1af14e1198e987a745b0ed2b95eb882984db5a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)封装器，由一个[shell](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127748&idx=3&sn=c1a3d178c1155c7613708f4f6384eb64&chksm=bc85952b8bf21c3d2d44139d072702367155b51c86221ea83618a580515205fc6dd139e1496c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)脚本实现。通过mussh可以实现批量管理多台[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的功能。在大规模自动化[运维](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127540&idx=3&sn=df473c90a503cb82fb276992233fb6e7&chksm=bc859a1b8bf2130d7020136d329d45df76667fbcc6b09b86db24ccd863ca6f14021a673b986f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)环境中，mussh可以起到很好的补充作用。

  

**1、mussh功能介绍**

  

mussh和pssh、pdsh类似，都用于批量管理[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，并且可以互为补充，通过mussh可以同时在多台[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上执行[SSH](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131079&idx=3&sn=794779c5c517f42260167e098ee06322&chksm=bc8588288bf2013ebd40fb2b944291585012bf1af14e1198e987a745b0ed2b95eb882984db5a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)命令或脚本，这里重点关注mussh执行脚本的功能。mussh可以把本地的脚本在多台[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上执行，这个功能十分有用，下面会做重点介绍。

  

要使用mussh的批量管理功能，仍然需要在本地管理[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)之间做[SSH](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131079&idx=3&sn=794779c5c517f42260167e098ee06322&chksm=bc8588288bf2013ebd40fb2b944291585012bf1af14e1198e987a745b0ed2b95eb882984db5a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的单向信任。

  

**2、mussh的安装与语法**

**3、mussh应用实例**

  

mussh的使用方法与pssh、pdsh基本类似，在完成本地管理[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)与[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)多台[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)之间的[SSH](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131079&idx=3&sn=794779c5c517f42260167e098ee06322&chksm=bc8588288bf2013ebd40fb2b944291585012bf1af14e1198e987a745b0ed2b95eb882984db5a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)认证之后，就可以使用mussh批量管理[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)了。首先看一个最简单的mussh应用实例，如图1所示。

![](../Images/fd714dd351f8ee67f5f20099e2f53a3d.png)

图1  mussh的应用实例

  

在这个例子中，主要使用了“-h”和“-c”参数（注意“-h”参数后面多台[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的写法），最后通过“-c”参数指定了在[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上要执行的命令，当需要执行多个命令时，每个命令之间用分号分隔。

  

当需要管理的[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)较多时，可以将所有[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)名或IP地址写到一个文件中，然后通过mussh的“-H”参数调用即可，如图2所示。

![](../Images/6b2e07d8248d8892b19a536f90aa947c.png)

图2  mussh通过文件读取远程主机列表信息

  

这个例子用到了“-H”和“-l”两个参数，“-H”参数用来指定需要读取的[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)列表文件，“-l”参数指定在[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上执行命令的用户为opsuser。

  

如果需要在[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上执行的命令较多，一个一个地执行mussh命令就会变得比较烦琐，此时可以将要执行的所有命令写到一个脚本文件中，然后通过mussh的“-C”参数调用即可，如图3所示。

![](../Images/acb32002d3dd0cf1a4c1f7e62a913ea8.png)

图3  通过mussh命令将本地脚本在多台远程主机上执行

  

这个例子用到了“-o”、“-s”和“-C”三个新参数，如果[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)开放的ssh端口不是默认的22，那么需要通过mussh提供的“-o”参数来指定ssh的端口，这实际上是通过“-o”参数调用ssh服务配置文件ssh\_config中的参数选项。在执行一个本地脚本时，可以通过“-C”参数实现，这个脚本可以是[shell](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127748&idx=3&sn=c1a3d178c1155c7613708f4f6384eb64&chksm=bc85952b8bf21c3d2d44139d072702367155b51c86221ea83618a580515205fc6dd139e1496c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)脚本、[Python](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133130&idx=3&sn=c7a8532965258388a9fede1c74106ef1&chksm=bc8580258bf2093376569166ef140d496526c805dfe1706ef6b327f79d52ebe0ca659fc0e62d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)脚本、Perl脚本等，只要[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上有这个执行环境就行，如果[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上的脚本执行路径不是默认的，那么需要通过“-s”参数指定执行脚本需要的[shell](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127748&idx=3&sn=c1a3d178c1155c7613708f4f6384eb64&chksm=bc85952b8bf21c3d2d44139d072702367155b51c86221ea83618a580515205fc6dd139e1496c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)路径。
