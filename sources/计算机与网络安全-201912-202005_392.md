pdsh的全称是parallel distributed shell，与pssh类似，pdsh可并行执行对[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)目标[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的操作，在有批量执行命令或分发任务的[运维](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127540&idx=3&sn=df473c90a503cb82fb276992233fb6e7&chksm=bc859a1b8bf2130d7020136d329d45df76667fbcc6b09b86db24ccd863ca6f14021a673b986f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)需求时，使用这个命令可达到事半功倍的效果。同时，pdsh还支持交互模式，当要执行的命令不确定时，可直接进入pdsh命令行，非常方便。

  

**1、pdsh应用场景**

  

pdsh的应用场景与pssh基本相同，都用于大批量[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的配置、部署、文件复制等[运维](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127540&idx=3&sn=df473c90a503cb82fb276992233fb6e7&chksm=bc859a1b8bf2130d7020136d329d45df76667fbcc6b09b86db24ccd863ca6f14021a673b986f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)操作。在使用pdsh时，仍需要配置本地[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)间的单向[SSH](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131079&idx=3&sn=794779c5c517f42260167e098ee06322&chksm=bc8588288bf2013ebd40fb2b944291585012bf1af14e1198e987a745b0ed2b95eb882984db5a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)信任。另外，pdsh还附带了pdcp命令，此命令可以将本地文件批量复制到[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的多台[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上，这在大规模的文件分发环境下是非常有用的。

  

pdsh可以通过多种方式在[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上运行命令，默认是rsh方式，另外也支持ssh、mrsh、qsh、mqsh、krb4、xcpu等多种rcmd模块，这可以在运行命令时通过参数指定。

  

**2、pdsh的安装与语法**

  

**（1）pdsh的安装过程**

  

pdsh的安装非常简单，有RPM包和源码包两种安装方式，可根据自己的喜好选择适合自己的安装方式。这里下载的源码包为pdsh-2.29.tar.bz2。编译安装过程如下：

  

\[root@server ~\]# tar jxvf pdsh-2.29.tar.bz2

\[root@server ~\]# cd pdsh-2.29

\[root@server pdsh-2.29\]# ./configure --with-ssh --with-rsh --with-mrsh --with-mqshell  \\

\>  --with-dshgroups --with-machines=/etc/pdsh/machines

\[root@server pdsh-2.29\]# make

\[root@server pdsh-2.29\]# make install

  

在执行configure阶段，“--with-ssh”参数表示启用ssh模块，其他参数都有类似的含义，而“--with-dshgroups”表示启用[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)组支持，启用此参数后，就可以将一组[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)列表写入一个文件并放到~/.dsh/group或/etc/dsh/group目录下，然后通过pdsh的“-g”参数进行调用。最后的参数“--with-machines”是“--with-dshgroups”参数的扩展，通过将所有要管理的[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)列表都写入指定的/etc/pdsh/machines文件中，接着通过pdsh的“-a”参数调用，最终完成所有[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的便捷管理。

  

完成安装后，可以通过“pdsh -V”命令查看pdsh的版本号以及可使用的模块信息，操作如下：

  

\[opsuser@server ~\]$ pdsh -V

pdsh-2.29

rcmd modules: ssh,rsh,exec (default: rsh)

misc modules: machines,dshgroup

  

此外，也可以通过“pdsh -L”命令来显示当前所有加载的模块信息。在pdsh安装完成后，还有一个可用的工具pdcp，后面将详细讲述pdsh和pdcp两个命令的用法。
