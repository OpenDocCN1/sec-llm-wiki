**1、pssh应用场景**

  

pssh的全称是parallel-ssh，是一个用[Python](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133130&idx=3&sn=c7a8532965258388a9fede1c74106ef1&chksm=bc8580258bf2093376569166ef140d496526c805dfe1706ef6b327f79d52ebe0ca659fc0e62d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)编写的可以并发在多台[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上批量执行命令的工具，它支持文件并行复制、[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)并行执行命令、杀掉[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上的[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)等。其中，文件并行复制是pssh核心功能，也是同类工具中最大的亮点，因此，要批量在[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上传、下载文件时，最好选用pssh这个[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)批量管理工具。

  

要使用pssh工具包，必须保证本地[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和要管理的[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)之间的单向信任，也就是要在本地[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)所有[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上配置密钥认证访问。创建本地[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)之间的单向信任非常简单，下面以创建本地用户opsuser和所有[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上opsuser用户之间的信任为例介绍配置的基本过程。

  

**（1）在本地主机上创建RSA密钥和公钥**

  

1）在本地[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上以opsuser用户登录。

  

2）在opsuser用户的根目录内创建.ssh目录并设置读取权限。

  

\[opsuser@server ~\]$ mkdir ~/.ssh  

\[opsuser@server ~\]$ chmod 700 ~/.ssh

  

3）使用ssh-keygen命令生成基于SSH协议的RSA密钥。

  

\[opsuser@server ~\]$ cd ~/.ssh  

\[opsuser@server ~\]$ ssh-keygen -t rsa

  

在提示保存私钥（key）和公钥（public key）的位置时，选择默认值，然后依次按回车键即可。

  

**（2）整合公钥文件**

  

在本地[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上以opsuser用户登录，执行如下操作：

  

\[opsuser@server ~\]$ cd ~/.ssh

\[opsuser@server ssh\]$ cat /home/opsuser/.ssh/id\_rsa.pub > authorized\_keys

\[opsuser@server ssh\]$ chmod 600 ~/.ssh/authorized\_keys

\[opsuser@server ssh\]$ scp authorized\_keys user001:/home/opsuser/.ssh/

  

这个操作过程是将本地节点生成的公钥文件整合为一个authorized\_keys文件，然后进行授权，并将authorized\_keys复制到所有[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上。这里以[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)user001为例。

  

**（3）测试SSH密钥认证**

  

在本地[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上执行如下命令：

  

\[opsuser@server ssh\]$ ssh user001 date

  

这里以user001[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)为例，其他[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)依此类推，如果不需要输入[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)就出现系统当前日期，说明SSH单向信任已经配置成功了。

  

**2、pssh的安装与用法**

  

pssh要求[Python](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133130&idx=3&sn=c7a8532965258388a9fede1c74106ef1&chksm=bc8580258bf2093376569166ef140d496526c805dfe1706ef6b327f79d52ebe0ca659fc0e62d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)版本大于2.4即可。这里下载的是pssh-2.3.1.tar.gz，安装过程如下：

  

\[root@server ~\]# tar zxvf pssh-2.3.1.tar.gz  

\[root@server ~\]# cd pssh-2.3.1

\[root@server pssh-2.3.1\]# python setup.py install

  

安装完成的pssh工具包附带了5个主程序，分别是：

  

parallel ssh（pssh），在[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)多台[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上并行运行命令。

  

parallel scp（pscp），把文件并行复制到[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)多台[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上，类似于scp命令。

  

parallel rsync（prsync），使用rsync协议将文件从本地[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)同步到[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)多台[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上。

  

parallel nuke（pnuke），在[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)多台[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上并行killall某一[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

pssh可调用tar命令解压[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上的文件，例如：

  

\[opsuser@server ~\]$ pssh -i -h /etc/pssh/hosts "tar -zxvf hadoop-2.0.0-cdh4.5.0.tar.gz"  

  

在这个例子中，pssh默认解压的是[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上/home/opsuser/hadoop-2.0.0-cdh4.5.0.tar.gz文件，而要解压root用户下的文件，可以执行如下操作：

  

\[opsuser@server ~\]$ pssh -i -h /etc/pssh/hosts \\ > "sudo "tar -zxvf /mnt/hadoop-2.0.0-cdh4.5.0.tar.gz -c /mnt" "

  

在这个例子中，pssh调用了“sudo”命令，因此，要操作具有root权限的文件时，opsuser用户在[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上必须开通可通过sudo命令切换到root用户的权限。

这个实例用到了“-x”参数，分别调用了ssh命令的两个参数“-l”和“-p”，用于指定在[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上登录的用户名和[SSH](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131079&idx=3&sn=794779c5c517f42260167e098ee06322&chksm=bc8588288bf2013ebd40fb2b944291585012bf1af14e1198e987a745b0ed2b95eb882984db5a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)端口，而“-o”参数指定将输出结果存放到/etc/pssh/info目录中。在此实例的最后指定了要在[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上执行的命令，可以指定多个命令，每个命令之间用分号隔开。

  

**（2）pscp与pslurp应用实例**

  

pscp命令的主要作用是将本地文件并行地复制到[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)多台[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上，而pslurp是把文件从[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)多台[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)复制到本地[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，这两个命令的功能刚好相反。在[运维](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127540&idx=3&sn=df473c90a503cb82fb276992233fb6e7&chksm=bc859a1b8bf2130d7020136d329d45df76667fbcc6b09b86db24ccd863ca6f14021a673b986f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工作中，需要进行文件批量传送时，这两个命令非常有用。

**（3）prsync与pnuke应用实例**

  

prsync的主要作用是通过rsync协议将文件或目录从本地[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)同步到[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)多个[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上，先看prsync的两个应用实例，如图7所示。

![](../Images/94132ed30748dedb4d6370c57e824694.png)

图7  prsync应用实例

  

图7的第一个实例中使用了“-l”、“-a”和“-r”参数，其中“-l”用于指定[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上的用户，“-r”用于递归复制指定目录下的所有文件，“-a”参数可以维持文件的属性值不变，例如文件的创建时间、修改时间、读写权限等，这个参数在做目录复制时非常有用，建议使用。

  

第二个实例中又引入了“-z”参数，这是一个压缩传输参数，在低带宽环境下，或者在对网络带宽有要求、传输文件压缩率比较大时使用。而在带宽充足或传输的文件比较大时，不推荐使用。

  

pnuke的主要作用是在[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)多[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上并行杀掉某一[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，相当于killall命令。它的用法非常简单，最简单的用法如下：

  

\[opsuser@server ~\]$ pnuke -h /etc/pssh/hosts httpd

  

这个命令将在[远程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132894&idx=3&sn=17847f10c973b834be00cec9b8b3cde6&chksm=bc8581318bf2082794a0d3f9796303983a6550c4865bc5ad0d1f210fc41d18a2828b8a90fc51&scene=21#wechat_redirect)所有[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上并行关闭httpd服务，pnuke的作用类似于killall，而后面跟的httpd是服务名，其实只要通过killall命令能关闭的服务，都可以通过pnuke来批量完成。
