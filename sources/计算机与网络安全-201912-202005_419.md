当[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)成功地感染目标系统，对其建立了完全的控制和操作，且活跃在内存中，它就可以支配所控制的系统资源来完成相关任务。一旦激活，它会立即采取行动，并执行其主要指令。根据[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的类型和执行任务的不同，指令可以多种多样。但是无论一个[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)拥有哪几种指令的组合，它们都是以下的一种或几种的组合：

  

● 在系统中长期潜伏

● 和[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者通信

● 执行有效载荷

  

例如，“打了就跑”战术，它不需要持续地访问受感染系统以及与[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者通信以获取指令，它所使用的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)只有单一的指令，那就是执行它的有效载荷。

  

打了就跑”战术是在非常短时间内发生的活动。如果是信息窃取任务，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)将收集指定时期内的所有信息，然后将其发送出去，最后销毁自己。如果它的任务只是破坏，它将简单地执行有效载荷，同时销毁所有痕迹，包括[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)自身。

  

**1、在系统中长期潜伏**

  

[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)一旦在目标系统中站稳了脚跟，其中一个关键任务就是保证自身能够长期潜伏。它必须能够长期控制目标系统。为此，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)必须能够做到以下几点：

  

● 隐藏自身

● 更新自身

● 保持对目标系统的独占性访问

  

**（1）隐藏自身**

  

[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)在感染目标系统后，会对目标系统进行修改，通常修改之处包括如下几种：

  

● 增加安装[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)

● 修改现有的文件

● 删除现有的文件

● 修改[Windows](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127496&idx=3&sn=21acddfe6a98fe94ae346db72555e138&chksm=bc859a278bf21331d775fdb2224b50832a618a13d3a8fa76c41786186e846f5126daf96682bc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)注册表

● 修改其他配置文件

  

如果知道到哪里去找，那么从修改之处就可以发现[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)存在的蛛丝马迹。但是上述的变化并不是[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)存在的唯一标志。[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的活动也是一个主要的标志。因此，为了保持在目标系统中的立足点，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)不仅必须隐藏在感染过程中对目标系统所做的修改，还应隐藏自身活动的迹象。也就是说，一方面需要隐藏上述修改动作，另一方面，还要在进行活动时隐藏自身。活跃的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，或者说是在运行的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，在运行的过程中会暴露它自己。另外，如果它拥有网络功能，它还会暴露自己的网络活动。因此为了有效地进行隐藏，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)需要处理以下两类能够证明它存在的证据：

  

● [主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上的证据

● 网络上的证据

  

在[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上的证据包括恶意软件感染期间添加、修改和删除的文件；注册表设置和配置文件设置上的修改以及运行过程中产生的痕迹。在网络上的证据包括网络访问痕迹，这些是由[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)在初始化网络连接时产生的。

  

**在**[**主机**](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)**上隐藏证据：**在安装过程中，可以通过简单的视觉技巧隐藏已安装的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)文件。对已存在文件的更改和删除并不需要[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)再去隐藏，因为在缺少足够[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)行为知识的情况下，在数十万份系统文件之中，判断哪一份文件被修改或删除是一件相当困难的事情。

  

当[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)在注册表设置和其他配置文件上做修改时，要有效地隐藏证据，就需要一些额外的工作，因为为了达到混淆目标所进行的任何操作都可能导致配置失效。因此，当需要隐藏这种特殊的[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)证据时，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)必须使用某些技术。隐藏活跃的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)活动也是一样的。因为这些技术只有在[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)运行时才能工作，所以[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)必须是活跃的，从而使系统中的证据能持久地保持隐藏状态。

  

当[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)在内存中保持活跃时，能隐藏这些证据的方法从最简单的到最复杂的都有。简单的方法比如使用一种同形异义的伪装程序名的方法来干扰简单的枚举，或者复杂的方法比如使用新的精巧的[rootkit](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130679&idx=3&sn=a22e5fd727a8e775029845ad9c1faf1d&chksm=bc8589d88bf200ce5a05db74595640e59318968fd176ea909d197e194d8b50f7e68eb5eb3aea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术。这取决于[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)所要执行的任务。如果这个[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)只需要在系统中存活短暂时间，就不需要隐藏它。但是如果计划永久存活在系统中，就必须隐藏自身。

在某些情况下，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)安装程序会在向[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)传递控制权之前修补[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

**2、和攻击者通信**

  

[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是否需要与[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者进行交互取决于[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的目的。如果[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)执行“打了就跑”的策略，那么与[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者之间的通信就是不必要的，因为大多数用于这类目的的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)已经携带了用来完成任务的全部指令。但对于需要在系统中保持长期驻存的任务来说，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)必须具备与[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者交互的能力。这样才能保证[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)拥有完成任务所需要的更新的指令。

  

**（1）恶意软件通信的两种类型**

  

在[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者之间有两种通信类型：

  

● 单向通信

  

[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发起

[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者发起

  

● 双向通信

  

**1）单向通信**

  

[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)或是[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者都能创建单向通信。[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)能够简单地按照预先的设置给[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者发送信息。这类[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)已经拥有完成任务所需要的指令。[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者通常不主动采取行动，只是等待[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发来信息。一些键盘记录器会向[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者发起单向通信。[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)按照预定的程序执行，在[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者没有亲自操控键盘记录器的情况下，将它窃取的所有数据发回给[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者。

  

当[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)只是简单地等待指令和行为，不发回任何信息时，这就是[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者发起的单向通信。大多数分布式拒绝服务（distributed denial-of-service,DDoS）的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)就使用这种方式。它等待[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者发来的指令，然后执行[DDoS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126795&idx=3&sn=9038ab35960660611a2d94ac3eb31681&chksm=bc8598e48bf211f22b26629df6dfe1cbf0a08bcf8acfa47740b1ce6afce1f9ab817d994e437a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)功能。[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者不需要从[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)那里接收任何信息来了解[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是否发生或是否成功；[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者只需尝试访问目标的服务，如果是访问失败，那么[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者就知道[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)成功了。

  

**2）双向通信**

  

双向通信是发生在[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者间的一个交互过程。这是一种更加健全的通信方式，因为[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者能够根据当前的条件和状态来修改和调整[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)向[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者发送信息，[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者根据这些信息向[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发送指令。

**（2）和攻击者通信的原因**

  

无论通信方式是单向的还是双向的，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)都需要和[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者进行通信以完成如下的一个或多个功能的组合：

  

● 报告它的状态

● 获得命令

● 发送信息

  

就像忠诚的仆人一样，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)会根据需要向[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发送自身的状态，如当前的感染状态和是否成功等。如果[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)需要完成多个不同的功能，它发送的信息对于[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者来说是非常重要的，每个功能的状态都会发送给[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者。发送的信息可以是简单的成功或失败，也可以是关于每个功能所输出的复杂报告。

  

基于[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的状态报告，如果[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)具备这个功能，[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者可以根据需要向它发送命令作为回应，特别是收到一个感染成功状态的报告后。从[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者那里获取命令是通信的最重要原因。没有这个，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)会失去它的目标，从而变得无用。除非设计用来执行“打了就跑”的任务，否则尽管[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)依然可以按照当前所有的指令来工作，但是它会在[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中失去关联性。从[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者发来的命令不只包括影响[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)行为的新指令，同时还包括关于[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)资源域名的更新信息。

  

从[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者那儿获取命令并不依赖于[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的发送状态报告功能，因为并不是所有的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)都具有这个功能。对于[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者而言，拥有这个功能虽然很好，但它并不是必需的。

  

除了状态报告之外，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)也会发送相关信息给[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者，这些信息包括受感染系统的信息，特别是在刚感染的时候，当然最重要的还是窃取的数据。

  

**（3）恶意软件发起的网络通信**

  

当[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者通信时，并不总是直接连接到[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)资源域名。相反，连接到[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)资源域名的通信只是所有网络通信的一部分。其余网络通信的目的是为了保护[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)自身。

  

下面是常见的由[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发起的网络通信：

  

● 使用著名合法域名，如google.com，来检查网络连通性

  

● 使用网络时间协议（Network Time Protocol,NTP）[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)来检查真实日期和时间

  

● 通过连接[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)服务域名来检查更新

  

● 通过连接命令和控制（C＆C）[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)来检查命令

  

● 将窃取的数据放置在相应区域

  

在所有事情开始之前，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)需要检查受感染系统是否有[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)连接。如果没有，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)就不能继续下面的通信步骤。这个步骤能够保证[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)不会留下任何的网络通信证据。一旦建立连接，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)需要检查受感染系统的时间是否准确。这个步骤是通过控制[操作系统](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122728&idx=2&sn=6b263bf85d6b8bd8bf1d3744a491a40c&chksm=bc8668c78bf1e1d1a0e813fcd7b41c7bc300c2c98eb269b77f92854c62f1fe5127a0e01c5574&scene=21#wechat_redirect)时间绕过沙盒的一个尝试。如果一切妥当，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)接下来会检查当前在[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)服务域名上是否有更新。如果有，那么需要更新本身；如果没有，它开始连接C＆C[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，然后等待[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者发来的命令。[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)能够持续地监听C＆C[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，或者为了避免怀疑，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)可以每隔一段时间查询一次。查询的周期可以设置为一毫秒到数天。

  

对于窃取数据的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)来说，当窃取的数据已经准备发送给[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者，如果放置区域是域名，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)会连接它。

  

**3、执行有效载荷**

  

这是[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)最重要的指令，也是[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)会存在的首要原因。在此之前做的所有事情都是为其做准备的。每种[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)都有属于它的有效载荷。例如，一个[后门](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129244&idx=3&sn=6df349d4b772425777487a77091f0f4d&chksm=bc8593738bf21a652bfc7213ea58a506986d7b1e6812c5132b33c598b7a8a210c10f13d7f900&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序的有效载荷是使[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者有持续地访问受感染系统的能力，而对于键盘记录器来说，它的有效载荷就是收集那些用户在击键时输入的口令等身份凭证。但是无论有效载荷是什么，它们总可以分为三个维度：

  

● 有效载荷严重度

● 有效载荷执行周期

● 有效载荷触发

  

**（1）有效载荷严重度**

  

有效载荷的严重度取决于它的类型。无论有效载荷是什么，它都可以像在[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)屏幕上显示一条信息那样无害，也可以像破坏性[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)那样危害巨大，后者能够使受感染系统无法正常工作，系统还原是系统重新工作的唯一办法。

  

**（2）有效载荷执行周期**

  

有效载荷的执行周期，换句话说，就是有效载荷达到预定目的之前需要执行的次数。执行周期可以是一次，也可以重复多次。例如，能够造成整个系统完全毁灭的破坏性[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)只需要执行一次，就像核爆炸能够毁灭它所覆盖的整个区域一样。它不需要一遍又一遍地爆炸去查看破坏结果。另一个执行周期只有一次的例子是带有本地域欺骗功能的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。通过本地域欺骗，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)只需要通过对[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)文件的一次修改就能实现信息的持久窃取。提供给[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者对受感染系统的持久的访问权限的[后门](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129244&idx=3&sn=6df349d4b772425777487a77091f0f4d&chksm=bc8593738bf21a652bfc7213ea58a506986d7b1e6812c5132b33c598b7a8a210c10f13d7f900&scene=21#wechat_redirect)程序的有效载荷则需要以重复的方式执行。

  

域欺骗技术指的是一种将原本打算发送至合法网站的域名重定向到伪造网站的一种[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术，通常通过修改[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)文件、[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[路由器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128332&idx=3&sn=4f8d018cff0a3582f18d8148aa992d91&chksm=bc8596e38bf21ff5457749ea5ce4c173811d108f169c96bf028bb2580044317582937601c489&scene=21#wechat_redirect)或感染域名系统（[DNS](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127816&idx=3&sn=2b8d5dfef1801f990c8b7cb25fc65d73&chksm=bc8594e78bf21df19918b7e41f11ca714d5f530030014dd6a48e278b45385d835489385f1cd8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)）来实现。

  

**（3）有效载荷触发**

  

[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)有效载荷的执行可以是即时的，也可以是有条件的。即时的执行意味着[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)在运行时立即执行它的有效载荷，而有条件的执行指的是需要满足特定的条件才能执行有效载荷。例如，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)可能等待某个特定的日期去激活它的有效载荷。最近的一种叫做Conficker蠕虫的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)非常引人注目，因为它拥有一个能够在指定的时间触发的神秘的有效载荷，这个时间是4月1号。

  

在实践中大多数的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)分析系统都有日期和时间控制特性，用来触发那些依靠这些因素的有效载荷。但是这种方法只对那些没有检查网络时间协议（NTP）[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)或者没有用任何时间检查机制来确定真实时间和日期的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)有效。

  

除了日期以外，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)还能使用不同的有效载荷触发机制。它们包括是否安装某个软件、是否在内存中有一个活跃[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、是否有因特网连接（[Wi-Fi](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129830&idx=3&sn=73c9e474164bf747598a0d9b363a5a8e&chksm=bc858d098bf2041fe0ea3241ab1cbbc4c506b0beaf705ef1bed314f12c3ba4cc2830a071fe54&scene=21#wechat_redirect)或[局域网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133196&idx=3&sn=fced3cdaddc933dbd9589c7cbfb41c3d&chksm=bc8583e38bf20af512424bf8e3328b45e53324d55ba487f38c118ae17454713ce6962aec0da9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)）或者是[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)能够收集和利用的任何条件。唯一的限制是[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者的想象力。
