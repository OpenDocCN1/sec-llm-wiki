**1、错误现象**

  

客户反映在执行“apachectl start”启动Apache时无报错信息，但是还是不能访问网页。客户的网站是基于Apache+[PHP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130803&idx=3&sn=32cc58b76d89e2ba3f9d132628ea3200&chksm=bc85895c8bf2004a26b780597e0b78206119e6b311d936b856b95ae2dbfdf4574840e4068298&scene=21#wechat_redirect)+[MySQL](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126890&idx=3&sn=1cdab8e3953ef5db6b337a3e91a3dafd&chksm=bc8598858bf21193106359c3a1855175328ce1420020eada002972457ce1145f91b55d5da09d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的在线交易平台，听到客户描述的现象后，第一反应是[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)屏蔽了[HTTP](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129052&idx=3&sn=6aa03cee097fa59206f385629b151800&chksm=bc8590338bf219253db54d9678c7924707d1e7d1195f8709817128e1ad501546618ac2f91cc7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)端口或SELinux的问题，于是登录[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)查看相关信息，如图1所示。

![](../Images/7c478e86a4c8e803410ffd7f61fd6319.png)

从输出可知，[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)所有策略都处于开放状态，并未作任何限制，而SELinux也处于关闭状态，应该不是[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)问题导致的。

  

既然不是[防火墙](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127250&idx=3&sn=d95a26c29b4a0cfedb211fbcab275a02&chksm=bc859b3d8bf2122be83cb08a61f278524cbf5337e2e08f432263ec6540f18928053e280de1d5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)拦截的问题，那么看看httpd[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是否存在及httpd端口是否正常启动，操作过程如图2所示。

![](../Images/7064167fcc353da0b9f39df128c24986.png)

这个操作首先查看了[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上的httpd[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，发现并没有httpd[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)运行，同时httpd对应的端口80也并没有启动，于是重新启动Apache，在启动Apache的过程中并没有报错，启动完成后发现仍然httpd[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)没有运行，由此看来，应该是Apache内部出现了问题。

  

**2、解决思路**

  

在判定是Apache的问题后，首先要看的就是Apache的启动日志，在查看Apache的error日志后，发现了一个可疑输出，内容为：

  

No space left on device: mod\_rewrite: could not create rewrite\_log\_lock Configuration Failed

  

看到这个错误提示，感觉应该是磁盘空间耗尽导致的，于是赶紧查看系统所有磁盘分区，结果发现所有磁盘分区都还有很多可用空间，这就有些奇怪了。

  

[Linux](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133968&idx=2&sn=2454fe58d155dec7738888c75a963aeb&chksm=bc85bcff8bf235e97db0692fa805d2eeecdcaaa1e2a26f03ab5112230e873a342fcaca0bdf4f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)下对磁盘空间的占用分为三个部分：物理磁盘、inode节点磁盘空间和信号量磁盘空间。通过检查[服务器](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128495&idx=3&sn=c1bf8a28be100a23ba990869e705ee2a&chksm=bc8596408bf21f5663ebe52e53d2e062f5a7f2149c642a2a9332c17b864f7a5f8758253d7e3f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的物理磁盘空间，发现仍有很多剩余，因此排除物理磁盘空间的问题。接着通过“df -i”命令查看系统可用的inode节点，发现每个分区可用的inode还有很多，这样inode节点问题也被排除了，那么应该是信号量磁盘空间耗尽导致的。

  

信号量是一种锁机制，用于协调[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)之间互斥的访问临界资源，以确保某种共享资源不被多个[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)同时访问。[Linux](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133968&idx=2&sn=2454fe58d155dec7738888c75a963aeb&chksm=bc85bcff8bf235e97db0692fa805d2eeecdcaaa1e2a26f03ab5112230e873a342fcaca0bdf4f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统的信号量是用于[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)间通信的。它有两种标准实现，分别是POSIX及System v，现在大多数[Linux](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133968&idx=2&sn=2454fe58d155dec7738888c75a963aeb&chksm=bc85bcff8bf235e97db0692fa805d2eeecdcaaa1e2a26f03ab5112230e873a342fcaca0bdf4f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统都实现了这两种标准。这两种标准都可用于进行线程间的通信，只是系统调用方式略有不同。

  

System v信号量通过系统调用semget来创建，通过[Linux](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133968&idx=2&sn=2454fe58d155dec7738888c75a963aeb&chksm=bc85bcff8bf235e97db0692fa805d2eeecdcaaa1e2a26f03ab5112230e873a342fcaca0bdf4f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)命令ipcs即可显示[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)间通信用的System v类型信号量及共享内存。

  

POSIX信号量可用于线程和[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)间通信，并可分为有名和无名两种。也可简单理解为是否保存在磁盘上。有名的信号量会以文件形式保存在/dev/shm下，因此可用于不相关的[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)间通信，而无名信号量只能用于线程间和父子[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)间通信。

  

在对信号量有了简单了解后，可以发现Apache使用的[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)间通信方式应该是System v，因此就可以通过ipcs命令查看和解决这个问题了。

  

**3、解决问题**

  

在解决这个问题之前，首先查看下[Linux](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133968&idx=2&sn=2454fe58d155dec7738888c75a963aeb&chksm=bc85bcff8bf235e97db0692fa805d2eeecdcaaa1e2a26f03ab5112230e873a342fcaca0bdf4f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统默认信号量的设置值是多少，执行如下命令：

  

\[root@localhost ~\]# cat /proc/sys/kernel/sem

250 32000 32 128

  

这4个输出值的含义如下：

  

SEMMSL，此参数用于控制每个信号集的最大信号数。

  

SEMMNS，此参数用于控制整个[Linux](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133968&idx=2&sn=2454fe58d155dec7738888c75a963aeb&chksm=bc85bcff8bf235e97db0692fa805d2eeecdcaaa1e2a26f03ab5112230e873a342fcaca0bdf4f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统中信号（而不是信号集）的最大数量。

  

SEMOPM，此参数用于控制每个semop系统调用可以执行的信号操作数。

  

SEMMNI，此内核参数用于控制整个[Linux](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133968&idx=2&sn=2454fe58d155dec7738888c75a963aeb&chksm=bc85bcff8bf235e97db0692fa805d2eeecdcaaa1e2a26f03ab5112230e873a342fcaca0bdf4f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)系统中信号集的最大数量。

  

接着通过ipcs命令查看httpd[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)占用了多少信号量，执行如下命令：

  

\[root@localhost ~\]# ipcs -s | grep daemon

  

其中daemon是启动Apache进程的用户，默认是daemon，也可能是nobody用户，根据实际环境而定。在执行完此命令后，发现很多基于daemon的信号量输出，问题终于找到了。解决信号量耗尽的方法很简单，通过ipcrm命令清除即可，最简单方法是执行如下命令组合：

  

\[root@localhost ~\]# ipcs -s | grep nobody | perl -e 'while(<STDIN>)'{@=split(^s+/)};print 'ipcrm sem $a\[1\]'

  

执行完上面这个命令后，再次启动Apache，然后查看httpd[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是否启动，可以看到，此时httpd[进程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131174&idx=3&sn=d79178100819b07634684563e1c10d38&chksm=bc858bc98bf202df4587cc903821ca88564b45742475b53bb542b44926dfacc0ca50d004240e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)启动了，那么Apache也工作正常了。
