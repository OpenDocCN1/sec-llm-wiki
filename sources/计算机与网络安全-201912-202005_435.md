ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)——这种流水线的方法称为“[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工厂”。

  

**1、恶意软件流水线**

  

[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)流水线的方法可以简化[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)装甲军队的建立过程。它也使得[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者能够回收重用旧的检测到的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)二进制文件和[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)来创建一个新的自我防护的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)军队，如图1所示。

![](../Images/5858e7f9e470b70aa2ffe027f79e1c9f.png)

图1  恶意软件工厂流水线

  

流水线方法可以分为如下三个步骤：

  

● 生成基本的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)样本

● 独立的防护工具

● 质量控制

  

**（1）生成基本的恶意软件样本**

  

[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)生产流水线的初始环节是生成基本的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，可利用下一代[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工具套件来产生，如图2所示，或者针对已有的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)二进制文件或源[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进行改造。第一步就是产生基本的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)样本，改造原有的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)二进制文件，对[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)源[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进行修改，并重新编译或汇编成新的二进制文件，最后利用下一代[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工具套件生成具有防护功能的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。在进入第二步之前，应使用加密器对回收重用的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和新生成的二进制文件进行加密，因为这两种类型的基本的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)都无法产生[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)样本的任意变种。加密器解决这个问题的方法是使用精确到微秒的日期和时间作为密钥。

![](../Images/a5f6ca828a15a9d3b2c09797b1f42b88.png)

**（2）独立的防护工具**

  

第二步才是最有意思的部分。在这一步中，要采用各种不同的防护措施对基本的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)样本进行保护，包括一个或一组工具：加密工具、[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)混淆工具、加壳工具、反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)规避工具和可执行文件绑定工具，最后生成的是具有较强自我防护能力，适应性极强的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。图3展示了独立的防护工具的一些例子。

![](../Images/5fd88759de8ff40172a54bfc00f1eb47.png)

图3  一些最常见的独立的防护工具

  

具有自我防护能力的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)通常也称为适应性极强的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

**（3）质量控制**

  

第三步是确保新创建的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)军队能够成功。由[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工厂生产的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)样本在部署之前需要经过严格的质量控制，其目的主要是为了规避反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)软件。因此这个过程的最后一步就是检查最终产品是否会被反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品检测到。一些[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工厂搭建了自己的多引擎[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)扫描器，如图4所示，而另外一些则使用免费的在线[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)扫描服务如NoVirusThanks，如图5所示。与其他的在线扫描服务相比，使用NoVirusThanks的优势在于它可以选择不将提交的文件发送给反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk 通常[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者会避免使用需要提交样本的在线[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)扫描服务，因为这些样本会被提交到反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)公司。在线的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)扫描服务通常采用散列搜索技术。

  

有两种类型的在线[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)扫描服务。一种是分析提交的文件或散列值，然后告诉提交者这个文件是否是[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。另一种是类似在线版的反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品，扫描用户磁盘中是否存在[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

在实践中并不是所有的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)样本都要能够绕过所有反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品的检测才能被部署。这依赖于目标公司所使用的反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品。例如，如果目标公司使用了赛门铁克公司反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品，[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者只需要考虑要部署的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)样本是否能够被赛门铁克公司检测出来就足够了，不用考虑其他反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品。

  

**2、攻击者工具的获得**

**3、恶意软件日益泛滥**

  

利用[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工厂生成高质量的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)装甲军队，这种方法给了[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者巨大的优势，大大提高了[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的成功率。几乎所有的[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)涉及的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)都来自于数以千计的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)军队。这带来了一个巨大的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)浪潮。这就像是上千个僵尸在后面追赶着您，只为了一个目的——吃掉您的大脑。

  

[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工厂是近几年来产生大量[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)样本的重要原因之一。另一个原因就是，[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工具很容易在因特网上扩散，任何有[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)意图的人都能创建自己的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)装甲军队。

  

根据AV-Test.org网站的统计，他们从2009年的平均每天处理33000个不同的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)样本上升到2010年的54000个。大约10年以前，他们每天大概只需要处理500个样本。图6展示了[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)样本的年增长曲线。
