 

为了达到这样的目的，需要建立一支[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)军队，其中的每个成员都有不同的形式，但做相同的工作。[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)军队是由大量[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)组成的，每个[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)都是独一无二的，任何两者之间都不同。它们唯一相同之处就是恶意目的。

  

**1、下一代恶意软件工具套件**

  

[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)开发者已经掌握现有的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术并能用来创建[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)军队。他们所需要做的就是将这些[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)放在一起。一种技术是利用变形[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中的变异引擎。变形[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)能够在每次感染时改变自己的形式，从而组成[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)军队。每个[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)都具有不同的形式，但都有相同的目的。唯一的不足是新形式的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的产生过程是比较缓慢的，因为这依赖于整个感染的过程。另外一种现有的技术是[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)创建工具套件。[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)创建工具套件可以很方便地创建[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)军队，因为它能够比变形[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)更迅速地批量产生[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。但是不足之处是早期[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)创建工具套件所产生的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)很容易被检测到，这是因为[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)在形式上的区别不大。

  

变异引擎能够在不牺牲功能的情况下修改[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，而[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)创建工具套件则能够创建批量的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)样本。两种技术都有创建相同目的的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的能力。单独来说，它们有各自的优势和劣势，但是两者的组合能够组成一种更有力的技术，既可以保留它们的优势，又可以将它们各自的劣势消除掉。这种新的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)套件能够批量产生形式各不相同但有相同意图的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)样本，但这是不够的。

  

军队指挥官是不会让他的士兵在没有任何保护的情况下上战场的。他们不仅仅需要装备[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)性武器，还需要最有效的保护装置来抵抗敌人的炮火。[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)军队也是一样的——对于用来感染的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，不管它们的数量有多少，各自之间的区别有多大，没有合适的装备，捕获的时候都会被很轻易地分析。因此[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)军队需要自我防护的装备。

  

之前，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)自我装备的过程是通过将一些特性编码到[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中。但是对于[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)军队，这种方法是不可行的。想象一下，每天将所有的特性一个个地编写进数以千计的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)样本中。这是非常不明智的。解决方案是将反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)规避技术编写进[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工具套件中。这就是下一代[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工具套件，如图1所示。这是个多合一的工具套件，能够将最先进的反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)规避技术加入到每个新创建的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482 **2、独立的防护工具**

  

下一代[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工具套件提供给[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者一种强有力的、危险的武器。但这并不是[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者拥有的唯一武器。为什么呢？因为即使[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的创建能够摆脱反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的检测，反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工程师还是能够拿到[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工具套件，对其进行[逆向](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132067&idx=3&sn=bc78826629c1a380b70cbf304ab57a73&chksm=bc85844c8bf20d5a78ab1410dccb317f44eaf355727afaaf9937239165e233c49caa7a589a2d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工程后就能够找到如何检测并消除该[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75 [攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者拥有的其他武器是独立的防护工具，如图2所示。这些工具可以用在下一代[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工具套件中，用来防止其被[逆向](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132067&idx=3&sn=bc78826629c1a380b70cbf304ab57a73&chksm=bc85844c8bf20d5a78ab1410dccb317f44eaf355727afaaf9937239165e233c49caa7a589a2d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工程分析以提高逃避反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)分析的能力。现在已经有许多独立的防护工具，但是最常用的有如下几种：

  

● 加密器

● 实时加壳器

● 可执行文件绑定器

加密器是[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)加密常用的独立的防护工具。它的主要目的是加密[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)来逃避检测。实时加壳器对[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进行加密的同时进行压缩，使得[逆向](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132067&idx=3&sn=bc78826629c1a380b70cbf304ab57a73&chksm=bc85844c8bf20d5a78ab1410dccb317f44eaf355727afaaf9937239165e233c49caa7a589a2d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工程更加困难。最初的实时加壳技术是用来向竞争对手隐藏专有的[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。但是和其他工具或技术一样，假如它服务于[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者的意图，它就会被用在[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)上。这种技术已经用来加密和压缩[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。最终结果是产生一种能够自我执行压缩和加密过程的文件。

  

一些实时加壳器，例如NeoLite，能够像变异引擎一样改变[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)本身。

  

加密器和实时加壳器已经给反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品提出一个挑战并能够有效地打败静态分析，而可执行文件绑定器的使用加大了这种挑战。可执行文件绑定器是常用的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)感染的独立的防护工具。它将[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)与一段正常的二进制[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)绑定——这也就是可执行文件绑定器名字的由来。这节省了[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者诱惑用户运行非感染类[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的时间。[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)者并不需要跟随早期的[木马](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127431&idx=3&sn=a2aff10bca3787bd2379ac69daeb2600&chksm=bc859a688bf2137e4d33653dc40478ef2255ae67105e5517ee31cd3d7bd3e42ca545f9a80f20&scene=21#wechat_redirect)制作者的路线；相反地，他只需要找到一个有趣的可执行文件，例如一个游戏，然后将他的恶意[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)绑定到上面。这个工具很特殊，因为其在[社会工程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132290&idx=3&sn=781d7217823c7966f9d1383b8ed08cb3&chksm=bc85876d8bf20e7bb8676d42b96315085f6f3b879f4ef53cdb993cf2eef49ef54eb14faab734&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中扮演了一个极为重要的角色。单独来说，所有的这些工具在逃避反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)检测方面都是非常有效的，但是将它们组合起来能够使[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)更为狡猾和危险。

  

加壳文件是指使用实时加壳器进行加壳的二进制文件。不要与由压缩工具如ZIP、RAR等生成的自解压包混淆。加壳文件会在内存中进行解压并解密自身；而后直接执行解压的[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。自解压包是将解压出来的文件释放到硬盘以供稍后使用的可执行文件，在这个过程中不需要调用与压缩文件相关的软件。

  

**3、恶意软件装甲军队的作用**

  

阻止一个[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)装甲军队并不简单。反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工程师通常需要使用两种类型的方法：快速修复和正确修复。快速修复是指部署多个特定[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)来进行检测。这是一对一的检测。每个捕获的军队成员都有一个[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)与之对应。正确修复则专注于部署单一的“抓取全部”的[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，目的是用一个[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)不仅能检测反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工程师研究的军队成员，而且能检测整个[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)军队。很明显地，第一种方法是不可行且不可扩展的。它既不是有效果的也不是有效率的解决方案。它不太有效是因为它只覆盖了反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工程师能够捕获的部分；它效率不高是因为其将使反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品中[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[数据库](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129320&idx=3&sn=e5b2f0721ffd54f8891e633fbc280f4b&chksm=bc8593078bf21a1184c1596a84ce0604e00b716de6081c5b27ea0311d1df5e2b5024ba752979&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的规模显著增大。但是由于反应时间的限制，反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)公司有时候使用这种方法。检测的[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)不得不立即部署到反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品中以阻止感染。因为没有足够的时间去分析和[逆向](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132067&idx=3&sn=bc78826629c1a380b70cbf304ab57a73&chksm=bc85844c8bf20d5a78ab1410dccb317f44eaf355727afaaf9937239165e233c49caa7a589a2d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)来找到一个更好的解决方法。

  

第二种方法是正确修复的方法，但是它需要许多[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[逆向](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132067&idx=3&sn=bc78826629c1a380b70cbf304ab57a73&chksm=bc85844c8bf20d5a78ab1410dccb317f44eaf355727afaaf9937239165e233c49caa7a589a2d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工程的工作。这通常是在快速修复解决方案已经部署完成后再来进行的。然而即使这是正确的方法，它仍有它自己的局限性：

  

第一，捕捉整个[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)军队的[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)是基于捕获的有限的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)样本数据集。它的作用受到捕获的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)样本的数量影响。

  

第二，如果[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)开发出了一种新的反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)规避技术，反[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127464&idx=3&sn=4b8c35715322b6881d3985a7bf0765eb&chksm=bc859a478bf21351aba0fb75e39859b004a7772a04c3d7cc2f7dc7c0e64ec570dbf7dcdadb1e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品必须要有合适的技术来处理它。

  

第三，这种方法很大程度上依赖于[逆向](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132067&idx=3&sn=bc78826629c1a380b70cbf304ab57a73&chksm=bc85844c8bf20d5a78ab1410dccb317f44eaf355727afaaf9937239165e233c49caa7a589a2d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工程。因此首要的条件是[逆向](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132067&idx=3&sn=bc78826629c1a380b70cbf304ab57a73&chksm=bc85844c8bf20d5a78ab1410dccb317f44eaf355727afaaf9937239165e233c49caa7a589a2d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工程必须能够成功。
