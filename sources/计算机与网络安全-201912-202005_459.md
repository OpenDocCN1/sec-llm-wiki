在[反病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=2&sn=a8e76542cfd1541046477370dbf884cc&chksm=bc859b348bf212226789cdafb4e418915d4d6407c8f85c9d1132a64119b0ff51f3107883a577&scene=21#wechat_redirect)专业领域，每次[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)感染都称为一个事件。每家[反病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126399&idx=2&sn=bf753a81f7b7b8d2873b4c9e041a153a&chksm=bc859e908bf2178665c154c1e9616fd47ef0e90532f4062f708d96faadc5136e0b7efcd4a213&scene=21#wechat_redirect)公司都有自己专业的[反病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=2&sn=a8e76542cfd1541046477370dbf884cc&chksm=bc859b348bf212226789cdafb4e418915d4d6407c8f85c9d1132a64119b0ff51f3107883a577&scene=21#wechat_redirect)团队，负责对世界各地的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)感染报告做出响应。他们是负责分析和创建[病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127474&idx=3&sn=3950d4440764aa903c4b36370abd0d5f&chksm=bc859a5d8bf2134b0051f67e1983e3be6593dbfa1f636d75ffa2ee3fec93b55bc76f4c29504a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)以检测和消除[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)方面的专业人才。检测和消除[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的过程称为[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)事件处理过程（MIHP）。这个处理过程比较简单，当捕获或者收集到[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)之后，需要经过以下几个处理过程：

  

● [恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)分析

● [恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[逆向工程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124804&idx=2&sn=07eeeeb7bf318aa3c3a36d2b7cb97503&chksm=bc8660ab8bf1e9bd0f229a3d88b44e33193141aa49f0c60a43b16f0dfc30292ba964ac5f6637&scene=21#wechat_redirect)

  

第一个阶段就是[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)分析，分为两个更小的阶段：静态分析和动态分析。如果静态分析阶段提取出来的信息不够，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)需再进行动态分析。如果此时仍然没办法获得所需的信息，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)就要再通过[逆向工程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132067&idx=3&sn=bc78826629c1a380b70cbf304ab57a73&chksm=bc85844c8bf20d5a78ab1410dccb317f44eaf355727afaaf9937239165e233c49caa7a589a2d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进行分析。分析的过程会产生很多信息，其中最重要的是[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，使用该[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)可以创建[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。该[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)会部署到[反病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126399&idx=2&sn=bf753a81f7b7b8d2873b4c9e041a153a&chksm=bc859e908bf2178665c154c1e9616fd47ef0e90532f4062f708d96faadc5136e0b7efcd4a213&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品中，使得[反病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=2&sn=a8e76542cfd1541046477370dbf884cc&chksm=bc859b348bf212226789cdafb4e418915d4d6407c8f85c9d1132a64119b0ff51f3107883a577&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品能够检测并移除这些[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，如图1所示。

虽然这个过程很直接，但需要付出的代价和[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的复杂程序成正比。[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)越高级、复杂，找到解决方法来处理该[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的代价就越高、时间就越长，因此[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)就会获得更多时间对系统进行破坏。为了打败[反病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126399&idx=2&sn=bf753a81f7b7b8d2873b4c9e041a153a&chksm=bc859e908bf2178665c154c1e9616fd47ef0e90532f4062f708d96faadc5136e0b7efcd4a213&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)开发者需要开发尽可能难处理的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，通常需要做以下两件事：

  

首先，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)必须能够抵抗分析，这样才不会被抽取出用于创建[特征码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124033&idx=2&sn=7e7781d415f84fa745d440fc9b96e9a9&chksm=bc8667ae8bf1eeb8d9f5690d9afdb7fd87fe2d54cbad890c8f077e4dc15ef1fefab0566a48cd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的信息。

  

其次，能够规避[反病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=2&sn=a8e76542cfd1541046477370dbf884cc&chksm=bc859b348bf212226789cdafb4e418915d4d6407c8f85c9d1132a64119b0ff51f3107883a577&scene=21#wechat_redirect)产品已推出的安全检测方案，这样才能避免被检测到或者[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)被阻止。

  

**1、恶意软件分析**

  

[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)分析是从[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)收集信息的过程，不包括[逆向工程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132067&idx=3&sn=bc78826629c1a380b70cbf304ab57a73&chksm=bc85844c8bf20d5a78ab1410dccb317f44eaf355727afaaf9937239165e233c49caa7a589a2d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。它是开发新的[反病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126399&idx=2&sn=bf753a81f7b7b8d2873b4c9e041a153a&chksm=bc859e908bf2178665c154c1e9616fd47ef0e90532f4062f708d96faadc5136e0b7efcd4a213&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术的主要驱动力之一。新的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)技术通常是在[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)分析和[逆向工程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132067&idx=3&sn=bc78826629c1a380b70cbf304ab57a73&chksm=bc85844c8bf20d5a78ab1410dccb317f44eaf355727afaaf9937239165e233c49caa7a589a2d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的过程中发现的。一旦发现这些技术，开发[反病毒](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=2&sn=a8e76542cfd1541046477370dbf884cc&chksm=bc859b348bf212226789cdafb4e418915d4d6407c8f85c9d1132a64119b0ff51f3107883a577&scene=21#wechat_redirect)解决方法就指日可待了。大部分情况下，需要的所有信息都能通过[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)分析获得，但是有些[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)相当复杂，此时就需要利用[逆向工程](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132067&idx=3&sn=bc78826629c1a380b70cbf304ab57a73&chksm=bc85844c8bf20d5a78ab1410dccb317f44eaf355727afaaf9937239165e233c49caa7a589a2d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)来处理。[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)分析可以分为两部分：

  

● 静态分析

● 动态分析

**（1）静态分析**

  

静态分析是在不运行[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的情况下提取信息的过程。[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的文件被提交给不同的静态分析工具，例如PEiD，如图2所示，这些静态分析工具专门用于从[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中提取尽可能多的信息。收集的信息可以简单如“文件类型”这种信息，也可以复杂到像“基于[代码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131576&idx=3&sn=e6f82839a1e2ae419a3882eb5e0fab36&chksm=bc858a578bf203410f36d848badab5f015dab135ca44a27abadc29e01d71576e60629954d991&scene=21#wechat_redirect)识别出的恶意特征”一样。

![](../Images/122add063ff805fb13c4bf67f2c22623.png)

图2  PEiD是常用的静态分析工具，用来从可移植的可执行文件（PE）中收集信息

  

虽然静态分析是最简单、低风险的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)分析过程，但它产生的结果的有用程度是比较低的。它是最简单的，因为分析过程中不需要任何特殊的条件，[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)只需要被简单地提交给不同的静态分析工具；它是低风险的，因为静态分析时不需要运行[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，所以在分析过程中没有被[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)感染的风险。它产生的信息的有用程度是较低的，因为只能收集到在[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)不活跃的情况下的信息。而大部分情况下，更重要的信息是在[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)运行的时候释放出来的。

  

在实践中大部分可分析[Windows](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127496&idx=3&sn=21acddfe6a98fe94ae346db72555e138&chksm=bc859a278bf21331d775fdb2224b50832a618a13d3a8fa76c41786186e846f5126daf96682bc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)文件的静态分析工具只能在[Windows](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127496&idx=3&sn=21acddfe6a98fe94ae346db72555e138&chksm=bc859a278bf21331d775fdb2224b50832a618a13d3a8fa76c41786186e846f5126daf96682bc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)平台上运行。也可以在[Linux](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133968&idx=2&sn=2454fe58d155dec7738888c75a963aeb&chksm=bc85bcff8bf235e97db0692fa805d2eeecdcaaa1e2a26f03ab5112230e873a342fcaca0bdf4f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)下通过WINE软件来运行这些工具。

  

通常会在[Linux](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133968&idx=2&sn=2454fe58d155dec7738888c75a963aeb&chksm=bc85bcff8bf235e97db0692fa805d2eeecdcaaa1e2a26f03ab5112230e873a342fcaca0bdf4f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)平台下运行静态分析工具，特别是在处理[Windows](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127496&idx=3&sn=21acddfe6a98fe94ae346db72555e138&chksm=bc859a278bf21331d775fdb2224b50832a618a13d3a8fa76c41786186e846f5126daf96682bc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的时候。即使[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)没有运行，最好也要采取一定的预防措施来防止分析机器受到感染。其中一种预防措施就是选择与[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)不同的[操作系统](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122728&idx=2&sn=6b263bf85d6b8bd8bf1d3744a491a40c&chksm=bc8668c78bf1e1d1a0e813fcd7b41c7bc300c2c98eb269b77f92854c62f1fe5127a0e01c5574&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

**（2）动态分析**

  

动态分析是在[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)运行时抽取信息的过程。不像静态分析只能分析到[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)部分的表征，动态分析可以深入了解[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的功能，因为它是在[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)执行指令的过程中进行[信息收集](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133509&idx=3&sn=5ec6d2d116de0d6e817389b5c401fc01&chksm=bc8582aa8bf20bbcec2ee9f9fefdabe5a344ac785fa549a7e4cccbaf899ed4a1ed785c307f5f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的。

**2、恶意软件逆向工程**

  

现在利用高级的[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)沙盒技术来进行自动化的动态分析，这使得[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)分析取得了很多的进步，但是仍然没有从[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中抽取所需信息的“银弹”出现。一些[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)确实“全身而退”，只暴露很少甚至零信息。虽然动态分析通过运行[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)可以进行深层次的观察，但这种观察仍然局限于在动态分析过程中[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)所执行到的功能。假如[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)有五个主要的功能来实现它的意图，但只有一个在动态分析的时候被观察到，那么分析的输出仍没办法描绘出该[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655134270&idx=2&sn=e16df482010debd92af0e2f2c1933698&chksm=bc85bfd18bf236c7e76e8a60b3c75ac34ea1edb5bc48fbb2447f36a48f0b52d4681a72b25965&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的行为。这是因为如果某种条件在动态分析的过程中没有满足，例如[恶意软件](http://mp.weixin
