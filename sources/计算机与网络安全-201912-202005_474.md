**一、范围和原则**

  

**（一）适用范围**

  

工业和信息化部主管行业范围内的工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)企业的[网络安全管理](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132272&idx=2&sn=e9dfbe410bbae373dd8c8ea4b81883ef&chksm=bc85879f8bf20e8997ddcbe1e76a53d7f8972d3a3529a40b2dc9c8654c028a6c6806842d9a56&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，适用本指南。

  

依据企业属性，工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)企业主要包括三类：

  

1\. 应用工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的工业企业（简称“联网工业企业”）， 主要涉及原材料工业、装备工业、消费品工业和电子信息制造业等行业；

  

2\. 工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)平台企业（简称“平台企业”，主要指对外提供工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)平台等[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)信息服务的企业）；

  

3\. 工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)基础设施运营企业，主要包括基础电信运营企业和标识解析系统建设运营机构。

  

本指南对联网工业企业[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127174&idx=3&sn=b0c84359114c49067f3728971b0f3233&chksm=bc859b698bf2127f130740c2ede1cf2f75fdf7c7d2dcaa4200f1055d9094c6b6d81b03b36fbc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)分级进行规范。工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)平台企业和基础设施运营企业按照《通信网络[安全防护](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132591&idx=3&sn=0e73014aa2b38745a8259c348e71680c&chksm=bc8586408bf20f564af8b79e6b9e006803065e2aa2f69af8d33554c7622c6ce7198559c23ced&scene=21#wechat_redirect)管理办法》的分级方式进行规范。

  

**（二）基本原则**

  

企业分级与行业[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126536&idx=2&sn=39c4220685f09a248d786269d734f524&chksm=bc8599e78bf210f1dc14c61a61a3df29707dc40e245ea220752d07d28b9aeb3b63631678ef3c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)影响程度相关联。以《国民经济行业分类》（GB/T 4754-2017）为基准细化联网工业企业行业类别，明确各相关行业[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655124760&idx=2&sn=96fb5d7fa9e05ea83b59cd7329fb4736&chksm=bc8660f78bf1e9e1acbd641806329c0233f135b238ef7a3395117a7aa455c2c59ffa555202eb&scene=21#wechat_redirect)影响程度，将企业所属行业[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126563&idx=3&sn=14023df84f67afb48342aae5f5fe71f7&chksm=bc8599cc8bf210da3250510eb9d50c84270e15141f1eac9d3f98a726114f5d3e51263495ecf9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)影响程度作为企业分级评定的关键参考因素。

  

行业指导与地方监管相结合。工业和信息化部对主管行业领域的工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)企业[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655120585&idx=2&sn=9622d3fd3041997ec13b8f8e385a717d&chksm=bc8671268bf1f830b295b492d4537709131dec78eb7e709d8a6b7d4bcbafd155c70245f182fb&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工作开展指导管理。地方主管部门对本行政区域工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)企业的[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131366&idx=2&sn=18215226672d73083f399efc8e61ab76&chksm=bc858b098bf2021f4ba79a264088a3761321619396e24fc817ec636744973de380bacdcd88b6&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工作开展指导监管。

  

企业自评与属地核查相结合。工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)企业根据分级评定细则开展自评，地方主管部门对企业自评结果进行核查和确认。

  

**二、企业分级**

  

**（一）联网工业企业分级**

  

联网工业企业分级主要考虑企业所属行业[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131340&idx=2&sn=f0a410fd4ac4d8564cf40c2f1284a3bf&chksm=bc858b238bf20235f42eff940d48927ae2b76820b953edb729e99bb5c9cd4d7938554025f20a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)影响程度、企业规模、企业应用工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的程度、企业发生[网络安全事件](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132557&idx=3&sn=0dab660075e0f09f21efe4a6675678e2&chksm=bc8586628bf20f74f027354e810c343911c4dc5e86f13275475aa5828ed08e375befe66c81b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的影响程度等要素。其中所属行业[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132059&idx=2&sn=59415d501c548f6795570f9b6b4f114c&chksm=bc8584748bf20d624ec9a84be5f53cef4a206290202e01409ebe0bd44d93064a41e28cbc18ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)影响程度由低到高分别划分为一类、二类和三类（见附件1）。

  

企业分级采用计分方式进行，满分为100分：

  

评分大于等于80分的，为三级企业；

  

评分大于等于60分的，且小于80分的，为二级企业；

  

评分小于60分的，为一级企业。

  

属于三类行业的规模以上联网工业企业原则上为三级。

  

**（二）多重属性企业的分级**

  

对于同时具有联网工业企业、平台企业、工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)基础设施运营企业中两种以上属性的企业，按照其业务活动涉及的不同属性分别定级。

  

**三、级别评定**

  

**（一）制定评定规则**

  

工业和信息化部选择重点行业或区域开展分类分级试点，细化联网工业企业分级评定参考规则（见附件2），形成评定规则。

  

**（二）企业自评**

  

依托工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)企业[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129537&idx=3&sn=1c8a2b85ec3a063ce7a132992d32a3be&chksm=bc85922e8bf21b3810cf89c2dba2b7041b8a93bf3df7d28e4ac4a87791862e41bf791bc21304&scene=21#wechat_redirect)分类分级管理服务平台， 联网工业企业在线填报问卷，形成自评报告。

  

**（三）核查确认**

  

地方主管部门可自行或组织第三方专业服务机构对企业提交的自评报告真实性、完整性进行核查，发现信息不真实、不完整的，通知企业予以补正后进行确认。

  

**（四）级别变更**

  

当联网工业企业[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122231&idx=3&sn=57f3acd6608aceda77cc67ed87383b3b&chksm=bc866ed88bf1e7cedfcc7786853506652034c8e32ed2e4460dcdbcbf472a787121cbc5fe2519&scene=21#wechat_redirect)风险程度发生重大变化时，应主动重新定级。

**四、安全管理**

  

省、市、县各级工业和信息化主管部门指导本行政区域内联网工业企业[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131366&idx=2&sn=18215226672d73083f399efc8e61ab76&chksm=bc858b098bf2021f4ba79a264088a3761321619396e24fc817ec636744973de380bacdcd88b6&scene=21#wechat_redirect)工作，省级通信管理局对本行政区域内平台企业、标识解析系统建设运营机构进行[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132299&idx=2&sn=63c1dcbd7f97f714da92874f310f10f0&chksm=bc8587648bf20e729f087d049ae13697e80b2db97f0f23e74f964c8478c70e1412aae7dfca73&scene=21#wechat_redirect)监督管理。地方工信和通信主管部门加强对三级工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)企业的安全监管。

  

**（一）组织管理**

  

1\. 三级工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)企业应当建立健全[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132339&idx=2&sn=de7971e30fcf3587682fbe7654d45bd9&chksm=bc85875c8bf20e4a70a5705f85b832ef159f887d807acd8fb18cc9c796a7ae3e4704564f12bd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)责任制， 设置专门[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132394&idx=2&sn=74e46284f6df2efd575e8ef7012ab5b2&chksm=bc8587058bf20e132f39918b084082d51a8db43f2318c27685dfa44b33c7844b716735df0c65&scene=21#wechat_redirect)机构和安全管理负责人，组织制定和实施[网络安全防护](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132591&idx=3&sn=0e73014aa2b38745a8259c348e71680c&chksm=bc8586408bf20f564af8b79e6b9e006803065e2aa2f69af8d33554c7622c6ce7198559c23ced&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和培训计划，加强[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131366&idx=2&sn=18215226672d73083f399efc8e61ab76&chksm=bc858b098bf2021f4ba79a264088a3761321619396e24fc817ec636744973de380bacdcd88b6&scene=21#wechat_redirect)考核，确保安全投入；

  

2\. 二级工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)企业应当明确专门的[网络安全管理](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132272&idx=2&sn=e9dfbe410bbae373dd8c8ea4b81883ef&chksm=bc85879f8bf20e8997ddcbe1e76a53d7f8972d3a3529a40b2dc9c8654c028a6c6806842d9a56&scene=21#wechat_redirect)负责人，逐步建立健全并落实[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131340&idx=2&sn=f0a410fd4ac4d8564cf40c2f1284a3bf&chksm=bc858b238bf20235f42eff940d48927ae2b76820b953edb729e99bb5c9cd4d7938554025f20a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)责任制，组织制定和实施[网络安全防护](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132591&idx=3&sn=0e73014aa2b38745a8259c348e71680c&chksm=bc8586408bf20f564af8b79e6b9e006803065e2aa2f69af8d33554c7622c6ce7198559c23ced&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和培训计划。

  

**（二）安全防护**

  

1\. 三级工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)企业应当根据工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[网络安全管理](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132299&idx=2&sn=63c1dcbd7f97f714da92874f310f10f0&chksm=bc8587648bf20e729f087d049ae13697e80b2db97f0f23e74f964c8478c70e1412aae7dfca73&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和技术防护系列标准规范要求，采取相应的技术防护措施；建设完善企业级工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)安全监测平台，并接入省级以上工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)安全监测平台。省级以上工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)安全监测平台定期向三级企业通报安全风险。

  

2\. 二级工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)企业应当根据工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[网络安全管理](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132339&idx=2&sn=de7971e30fcf3587682fbe7654d45bd9&chksm=bc85875c8bf20e4a70a5705f85b832ef159f887d807acd8fb18cc9c796a7ae3e4704564f12bd&scene=21#wechat_redirect)和技术防护系列标准规范要求，采取相应的技术防护措施；积极建设企业级工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)安全监测平台，并与省级工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)安全监测平台对接。

  

**（三）风险评估**

  

1\. 三级工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)企业应当至少每年进行一次[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127174&idx=3&sn=b0c84359114c49067f3728971b0f3233&chksm=bc859b698bf2127f130740c2ede1cf2f75fdf7c7d2dcaa4200f1055d9094c6b6d81b03b36fbc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)风险评估与审计。

  

2\. 二级工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)企业应当至少每两年进行一次[网络安全风险评估](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655122231&idx=3&sn=57f3acd6608aceda77cc67ed87383b3b&chksm=bc866ed88bf1e7cedfcc7786853506652034c8e32ed2e4460dcdbcbf472a787121cbc5fe2519&scene=21#wechat_redirect)与审计。

  

**（四）应急管理**

  

工业[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)企业应当制定切实可行的应急预案，建立应急响应机制，定期开展应急演练，采取必要措施消除安全隐患。发现重大[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655133386&idx=3&sn=b451bec2855049219318a74e2cb60c80&chksm=bc8583258bf20a3307f968c0062801aab8212bed6a118c1b8c1b4cd3b4ba52d8296b59aa1c5e&scene=21#wechat_redirect)风险和安全事件应当及时上报。
