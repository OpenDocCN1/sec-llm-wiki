=&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的网络组件，[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)就变得一无是处。

  

处理[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的网络组件的第一步是理解和识别出它是什么，然后利用这个信息来拆解它。为了完成这个目标，需要采取下面的行动：

  

● sinkhole

● 拆解

  

**1）sinkhole**

  

控制[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的网络资源可以帮助研究人员更好地理解[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的通信和网络行为。这是通过sinkholing技术实现的。

  

sinkholing是一个掌控[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)资源的过程，特别是它的C＆C，通过劫持[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的域名从而连接上C＆C。由于这些域名已经被注册了，所以研究人员可以截获[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)发往网络资源的信息。换句话说，研究人员接管了[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的操作。他们能够截获来自[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的信息，并发布命令来控制[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。两者之间的唯一区别就是意图，此时，已经变成了一种好奇心，希望能够找到停止[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的解决方案。

  

通过sinkholing技术，研究人员可以得到以下信息：

  

● [僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的规模

● 受感染系统的位置

● bot发送的是什么信息

  

最后一项内容存在争议，因为研究人员能访问bot所发送的所有窃取的信息，而这些有可能是私有信息，所以可能导致道德和[隐私](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127519&idx=3&sn=a846e840b4ac021e01ba4a64c9228817&chksm=bc859a308bf2132693545d19fdb92d481e63a9d92d18960c1e4c198747b628d2bad763105175&scene=21#wechat_redirect)侵犯。

  

**2）拆解**

  

sinkholing帮助分析和理解[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的网络通信。它也有助于与[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)组件分析结果的结合以及识别[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)背后的网络基础设施。并且一旦网络资源被识别出来，下一步就是把它们拆解掉。

  

[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的拆解是指拆解掉[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)资源或者使支持、提供[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)网络资源的网络服务账户或基础设施不可用。拆解一个网络资源可以很简单也可以很困难，这依赖于网络资源的类型。例如，使用一个Facebook账户作为它的感染途径的[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，那么只要通过向Facebook报告违规账户就可以轻松地拆解掉。用来控制[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的Twitter账户也很容易拆解。尽管这些很容易拆解，但使用防弹[主机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655121891&idx=3&sn=b561a81bf303f985ec557f80f563b467&chksm=bc866c0c8bf1e51a40e42cee625ef8e4eb144cfb899422ed59749dd1f708a46ee54fdd083337&scene=21#wechat_redirect)提供商提供的网络资源的[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)则很难拆解。

  

拆解经常是通过与执法部门、网络服务提供商、工业和学术专家以及全世界的政府机构合作。比如，Rustock[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的拆解是由微软数字犯罪组织发起，并获得了上述组织和机构的帮助。

**2、法律战线**

  

另一个发起反对[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的战争是在法律战线上。拆解技术组件明显是不够的。[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)背后的人可以轻易地创建一个新的[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)并获利，甚至不用关心前面拆解掉的[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

在法律战线上对抗[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)可以使用以下形式：

● 协助执法部门工作人员。

● 引进反[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)或反[网络犯罪](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126931&idx=3&sn=a58896bfaaeffc97770ba2f995293d33&chksm=bc85987c8bf2116aff0433dc4c949b126d04cb80cf809250bf2255b3bad23aae7ee3d6350fac&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的相关法律。

  

**（1）法律补救措施**

  

[僵尸网络](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116893&idx=3&sn=95ddf36012824dc77900667e4746d03d&chksm=bc8643b28bf1caa4d4b45525e90de1e98d227b888ef6022ccab3494d2262e538faf54a650e50&scene=21#wechat_redirect)背后的人必须为他们的行为负责，所以要防止他们再次犯罪，并将其作为其他仍逍遥法外的网络罪犯的例子。一个政党可以采取的法律补救措施是向罪犯提起刑事诉讼，就像微软对Rustock操作者所做的，如图1所示。

**（2）协助执法部门的工作人员**

**（3）反网络犯罪的立法**
