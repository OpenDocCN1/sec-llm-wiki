**个人信息依旧在网上“裸奔”**

  

[互联网时代](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130709&idx=1&sn=e99f6f3aec09521994925ae41a1d9bcd&chksm=bc8589ba8bf200ac93cb0e32c16eb192c2cf1f016463455c561d9e8cbeb44b3f78310756880f&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，网络上遗留的[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，如发表评论、网上求职、购物记录、填写测试问卷等，以及随意丢弃的纸质单据，如火车票、售后服务单、快递单、证件复印件等，都容易被不法分子搜集利用。部分不法分子还假借“促销”“中奖”“会员填表”等名义，诱使用户填写[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

不仅如此，聊天记录、消费记录、出行住宿、行车线路等[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)被各种服务类[App](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130459&idx=3&sn=f8b64d61307c28f9f3265b4eb1eda12a&chksm=bc858eb48bf207a2b2fd53128cd92797950f71b4e141abfb9c60970b6a662bc1849e85912dff&scene=21#wechat_redirect)掌握，过度索权、[隐私](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127519&idx=3&sn=a846e840b4ac021e01ba4a64c9228817&chksm=bc859a308bf2132693545d19fdb92d481e63a9d92d18960c1e4c198747b628d2bad763105175&scene=21#wechat_redirect)条款不合规、强制授权、超范围收集[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)等[App](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130459&idx=3&sn=f8b64d61307c28f9f3265b4eb1eda12a&chksm=bc858eb48bf207a2b2fd53128cd92797950f71b4e141abfb9c60970b6a662bc1849e85912dff&scene=21#wechat_redirect)违法违规收集使用[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)问题依然突出。

  

为解决这一问题，今年1月25日，中央网信办、工信部、公安部、市场监管总局四部门联合发布《关于开展[App](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130459&idx=3&sn=f8b64d61307c28f9f3265b4eb1eda12a&chksm=bc858eb48bf207a2b2fd53128cd92797950f71b4e141abfb9c60970b6a662bc1849e85912dff&scene=21#wechat_redirect)违法违规收集使用[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)专项治理的公告》，成立了[App](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130459&idx=3&sn=f8b64d61307c28f9f3265b4eb1eda12a&chksm=bc858eb48bf207a2b2fd53128cd92797950f71b4e141abfb9c60970b6a662bc1849e85912dff&scene=21#wechat_redirect)违法违规收集使用[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)专项治理工作组。

  

据统计，我国境内应用商店数量已超过200家，上架应用近500万款，下载总量超过万亿次。中央网信办[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132059&idx=2&sn=59415d501c548f6795570f9b6b4f114c&chksm=bc8584748bf20d624ec9a84be5f53cef4a206290202e01409ebe0bd44d93064a41e28cbc18ca&scene=21#wechat_redirect)协调局相关负责人称，至今，针对[App](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130459&idx=3&sn=f8b64d61307c28f9f3265b4eb1eda12a&chksm=bc858eb48bf207a2b2fd53128cd92797950f71b4e141abfb9c60970b6a662bc1849e85912dff&scene=21#wechat_redirect)专项治理的举报平台已收到8000多条举报信息，其中实名举报占到近三分之一；将400余款下载量大、用户常用[App](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130459&idx=3&sn=f8b64d61307c28f9f3265b4eb1eda12a&chksm=bc858eb48bf207a2b2fd53128cd92797950f71b4e141abfb9c60970b6a662bc1849e85912dff&scene=21#wechat_redirect)纳入了评估，向100多家[App](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130459&idx=3&sn=f8b64d61307c28f9f3265b4eb1eda12a&chksm=bc858eb48bf207a2b2fd53128cd92797950f71b4e141abfb9c60970b6a662bc1849e85912dff&scene=21#wechat_redirect)运营企业发送了整改建议函。

  

[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)与[隐私](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127519&idx=3&sn=a846e840b4ac021e01ba4a64c9228817&chksm=bc859a308bf2132693545d19fdb92d481e63a9d92d18960c1e4c198747b628d2bad763105175&scene=21#wechat_redirect)泄露事件频发，与之相关的电信和[网络犯罪](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126931&idx=3&sn=a58896bfaaeffc97770ba2f995293d33&chksm=bc85987c8bf2116aff0433dc4c949b126d04cb80cf809250bf2255b3bad23aae7ee3d6350fac&scene=21#wechat_redirect)行为也成为社会的一大顽疾。据《2018年网民[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655129537&idx=3&sn=1c8a2b85ec3a063ce7a132992d32a3be&chksm=bc85922e8bf21b3810cf89c2dba2b7041b8a93bf3df7d28e4ac4a87791862e41bf791bc21304&scene=21#wechat_redirect)感满意度调查报告》显示，过半网民认为在购物、社交聊天时，个人[信息泄露](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127361&idx=3&sn=4c76bb9efc3b43bd56fbff3d96a594a3&chksm=bc859aae8bf213b88ea93d3438b436317a1aabf7b69bb53366f148ae9ac7f9c613365268779d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)风险更大；近四成网民认为手机[App](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130459&idx=3&sn=f8b64d61307c28f9f3265b4eb1eda12a&chksm=bc858eb48bf207a2b2fd53128cd92797950f71b4e141abfb9c60970b6a662bc1849e85912dff&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、搜索信息对[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保护不够安全。

  

公安部第一研究所专家表示，采集、存储和管理[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)及[隐私](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127519&idx=3&sn=a846e840b4ac021e01ba4a64c9228817&chksm=bc859a308bf2132693545d19fdb92d481e63a9d92d18960c1e4c198747b628d2bad763105175&scene=21#wechat_redirect)数据，并非相关企业开展[互联网](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655130656&idx=3&sn=5e24d5c1f45b3a4850ac2ff4623bbadf&chksm=bc8589cf8bf200d9034d12651182a30456e90e39577dbcfa6285e8755cccadc72fb2518960b7&scene=21#wechat_redirect)业务的必要前提，却成了[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)犯罪难以遏止的“泛滥洪水”之源。

  

比如商家对收集来的用户信息进行[大数据](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128534&idx=3&sn=e679f67ac39ab11dbf5f8cae74032564&chksm=bc8596398bf21f2ffb6d133b630409ede8176bcaaa3d12565e956b45bfd287978ab050e9230d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)分析，画出用户画像，再进行精准网络营销，就可以给每个用户推荐特定新闻、购物和服务信息。还有一些公司，或者被动地被网络[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127259&idx=3&sn=c82f59076adf4123590301c9577f3c59&chksm=bc859b348bf21222ef281bc9aebb654a06582273fd7d9eb73ab84b56adbf383f74305efe3c3c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)[攻击](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132417&idx=3&sn=47c1d21230bf18b89e7b587c912c9f4b&chksm=bc8586ee8bf20ff871fd6248ddaed9ef07d50c752ef7e9b1b60916bf673c1ff4b0c578202c15&scene=21#wechat_redirect)后泄露信息，或者主动地将用户信息加工后转卖，成为推销电话或[短信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131436&idx=3&sn=1f2181a408e8114cc1be4914a60503e9&chksm=bc858ac38bf203d54938a44c7d1bc03e63b1c674f583b59d9bf65d0900ac0d31ceb0886b591d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、[诈骗电话](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655116904&idx=2&sn=e6593304282eb2f54df80c4b3d1b32e3&chksm=bc8643878bf1ca91be9ce2bcb8eea58c79d1a081ad4be7fa65d50584a68b36d3dce4dd830a56&scene=21#wechat_redirect)、垃圾邮件的“工作对象”。

**生物识别信息泄露“暗箭难防”**

  

随着窃取[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)手段的技术不断发展，个人生物识别信息逐渐成为[信息泄露](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127361&idx=3&sn=4c76bb9efc3b43bd56fbff3d96a594a3&chksm=bc859aae8bf213b88ea93d3438b436317a1aabf7b69bb53366f148ae9ac7f9c613365268779d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的新内容。

  

个人生物识别信息是指直接采集于人体，体现人的生理特征，与个人身份识别具有唯一对应性的[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，如指纹、虹膜、面部特征等。目前， 该技术已被广泛运用到如居民身份证、手机、安全门禁、零售店支付以及银行等领域。

  

近日，[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127174&idx=3&sn=b0c84359114c49067f3728971b0f3233&chksm=bc859b698bf2127f130740c2ede1cf2f75fdf7c7d2dcaa4200f1055d9094c6b6d81b03b36fbc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)专家称拍照比“剪刀手”可能泄露指纹，在网上引起热议。该消息称，基本上1.5米内拍摄的“剪刀手”[照片](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128912&idx=3&sn=ba2c0b428e17d9fec29608d96e86984d&chksm=bc8590bf8bf219a9fdc650848d21a1b5f4facbf61cd091ebc242a3b9f38d3dbe62290b7fe129&scene=21#wechat_redirect)能100%还原出被摄者的指纹，1.5米至3米内拍摄的[照片](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128912&idx=3&sn=ba2c0b428e17d9fec29608d96e86984d&chksm=bc8590bf8bf219a9fdc650848d21a1b5f4facbf61cd091ebc242a3b9f38d3dbe62290b7fe129&scene=21#wechat_redirect)能还原出50%的指纹，超过3米拍摄的[照片](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128912&idx=3&sn=ba2c0b428e17d9fec29608d96e86984d&chksm=bc8590bf8bf219a9fdc650848d21a1b5f4facbf61cd091ebc242a3b9f38d3dbe62290b7fe129&scene=21#wechat_redirect)才难以提取其中的指纹。

  

据了解，早在2015年，欧洲的[黑客](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127290&idx=3&sn=04aaac7d1e20fd446929032cb45b6b38&chksm=bc859b158bf212032e5e2121f6066cd1ec171afc141be62e32ad671879a403ade7b3435495bb&scene=21#wechat_redirect)联盟“Chaos[计算机](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131503&idx=3&sn=f62412891c2b54c5d0605f31efac2aee&chksm=bc858a808bf203967f5e9c9c06b6f2195c5ec361d6850bd333a77a6d4cc8da794a161b9470fc&scene=21#wechat_redirect)俱乐部”就表示，只要使用“相机拍摄的标准[照片](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128912&idx=3&sn=ba2c0b428e17d9fec29608d96e86984d&chksm=bc8590bf8bf219a9fdc650848d21a1b5f4facbf61cd091ebc242a3b9f38d3dbe62290b7fe129&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”，就可以获得某人的指纹；2017年，日本一家研究所也表示，将强光集中在[照片](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128912&idx=3&sn=ba2c0b428e17d9fec29608d96e86984d&chksm=bc8590bf8bf219a9fdc650848d21a1b5f4facbf61cd091ebc242a3b9f38d3dbe62290b7fe129&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中人像的指纹，指纹数据就可以再现。

除指纹信息外，被拍摄者的虹膜信息也可能成为不法分子在[照片](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128912&idx=3&sn=ba2c0b428e17d9fec29608d96e86984d&chksm=bc8590bf8bf219a9fdc650848d21a1b5f4facbf61cd091ebc242a3b9f38d3dbe62290b7fe129&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中的提取对象。1985年，[美国](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128142&idx=3&sn=7b8de69bbda3be653df725fdec9fc24a&chksm=bc8597a18bf21eb7f39f365987d171669e5868d0a43659b4fa35b9053cfc346b30c80e7c5e8a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)摄影家史蒂夫·麦凯瑞拍摄的一张“阿富汗少女”照登上《国家地理》杂志封面，十多年后，为了寻找当年的那个神秘少女，通过提取比对当时[照片](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128912&idx=3&sn=ba2c0b428e17d9fec29608d96e86984d&chksm=bc8590bf8bf219a9fdc650848d21a1b5f4facbf61cd091ebc242a3b9f38d3dbe62290b7fe129&scene=21#wechat_redirect)中阿富汗少女的虹膜信息，麦凯瑞竟成功找到了当事人。

  

那是30多年前的相机拍摄的[照片](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128912&idx=3&sn=ba2c0b428e17d9fec29608d96e86984d&chksm=bc8590bf8bf219a9fdc650848d21a1b5f4facbf61cd091ebc242a3b9f38d3dbe62290b7fe129&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，已经能够清楚分离出虹膜信息了，以现在摄影器材的能力，想要获取某个人的虹膜信息并非难事。

  

无独有偶，近日，一则“小学生发现刷脸取件[漏洞](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655131786&idx=3&sn=215ae89e5806b5a1c89f7ac0a1bfc798&chksm=bc8585658bf20c7340c356fc4acfe8a757f76f65e98e431c561457a3dee2bcca64d4179e27a5&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”的消息引发网民热议。有媒体报道，多位小学生通过打印取件人[照片](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128912&idx=3&sn=ba2c0b428e17d9fec29608d96e86984d&chksm=bc8590bf8bf219a9fdc650848d21a1b5f4facbf61cd091ebc242a3b9f38d3dbe62290b7fe129&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的方式，用照片“刷脸”就轻而易举地打开快递柜取件。

  

有媒体也对智能快递柜“刷脸取件”做了实验，结果发现：用[照片](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128912&idx=3&sn=ba2c0b428e17d9fec29608d96e86984d&chksm=bc8590bf8bf219a9fdc650848d21a1b5f4facbf61cd091ebc242a3b9f38d3dbe62290b7fe129&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，一秒钟时间识别成功，连续试了5次，其中4次成功打开，1次失败是因为[照片](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128912&idx=3&sn=ba2c0b428e17d9fec29608d96e86984d&chksm=bc8590bf8bf219a9fdc650848d21a1b5f4facbf61cd091ebc242a3b9f38d3dbe62290b7fe129&scene=21#wechat_redirect)没有拿稳。而后，把正脸自拍照换成[偷拍](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127790&idx=3&sn=62a5ac394be74c4bec137904ff6082c0&chksm=bc8595018bf21c172946998a5ba5b6a55c949786779346d17ce973b64abb4bca540f7e16f154&scene=21#wechat_redirect)的[照片](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655128912&idx=3&sn=ba2c0b428e17d9fec29608d96e86984d&chksm=bc8590bf8bf219a9fdc650848d21a1b5f4facbf61cd091ebc242a3b9f38d3dbe62290b7fe129&scene=21#wechat_redirect)进行测试，快递柜又被打开了。

  

仅就技术本身而言，生物识别具有防伪性能好、私密性强、随身“携带”等优点，是一种更安全的技术。但是所有的生物识别技术本质上与数字[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)一样，具有可复制的特性，因此生物[密码](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132761&idx=3&sn=f92cd4f0624a6fb17cdad04596f92154&chksm=bc8581b68bf208a0d0d807e1aff38dd630003803a07db514e78f17d8fa46d903b46fa8da9fea&scene=21#wechat_redirect)不适合单独使用。目前生物识别和[身份认证](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132934&idx=3&sn=c223647cd49215a6e61ad18746b73bf2&chksm=bc8580e98bf209ff5e0c0eb5672251fcc8b8a3502c9afccc890b879965be804c96c98c785ba0&scene=21#wechat_redirect)商用产品方案不仅仅依赖于静态的指纹信息、人脸图像，还应附加活体检测技术、多因子认证技术或基于风控的隐式认证技术，来保证“我就是我”。

  

个人生物识别[信息泄露](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127361&idx=3&sn=4c76bb9efc3b43bd56fbff3d96a594a3&chksm=bc859aae8bf213b88ea93d3438b436317a1aabf7b69bb53366f148ae9ac7f9c613365268779d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)后，还有可能被伪造甚至破解高级[安全防护](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655132591&idx=3&sn=0e73014aa2b38745a8259c348e71680c&chksm=bc8586408bf20f564af8b79e6b9e006803065e2aa2f69af8d33554c7622c6ce7198559c23ced&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。人脸、指纹、声纹的伪造，比如，[微信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127698&idx=3&sn=5101f6a80628d7caf6bc8ed7eec325c5&chksm=bc85957d8bf21c6bad5b83f1dcf2124043fa2e6a19eee9f604e23db0211b7ddcf8d94ecf97b8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)验证可以用声纹验证，如若通过录音来获取用户声音后，可以轻松打开[微信](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127698&idx=3&sn=5101f6a80628d7caf6bc8ed7eec325c5&chksm=bc85957d8bf21c6bad5b83f1dcf2124043fa2e6a19eee9f604e23db0211b7ddcf8d94ecf97b8&scene=21#wechat_redirect)。

  

**为个人信息穿上“安全服”**

  

个人[信息泄露](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127361&idx=3&sn=4c76bb9efc3b43bd56fbff3d96a594a3&chksm=bc859aae8bf213b88ea93d3438b436317a1aabf7b69bb53366f148ae9ac7f9c613365268779d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)背后，是我国个人数据保护立法尚不完善。公安部第三研究所相关专家认为，2017年[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保护虽已写入[《网络安全法》](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655113238&idx=4&sn=7a6aeba5889cedfd8cd21ca949ce6dbb&chksm=bc864df98bf1c4efdc987d135c7571718de3351fdd3c22ba266e90e0f2162ee811ee420143f4&scene=21#wechat_redirect)，个人数据保护力度大大提升，但仍存在立法分散、可操作性不强等问题。专家建议，需尽快推进专门的[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保护法规的制定和出台，为[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)提供系统性、体系化的保护。

  

据《2019全国网民[网络安全](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126536&idx=2&sn=39c4220685f09a248d786269d734f524&chksm=bc8599e78bf210f1dc14c61a61a3df29707dc40e245ea220752d07d28b9aeb3b63631678ef3c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)感满意度调查统计报告》显示，37.4%网民认为网络个人[信息泄露](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655127361&idx=3&sn=4c76bb9efc3b43bd56fbff3d96a594a3&chksm=bc859aae8bf213b88ea93d3438b436317a1aabf7b69bb53366f148ae9ac7f9c613365268779d&scene=21#wechat_redirect)非常多和比较多，58.75%的公众网民表示曾遇到[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)被侵犯。

  

报告称，网民要求加强法治建设，[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保护和网络平台责任为亟待加强立法的内容，其中82.61%的网民要求对[个人信息](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5OTk4MDE2MA==&mid=2655126737&idx=3&sn=b13b1ac36f244fb36367f7666640d20d&chksm=bc85993e8bf21028fab32c6e20bd0945706c3a409905ba246f9a6befb131a6666bfc9c256772&scene=21#wechat_redirect)保护立法，对网络平台责任的立法要求是73.36%。
