**第四届智能家居亚洲峰会暨精品展(Smart Home Asia 2020 ) 将于2020年7月30-31日正式召开** 尊敬的各位领导、各位嘉宾： 原定于2020年2月13-14日在上海举办的“第四届智能家居亚洲峰会暨精品展(Smart Home Asia 2020)”将延期至2020年7月30-31日举办。 做此决定前，智能家居组委会进行了严格的多方评估。与受邀嘉宾进行了密切的沟通，听取了受邀嘉宾的宝贵意见，同时仔细参考了国家和地方对于新冠疫情防控方面的政策和指导意见。  
5月8日，《国务院应对新型冠状病毒感染肺炎疫情联防联控机制关于做好新冠炎疫情常态化防控工作的指导意见》发布，指出“在落实防控措施前提下，全面开放商场、超市、宾馆、餐馆等生活场所；采取预约、限流等方式，开放公园、旅游景点、运动场所，图书馆、博物馆、美术馆等室内场馆，以及影剧院、游艺厅等密闭式娱乐休闲场所，可举办各类必要的会议、会展活动等。”。自5月9日零时起，上海市重大突发公共卫生事件应急响应级别由二级响应调整为三级响应。 我们非常有信心会给大家带来一场安全的、富有成效的行业盛会。我们将持续密切关注疫情防控情况，并与酒店等各方保持密切沟通。同时我们也会与各位演讲嘉宾、与会人员及合作伙伴保持及时沟通，竭诚做好后续服务。如您对于本次会议有任何意见或建议，可以随时联系我们。 因会议延期给您带来的不便，我们深表歉意。在过去4年里，Smart Home Asia与业内嘉宾一起经历了行业的风雨，见证了行业的蓬勃发展。在大家的连续支持下，该活动逐渐成为行业专家年度聚会，被称为行业风向标活动。但在抗争疫情的关键时刻，我们更关心大家的生命安全，给您带来的不便，敬请理解。  相信我们一定能战胜这次疫情，工作和生活都会重新回到正轨。 感谢大家的理解和支持！                                        第四届智能家居亚洲峰会组委会 上海探偲商务信息咨询有限公司                                        2020年5月18日  

  

**Notice of Postponement: 4th Smart Home Asia Summit & Mini Expo 2020 Will be Opened on July 30-31, 2020**

 

Dear distinguished leaders, attendees,

 

The 4th Smart Home Asia Summit & Mini Expo 2020(Smart Home Asia 2020) will be postponed to July 30-31, 2020, originally scheduled to be held in Shanghai on February13-14, 2020.

 

The Organizing Committee of Smart Home Asia had conducted rigorous evaluations before making this decision. We had close communication with the invited guests and took their valuable advices. At the same time, we carefully referred to the national and local policies and guidelines on covid-19 revention and control.

  

On 8th May,The Joint Prevention and Control Mechanism of the State Council issued a guideline on regular prevention and control of COVID-19, says “With strict prevention measures in place, shopping malls, supermarkets, hotels and restaurants should be all opened, reservations will be used for parks and tourism scenic spots, and indoor areas and closed entertainment places, such as libraries and cinemas will be allowed to hold conferences and exhibitions if necessary. ”According to relevant national laws and regulations and the“Special Emergency Plan for Emergent Public Health Incidents in Shanghai”, the Shanghai Municipal Government decided to adjust the emergency response level of major public health emergencies in Shanghai from second-level response to third-level response from 0:00 on May 9.

 

We’ are very confident to bring a safe and efficient smart home event for you. We will continue paying close attention to the prevention and control of the epidemic and maintaining close and timely communications with the hotel, speakers, sponsors, delegates,and other parties. We sincerely at your follow-up service. Have you any suggestions or advice, please be free to contact us.

  

We apologize for any inconvenience caused by the postponement of the event.And thank you for your support and participation over the past 4 years to help Smart Home Asia become the most leading smart home industry event in China.However, at this critical moment we are primarily concerned with your health and safety. Thank you for your understanding. 

 

Believe we will overcome the epidemic soon. Both work and life will be back on track. Thanks again for your kindly understanding and support!

                                        

Organizing Committee of Smart Home Asia 2020

 Shanghai Taaslabs Business Consulting Co., LTD

                                          May 18th, 2020
