前言

UNECE R155是针对整车OEM的汽车网络安全准入标准：

  • 2022年7月起，UNECE成员国所有上市新车型，都要满足该要求

  • 2024年7月起，UNECE成员国所有在售车型，都要满足该要求

整车OEM为满足R155要求，会将该要求传递给整个供应链。本文结合ISO/SAE 21434，对供应商如何满足R155的要求，给出建议。

 

1.  汽车供应链和适用标准

1.1  汽车网络安全行业标准

ECE R155(Proposal for a new UN Regulation on uniform provisions concerning the approval of vehicles with regards to cyber security and cyber security management system)是UNECE对整车网络安全方面的准入强制标准。主要内容包括对CSMS（Cyber Security Management System）的要求、以及对vehicle types的要求。


ISO/SAE 21434 (Road Vehicles Cybersecurity Engineering) 是ISO/SAE在2021年8月发布的车辆网络信息安全国际标准。主要内容包括：网络安全管理、分布式开发、持续网络安全活动、概念阶段和产品开发阶段、生产运维、直到产品报废各个阶段的要求。

 

Automotive SPICE® for Cybersecurity是VDA QMC发布的车辆网络信息安全相关的过程模型，是对ASPICE PAM V3.1模型的补充。新增的过程包括：ACQ.2 supplier request and selection, SEC.1 -SEC.4 cybersecurity engineering process, SUP.7 cybersecurity risk management


1.2  如何选择适用的标准

R155是针对OEM的强制要求，但R155中的要求比较抽象，项目实施过程中，需要参考ISO/SAE 21434。


OEM为了获得整车准入资质，会把R155中的相关要求，传递给下游的tier 1和tier2供应商，比较难进一步传递给tier 2以下的供应商。

1.3  供应商如何满足OEM传递的R155中关于CSMS流程体系的要求

UNECE R155条款7.2.2.4要求：车辆制造商应该展示其对供应商按照7.2.2.2的要求进行管理。也就是说供应商要满足R155条款7.2.2.2中关于CSMS体系的要求。


接下来看下R155 7.2.2.2中的要求，讨论下如何参考ISO/SAE 21434。

 

1.3.1  建立组织级网络安全管理流程

UNECE R155标准要求：

7.2.2.2 (a) The processes used within the organization to manage cyber security；


ISO/SAE 21434标准中对应内容：

[RQ-05-01] The organization shall define a cybersecurity policy that includes:

a) acknowledgement of road vehicle cybersecurity risks; and

b) the executive management’s commitment to manage the corresponding cybersecurity risks.

 

[RQ-05-02] The organization shall establish and maintain rules and processes to:

a) enable the implementation of the requirements of this document; and

b) support the execution of the corresponding activities.

 

[RQ-05-03] The organization shall assign and communicate the responsibilities and corresponding organizational authority to achieve and maintain cybersecurity.

 

[RQ-05-06] The organization shall foster and maintain a strong cybersecurity culture.

 

[RQ-05-07] The organization shall ensure that persons to which cybersecurity roles and responsibilities are assigned have the competences and awareness to fulfil these.

 

[RQ-05-08] The organization shall institute and maintain a continuous improvement process.

 

【实施建议-1】建立公司网络安全方针，成立网络安全委员会，进行人员任命和授权。公司管理层定期评审网络安全事务。

 

【实施建议-2】参考ISO21434附录B中给出的好的安全文化示例，建立公司网络安全文化，并细化网络安全文化可落地的措施。

 

【实施建议-3】建立公司网络安全流程体系，包括网络安全流程描述、作业指导书、工作产出物模板、检查单。包括网络安全管理、分布式开发、持续网络安全活动、概念阶段和产品开发阶段、生产运维、直到产品报废各个阶段的流程。


【实施建议-4】组建组织级网络安全团队，明确角色职责和授权；增加项目网络安全角色职责，任命项目网络经理，并给予充分的授权。

根据每个角色的职责，确定相应的能力要求。

人员的能力要求，可以从教育背景、专业技能和经验、网络安全项目经验、人员资质等方面考虑。

 

【实施建议-5】建立持续改进流程，收集流程使用中的反馈意见、流程内审和外审的建议等，对建立的网络安全流程持续优化。


  • 更多内容，参考：如何基于ISO26262导入ISO21434：总体安全管理篇

 

1.3.2  建立威胁分析和风险评估流程

UNECE R155标准要求：

7.2.2.2 (b) The processes used for the identification of risks to vehicle types;

7.2.2.2 (c) The processes used for the assessment, categorization and treatment of the risks identified;

 

ISO/SAE 21434标准中对应内容：

[RQ-15-01] Damage scenarios shall be identified.

 

[RQ-15-02] Assets with cybersecurity properties whose compromise leads to a damage scenario shall be identified.

 

[RQ-15-03] Threat scenarios shall be identified.

 

[RQ-15-04] The damage scenarios shall be assessed again stpotential adverse consequences for road users in the impact categories of safety, financial, operational, and privacy (S, F, O, P) respectively.

 

[RQ-15-05] The impact rating of a damage scenario shall be determined for each impact.

 

[RQ-15-08] The threat scenarios shall be analysed to identify attack paths.

 

[RQ-15-09] An attack path shall be associated with the threat scenarios that can be realized by the attack path.

[RQ-15-10] For each attack path, the attack feasibility rating shall be determined.


[RQ-15-15] For each threat scenario the risk value shall be determined from the impact of the associated damage scenarios and the attack feasibility of the associated attack paths.

 

[RQ-15-17] For each threat scenario, considering its risk values, one or more of the risk treatment options shall be determined.

 

[RQ-09-05] If the risk treatment decision for a threat scenario includes reducing the risk, then one or more corresponding cybersecurity goals shall be specified.

 

[RQ-09-06] If the risk treatment decision for a threat scenario includes:

a) sharing the risk; or

b) retaining the risk due to one or more assumptions used,

then one or more corresponding cybersecurity claims shall be specified.

 

【实施建议】制定TARA流程，包括资产识别、威胁场景分析、影响分析、攻击路径分析、攻击可行性分析、风险评级、风险处置措施。


  • 具体案例，参考：汽车信息安全 TARA 分析方法实例简介

 

1.3.3  建立网络安全风险管理流程

UNECE R155标准要求：

7.2.2.2 (d) The processes in place to verify that the risks identified are appropriately managed;


ISO/SAE 21434标准（DIS版本）中对应内容：

[RQ-05-10] Cybersecurity risk management shall be in accordance with ISO 31000.

[PM-05-01] The organization may align its cybersecurity risk management and its corporate risk management.

说明：ISO/SAE 21434正式版本中，删除了如上两个条款。

 

【实施建议】对TARA分析过程中识别出的风险，制定风险管理流程，进行管理。

 

1.3.4 建立网络安全测试流程

UNECE R155标准要求：

7.2.2.2 (e) The processes used for testing the cyber security of a vehicle type;


ISO/SAE 21434标准中对应内容：

[RQ-10-09] Integration and verification activities shall verify that the implementation and integration of components fulfil the defined cybersecurity specifications.

 

[RC-10-12] Testing should be performed in order to confirm that unidentified weaknesses and vulnerabilities remaining in the component are minimized.

 

【实施建议】制定网络安全测试流程，制定网络测试策略、测试规范、测试报告的模板。

  • 测试方法，包括：requirement-based test, interface test, resource usage evaluation, verification of control flow and data flow, dynamic analysis, andstatic analysis.

  • 测试用例设计方法，包括：analysis of requirements, generation and analysis of equivalence classes, boundary values analysis, and error guessing based on knowledge or experience.

  • 测试覆盖度，比如软件单元的分支覆盖率达到100%


除了以上要求，为了确认产品中残余的weaknesses and vulnerabilities最少。还需要进行功能测试、渗透测试、漏洞扫描和模糊测试。

1.3.5  建立网络安全持续监控流程

UNECE R155标准要求：

7.2.2.2 (f) The processes used for ensuring that the risk assessment is kept current;

7.2.2.2 (g) The processes used to monitor for, detect and respond to cyber-attacks, cyber threats and vulnerabilities on vehicle types and the processes used to assess whether the cyber security measures implemented are still effective in the light of new cyber threats and vulnerabilities that have been identified;

7.2.2.2. (h) The processes used to provide relevant data to support analysis of attempted or successful cyber-attacks.

 

ISO/SAE 21434标准中对应内容：

[RQ-06-09] The work products identified in the cybersecurity plan shall be updated and maintained for accuracy until and at the release for post-development.

 

[RQ-08-01] Sources shall be selected for collection of cybersecurity information.

 

[RQ-08-02] Triggers shall be defined and maintained for the triage of cybersecurity information.


[RQ-08-03] Cybersecurity information shall be collected and triaged to determine if the cybersecurity information becomes one or more cybersecurity events.

 

[RQ-08-04] A cybersecurity event shall be evaluated to identify weaknesses in an item and/or component.

 

[RQ-08-05] Weaknesses shall be analyzed to identify vulnerabilities.

 

[RQ-08-07] Vulnerabilities shall be managed such that for each vulnerability:

a) the corresponding cybersecurity risks are assessed and treated in accordance with 15.9 such that no unreasonable risks remain; or

b) the vulnerability is eliminated by applying an available remediation independent of a TARA.

 

[RQ-07-06] If there is an identified vulnerability to be managed in accordance with [RQ-08-07], the customer and supplier shall agree on actions and responsibility for those actions.

 

[RQ-13-01] For each cybersecurity incident, a cybersecurity incident response plan shall be created.

 

[RQ-13-02] The cybersecurity incident response plan shall be implemented.

 

【实施建议】制定网络安全持续监控活动的流程。收集网络安全信息，判定是否和产品相关，如相关，进行漏洞分析和风险评估，进行漏洞管理。如下图示例：



以上UNECE R155的要求，能被ISO/SAE 21434的内容覆盖。

 

如下两点，ISO/SAE 21434标准中没有直接相关的内容。需要特别留意：


UNECE R155标准要求：

7.2.2.3 cyber threats and vulnerabilities which require are sponse from the vehicle manufacturer shall be mitigated within a reasonable time frame.


【实施建议】对于网络安全威胁和漏洞的处理，应在一个合理的时间内，及时处理。

 

7.2.2.4 The vehicle manufacturer shall demonstrate that the processes will ensure that the monitoring shall be continual. This shall:

(b) Include the capability to analyse and detect cyber threats, vulnerabilities and cyber-attacks from vehicle data and vehicle logs. This capability shall respect paragraph 1.3. and the privacy rights of car owners or drivers, particularly with respect to consent.


【实施建议】网络信息的持续监控，应包括车辆数据和个人隐私的处理能力，并需满足相应的法规要求。

 

1.4  Automotive SPICE® for cybersecurity与UNECE R155的关系

UNECE没有明确要求Automotive SPICE® for Cybersecurity，但是提及”cybersecurity process risk shall be managed”。Automotive SPICE® for Cybersecurity标准可以为“process-related product risk is managed”的实施提供证据。
