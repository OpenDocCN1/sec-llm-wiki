突如其来的疫情打乱了我们的脚步，因疫情防控原因，大赛组委会将赛事按下暂停键。在这期间，特别感谢所有参赛选手对赛事延期的理解与支持。

  

今天，承载着大家的关注和期望

我们迎来了**赛事重启**！

经过组委会多方确认

第五届“强网”拟态防御国际精英挑战赛

将于**12月15-18日**重磅归来！

线上举办

不见不散

  

目前，赛事筹备工作正在有条不紊的推进中，我们相信即使面对重重困难，大家仍然坚守本心，斗志昂扬，带着对CTF的热爱，让我们重聚紫金之巅！重启巅峰之战！

  

  

  

  

The sudden epidemic has disrupted our steps. For the reason of epidemic prevention and control, the organizing committee of the competition pressed the pause button. During this period, I would like to thank all the contestants for their understanding and support for the postponement of the event.

  

Today, we are carrying your concerns and expectations

We welcome the restart of the event!

It has been confirmed by the organizing committee

The Fifth "Strong Network" Mimic Defense International Elite Challenge

Will be back on December 15-18!

See you later

  

At present, the preparations for the event are progressing in an orderly way. We believe that even in the face of many difficulties, everyone will still stick to their roots and keep high morale. With love for CTF, let's reunite with Zijin! Restart the peak battle!
