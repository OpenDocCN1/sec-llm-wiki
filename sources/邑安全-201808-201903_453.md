## MyFileUploader

> This is my new file uploader server. I bet you can't hack it!  
> http://chal.noxale.com:8079

随便拖一个文件上传，得到：

![](../Images/bc698d5de505a17db4457525b63e7b14.png)

直接访问该目录，发现可列目录且存在名为`Don't open`的文件夹，打开发现htaccess：
