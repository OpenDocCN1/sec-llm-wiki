**陈楠**

  

长亭科技安全服务中心

**Hack Out of The Box: Discovering 10+ Vulnerabilities in VirtualBox**

  

This talk presents how we found some vulnerabilities in VirtualBox. In particular, we designed a special fuzzer for the virtualbox, which [effectively found 10+ vulnerabilities](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzIwNDA2NDk5OQ==&mid=2651376922&idx=4&sn=634803e32ba1fa74903c01f569e02f76&chksm=8d39bc92ba4e358476d02afa5515b483c4f5391b3ca51853054a857b1a2e4501092e8a6dd653&scene=21#wechat_redirect).  

We will introduce the design idea and implement skills used in the fuzzer, and disclose the details of some vulnerabilities. **We will introduce in detail how to exploit these vulnerabilities, including some new exploit primitives. **Finally , We will demonstrate the exploit of box escpae.

**一句话推荐：**你见过这样批量挖漏洞的吗？  

  

  

**张焱宇（flyyy）**

  

长亭科技安全服务中心

**A QEMU Black Box Escape via USB Device**

  

In this paper, we briefly introduce the Qemu-KVM architecture at first, then we propose the new concept of a black box escape.** After an in-depth analysis of ****[CVE-2020-14364](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzIwMDk1MjMyMg==&mid=2247485565&idx=1&sn=0bf3af464d79358261233bc34913abaa&chksm=96f41710a1839e06f67bf128ad4622480cdc91213006f8b00c48be241af9ec7347030eb1d37a&scene=21#wechat_redirect)****, we present our approach to achieving a black box escape of a QEMU virtual machine.**
