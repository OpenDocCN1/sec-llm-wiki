“黑客圈”大咖Jeff Moss现身活动

本次邀请到Blackhat创始人Jeff Moss 与 LAZADA安全部负责人Alan 共同开幕致辞。  

Jeff Moss来到现场时，在场的黑客们都在欢呼～

![](../Images/fa1273c543f75688afc5d2896e0eaad1.png)

Jeff Moss 在黑客圈有多牛？

他被媒体誉为“全球身价最高黑客”，不仅是Blackhat、Defcon两大世界知名安全会议的创始人，还是美国国土安全咨询委员会顾问、ICANN（互联网名称与数字地址分配机构)首席安全官！

JeffMoss在“黑客圈”有着非常卓越的影响力，有他的到来，现场的小伙伴们看到Jeff的到来，眼神都发出了闪亮的光芒！

![](../Images/543a270bde80286a02b1121346256afa.png)

LAZADA安全负责人Alan在致辞中说到，这是Alibaba Security Meetup首次在新加坡LAZADA总部举办，欢迎每一位来参加活动的朋友们，希望大家可以在这次活动中认识更多志同道合的伙伴，共同探讨交流技术，享受音乐，享受美食！

  

**The Blackhat founder Jeff Moss and Alan delivered the opening speech jointly！**

Jeff Moss is acclaimed by the media as "the most valuable hacker in the world" who is the founder of two world-renowned security conferences, namely Blackhat and Defcon. 

He is also a member of the U.S. Department of Homeland Security Advisory Council, and the Chief Security Officer of the Internet Corporationfor Assigned Names and Numbers (ICANN).

Jeff Moss has aconsiderable influence within the hacker community. His presence drew much attention of security practitioners and enthusiaststo this event.

LAZADA security director Alan said in his speech，this is our first event in Singapore where security practitioners and enthusiasts will join each other and share their thoughts about information security domain. Welcome to  Alibaba Security Happyhour! Games, gifts, shows, music, and food were available in our event. Hope everyone enjoy yourselves at Alibaba Security Happyhour!

Alibaba Secunity Meetup

Alibaba Secunity Meetup是由LAZADA和ASRC共同举办的安全活动。每月会在LAZADA visitor center举办一次。

Alibaba Secunity Meetup的使命是为阿里安全全球业务打造国际黑客生态社区。

1、干货议题分享让你学习到网络安全的专业知识。

2、轻松愉快的happyhour让你结识志同道合的网络安全从业者及爱好者。

3、妙趣横生的happygame让你体验不一样的网络安全。

4、奖励丰厚的众测活动。

Alibaba Secunity Meetup is a security event jointly hosted by LAZADA and ASRC, which is held monthly at the LAZADA Visitor Center.  
The mission of this event is to establish a security community in Singapore for network security practitioners and enthusiasts to communicate.  
1\. Topical sharing sessions allow you to learn professional knowledge about network security.  
2\. The relaxing HappyHour allows you to meet like-minded network security practitioners and enthusiasts.  
3\. The interesting and lively HappyGame allows you to experience a different network security world.  
4\. There are also public test activities with big rewards.

  

主办方介绍

**LAZADA Security**

LAZADA 是东南亚最大的购物平台。总部设立在新加坡。LAZADA有一个非常专业的安全团队，有很多优秀的安全技术人才，在保障LAZADA的网络安全。目前LAZADA安全正在和ASRC合作，共同在新加坡举办Alibaba Security Meetup活动 ，扩大LAZADA安全在新加坡乃至东南亚的影响力。

LAZADA is South East Asia's biggest shopping platform. To keep the data of its sellers and the customers safe and secure, LAZADA has adedicated and a very talented security team which keeps the bad guys at a distance. We are parterning with the ASRC and helping them to grow their presence in this region. After all, we are a part of one big Alibaba family.

  

**ASRC 阿里安全响应中心**

阿里安全响应中心（ASRC）是阿里巴巴的安全技术联络点（PoC），作为面向国际的网络安全合作平台，ASRC致力于建设公平可信、合作共赢的互联网安全生态圈，用繁荣的安全生态反哺安全技术建设，与安全伙伴一起为上亿用户的生命及财产安全保驾护航。

The Alibaba Security Response Center (ASRC) is Alibaba Security’s single point of contact for the outside world. A global-minded open plat form for cooperation on cybersecurity, the ASRC is dedicated to building a reliable cyber security ecosystem for the benefit of all. A mature security ecosystem is vital tosupport the development of security technologies as we work with our partners to safe guard the well being and financial security of hundreds of millions of users.
