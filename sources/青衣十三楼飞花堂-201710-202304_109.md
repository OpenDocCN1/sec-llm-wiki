Q1. Describe one time when you got sick. Describe how you got sick, how you felt, what you did to get better, and how long it took you to get well.  
  
A1. Ond day when I got back from school, mom found that I had a low fever, I felt alright, but the second day, I got really sick.  
  
At midnight, my mom used a temperature gun to check my body temperature, and I had a very high fever! We went to many hospitals, finally, at the last hospital, the doctor said I got an influenza B virus. I felt very unhappy just staying home, I took a lot of medicines, and after a week I got better and went back to school.  
  
I think we should always wear a mask when we go to a hospital, and be sure not to get too close to a people who had Covid-19!

A2. There are many things in the world that may attract us, and some may get us addicted, let's look at some examples.  
  
Many kids in the world are addicted to online games, many adults are very angry because their children don't have enough self-control, they will hit them, and the children will feel bad.  
  
Some grow-ups are also addicted to online shopping, and don't have enough time to work.  
  
But some are addicted to study which makes them smart. I think we should be addicted to good things which helps us, and not to bad one's which harm us.

Q1. Describe one of the traditions in your country. When does this happen, what do people do, and where did this tradition originate from?  
  
A1. In January first, we have the Spring Festival. People eat dumplings, set off firecrackers, paste spring festival couplets. Eating dumplings originates from the ming dynasty. Setting off firecrackers originates from the tang dynasty. Pasting spring festvial couplets originates from the late shu period.

In Dragon Boat Festival, we eat rice dumplings, doing dragon boat races to commemorate QU YUAN. I think the purpose of having traditions is to offer sacrifice to great people in our history.
