The Continuous Delivery Maturity Checklist

持续交付成熟度清单

译者：杜开亮、魏亚萍、景韵

原文出处为 http://www.dzone.com

Continuous Delivery is aboutcontinuously making software provide more value in the real world. And your CDprocess should itself  improve.

 持续交付使软件在现实世界中不断创造更多的价值并且你应当不断地改进持续交付的流程

 This checklist will help youset delivery goals and find ways to deliver software more effectively at bothtechnical and organizational levels.

这份清单能帮你设定交付目标，并且帮助你在技术层面及组织层面上更有效率地交付软件

 TO USE: simply divide thenumber of items you checked off by 47 to get your maturityscore as a percent.

 使用方法：将已勾选的条目数量除以47（清单条目总数）就能够算出成熟度
