![](../Images/328996c39b8f82cef3def0b661c56173.png)

  

下面进行基本的信息收集，发现目标开启了22(ssh),与80(http):

  

![](../Images/f32d5094bc18b318fb0e7c3a508ce201.png)

  

Udp开放了163(snmp)

  

![](../Images/1286979a9682c46d7021ad90d0b8b6d9.png)

  

使用msf进行snmp枚举，获的部分信息，留存备用。

  

![](../Images/cf7b99f5dadcb1f845e20e24c1c60f0c.png)

![](../Images/7f91ad478ae1629118f8ee6c38015890.png)

![](../Images/7589362d646bf50a93347a2900274597.png)

  

获得目录

  

![](../Images/07e90fc312b497e501170d06c9a3a682.png)

![](../Images/d536ef79df48925c0aed6040543f000c.png)

![](../Images/3ca767e9db062d39cb1483ae09753f6a.png)

![](../Images/2e467f3a07031db9a2d7bdf4622b1273.png)

![](../Images/bea96bc21ca904b3210f11f2e00c5885.png)

获的第三个flag，。那么接下来怎么办呢？突然发现每一个flag后面都带有to root，那么把这些东西都连接起来，搜索看看

  

![](../Images/cba1b4a99871168b1052f927f58181c1.png)

  

是md5的值。可能就是我们root的密码，先登录已经获取的账户

![](../Images/8c95118d48cc3524ef97a60a18726156.png)
