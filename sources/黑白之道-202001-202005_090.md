# Windows下批量卸载安装补丁

> 原文：[http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?\_\_biz=MzAxMjE3ODU3MQ==&mid=2650461120&idx=3&sn=34981381a0ad8552b7b6c4f8d998f00a&chksm=83bbb624b4cc3f32c283013ffab316e840711c1bcc52aa9ac383358e6b9c0b77ed6419b5b296&scene=27#wechat\_redirect](http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzAxMjE3ODU3MQ==&mid=2650461120&idx=3&sn=34981381a0ad8552b7b6c4f8d998f00a&chksm=83bbb624b4cc3f32c283013ffab316e840711c1bcc52aa9ac383358e6b9c0b77ed6419b5b296&scene=27#wechat_redirect)

![](../Images/8e5d77bf7751a0da7834b3686fe0b4b6.png)  

1\. 以管理员的身份运行cmd，输入命令：wmic qfe get hotfixid >> c:\\list.txt

  

![](../Images/eab4595f00d0f4082959cba298748ccc.png)

  

2. 打开C盘下的list.txt

  

![](../Images/1ddfd21701ab1c73fcdb46b9b8d29094.png)

  

3. 将第一行的内容删除后保存

  

![](../Images/ec5973c299951b0eaaf8bfbdf1081f44.png)

  

4.回到cmd，输入命令：for /f %i in ('type c:\\list.txt') do echo wusa /uninstall /kb:%i /quiet /norestart >> c:\\uninstall.cmd

  

![](../Images/137580d78aef85b6cbd518864a2991cc.png)

  

![](../Images/054ed3fc0f84046b34583349aca1955f.png)

  

5. 选择uninstall文件右键“编辑”

  

6\. 打开文档之后，需要将kb:KB全部替换成KB：

  

![](../Images/dc2bcdd7181a7da513302752fb188d2f.png)

  

7. 单击文本窗口上的【编辑】，选择【替换】，替换完成后保存

  

![](../Images/6865d758ffd919bc33b2b4fa089d7ddf.png)

  

8. 双击运行uninstall.cmd，等待运行结束，重启计算机就可以了

  

![](../Images/9f402c757d61948f1be1d60af085400b.png)

![](../Images/4d9f27fae52f64177a05b19d00144c73.png "微信公众号文章素材之分割线大全")
