**3.解决方案配置**首先勾选release  

![](../Images/97b378373273e73bfab58fd25ea688d9.png)

![](../Images/ed9e894b830cdb60586510a8c3cb9ba1.png)

![](../Images/9838ee1746a6a9ce22908935c81ade6a.png)
